## 2. Understanding

PASS. Crocs, Inc. is a Delaware footwear group that designs casual shoes under two brands, has them made by third-party factories, mainly in Vietnam, and sells them about half wholesale and half direct-to-consumer in 85+ countries.

- **Crocs Brand** (82% of FY2025 revenue): molded Croslite clogs, sandals and Jibbitz charms sold to retailers, distributors and consumers worldwide; the category-defining clog, two decades old, growing slowly.
- **HEYDUDE Brand** (18%): canvas and EVA casual loafers sold mainly in North America to wholesale accounts and consumers; acquired February 2022, shrinking until the third quarter of 2026.
