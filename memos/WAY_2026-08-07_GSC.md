# WAY — Waystar Holding Corp.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS · Past WEAK · Future ADEQUATE · SF FAIL · Cheapness EXPENSIVE → **AVOID** (two gates: Past and SF).
A genuinely sticky healthcare-payments software franchise growing OI/share at ~28%/yr sits on an LBO balance sheet that poisons the returns record: $5.3bn of goodwill and intangibles against $5.8bn of assets pins worst-of-four ROCE at 2.8%, and the board pays the CEO $30–37m a year against owner earnings that cap the P17 ceiling at ~$5.6m — a hard strike in both proxy years on record. What would flip it: nothing near-term. The comp strike needs a post-IPO-grant normalization visible in the 2027 proxy, and the returns record needs years of OI growth against a static capital base; price is a tenth of the problem — the band sits ~90% below the quote.

## 2. Understanding
Waystar sells cloud software that moves money between US healthcare providers and payers — claims clearing, eligibility checks, patient payments, denial management, and (since the 2025 Iodine acquisition) AI clinical documentation — on subscription-plus-volume contracts to ~30,000 provider organizations (hospitals, health systems, physician practices are the paying customers), in the healthcare revenue-cycle-management industry; a Delaware C-corp assembled by private equity (Navicure/ZirMed 2017, eSolutions 2020; EQT, CPPIB, Bain and Advent nominees still seat the board), HQ Lehi/Louisville, ~$1.1bn revenue, NASDAQ-listed June 2024. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **2.78%** (end-wc; range 2.78–3.37%), worst-of-four FCFROCE **2.65%** — a fraction of the 10% ADEQUATE floor, and the label decides nothing the level doesn't: even FY2025's best-year 4.5% is a third of the floor. The engine is real (OI/sh +28.2%/yr geometric FY21→25, FCF/sh +19.2%/yr, L/E 49%, deleveraged from $2.2bn to $1.5bn debt post-IPO) but it runs inside a capital base bought at private-equity prices. Trend is up and monotonic since FY2022; no P4 decline. **WEAK** — level, not trend.

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth: customer PASS (inferred — ~30k fragmented provider clients; no >10%-customer disclosure located; verify sentence at first REVIEW), supplier PASS (commodity cloud infrastructure, ready alternatives). Forces: customer power WEAK (fragmented, prohibitive switching costs), supplier power WEAK, substitutes WEAK (doing-without impossible; rip-and-replace costs high), new entrants **STRONG** — Epic's expansion from EHR into payer connectivity crosses the integration barrier natively and is the one force with teeth. Tally 1 strong, contained. Moat: switching costs (deep EHR/workflow integration, multi-year contracts, 99%+ net revenue retention class), stable-to-strengthening. Market growth YES (claims volume, denial complexity, AI spend). **ADEQUATE.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Healthcare claims-volume cyclicality is low, but payer-mix policy shocks (Medicaid redeterminations, RCM insourcing mandates) are not.
