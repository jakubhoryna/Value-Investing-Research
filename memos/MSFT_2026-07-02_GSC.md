# MSFT — Microsoft Corporation: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

One of the highest-quality past records in this register: ROCE_xc never below ~40% in ten years, OI/share compounding at ~20% p.a., leverage falling throughout. What stands between here and a BUY is purely price — at ~$385 the stock sits at 3–5× every framework threshold even after a 22% YTD 2026 drawdown on AI-capex anxiety. ## 2. Understanding

Microsoft is a US-listed, single-class Washington corporation that develops and sells enterprise and consumer software (Windows, Microsoft 365, Dynamics), hyperscale cloud infrastructure and AI platform services (Azure, Copilot, the OpenAI partnership), gaming (Xbox, Activision Blizzard), devices and advertising (LinkedIn, Bing), paid for predominantly by businesses and institutions worldwide on subscription and consumption contracts, with revenue roughly evenly split US/international; it is the world's largest software company by revenue and the #2 cloud provider, still growing double-digits. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Exceptional. ROCE_xc ranged 39.7–92.9% across FY2016–FY2025 (≥15% in 10 of 10 years); FCFROCE_xc 22.1–92.9% (≥8% in all years). OI/share grew every single year, 5.3× over the decade (~20% CAGR); FCF/share 3.1×; TBV/share 3.5×. L/E fell from 146% to 80%, breaching the 200% flag only once (FY2018, 213%, against 48% ROCE_xc — passes on exceptional returns). FCFROCE has declined for three years (52% → 22%) purely because capex tripled to $64.6B in the AI buildout — treated as an investment phase, not deterioration, per the established capex-cycle judgment (see Appendix C). **STRONG.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASS: hundreds of millions of customers, none >10% of revenue; supplier base broad except concentrated AI-GPU supply (Nvidia), partially mitigated by AMD and in-house Maia silicon. Forces MIXED: customer power LOW, supplier power MEDIUM (GPUs), substitutes MEDIUM (agentic AI could disintermediate the application layer it dominates), entrants LOW at hyperscale, rivalry HIGH (AWS, Google in cloud and AI). Moats: switching costs + ingrainedness (enterprise workflow embedment, Entra/Teams/Office estate), network effects, and hyperscale cost — dominant and stable, with AI both an attach opportunity and the first credible threat to the moat's mechanism in decades. Market growth YES, attracting an unprecedented capital arms race — Marshall's caveat applies squarely. Per the moat-decides rule (ASML/TSMC/GOOGL precedent: dominant moat + credible-but-unrealised threat floors at ADEQUATE, MIXED forces caps below STRONG): **ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Comp: Nadella FY2025 total $96.5M, ~87% performance stock — large absolutely, ~0.08% of OI, genuinely performance-linked. Single share class; no control structure; Ballmer (~4%) is an aligned passive holder. RPTs: none material. Buybacks: continuous ($16–33B/yr), net share reduction ~7% over the decade, but executed at 15–30× EV/OI — well above framework-conservative value; caveat, not strike, given real net reduction and no leverage abuse (META/GOOGL precedent). Dividends growing ~10%/yr, ~25% payout, retention justified by 30%+ ROCE. Activision ($69B) was pricey but strategic, cash-funded. **PASS** (buyback-price caveat documented).

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- AI capex super-cycle (FY2025 $64.6B, FY2026 run-rate far higher) may earn returns structurally below the legacy 40%+ ROCE_xc, permanently derating the quality record.
- Agentic AI could disintermediate the Office/application layer — the first substitute threat aimed at the ingrainedness moat itself.
- OpenAI dependency and the restructured (~27%) stake concentrate both upside and governance/strategic risk in a single external partner.
- Cloud rivalry with AWS and Google plus Nvidia GPU concentration squeeze both pricing and input costs simultaneously.
- Pending securities class action over AI-capex disclosures; 15,000+ layoffs signal margin defense under strain.
