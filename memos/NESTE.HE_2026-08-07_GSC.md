# NESTE.HE — Neste Oyj: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS · Past **WEAK** (ΔOI/sh −8.8%/yr and ΔEPS −18.8%/yr across the window — the growth limb fails; FCFROCE 6.6%) · Future **WEAK** (two strong forces: demonstrated entry flood + EV substitution) · SF PASS (1 flag — capital-allocation CAUTION; state caveat unpaired) · Cheapness EXPENSIVE (FY25 EV/OI ~50–54×; the no-cash band bound is *negative*) → **AVOID (Past and Future gates)**.

Neste is the register's cleanest case of a moat that entry destroyed: the renewable-diesel pioneer earned 16–29% ROCE for years, bet the balance sheet on a capacity supercycle (debt €1.1bn → €5.2bn), and then US refiners flooded the market exactly as EV adoption began shrinking the road-fuel pool — margins, profits and the dividend collapsed. 2025–26 shows recovery (the stock has doubled off €14), but the decade's per-share arithmetic is a wealth destruction and the industry's structure — policy-made demand, commodity-capacity supply — no longer supports a durable-moat claim. Flip needs a full margin cycle proving the glut cleared *and* per-share compounding restored; nothing shorter re-tests the gates.

## 2. Understanding
Neste refines renewable diesel and sustainable aviation fuel from waste fats and vegetable oils (Rotterdam, Singapore, Porvoo, the Martinez US JV) plus a legacy Finnish oil-refining and fuel-retail arm — selling road- and aviation-fuel volumes whose demand is largely created by blending mandates and credits, to wholesalers, airlines and its own station network; Espoo-HQ'd, Finnish state ~36%, the former Neste Oil. **PASS.**

## 4. Quality — future
