# 8002.T — Marubeni Corporation: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS · Past ADEQUATE (substitution rule: worst normalized ROE 12.3%) · Future ADEQUATE · SF PASS (1 flag — capital-allocation CAUTION) · Cheapness EXPENSIVE → **WATCH**.

Marubeni is worth owning as a deleveraged trading-and-investment conglomerate that compounded book value ~12%/yr and EPS ~16%/yr across a decade containing one loss year, now guiding profit higher again. What stands between here and a BUY: the stock has re-rated from ~0.7× to 1.84× book and 14.8× earnings — nearly 2× the 8×-NI anchor. The verdict flips toward the band on a commodity/Japan de-rating; it flips to AVOID if ROE settles back below 10% (FY20-style resource impairments) or the now-live buyback programme hardens into paying ever-higher multiples without any stated price discipline.

## 2. Understanding
Marubeni is a 168-year-old Japanese sōgō shōsha — legally a trading company, operationally an investment holding over ~15 business groups — that earns through commodity trading margins (grain, energy, metals, chemicals) and, mostly, through dividends and equity-method profits from owned operating businesses (Helena US agri-inputs, US grain handling, power generation, aircraft leasing, food distribution), serving business customers worldwide from Tokyo, with earnings concentrated in agri-food and resources. **PASS** — a stretch by parts, not incomprehension; the parts are individually plain.

## 3. Quality — past record
**Substitution rule applied (§2·1A):** consolidated OI excludes ¥338bn of associate profits (62% of pre-tax economics below the OI line) — the OI/CE machinery is structurally dark; ROE replaces ROCE, FCFROCE n.m. Worst normalized ROE (Σ NI ÷ Σ equity, end-basis) **12.3%** (beginning-basis 14.0%) — ADEQUATE (≥10%), not STRONG (<15%). Path: ~9–13% pre-2020, **−13.0% FY20** (Gavilon-era impairments), then 18.9% twin peaks FY22–23 fading to 12.5% FY26. EPS geo-mean **+15.8%**, BV/sh **+11.8%** (loss year flagged). Leverage: net IBD/equity **0.43×**, gross 0.55×, down from 2.1× (Mar-16) — a decade of deleveraging. **ADEQUATE** — the loss year and sub-15% normalized ROE deny STRONG.

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth: PASS both sides — thousands of customers and suppliers across unrelated value chains; no ≥10% concentration conceivable at group level. Forces (called on the two dominant earnings clusters): agri-food distribution — customer power WEAK (fragmented farmers/retail); resources/energy legs — **substitutes STRONG** (energy transition directly attacks coal/LNG rents); supplier power WEAK (producers need offtake networks); entrants WEAK (century-scale networks, balance-sheet barriers). Tally **1 strong, contained** by portfolio breadth. Moat: **Ingrainedness** — the shōsha position in Japanese industrial supply chains; stable. Market growth MIXED (agri volumes grow; resource legs secularly contested). **ADEQUATE.**
