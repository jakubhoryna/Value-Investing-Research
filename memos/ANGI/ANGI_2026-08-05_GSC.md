# ANGI — Angi Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; past quality WEAK; future quality WEAK; shareholder-friendliness FAIL; cheapness FAIR — **AVOID on three gates**.

Angi sells consumer job leads to small contractors. Over ten years it earned cumulative operating income of **−$110.4m** on $12.1bn of revenue, and revenue has fallen three years running at −16.4% a year from the FY2022 peak. The balance sheet is not what stands in the way — net debt $249m, and at $4.82 the $196m market capitalisation is roughly the cash pile. The business is. Paying Pros fell from 111,000 to 106,000 in two quarters, demand is rented from platforms Angi does not control, and $235.2m of U.S. goodwill and trade names was written off in Q2-26. A verdict change needs two years of stable revenue with rising operating income and pay proportionate to owner earnings.

## 2. Understanding

Angi Inc. is a Delaware-incorporated, Denver-headquartered digital marketplace that sells consumer job leads, advertising and subscription packages to roughly 106,000 small independent home-service contractors ("Pros") — who are the paying customers — while providing matching, ratings and pre-priced booking free to homeowners, operating the Angi, Angie's List, HomeAdvisor and Handy brands across more than 500 service categories in the United States (88% of revenue) plus Austria, Canada, France, Germany, Italy, the Netherlands and the UK, as an asset-light advertising-services business spun off from IAC on 31 March 2025 whose revenue has fallen 42% from its 2022 peak. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **−1.3%**, worst-of-four FCFROCE **3.9%** — both below the ADEQUATE floor. Operating income was negative in five of ten years; the FY2025 recovery to $65.4m came from cutting marketing spend by $94m, not from growth. Free cash flow aggregates to $587.1m but is front-loaded into FY2018–FY2020. Per-share growth is unreadable — operating income per share crosses zero four times — and tangible book value per share is negative. Leverage is the one clean metric: L/E 81%. **WEAK** — normalized returns are negative and the trend is decline, not cycle.

## 4. Quality — future

Customer breadth passes: no single Pro approaches 10% of revenue, and U.S. lead revenue of $587.1m is explicitly a high volume of low-dollar transactions. Supplier breadth **fails** on the effective-supplier reading — the 10-K names dependence on Google, Apple, Meta and TikTok for traffic, selling and marketing is $507.5m (55.3% of operating costs), and no ready alternative to search-sourced demand exists. All four forces call STRONG: Pro switching costs are near zero; platforms price the traffic and Google competes for the same query; word-of-mouth referral is named by Angi as its biggest competitor, with AI assistants now a further substitute; entry barriers are low. Moat: brand plus review corpus, **eroding**. Market MIXED. **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Top pay (Kip, $5.655m) breaches 5% of the lower of levered FCF and net income on **both** reads — 2.6× fiscal-year, 8.4× trailing-three-year. Hard strike. Director totals top out at $355k against a ~$672k stop; insider ownership 3.3%. Related-party dealing with IAC is extensive but transparent, at cost and terminating — no flag. Capital allocation is **CAUTION**: seven repurchase years, $351.3m, not one tranche at either bargain line — but the programme is exhausted, nothing was bought in 2026, and $100m of notes was retired at a discount. **FAIL** — P17 strike, P19 flag alongside.
