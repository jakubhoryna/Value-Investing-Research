# IEX — IDEX Corporation: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS, Past WEAK, Future STRONG, shareholder-friendliness PASS (one capital-allocation flag), cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID (Past)**.

IDEX owns about fifty niche engineering businesses whose products are specified into customers' equipment and plants, with no customer above 3% of sales and gross margins near 44–45% for a decade. The past-performance gate fails on direction, not level. Return on capital has fallen for three straight years, from 16.6% in FY2022 to 11.0% in FY2025, as capital employed grew by about $1.8bn on acquisitions (Muon, Iridian, Mott) while operating income went from $751m to $699m. Price is a second, distant obstacle: the band sits near $39–48 against $232.99. The verdict moves to WATCH if FY2026 arrests the return decline. The first-half run-rate points that way but does not yet prove it.

## 2. Understanding
IDEX designs and makes differentiated, engineered niche components and equipment: precision fluidics, optics and porous-metal parts (Health & Science, 43% of FY2025 sales); positive-displacement pumps, valves, flow meters and sewer-inspection systems (Fluid & Metering, 35%); and fire pumps, rescue tools, paint-tinting dispensers and stainless banding (Fire & Safety/Diversified, 22%). It sells them to instrument and semiconductor-equipment OEMs, process plants, utilities and municipalities, fire-apparatus makers and fire departments, and paint retailers, directly and through distributors. It is a decentralised, acquisition-built Delaware holding company of over fifty operating subsidiaries, headquartered in Northbrook, Illinois, with 49% of sales outside the US. It was formed in 1987, is NYSE-listed, and had $3.5bn of FY2025 sales. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
**WEAK.** The worst-of-four average ROCE is 13.6% (end-of-period, cash in) and the best is 17.2%. Worst FCFROCE is 11.2%. On level alone that is adequate. The deciding fact is the trend. Beginning-of-period ROCE fell three consecutive years: 16.6% → 14.4% → 12.3% → 11.0%. FCFROCE fell in FY2024 and FY2025, and the five-year halves fell from 15.4% to 12.5% (ROCE) and 12.9% to 10.1% (FCFROCE). From FY2021 to FY2025, operating income rose $62m while capital employed rose $2.2bn, an incremental return near 3%. Per-share growth clears the floor: geometric OI/share +5.2%, FCF/share +7.3%, BV/share +11.2%. OI/share fell in FY2023 and FY2024. Liabilities to equity were 72% at FY2025 and 73% at 30 June 2026.

## 4. Quality — future
**STRONG.** Breadth passes on both sides. No customer exceeded 3% of FY2025 sales, and substantially all purchased materials are available from a large number of independent sources. All four forces are weak in all three segments. The products are specified or certified into the buyer's equipment at a small share of its cost, in niches too small to draw scaled entrants. The moat is switching costs from spec-in and installed-base replacement demand. It is stable: gross margin was 45.0% in FY2018 and 44.2–44.5% in FY2023–25, and operating margin stayed between 19.5% and 23.6% in every window year. Market growth is mixed: semiconductor, data-centre and space demand is rising, while pump, agriculture and fire volumes fell in 2025.
