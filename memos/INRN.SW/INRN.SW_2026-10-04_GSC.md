## 2. Understanding

Interroll makes and sells standardised conveyor components and sub-systems: drum motors and roller drives (35% of FY2025 sales), conveyor rollers (21%), conveyor and sorter modules (36%) and pallet-flow storage (9%). Its customers are the system integrators and OEMs who build material-handling lines for parcel carriers, e-commerce warehouses, airports and food plants, about 18,000 invoiced accounts. It is a vertically integrated component manufacturer, headquartered in Switzerland, selling 62% in EMEA and 28% in the Americas, listed since 1997, mid-sized (CHF 514m sales), and currently adding acquisitions under a CEO appointed in 2025. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized ROCE is 20.3% and FCFROCE 12.2% (both end-of-period, cash-in). Per-share growth over the window clears the floor: OI/sh +6.3% and FCF/sh +9.8% geometric, the latter flattered by a weak FY2015 base. Leverage is negligible: L/E 22%, and lease liabilities are the only debt. The trend decides. Operating income peaked at CHF 105m in FY2022 and fell three straight years to CHF 72m, while cash piled up to CHF 216m. ROCE_wc fell in each of FY2022–FY2025, and FCFROCE_wc fell in FY2024 and FY2025. Cash-out ROCE held at 24–26% since FY2023, which is the counter-reading logged in C. **WEAK — P4 sustained-decline route.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth passes. No customer exceeds 10% of sales per the IFRS 8 note, and the inputs (steel, bearings, motors, electronics) have ready alternative vendors. Forces: customer power WEAK, because accounts are fragmented and material cost fell from 45% of sales in FY2018 to 33% in FY2025, so pricing held. Supplier power WEAK. Substitutes STRONG: autonomous mobile robots and goods-to-person storage replace fixed conveyor runs in warehouses, though parcel hubs and airports stay conveyor-native. New entrants WEAK, held back by scale, a global service footprint and integrator qualification. The moat is cost: scale production of standardised platforms. It looks stable, and the material-cost ratio is the evidence. Market growth is MIXED. **ADEQUATE.**
