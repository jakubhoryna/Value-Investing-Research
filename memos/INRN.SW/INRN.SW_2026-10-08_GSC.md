## 2. Understanding

Interroll makes and sells standardised conveyor components and sub-systems: drum motors and roller drives (35% of FY2025 sales), conveyor rollers (21%), conveyor and sorter modules (36%) and pallet-flow storage (9%). It sells them to about 18,000 system integrators and OEMs who build material-handling lines for parcel carriers, e-commerce warehouses, airports and food plants. It is a vertically integrated component manufacturer, headquartered in Switzerland, selling 62% in EMEA and 28% in the Americas, listed since 1997 and mid-sized (CHF 514m sales). It is adding bolt-on acquisitions under a CEO appointed in 2025. **PASS.** Lens STD; one reportable segment, so no group rules apply.
