## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS (four material units); Past WEAK (3.8% ROCE); Future ADEQUATE (unit customer conc.); Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (compensation); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID** (Past + SF).

Knowit is a Stockholm-based IT consultancy that builds and runs software, digital services, embedded systems and data and management advice for Nordic public agencies and companies. It grew by buying other consultancies, above all Cybercom in 2021, and its capital employed quadrupled while operating income stalled and then fell: the worst-of-four ROCE is 6.7% over ten years and 3.8% over five, and a SEK 399m goodwill impairment made 2025 operating income negative. The cash record is steadier (MCap/FCF 11.0 at SEK 120.00). The CEO's 2024 pay was 12% of that year's net profit, a hard pay strike. With negative trailing operating income no trigger price exists.
