## 2. Understanding
Warby Parker designs and sells prescription eyeglasses, sunglasses, contacts and eye exams — differentiated branded eyewear at a value price point — direct to US/Canadian consumers (the retail customer pays; vision-insurance reimbursement growing) through ~300 own stores, e-commerce and in-house optical labs, in the optical retail industry; a founder-led (co-CEOs Blumenthal/Gilboa) Delaware C-corp with a 10:1 dual-class structure, NYC HQ, direct-listed September 2021, ~$872m revenue, ~10× smaller than the EssilorLuxottica vertical it undercut. **PASS.**
