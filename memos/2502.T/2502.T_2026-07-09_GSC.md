# 2502.T — Asahi Group Holdings, Ltd.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Gate | Result |
|---|---|
| Understanding | PASS |
| Past | WEAK |
| Future | ADEQUATE |
| Shareholder-friendliness | FAIL |
| Cheapness | EXPENSIVE |
| **FINAL** | **AVOID** |

Asahi is a competent global brewer with real brands — Super Dry, Peroni, Pilsner Urquell, Grolsch, Carlton — and demonstrated pricing power through premiumization. The verdict is AVOID on two independent gates. First, returns on capital are structurally mediocre: ROCE ≈ 6% against a 10% adequacy floor, because a decade of large debt-funded acquisitions has buried ¥3.35tn of goodwill and acquired brands (62% of assets) in the capital base, and returns on that total capital are thin. Second, shareholder-friendliness fails on capital allocation — the ¥1.14tn all-debt CUB purchase at 16–18× pre-deal operating profit (a business whose profit was already falling) plus buybacks executed above the discipline line. Cheapness is moot: at ¥1,627 the enterprise trades at ~14× clean operating profit versus a 7× threshold. A structural change in returns and allocation, not a lower price, is what this needs.

## 2. Understanding

PASS — Asahi makes and sells branded beer, other alcoholic and soft drinks, and food to consumers across Japan, Europe, Oceania and Southeast Asia through retail and on-trade channels, and has grown chiefly by serial large debt-funded acquisition.

## 3. Quality — past record

Returns are the decisive failure. FY2024 ROCE 6.0% (with-cash) / 6.1% (ex-cash); FCFROCE 5.9% / 6.1%; FY2023 ≈ 5.6%. The normalized average across the sourced window sits ~5.5–6% — far below the 10% ADEQUATE floor and nowhere near 15% STRONG, on all four worst-of-four variants. Goodwill correctly stays in the capital base for a serial acquirer, and that is the point: Asahi paid high multiples, so returns on total capital employed are thin. Per-share growth is positive (OI/sh ≈ +8%/yr, NI/sh ≈ +8%/yr FY2021–24, both above the 3% floor) and leverage is acceptable (L/E 102% < 200%; net debt/EBITDA 2.5×). Growth funded by value-dilutive M&A does not rescue the returns level. **WEAK.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASS on both sides — no customer or supplier above 10% of a diversified ¥2.9tn group. Four forces: customer power WEAK (brands confer pricing power), supplier power WEAK (many commodity vendors), threat of substitutes **STRONG** (structurally declining beer volumes in Japan and Australia, younger cohorts drinking less, wine/spirits/RTD/no-alcohol substitution), new entrants WEAK (scale, brand, licensing barriers). Tally = 1 strong → judgment zone. The moat is Brand — Super Dry (~40% Japan share), Peroni, Pilsner Urquell, the CUB portfolio — stable, with premiumization offsetting volume erosion. Market MIXED (mature core, premium/emerging growth). The moat floors the gate. **ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Compensation is clean: CEO Katsuki ¥424m (~$2.7m) = 0.22% of net income, far below the 5%-of-NI hard strike (¥9.6bn) and the ~$40m scrutiny line; ISS governance score 1; no controlling shareholder, RPTs immaterial. The failure is capital allocation — two independent P19 flags. (1) The 2020 CUB acquisition: ¥1.14tn (A$16.0bn) enterprise value, 100% debt-funded, at 16.2–18.0× pre-deal operating profit (A$990m FY2018 / A$967m 3-yr avg / A$888m and *falling* FY2019) — above the 15× flag. (2) FY2024 buybacks executed across a ¥1,700–2,000 split-adjusted range, all above the ¥1,619 / 14×-EV/OI flag line; the ~150m-share programme from 2026 extends this. Two flags → **FAIL.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- FY2025 (reported 8 Jul 2026) was depressed by the September 2025 cyberattack — OI ¥185.9bn, net ¥121.6bn — plus impairments; on that distorted year EV/OI is ~20×, an artifact that resets with FY2026 recovery.
- Structural beer-volume decline in Japan and Australia is the core-market headwind; premiumization must keep outrunning it.
- Continued debt-funded M&A (EABL, ~$2.3bn in 2026) keeps loading goodwill and leverage and diluting returns on capital — the exact pattern behind the WEAK/FAIL.
- The ~150m-share buyback (2026+) reverses CUB-era dilution but is being executed above the cheap line.
- Cyberattack aftermath raises operational-resilience and information-security governance questions.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

To BUY, all must change: returns climb toward and through 10% ROCE (a multi-year deleveraging-plus-integration story, not a price move); capital allocation shows discipline (no more high-multiple deals; buybacks only below worth); and price falls to ~¥377 for cheapness. Past WEAK or SF FAIL alone holds AVOID regardless of price. Next review: FY2026 full-year results (~mid-February 2027), which reset the cyberattack-depressed OI basis; earlier only on a structural returns or allocation change. Not held — no zone.
