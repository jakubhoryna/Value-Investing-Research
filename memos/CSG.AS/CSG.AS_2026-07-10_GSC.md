# CSG.AS — CSG N.V. (Czechoslovak Group): GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | WEAK | WEAK | FAIL | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** |

Three gates fail independently. CSG is an impressive industrial build — revenue up 12× in four years to €6.7bn, 24% operating margins, a €17bn backlog — but it is a debt-funded acquisition machine selling a semi-commodity product to a handful of government customers, run at 477% liabilities-to-equity, with a related-party web around an 84.8% owner and a NATO-agency blacklisting of a subsidiary the IPO prospectus omitted. The −59% fall from the January high does not make it cheap: FY2025 FCF was −€470m and EV/OI sits at 10.7–11.6× against the 7× threshold. A flip needs years: L/E below 200%, customer breadth beyond the Ukraine/Czech MoDs, positive through-cycle FCF, a clean post-probe governance record. None is observable today.

## 2. Understanding

CSG is a Prague-headquartered, founder-controlled (84.8% Michal Strnad) defence holding listed in Amsterdam since January 2026, manufacturing large/medium-calibre artillery and tank ammunition, military vehicles, radars and defence electronics (Defence Systems, ~79% of revenue) and small-calibre ammunition under Federal, Remington, CCI and Fiocchi brands (Ammo+, ~20%), sold mainly to NATO-member defence ministries — Ukraine's and Czechia's MoDs alone were ~37% of 2025 revenue — plus US civilian retail, from plants in Czechia, Slovakia, Spain, Serbia, Italy, Germany and the US, its scale built largely by debt-funded acquisitions (Fiocchi 2022, Kinetic 2024). **PASS** — products and customers are plain; the comprehension risk is the conglomerate accretion speed, not the business.

## 3. Quality — past record

Only five EUR-presented IFRS years exist (2021–2025) — below P1, so STRONG is unavailable. Worst-of-four normalized ROCE is 17.8% (end, cash-in) — genuinely high — but worst-of-four FCFROCE is 3.2%: profits are absorbed by inventory and receivables, and FY2025 FCF was −€470m. Growth is explosive (OI +136%/yr geometric) but acquisition-driven and unpriced per share pre-IPO. The gate fails on leverage: L/E 476.5% at FY2025 (211% → 646% → 579% → 564% → 477%), far above the 200% P5 flag, and P6 does not hold — the borrowing funded acquisitions and working capital, not under-priced repurchases, and FCF metrics are not "great." Post-IPO Q1-2026 L/E ≈265% still breaches. **WEAK** (P4: leverage breach without P6 conditions).

## 4. Quality — future

Customer breadth **FAILS**: Ukraine's MoD (€1,343m, 19.9% of revenue) and the Czech MoD (€1,174m, 17.4%) each exceed the 10% line. The captive-customer waiver is refused: NATO-standard ammunition is multi-sourced, switching costs are not prohibitive — NSPA kept procuring elsewhere while suspending CSG's Spanish plant — and the Ukraine concentration is conflict-driven, not structural. Forces: customer power STRONG (monopsony ministries, tenders, cancellable frameworks); supplier power STRONG but being bought out (energetic-materials chokepoint — hence Walsrode and Alzchem); substitutes a live signal (cheap drones vs heavy shells); new entrants dulled by permits and installed capacity. Moat: Government-type (licences, clearances, qualified-supplier status) plus scale — real but cyclical. Market growth YES today, with visible peace-scenario reversal risk. **WEAK** (breadth FAIL; 2 strong forces corroborate).

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

P17: no individual figure disclosed; 195 key managers aggregate €35.1m (avg ~€180k) against a 5%-of-NI line of ≈€34.7m — no plausible individual strike; the FCF leg is negative (logged artifact). Buybacks: none — P19 n/a. Acquisition audit (triggered, TBV −€298m): Kinetic at $2,225m ≈ 5.7× pre-deal OI proxy (FY2024 adj. EBITDA $416m) — passes. But two independent flags force FAIL under §2·1C: (1) **RPT scale and timing** — €296m receivables from the ultimate owner's sphere, including pre-IPO disposals of subsidiaries (YTARA SPV et al.) to owner entities at appraised prices, €16.3m gain booked; (2) **documented governance negative** — NSPA blacklisted subsidiary FMG in July 2025 amid NATO's corruption probe, the suspension was extended indefinitely, and the 728-page January 2026 prospectus omitted it. The 84.8% control and 80%-secondary IPO (€3.0bn to the owner) are the logged caveat. **FAIL (flag pair)**.
