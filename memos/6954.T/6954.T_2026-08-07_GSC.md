## 2. Understanding
Fanuc designs and manufactures factory-automation control: CNC systems and servo motors that run the world's machine tools (~half the global market), industrial robots (the yellow arms), and robomachines (compact machining centres/injection moulders), plus the lifetime service that follows every unit — sold to machine-tool builders and manufacturers worldwide from its vertically-integrated Yamanashi campus; 54-year-old, famously secretive, debt-free. **PASS.**
