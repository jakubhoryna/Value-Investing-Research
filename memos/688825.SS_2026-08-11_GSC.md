# 688825.SS — CXMT Corporation: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS · Past WEAK · Future WEAK · SF PASS (one P19 CAUTION flag) · Cheapness EXPENSIVE → **AVOID** (first failing gate: Past).

CXMT is China's only volume DRAM IDM (global #4, ~7.7% Q4-2025 share) and a strategic asset — but the framework asks whether the *minority equity* at ¥51.88 is a good business bought cheap, and every gate that can fail, fails. The audited record is three disclosed years: two deep loss years (total NI −¥19.2bn, −¥9.1bn) and one thin first profit (+¥7.1bn) delivered entirely by the H2-2025 DRAM price spike; free cash flow has never been positive against a ¥183bn fixed-asset build-out. The product is a pure commodity sold through a concentrated distributor channel (top-5 = 67% of revenue), the tool supply chain sits under US export control, and minority interests at fab level took 74% of 2025 profit. At ≈ ¥3.47tn — 56× FY2025 revenue, ~486× total FY2025 net income — no positive share price clears the framework's cheapness lines on the latest audited year: the trigger band is not merely distant, it does not exist.

## 2. Understanding

CXMT designs, fabricates and sells commodity DRAM memory chips and modules (DDR4/DDR5, LPDDR4X/5) from fabs in Hefei and Beijing, paid for by a small set of Chinese semiconductor distributors serving domestic server, smartphone and PC makers (end customers: Alibaba Cloud, ByteDance, Tencent, Lenovo, Xiaomi, OPPO/vivo), in the most cyclical commodity segment of semiconductors; it is a 2016-founded, state-anchored IDM with no controlling shareholder, large state co-investment sitting as minority interests inside its fab subsidiaries, >90% domestic revenue, and — since 27 July 2026 — the largest company on China's mainland exchanges. **PASS.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- DRAM price reversal: ¥24.7bn annual depreciation plus ¥9.6bn R&D means a 2024-level price deck returns the company to heavy losses within quarters.
- Distributor channel de-stocking compounds any price downturn (top-5 = 67%); 2023 showed the mechanism (−43.5% ASP, −¥19.2bn loss).
- US export controls on tools cap the 15nm/HBM roadmap; CXMT has no HBM product and a 1.5–2 generation process gap on a costlier DUV path.
- Free float 6.73% until lock-up expiry 27 Jan 2027 — the quote rests on a sliver of the register.
- NCI wedge: fab-level state co-investors took 74% of FY2025 profit; attributable economics are structurally junior.
