## 2. Understanding

Geberit develops, makes and sells sanitary products: behind-the-wall installation and flushing systems (37% of FY2025 sales), piping systems (33%) and bathroom ceramics and furniture (30%). It sells to sanitary wholesalers, who resell to plumbers and installers; one wholesale customer takes 18% of sales. It is a vertically integrated Swiss manufacturer run as one business unit, selling 89% in Europe (Germany 29%), listed since 1999 and the category leader, its Sanitec ceramics acquisition of 2015 long integrated. **PASS.** Lens STD; one reportable segment, so no group rules apply.

## 3. Quality — past record
