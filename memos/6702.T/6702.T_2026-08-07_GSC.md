# 6702.T — Fujitsu Limited: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS · Past **WEAK** (worst retrievable normalized ROCE 8.5%, FCFROCE 5.4% — and the unretrieved years bound the average lower, not higher) · Future ADEQUATE · SF PASS (1 flag — capital-allocation CAUTION) · Cheapness EXPENSIVE → **AVOID (Past gate)**.

Fujitsu is mid-transformation from a sprawling hardware conglomerate into a services/Uvance company, and the direction is real: EPS has compounded ~22%/yr on divestments, margin repair and ¥450bn of buybacks in three years, and FY26 ROCE reached 11.8%. But the framework grades the record, not the turnaround narrative: even the best three-year stretch in the company's modern history averages 8.5% with-cash returns and 5.4% cash returns on capital — under both floors — and every earlier year the secondary series can see (FY17–23 OI of ¥117–266bn on a similar capital base) would drag the 10-year average further down. Flip path: two more FY26-class years lift a full window through the floor; the rebuild flag (App A) is the first step.

## 2. Understanding
Fujitsu sells enterprise IT services and solutions — system integration, managed services/modernisation for Japanese government and corporates (~half of revenue under the Uvance banner), plus remaining server/network hardware — as Japan's largest domestic IT-services firm, Kawasaki-HQ'd, 90 years old, actively shedding non-core units (devices, scanners, air-conditioning JV, overseas legacy) to become a services pure-play. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
**Severely record-limited (3y).** On the retrievable complete years: normalized ROCE (end-of-period, with-cash) **8.5%** (path: 5.0% → 8.7% → 11.8%), ex-cash 9.6%; normalized FCFROCE **5.4%**. Both under the floors (P3 ROCE ≥10%; P2 FCFROCE ≥8%). The bounding logic for the missing years is one-directional: FY17–23 official OI (¥117–336bn per rejected-tier-2 and KPI-table series) on a ¥3.1–3.3tn asset base cannot lift a 10-year average that the best years leave at 8.5%. Per-share story is the opposite and stated: ΔEPS geo **+21.9%** (FY17→26, split-neutral), BV/sh rising, L/E 67.9%, debt trivial. The engine improved; the decade's capital earned too little. **WEAK.**

## 4. Quality — future

Forces: customer power WEAK (incumbent switching costs on mission-critical systems) · supplier power WEAK · **substitutes STRONG** — hyperscaler cloud plus global/offshore delivery is a live doing-without substitute for domestic SI labour · entrants WEAK (public-sector entrenchment, clearance/scale requirements). Tally **1 strong, contained**. Moat: **Switching costs** — decades of bespoke government/enterprise systems only Fujitsu can economically maintain; stable in the core, eroding at the modernisation edge. Market growth YES (Japan's forced IT modernisation). **ADEQUATE.**
