The corpse of the 2020–21 telehealth mania, five years on: $13.4bn of Livongo goodwill incinerated in one 2022 line, BetterHelp shrinking, revenue drifting down from the 2023 peak — and yet CFO holds near $290m a year and the stock trades at 0.87× a book already scrubbed of most goodwill, with the MCap/FCF trigger leg ($13.00) nearly double the price. This is the terminal-value question (is telehealth a business or a feature?) priced as an equity stub — nothing in the record answers it in the company's favour.

## 2. Understanding

Teladoc operates two telehealth segments: Integrated Care — virtual primary/specialty care, chronic-condition programs (ex-Livongo) sold B2B to US health plans and employers per-member-per-month — and BetterHelp, direct-to-consumer subscription mental-health counselling; global delivery, New-York-listed, on its second post-crash CEO (Divita, ex-GuideWell, June 2024). **PASS.**
