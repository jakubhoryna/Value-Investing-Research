# ABB — ABB Ltd: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

ABB passes understanding, rates ADEQUATE on its past record and STRONG on its future, fails shareholder-friendliness on an ADVERSE capital-allocation judgment, and is EXPENSIVE — **AVOID**, decided at the shareholder-friendliness gate.

ABB is worth owning for an electrification franchise pruned into a high-return business: return on capital (ex-cash, opening capital) climbed from 7% in 2020 to 31% in 2025 as Power Grids, Dodge and now Robotics left and the installed base kept compounding. Two things stand between here and a BUY. Management keeps converting that return stream into its own stock at about 30× operating income — 9.3 million shares bought this year through 23 September, the September weekly pace about five times the Q2 average — and the price, $97.88, sits 423% above a band of ≈$18.1–18.7. The verdict moves to WATCH only if repurchases stop executing above worth or gain a published price discipline; BUY also needs a price roughly 80% lower.

## 2. Understanding

ABB Ltd is a Zurich-headquartered Swiss holding company with over 140 years of history (US GAAP, USD reporting; shares on SIX and Nasdaq Stockholm, ADR 1:1 over the counter) whose subsidiaries design, make and service differentiated electrical-distribution equipment (breakers, switchgear, installation products, UPS), industrial motors and drives, and process-automation control systems, sold directly and through distributors, panel builders and OEMs to utilities, data-centre operators, process and discrete industry, building contractors and transport makers across the Americas, Europe and Asia/Middle East/Africa from local-for-local plants; it claims #1–#2 global positions in most lines (FY2025 revenue $33.2bn) and is narrowing toward electrification and automation, with Robotics being sold to SoftBank and Rotork being acquired. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized ROCE is 13.2% (year-end capital including cash, FY2016–25); the best reading is 17.1%, and worst FCFROCE is 8.7%. The average blends two companies: 2017–20 earned 6–12% as a Power Grids-laden conglomerate, then pruning and pricing lifted ROCE to 18–24% (with cash) in 2023–25; 2021's 19% includes a $2.2bn Dodge disposal gain. Operating income per diluted share compounded 16.4% on like-for-like links (10.1% raw) and FCF per share 11.6%, while the diluted count fell 15%. Liabilities-to-equity is 176%, after breaches of 200% in 2018–19 and 2022–23. The 2017–20 decline reversed five years ago and is not treated as sustained. **ADEQUATE** — the conglomerate years hold the worst average below 15%.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth passes both ways: no customer is disclosed above a tenth of revenue, and inputs are commodity metals and electronics from many vendors. All four forces are WEAK — customers are fragmented and locked in by the installed base; suppliers sell commodities; nothing substitutes for circuit protection, switching or drives; entrants face type-testing, spec-in positions and a global service footprint, with Chinese low-voltage and drive makers credible mainly at home. The moat is switching costs: breakers type-tested into panel builders' certified assemblies, drives and control systems embedded in plant maintenance routines, so replacing ABB means re-certifying and retraining for little saving. Stable-to-strengthening as data-centre and grid build-outs enlarge the installed base; H1 2026 orders rose 31%. **STRONG.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Pay is proportionate — CEO total ≈$7.66m (carried figure) against $193–212m lines — and related parties raise nothing. Capital allocation is **ADVERSE**. Divestments, dividends, reinvestment and 0.3× leverage are sound, but every programme in the window executed above the bargain lines, and the live $2.0bn programme, running to January 2027, reads 25×-class on all four measures: 9,321,098 shares bought through 23 September, 584,551 that week at CHF 79.6–83.0, timed by a bank within undisclosed parameters, with no published price criterion, while the stock trades 423% above band. **FAIL.**
