# 6861.T — Keyence: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS; Past STRONG; Future STRONG; Shareholder-friendliness PASS (no flag); Cheapness EXPENSIVE. Final verdict: **WATCH**.

Keyence sells factory sensors, measuring instruments and machine vision directly to manufacturers, and has its products made by contract factories. Over ten years revenue rose from ¥413bn to ¥1,169bn, gross margin held at 81–84%, and operating income per share compounded 11.5% a year. All of this was done without debt and while a liquid hoard of about ¥3.0tn built up. The business clears every quality gate. The price does not: at ¥80,270 the shares trade at 52.6× FY2025 free cash flow and 30–33× EV/OI, so the band prints at ≈ ¥12,210, about 85% below the price. The verdict turns to AVOID if the hoard funds value-destructive M&A or if returns on capital start a sustained decline.

## 2. Understanding
Keyence designs and sells factory-automation components: sensors, measurement and inspection systems, machine-vision and code readers, laser markers, digital microscopes and 3D printers. It sells them through its own direct, consultative sales force to manufacturers in every industry, no one of which is a tenth of sales. Production is fabless (contract factories plus one in-house manufacturing subsidiary). The company is headquartered in Osaka and 67% of FY2025 sales were outside Japan (US 19%, China 16%). Founded in 1972, it reported ¥1,169bn of revenue in FY2025 and remains founder-influenced. **PASS**.

## 3. Quality — past record
The worst-of-four normalized ROCE is **17.7%** and FCFROCE **11.6%**, both on end-of-period capital with cash. Ex-cash readings are 32–37% and 21–24%. ROCE_wc ranged from 14.3% to 20.8% with no sustained decline. The FY2022–25 drift from 19.7% to 16.9% is the cash pile growing; ex-cash returns are flat at 28–31%. OI per share grew 11.5% a year, FCF per share 13.7% and BV per share 12.8%, with a flat share count. FY2025 liabilities-to-equity was 5.7% with no borrowings. **STRONG**.

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth PASS. The yūhō states that no customer reaches 10% of sales. Production is spread across contract factories and the company says it avoids dependence on any specific supplier; supplier shares are not disclosed.

Forces:
- Customer power WEAK: hundreds of thousands of plants buy low-ticket items.
- Supplier power WEAK: contract assembly and standard components, with alternatives.
- Substitutes WEAK: inspection and measurement are needed on every automated line.
- New entrants WEAK: a direct engineering sales force, a broad catalogue and immediate delivery (即納) are barriers to success.

Moat: **switching costs**. Sensors and vision programs are validated into production lines, and re-qualifying a line costs more than the part. Stable: gross margin was 81–84% throughout. Market growth YES. **STRONG**.

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness
P17: the top disclosed pay is ¥182m (one director, all fixed), against a ≈ ¥18.5bn cap on both reads. Seven outside officers shared ¥39m. No related-party transactions are disclosed. The founder's asset company, the founder and the Keyence Foundation together hold ≈ 22.8%, a control caveat. Capital allocation is **SOUND**. There were no programme buybacks at 30–50× FCF, and no debt. Dividends were paid from free cash flow, and the ¥550 FY2025 dividend was up 57%. The one acquisition (CADENAS, FY2025) was small. Retained cash sits in bonds and certificates of deposit. **PASS**.
