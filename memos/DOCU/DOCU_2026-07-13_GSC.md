# DOCU — Docusign, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | WEAK | WEAK | FAIL | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** |

Docusign fails three gates independently, each disqualifying. The decade's economics are an SBC transfer machine: cumulative FY2017–26 operating income is **−$581M** while cumulative derived FCF is +$4.03B — a wedge fully explained by **$3.77B of cumulative stock-based compensation** added back to CFO. The worst-of-four normalized ROCE is −10.9%. Shareholder-friendliness fails on multiple independent hard strikes: buybacks executed four consecutive years above the 20× P19 line (FY2024's tranche at an implied **286× EV/OI**), a repurchase program that until FY2026 didn't even hold the diluted share count flat (textbook dilution sterilisation), and CEO comp at 166% of the P17 5%-of-net-income line in the latest year. GAAP profitability arrived only in FY2024 and is scaling fast (OI +49% in FY2026), so the trigger band will migrate upward at each re-anchor — but no cheapness cures a triple quality/SF failure. Nothing short of a multi-year rebuild of the record flips this verdict.

## 2. Understanding

Docusign sells subscription software — the world's leading e-signature product plus a contract-lifecycle-management suite, now packaged as an AI-enabled "Intelligent Agreement Management" platform — to over 1.8 million paying customers, from individuals to enterprises across virtually all industries (no customer >10% of revenue), roughly 71% of revenue from U.S. customers; a single-segment, Delaware-incorporated, San Francisco-headquartered cloud SaaS operation, founded 2003, the category creator and still the market leader in a maturing category it is trying to outgrow via the IAM/AI pivot. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized ROCE (FY2018–26 denominators; FY2017 balance sheet unavailable) is **−10.9%** — all four variants are negative — against the +10% ADEQUATE line. Worst-of-four FCFROCE is +33.3%, but the divergence is stock compensation, not operating strength: OI carries the ~$600M/yr SBC cost, FCF does not. OI/share was positive in only the last three of ten years; FCF/share compounded 65%/yr off a trivial FY2020 base. L/E breached P5 in FY2020–23 (peak 822%, convertibles against loss-depleted equity) without P6 conditions; today it is a clean 121%, debt-free. **WEAK** — the record, not the trajectory, is what the gate tests.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASSes both sides (explicit no->10%-customer disclosure; commodity cloud/compute suppliers with ready alternatives). Forces: customer power WEAK (1.8M+ customers, workflow switching costs); supplier power WEAK; **threat of substitutes STRONG** (Adobe bundles signing into Acrobat; the 10-K itself concedes AI makes core agreement capabilities "cheaper and easier to replicate", down to customer homegrown builds); **threat of new entrants STRONG** (dozens of live low-cost rivals; entry demonstrably succeeds). Tally 2 strong → the book's stop signal. The brand/ingrainedness moat ("DocuSign" the verb, enterprise compliance trust) is real but **eroding** at the commodity end — no moat-decides floor. Market growth YES (~8–9%). **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

**FAIL — multiple independent hard strikes.** P19: buybacks above the 20× hard-strike line in four consecutive years — FY2023 26.8× MCap/FCF (while OI was negative), FY2024 285.9× EV/OI, FY2025 61.6×, FY2026 49.5× — and FY2023–25 repurchases ($894M) didn't stop the diluted count rising 200.9M→210.3M: dilution sterilisation, a strike at any price. P17: CEO Thygesen's FY2026 total $25.65M vs the $15.45M line (5% of NI); FY2024 saw three NEOs above a $3.7M line. Director comp: one $523k total (one-time initial grant) technically above the $500k stop; recurring ~$310–352k passes. RPTs: none disclosed.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- AI/LLM platforms commoditise agreement workflows faster than the IAM pivot monetises them — the company's own filing names this vector.
- Adobe's bundled distribution keeps compressing pricing at the volume end of e-signature.
- SBC at ~$620M/yr (19% of revenue) keeps GAAP economics far below "cash flow" optics; buybacks continue to fund employee comp at shareholder expense.
- Growth already decelerated to 8–9%; a maturing core with a re-rating platform story attached.
- Upside risk to an AVOID: GAAP OI is compounding ~50%/yr — the band will rise materially at each annual re-anchor.
