# Henkel AG & Co. KGaA (HEN3) — ANALYSE v1 — AVOID

**Verdict: AVOID — Past WEAK + Shareholder-Friendliness FAIL (P19 hard strike), independently disqualifying; the stock is also EXPENSIVE at 17× MCap/FCF.**

**Understanding — PASS.** Henkel sells differentiated branded adhesives, sealants and functional coatings (~52% of FY2025 sales, ~2/3 of segment profit) to industrial customers globally, plus consumer laundry, home-care and hair brands (Persil, Schwarzkopf) sold through the retail trade. German KGaA, family-controlled through unlisted-majority ordinary shares (HEN), with the liquid listed line the non-voting preferred (HEN3). HQ Düsseldorf; ~140 years old; FY2025 sales €20,495m, ~46% Europe, USA €11.1bn region-reported largest single market. A prominent, comprehensible multinational — P1 clean.

**Past — WEAK (knife-edge; deciding leg is growth).** Worst-of-four normalized 10y average ROCE is 10.27% (CE incl. cash, year-end) and FCFROCE 7.71%. The ROCE floor itself straddles 10% on NIBCL treatment: conservative (all current provisions in CE) 9.52%, primary 10.27%, refined per FY25 Note 17 split (sales/personnel accruals into NIBCL) 10.11%. What decides the gate is P3's flat-to-positive growth requirement: **ΔFCF/share is −2.4%/yr geometric 2016→2025** (€5.22 → €4.18), and yearly ROCE shows a level shift down from ~14–16% (2016–18) to ~7–11% since 2020. ΔOI/share is only +0.7%/yr over a decade of major M&A. An ADEQUATE alternate reading exists (OI-anchored, refined NIBCL, 2024 endpoint gives ΔFCF/sh +1.1%), stated for the record — but the framework takes the as-computed window, and a negative FCF/share decade with a downshifted ROCE plateau is WEAK. Past WEAK alone → AVOID.

**Future — ADEQUATE.** Breadth passes both sides: AR2025 states no customer above 10% of sales (sourced); supplier base is broad commodity chemicals (inference). One strong competitive force: retailer customer power over Consumer Brands (trade consolidation, private label). Adhesives — the dominant profit pool — enjoys spec-in switching costs; supplier, substitute and entrant forces weak. One strong force, contained: no stop signal.

**Shareholder-Friendliness — FAIL on P19.** The 2022–23 buyback program repurchased €998m of stock (14.92M shares, avg ≈ €66.9). The 2022 tranche (€803m) executed at an implied market cap of ≈ €28.7bn against FY2022 FCF of €563m derived (Henkel's own FCF €653m): **MCap/FCF ≈ 51× derived / 44× company-basis — a hard strike far beyond the 20× line**, with EV/OI that year ≈ 17× as corroboration. Never average away a strike. The 2023 tranche (11×) and the 2025/26 program (€856m spent in 2025, avg €68.05; MCap/FCF ≈ 16.3×, flag) do not cure it. No below-worth exemption: €67–68 is roughly double the conservative worth band (≈ €34–41). Not sterilization — share count genuinely fell 419.4M → 406.8M. P17 clean: Management Board total comp €17.84m vs €86.7m threshold (5% × lower of FCF/NI); Supervisory Board and Shareholders' Committee fees unremarkable; KGaA dual-class family control logged as governance caveat, not a strike. Capital-allocation record is chronically acquisitive (BV ≫ TBV); pending Stahl Holdings (€2.1bn EV, ~est. 12–15× EV/OI, flag-line) and Olaplex ($1.4bn; ~74× run-rate FY25 OI vs ~10× on 4y-normalized OI — flag-level concern, audited at consolidation).

**Cheapness — EXPENSIVE.** At €74.98 (MCap €29,396m; EV €30,079m all-cash / €33,190m ex-cash): MCap/FCF **17.0×** vs ≤8 (+112%); EV/OI 10.7×/11.8× vs ≤7. MCap/BV 1.43× passes; MCap/TBV 9.1× breaches 3× — the mandated acquisition audit is the P19/acquisition record above (chronic goodwill accretion, two pending deals at or above flag lines). **Trigger band: CHEAP below ≈ €34** (MCap/FCF binding; EV/OI leg ≈ €39 conservative). Current price is +119% above the band. No P12 relief — Past is not STRONG.

**What would change the verdict.** (1) P19: the strike is historical and permanent under v3.3.1; only a framework change reopens it. (2) Past: two consecutive years of ROCE ≥ 12% with positive ΔFCF/share would flip the growth leg by FY2027. (3) Price: sub-€34 makes cheapness moot to the verdict but irrelevant while two gates fail. Next review FY2026 AR (~Mar 2027): re-test P19 on the completed 2025/26 program, run the acquisition audit on Stahl/Olaplex/ATP at consolidation.
