# XPEL — XPEL, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Past **WEAK** — P4 fires on three consecutive ROCE declines (31.7 → 30.5 → 23.8 → 19.1%) over a level that is otherwise STRONG-class (worst-of-four 25.1%, FCFROCE 17.0%) · Future **WEAK** (supplier-side breadth FAIL) · SF **FAIL** (P17 hard strike, both reads, on primary proxy data: $2.692m against $2.579m and $2.251m ceilings) · EXPENSIVE (21.9× FCF, 22.4×/21.6× EV/OI) → **AVOID (four gates)**.

XPEL is the best business this run has touched and it fails all four gates. Paint protection film sold through 2,500 trained installer bays with proprietary pattern software is a real franchise: 25% returns on capital, 17% cash returns, no debt, $51m of net income on $476m of revenue. The record shows that franchise being diluted by its own balance sheet — total assets doubled to $383m over four years while operating income rose 16%, so returns fell every year. Add a supply chain running three-quarters through one film maker and a chief-executive package clearing both ceilings, and there is no route to a WATCH.

## 2. Understanding
XPEL designs, sources and sells automotive protective films — paint protection film, window tint, ceramic coatings — plus the DAP pattern software that cuts them to vehicle-specific templates, sold through independent installers, dealerships, its own installation centres and international distributors, with a small OEM channel; San Antonio-HQ'd, no controlling holder (officers and directors 9.4%). **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
The level is excellent and the trend is the finding. Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **25.11%** (end-basis, with-cash; best variant 33.94%) and worst FCFROCE **16.95%** — both far clear of the STRONG floors; operating income per share +5.0%/yr, book value per share +31.8%/yr, no debt (L/E 34.1%). But the path reads **31.7 → 30.5 → 23.8 → 19.1%**: three consecutive declines, and the cause is in the balance sheet — capital employed went $170m → $328m while operating income went $54m → $63m. Cash built to $51m and acquisitions absorbed $55m over three years; the incremental capital has not earned anything like the base. A sustained decline forces the downgrade regardless of level — the shape the register logged at ATKR and Carter's. **WEAK.**

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth: **FAIL on the supplier limb** — the in-window disclosure has XPEL sourcing roughly 75% of inventory purchases from a single film manufacturer under exclusivity plus a quarterly minimum; that is concentration on a proprietary formulation, not commodity bulk buying, so the bulk-terms exception is not established. The customer limb carried an 18% China distributor in the same era; China has since weakened, so it may now pass — unverified (App A). Forces: **supplier power STRONG — demonstrated by the concentration itself** · customer power WEAK (≈2,500 fragmented service points) · substitutes WATCH (ceramic coatings, cheaper films) · new entrants WEAK (installer training, DAP pattern library). Moat: **Ingrainedness** — the installer channel and the software the bays plan their day around; stable. Market growth YES. Moat and growth are both real; the breadth FAIL is decisive. **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness
Comp: **P17 hard strike on primary data.** FY2025 — ceiling **$2.579m** (5% of net income, the lower leg) against Pape's **$2.692m**, a 4.4% breach. Trailing-3-year — ceiling **$2.251m** (free cash flow binds at a $45.0m average), breach 19.6%. Both reads breached, and unlike the register's other narrow strikes this figure comes straight from the Summary Compensation Table, so there is no sourcing contingency. RPT: none surfaced. Insider alignment is a real positive — Pape 3.9% outright, directors and officers 9.4%. Capital allocation: **SOUND-leaning, acquisition ledger INDETERMINATE** — no dividends, no meaningful repurchases, and $54.8m of tuck-in acquisitions over three years at undisclosed prices, which is exactly the spend showing up in the returns decline. No second flag on the available evidence. **FAIL** on the strike alone.

## 6. Price
At $50.00: **MCap/FCF 21.9×**, **EV/OI 22.4× / 21.6×**. The band is clean and computable — **CHEAP below ≈ $15.08 / $16.92**, the free-cash-flow leg above at $18.26 — putting the price **+195%** above the all-cash bound. No P12 once P4 fires, though at 1.25× the thresholds the name would still be nowhere near. Qualifiers: 4.8× book. **EXPENSIVE.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers (context for the AVOID)
The bull case is that the returns decline is deployment lag — cash and freshly acquired bays sitting in capital employed before earning — and that supplier diversification has quietly removed the concentration. Both are plausible and both are testable at the FY2026 10-K. The bear case is simpler: this is a discretionary aftermarket product, and the returns slowdown coincided with softness in China and new-vehicle sales.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review
Past flips when the decline breaks — a FY2026 ROCE above 19.1% restores the trend limb, and the level never left STRONG territory. The strike clears on a package under ~$2.25m or another step up in free cash flow. Price needs a two-thirds fall. Next review: **FY2026 10-K (~Feb 2027)** — the fourth-decline test, both concentration disclosures, acquisition pricing. **Alert < ≈ $17.**
