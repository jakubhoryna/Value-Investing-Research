# BBBY — Bed Bath & Beyond, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS, Past WEAK, Future WEAK, shareholder-friendliness FAIL (P17 hard strike under the loss-legs rule), cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID on three gates**. **Queue-flag resolved:** the original retailer's estate was liquidated in the 2023 Ch. 11 and its listing is gone; Overstock.com bought the brand IP ($21.5m, 2023), renamed to Beyond, Inc., then in August 2025 to Bed Bath & Beyond, Inc., reclaiming the ticker BBBY. There is no second analysable entity. The thesis would be a brand-licensing turnaround on its ninth pivot in a decade; nothing in the record evidences a returns engine, the pay practice strikes against negative owner earnings, and the row's main value is saving future time — including at the NXH re-listing, the same company under a fourth name.

## 2. Understanding

Bed Bath & Beyond, Inc. is an online-first US home-goods retailer (HQ Utah) selling furniture and furnishings to US consumers under acquired brands — Bed Bath & Beyond, Overstock, Zulily, buybuy BABY, and, since the late-2025/2026 acquisition of The Brand House Collective (Kirkland's), ~300 physical stores — a serial self-transformer that has pivoted from closeout e-commerce through blockchain ventures (Medici/tZERO) to a brand roll-up now renaming itself Neighborhood Intelligence. PASS — describable in one sentence, though serial transformation is itself the status finding.

## 3. Quality — past record

Operating income is negative in six of the ten window years and sums to ≈ **−$454m** (FY2016–FY2025; sourced series below), so every normalized ROCE variant is negative — below zero, not near the 10% floor, and no CE treatment repairs a negative numerator. FY2025: revenue $1,044.6m (−25.1% YoY; −62% from the FY2021 peak $2,756m), operating loss $61.2m, FCF −$64.1m (CFO −$56.7m − capex $7.4m); FY2024 FCF −$188.6m. Per-share growth n.m. on negative endpoints, with dilution running the wrong way (46.5m weighted diluted FY2024 → 60.1m FY2025 → 95.33m outstanding now, plus a 46.23m-share offering filed 5 Aug 2026). L/E 95.2% (Dec-2025: liabilities $207.5m / equity $217.9m) is the one clean metric and is irrelevant to the gate. **WEAK** — on level, trend, and dilution simultaneously.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASSES trivially (millions of consumers; commodity home-goods supply base). Forces: customer power WEAK (many customers); supplier power WEAK (many suppliers); **substitutes STRONG** (identical goods one click away at Amazon/Wayfair/Target; zero switching costs); **new entrants STRONG** (no barriers to online home-goods retail; the company itself is subscale at ~$1bn revenue against Wayfair ~$12bn). Tally 2 strong → the stop signal. Moat: none — the brand is a purchased memory of a bankrupt chain, licensing revenue is small, and there is no cost, network or switching-cost source; the store acquisition adds fixed costs, not fewness. Market growth MIXED (US home goods GDP-ish, online share mature). **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

P17: FY2025 has **both legs negative** (FCF −$64.1m, NI −$84.6m; trailing-3y both negative too), so any more-than-token pay is a hard strike — Executive Chairman/PEO Marcus Lemonis's March-2025 award alone is 500,000 RSUs + 500,000 performance shares (grant-date value ≈ $6–7m at the ~$6.5 then-price — inference, labelled; exact SCT figure owed from the 2026 proxy, which truncated on retrieval), plus a further 1.5m-RSU award after the BBBY rename. **Hard strike → SF FAIL.** RPT surface logged: Beyond held equity, loans, and trademark-license agreements with Kirkland's/The Brand House Collective and then acquired it (completed 2026) — fairness INDETERMINATE, cannot complete a pair, named re-test at the FY2026 proxy. Capital allocation **CAUTION**: no repurchases; serial acquisitive pivots (BBBY IP, Zulily, buybuy BABY, Kirkland's) under changing allocators with no completed year of demonstrated discipline; equity issued at depressed prices to fund it (46.23m-share shelf, 5 Aug 2026). **FAIL (P17 hard strike).**
