INFICON is a genuinely high-quality niche compounder — a net-cash, ~25%-ROCE vacuum-instrumentation leader with a real switching-cost moat, unusually modest Swiss-style compensation, no related-party dealings, and effectively no buybacks to police. It clears every quality and governance gate cleanly. The only thing between here and a BUY is price: at CHF 158 the stock trades at ~43× EV/operating income and ~70× free cash flow, roughly six-to-nine times the framework's bargain lines. Even valued on peak-2024 earnings it is 35×/54×. Nothing about the business needs to change; the price would have to fall by ~85% (to ≈ CHF 18) for cheapness to flip. It enters the register as the farthest-from-range WATCH name — a top-tier business parked for a genuine dislocation.

## 2. Understanding

INFICON Holding AG is a Swiss-headquartered, USD-reporting manufacturer of vacuum-based instrumentation and Industry-4.0 process software — leak detectors, vacuum gauges, thin-film deposition controllers, and residual-gas analysers/mass spectrometers — sold to semiconductor equipment makers and fabs (~49% of sales), refrigeration/air-conditioning/automotive service and OEM customers, general-vacuum users (research, coatings, life science), and security/energy end-users, predominantly across China and Asia-Pacific (~53%), as a differentiated, high-switching-cost, installed-base-driven, family-anchored mid-cap (~CHF 3.9bn). **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Ten years (2016–2025). Worst-of-four normalised **ROCE 22.2%** (range 22.2–29.9%; conservative CE = total assets + cumulative goodwill − NIBCL) and worst-of-four **FCFROCE 12.3%** (12.3–16.0%) — both clear the STRONG bars (≥15% / ≥8%) even on the darkest read. Per-share growth: OI/sh +8.8%/yr, BV/sh +10.5%/yr, FCF/sh +3.8%/yr (lumpy — a 2022 working-capital trough and a 2025 cyclical dip). Balance sheet is fortress: L/E 35%, net cash ~USD 81m, no long-term debt across the whole window. FY2025 is a cyclical down-year (OI −17%) but there is no *sustained* multi-year decline. **STRONG.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: supplier base broad (core sensors made in-house); customer base diversified across four end-markets and geographies — **PASS**, but no explicit "no customer >10%" disclosure was found and semiconductor (49%) runs through a concentrated set of large OEMs/fabs, logged as a caveat. Four forces: customer bargaining power **STRONG** (sophisticated, concentrated semi OEMs/fabs) — the single contained strong force; supplier power, substitutes, and new entrants all **WEAK** (in-house sensor tech, essential measurement function, high qualification/brand barriers). Tally 1 → judgment zone. Moat: **switching costs** (instruments designed into OEM tools and qualified into fab process recipes; recurring service/consumables/software), dominant and **stable-to-strengthening**, which contains the one strong force. Market growth YES (semi secular, refrigerant/EV transition, life science) — cyclical. **ADEQUATE.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Semiconductor cyclicality: ~49% of sales swings with the WFE cycle; FY2025 OI fell 17% and margins slipped below the 20% target.
- Customer concentration in the semi channel (large OEMs/fabs) is the main un-verified quality point; a disclosed >10% customer would pressure the breadth call.
- China/Asia-Pacific exposure (~53%) plus tariff/trade friction drove the 2025 manufacturing relocation and margin hit; further escalation is a live risk.
- CFO transition (Tröndle → Lisak, mid-2026) and the family-anchored ownership are governance items to keep watched, though neither is a flag today.
- The whole thesis break here is valuation, not the business — the risk is paying up and de-rating.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

Verdict flips to BUY only on price: MCap/FCF ≤ 8 and EV/OI ≤ 7 together, i.e. ≈ CHF 18 (P12 outer edge ≈ CHF 22.5 given Past STRONG) — a level that in practice needs a severe cyclical drawdown. Downgrade risk: a confirmed >10% customer (breadth) or a sustained multi-year return decline. Next review: FY2026 annual report (~Mar 2027) to reset the cyclical FY2025 basis and extend the record; earlier if price < ≈ CHF 30 or on a semiconductor up-cycle inflection.
