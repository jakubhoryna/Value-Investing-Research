# BRK.B — Berkshire Hathaway Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS, Past WEAK, Future ADEQUATE, shareholder-friendliness FAIL and cheapness EXPENSIVE give **AVOID** (Past and SF).

Berkshire owns a fortress balance sheet and strong franchises (GEICO, BNSF, PCC), but the whole earns a poor return on the capital it employs. Operating returns are 6–8% pre-tax on capital carrying $118bn of acquisition goodwill and intangibles and a capital-hungry utility. The $569bn of insurance and equity-method investments adds dividends and interest; its price gains never count as operating income. Book value per share compounded 12.4% a year, mostly through unrealised gains carried by cost-free float, which ROCE does not see. Shareholder-friendliness fails on a flag pair: repurchases clearly above worth on the model's yardsticks, and new capital going mostly to units earning below 10%. At $539.82 the stock is at 34× EV/OI. WATCH would need worst-of-eight ROCE of at least 10%, which the record never approaches.

## 2. Understanding
**Group sentence.** Berkshire Hathaway Inc., an Omaha holding company led since January 2026 by CEO Greg Abel, owns licensed property-casualty insurers and reinsurers whose float funds a $569bn portfolio, a western freight railroad (BNSF), regulated utilities and pipelines (BHE), and manufacturing, service, retail, food-distribution (McLane) and truck-stop (Pilot) businesses, selling mainly to US consumers, businesses and ratepayers. **PASS.**

**Units (all material).**
- *Insurance:* GEICO sells auto cover direct to individuals; BH Primary sells commercial property-casualty; BHRG sells reinsurance to insurers worldwide.
- *BNSF* hauls freight for shippers over 32,500 western route miles.
- *BHE* sells regulated power and gas to 5.4m retail customers, and runs pipelines and UK and Alberta grids.
- *Manufacturing* covers three groups: industrial (47.5%: PCC, Lubrizol, Marmon, IMC, OxyChem), building (34.1%: Clayton homes and loans, flooring, insulation, paint) and consumer (18.4%: RVs, batteries, apparel).
- *Service and retailing:* aviation services, electronics distribution, leasing, auto dealerships and furniture retail.
- *McLane* supplies convenience stores and restaurant chains.
- *Pilot* sells diesel and food at 757 travel centres to truckers.

## 4. Quality — future
