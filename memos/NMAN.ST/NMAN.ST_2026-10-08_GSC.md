## 2. Understanding

Nederman Holding AB, a Helsingborg-headquartered industrial company listed in Stockholm since 2007 and anchored by Investment AB Latour (≈30%), designs, manufactures and services differentiated equipment that captures, filters and measures industrial air — extraction arms and filter units, process filtration systems, ducting and emissions analysers — paid for by manufacturers, process plants, workshops and the distributors that serve them across EMEA (47% of 2025 sales), the Americas (39%) and Asia-Pacific (14%). **PASS.**

It is a group: four reportable segments, each above 10% of revenue. Extraction & Filtration Technology sells standard and engineered extraction and filtration systems to manufacturing plants and workshops, directly and through distributors. Process Technology sells engineered filtration and air-handling systems (MikroPul, Luwa, Pneumafil, LCI, Euro-Equip) to process industries such as textiles, metals, minerals and chemicals. Duct & Filter Technology sells modular ducting and filter components (Nordfab, Menardi) to installers, OEMs and sister divisions. Monitoring & Control Technology sells gas analysers and emissions-monitoring instruments (Gasmet, NEO Monitors) and IIoT software to industrial operators and authorities. Each is one six-parameter sentence. Lens STD.
