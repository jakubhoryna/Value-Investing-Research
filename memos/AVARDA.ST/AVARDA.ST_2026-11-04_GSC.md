# AVARDA.ST — Avarda Bank AB (publ): GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS (FI lens, a Swedish licensed bank), Past ADEQUATE (ROE 20.0%, capped by a three-year ROE decline), Future WEAK (funding concentration: one deposit platform carries about a third of liabilities), shareholder-friendliness PASS with one caution (related-party dealings), cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID (Future)**.

Avarda Bank, until April 2026 TF Bank, is a fast-growing digital consumer-credit bank: profit per share rose about 18% a year over ten years with ROE above 20%. What stops it is funding. Germany's deposits are gathered directly, but the Dutch, Irish and Spanish deposit books are sold through the Raisin platform and reached SEK 10.6bn, 33.7% of liabilities, at 30 September 2026. A platform is one counterparty, and one counterparty above a tenth of liabilities is a funding concentration that no moat carries. The price is far away as well: 223.50 SEK is 49.7 times the ten-year average profit per share, against a 36.00 SEK trigger. A flip needs the platform share brought under 10% of liabilities and a price far lower.

## 2. Understanding

Avarda Bank AB (founded 1987 in Borås as TF Bank, listed on Nasdaq Stockholm in June 2016, renamed in April 2026) is a Swedish-licensed, branchless bank that lends to about 3.9 million creditworthy private individuals, who pay interest and fees on credit cards (Germany, Norway, Austria, Spain, Italy and the Nordics), unsecured consumer loans (the Nordics, the Baltics, Poland) and buy-now-pay-later and invoice credit at the online checkouts of Nordic merchants (Ecommerce Solutions, the Avarda brand, where merchants pay service fees), funded by insured household savings and term deposits taken in Germany, the Netherlands, Ireland, Spain and the Nordics; supervised by the Swedish FSA, mid-sized and growing loans about 20% a year. **PASS.**

The three FY2025 segments (Credit Cards, Consumer Lending, Ecommerce Solutions; Everyday Finance and Consumer Lending from 2026) are lending products of one deposit-funded bank on one balance sheet and capital base. Under FI the whole entity is graded; no part is non-financial and no listed subsidiary or holding exists. Not a group for the group rules.
