Humble is a Stockholm-based roll-up of small consumer-goods companies, assembled since 2020 from a listed sweetener developer through about SEK 3bn of cash deals and repeated share issues. The record shows SEK 7.5–8.3bn of capital earning SEK 250–300m of operating income at best, and the 2026 impairment of SEK 604m turned trailing operating income negative. ## 2. Understanding
