## 2. Understanding

Avanza sells low-cost savings and investment services — brokerage, funds, unit-linked pensions (Avanza Pension), margin lending, mortgages, and deposits — to 2.24m Swedish retail savers who pay commissions, spreads, fund fees, and net interest; it operates as a listed bank holding company with a banking subsidiary and a captive insurance company, entirely in Sweden, where after 25 years it is the largest broker on Nasdaq Stockholm by transactions with an 8.3% share of the national savings market. **PASS.**
