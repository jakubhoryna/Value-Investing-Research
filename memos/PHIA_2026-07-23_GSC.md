# PHIA — Koninklijke Philips N.V.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | **WEAK** | ADEQUATE | **FAIL** (P19 hard strikes) | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** (Past WEAK + SF FAIL) |

Philips owns genuinely entrenched hospital franchises — imaging, image-guided therapy, monitoring — but a decade of its accounts shows a business that never earned its capital: worst-of-four 10-year ROCE is **3.5%** and FCFROCE **4.4%**, dragged by ~€9–10bn of acquisition goodwill and the 2021–2024 Respironics catastrophe (FY2022 OI −€1.5bn incl. a €1,357m goodwill impairment). Governance compounds it: roughly **€6.9bn was spent on repurchases FY2016–2024 at implied 23–70× EV/OI — five of six measurable tranche-years are P19 hard strikes on both dual-reads — while the diluted share count still *rose* ~5%** because dividends were paid largely in scrip. Verdict is a two-gate AVOID; price (28× MCap/FCF, 19–21× EV/OI) is a third independent miss. Flip requires a rebuilt multi-year returns record plus, for SF, all three v3.5.0 redemption limbs — only allocator turnover is satisfied today. Nothing plausible flips this before decade-scale evidence accumulates.

## 2. Understanding

Philips is a 130-year-old Dutch former electronics conglomerate, since 2021 a pure health-technology company (HQ Amsterdam, two-tier N.V., NYSE ADR), that develops, manufactures and services regulated, differentiated diagnostic-imaging, image-guided-therapy, patient-monitoring and sleep/respiratory equipment plus health informatics — sold to hospitals and health systems worldwide (North America largest) via capex budgets, tenders and service contracts — alongside a consumer Personal Health arm (power toothbrushes, shavers) sold through retailers; a prominent global #3 in medtech, wounded and mid-turnaround after the 2021 Respironics recall. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized averages (FY2016–25): **ROCE 3.5%, FCFROCE 4.4%** — far under the 10% ADEQUATE floor on every one of the four bases (best: 3.9%/5.0%). Yearly beginning-CE ROCE never reached double digits (peak 8.7% in 2018), then declined into negative territory 2022–23 — a sustained decline that would force WEAK regardless of level. Per-share growth is noise off distorted 2015 bases (OI/sh +7.5% geo, FCF/sh n.m.-quality); BV/sh −1.0%/yr; TBV negative most of the decade. Leverage is the one clean line: L/E 146%, within P5. **Verdict: WEAK** — a decade averaging ~3–4% returns on capital employed is the definition of it.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: PASS both sides — thousands of hospital and consumer buyers, none over 10% of revenue (no 20-F dependence disclosure); supplier base broad with ready alternatives, though Philips itself flags rising supplier concentration from supply-base leaning. Forces (binary): customer power WEAK; supplier power WEAK; substitutes WEAK (no functional alternative to imaging/monitoring); new entrants WEAK globally (FDA/MDR, scale, installed-base switching costs) though Chinese champions erode emerging-market share. **Tally: 0 strong — favourable.** Moat: switching costs (installed base, service contracts, clinician training) — real, but stable-to-eroding: Respironics destroyed the respiratory franchise's brand and share losses persist at the margin. Market growth: YES. **Verdict: ADEQUATE** — plausible moat, not durable-and-strengthening.

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Comp clean: CEO Jakobs FY2025 total €6.97m (IFRS-cost basis, fattest) = 0.9% of lower(FCF €767m, NI €897m); far under the ~$40m absolute trigger; SB chair fee €175k base — below even the CPI-indexed floor-default stop. RPT immaterial (€50m purchases, arm's-length; PMC vendor-finance JVs disclosed). **Buybacks: hard strike.** Dual-read worksheet (App. E): 2019, 2020, 2021, 2023 and 2024 tranches each breach 20× on both year and 3-year-normalized reads on at least one basis (2021: 70.6×/36.8× EV/OI); no rescue tranche exists; flagged/struck executions are the window's entire pattern. v3.5.0 redemption fails limbs (a) and (b). **FAIL.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Residual Respironics tail: international claims and reputational damage in respiratory care persist even after the US settlements.
- Chinese localization (Mindray, United Imaging) structurally erodes emerging-market imaging share and pricing.
- Goodwill (€9.3bn) still exceeds equity headroom — another impairment cycle would again wipe reported OI.
- Turnaround dependence: FY2025 OI margin 8.0% is one good year against a 3.5% decade; mean-reversion is unproven.
- Scrip-dividend re-dilution quietly undoes any future repurchase arithmetic, as it did in 2016–2025.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

AVOID → WATCH requires both: (i) Past rebuilt to ≥10% worst-of-four ROCE — arithmetically a multi-year matter, roughly sustained OI ≥ €2.2bn against today's ~€22bn CE; and (ii) SF redemption under v3.5.0 — published worth-referenced repurchase discipline **and** a bargain-line or refusal year **and** allocator turnover (only the third limb holds: CEO 10/2022, CFO 10/2024). Cheapness alone can never flip an AVOID. Next review: FY2026 20-F (~Feb 2027), informational.
