## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past WEAK (9.9% ROCE); Future ADEQUATE (customer conc.); Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (flag pair); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID (Past + SF)**.

Descartes runs a logistics data network and a large catalogue of subscription software for shippers, carriers, forwarders and customs filers, and it has grown operating income per share by about 20% a year for a decade, almost entirely through steady bolt-on acquisitions. The acquired goodwill and intangibles that buy that growth hold the return on all capital employed just under the line: 9.9% over FY2017–FY2026. Pay and deal-making are two separate flags. At CAD 126.77 the stock trades at about 31 times free cash flow and 38 times operating income, five times the trigger of CAD 23.18. The verdict would flip only with the ten-year return above 10%, pay back inside the lines and acquisitions bought on demonstrated profits — and then the price would still have to fall about 80%.

## 2. Understanding

The Descartes Systems Group is a Waterloo, Ontario-headquartered provider, listed on the TSX and Nasdaq, of cloud logistics and supply-chain software and data, sold mostly on subscription and per-transaction fees (93% of FY2026 revenue) to organisations — shippers, retailers and ecommerce sellers, manufacturers and distributors, road, ocean and air carriers, freight forwarders and customs brokers — chiefly in the United States (68%), Europe and Canada: its Global Logistics Network exchanges shipping, customs and trade documents between those parties and with customs authorities, and its applications cover routing and last-mile delivery, transportation management, customs filing and trade compliance, global trade data and denied-party screening, ecommerce shipping and warehouse tools, built up largely through acquisitions of small software and data businesses. **PASS.**
