# UAA — Under Armour, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past **WEAK** (w-o-4 ROCE 3.62%, four consecutive OI declines — P4 fires twice over; L/E 212% past P5); Future **WEAK** (3-strong tally, eroding brand); SF **FAIL** (P17 hard strike — $11.6m of pay against a year with both legs negative); cheapness n.m.-EXPENSIVE — **AVOID (four gates)**.

A two-decade brand in year five of a turnaround that keeps receding: reported operating income has fallen every year of the window and went negative in FY2026, free cash flow has been negative in three of five years, and the equity cushion is being consumed by restructuring while the controlling founder-CEO's pay rose 5% to $11.6m. The SHOO memo's force analysis applies here without edits — athletic apparel cannot contain substitutes or entrants — but SHOO earned 19% on capital; Under Armour earns 3.6%. Nothing here is close to a gate.

## 2. Understanding

Under Armour designs and sells performance athletic apparel, footwear and accessories under the Under Armour brand, mainly wholesale to sporting-goods, department and off-price retailers and through its own DTC stores and e-commerce — retailers and consumers pay; a Baltimore-headquartered Maryland corporation founded 1996, listed 2005, with a three-class share structure that leaves founder-CEO Kevin Plank 64.6% of the votes; global sourcing from independent Asian factories, revenue ~60% North America; a prominent, formerly high-growth mid-cap now in multi-year restructuring. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **3.62%** (beg-wc; end-wc 5.16%), and FCFROCE is effectively **zero** (Σ FCF over five years: $8m on ~$3.7bn of average capital; two variants negative). Reported OI declined **every year of the window** — 420 → 266 → 236 → 202 → **−163** — so P4's sustained-decline limb fires independently of the level test. FCF negative in FY2023, FY2025, FY2026. ΔOI/sh −$0.90 → −$0.38 (n.m. into a loss); ΔBV/sh −5%/yr. **L/E 212.2%** (FY2026) breaches P5 with no P6 case. Every limb of the Past test fails. **WEAK.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: the customer-concentration sentence was not retrieved this session (gap) — and is moot, because the force tally decides alone. Forces: customer power **STRONG** (wholesale retailers with private labels control shelf and markdowns), supplier power **WEAK** (broad independent factory base), substitutes **STRONG** (every athletic brand is a direct substitute; deferral costless), new entrants **STRONG** (On, Hoka, Lululemon and Vuori took exactly this company's share inside a decade — the demonstration case). Tally **3 STRONG**. Moat: **Brand — eroding**: fifteen years of price-promotion and off-price channel dependence is the opposite of pricing power, and the FY2026 revenue decline came despite a full restructuring cycle. Market growth YES (global sportswear grows; UA's share does not). **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

**P17 hard strike.** Test year FY2026: NI **−$495.6m**, derived FCF **−$162.2m** — **both legs negative**, and under the §2·1C loss-leg rule any more-than-token pay is a hard strike regardless of the normalized read.
