## 2. Understanding

HMS Networks is a 1988-founded, Halmstad-headquartered Swedish Large Cap that designs and largely manufactures differentiated industrial-communication hardware and software. Its products are embedded network interfaces and gateways (Anybus, Ixxat, PEAK), remote-access and data gateways (Ewon), Red Lion HMIs, controllers and N-Tron switches, and Intesis building-automation gateways. Machine builders, device OEMs, system integrators and distributors pay for them, worldwide (FY2025: EMEA 44%, Americas 42%, APAC 14%). It is now a three-division group after the 2024 Red Lion and PEAK purchases. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized ROCE is 16.1% and FCFROCE 13.0%, both at STRONG levels. Per-share growth is far above the floor: OI per share compounded 21.0% a year over FY2015–25 and FCF per share 26.3%. L/E was 114% after the FY2024 deals and is 90% now. The trend is the cap. End-basis ROCE ran 25–31% in FY2021–23, fell to 7.1% in FY2024 and recovered to 12.5% in FY2025 (≈ 14.9% LTM) once SEK 4.5bn of acquisitions entered the base. On the beginning basis that shows as three consecutive declines. As at Axfood, the no-decline condition is not met cleanly. **ADEQUATE.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASS. No customer exceeds 10% of revenue (FY2025 annual report). Production is split between its own plants in Halmstad and York and several EMS partners, and the components are standard. Forces, binary: customer power WEAK (thousands of OEMs, design-in switching costs); supplier power WEAK (multiple EMS partners and distributors, though memory price squeezes are flagged for H2 2026); substitutes STRONG (multiprotocol silicon from chip vendors and the Hilscher netX family lets high-volume OEMs design the module out); new entrants WEAK (protocol certifications, installed base, long design-in cycles). That is one strong force, contained. Moat: switching costs, because a designed-in interface needs redesign and recertification to swap, and it is stable. Market growth YES. **ADEQUATE.**
