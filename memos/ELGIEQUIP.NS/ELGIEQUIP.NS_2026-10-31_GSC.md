## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS, Past WEAK, Future ADEQUATE, shareholder-friendliness PASS with one caution, price EXPENSIVE: **AVOID**, on one gate (Past).

Elgi is an Indian maker of industrial air compressors that has grown revenue from INR 14.4bn to INR 39.5bn in ten years, earns about 27% on operating capital excluding cash, carries little debt and is run by a founding family that owns a large stake. The Past gate fails on two facts together: its return on all capital employed averaged 14.1% over ten years on the worst of the four readings, below 15%, and that return fell three years running to FY2026 as cash built up on the balance sheet. At INR 509.05 the shares trade at 51 times maintenance free cash flow and 35 times operating income; the trigger is INR 79.7. The verdict would turn WATCH if two clean years cleared the decline and the ten-year ROCE reached 15%; a buy would also need the price to fall by about 84%.

## 2. Understanding

Elgi Equipments, a Coimbatore company founded in 1960 and run by the Varadaraj family, designs and makes air compressors (oil-lubricated and oil-free screw, reciprocating and portable machines, with dryers, filters, spare parts and service), in India and through subsidiaries in Italy, the United States and Australia, and sells them through dealers, distributors and its own sales companies to factories and contractors in India (53% of FY2026 revenue), the Americas (24%), Europe (10%), Australia (4%) and elsewhere; a small automotive-equipment unit (garage equipment, 9% of revenue) is the second reportable segment. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Lens STD; one material segment. Worst-of-four ROCE is 14.1% over FY2017–FY2026 and 16.1% over the latest five years, both on capital that includes a growing pile of cash and deposits (INR 8.7bn in FY2026); excluding cash it runs 18% to 27%. FCFROCE, on the constructed maintenance basis, is 7.4% and 7.7% (all-capex 6.9% and 7.1%). ΔOI/sh is 19.8% (geometric) and ΔFCF/sh 20.7% (endpoint read governs; FY2020 FCF was negative). L/E is 58.9%. ROCE on beginning capital including cash fell in FY2024, FY2025 and FY2026 (22.3%, 19.2%, 18.5%, 18.0%): a sustained decline, and the ten-year level is under 15%. **WEAK** (ROCE decline).

## 4. Quality — future

The filer states that no external customer reaches 10% of revenue, so breadth is clean; inputs (castings, motors, electronics, steel) come from many suppliers. All four forces are WEAK: a fragmented industrial customer base, commodity inputs, no practical substitute for compressed air in most plants, and entry barriers in scale, airend design and a service network. The moat is plausible but not proven: a domestic brand and dealer-service network, with larger global rivals abroad and no printed market share; stable. Market growth is YES. **ADEQUATE.**
