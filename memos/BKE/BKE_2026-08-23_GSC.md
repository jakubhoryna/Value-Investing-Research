# BKE — The Buckle, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS · Past ADEQUATE · Future WEAK · Shareholder-friendliness FAIL · Cheapness FAIR → **AVOID**, on two gates: Future (1B) and shareholder-friendliness (1C).

Nothing has broken operationally: H1 comparable sales rose 3.5%, the store count is expanding for the first time in a decade, and returns held at a 32.7% worst-of-four ROCE on a debt-free balance sheet. The verdict moves because v1 predates v3 and three of its readings fail the live framework. FY2025 CEO pay of $11.71m breaches 5% of owner earnings on both reads — a hard strike, not v1's caveat. Price is not the obstacle at $43.81, and no price rescues a structural AVOID.

## 2. Understanding

The Buckle sells denim-led casual apparel, footwear and accessories — roughly half of it exclusive private-label brands it designs itself — to fashion-conscious teenage and young-adult consumers who pay at the till, through 447 company-operated stores across 42 mostly central-US states plus a ~15%-of-sales online channel, as a single-share-class, founder-controlled, borrowing-free specialty retailer headquartered and warehoused in Kearney, Nebraska, listed since 1992. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized ROCE is 32.7% and FCFROCE 27.3% (both end-of-period, cash-included), far clear of the STRONG thresholds; on the cash-excluded read no window year fell below 30%. Growth is what fails. Operating income per diluted share compounded at **+0.80%** a year FY2015→FY2025 against the +3% nominal floor; FCF/share managed +4.60% and book value/share −0.22%, the last by design under a ~100% payout. Leverage is lease-only at 133% of equity, against net cash and zero borrowings. The slide from 92.7% to 53.6% ROCE is normalisation off a stimulus peak, not a P4 sustained decline. **ADEQUATE** — elite returns on a flat capital base, without the compounding leg.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth passes. No customer exceeds a tenth of revenue, and the Axis Denim concentration (20.6% of net sales, ≈13% of COGS+opex, no long-term contract) clears on the ready-alternatives exception — denim cut-and-sew is not a scarce capability. Forces: customer power WEAK (atomised consumers, backward integration impossible); supplier power WEAK (contained and switchable); substitutes **STRONG** — casual apparel is the archetypal substitutable purchase and doing without costs nothing; new entrants **STRONG** — entry barriers are near zero, and neither scale nor switching costs dull them. Tally two. The moat is genuine but narrow: ingrainedness with a niche, stable rather than widening. Market growth MIXED. **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

**FAIL — P17 proportionality hard strike.** Dennis Nelson's FY2025 total of $11.71m exceeds 5% of the lower of levered FCF and net income on both reads: $10.29m fiscal-year and $10.38m trailing-three-year normalized, breaches of 1.14× and 1.13×. The absolute trigger (~$40m indexed) is untouched, and the FY2024 read cleared at 0.90× — the strike is new, produced by a 33% step-up in the CEO's total. Capital allocation is **SOUND**: fifteen years of near-full FCF payout, no repurchases at any price, no M&A, high-return capex, all funded from cash flow. Related-party items are a documented caveat, not a second flag.
