## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Capital allocation is the binding defect and it is live: $1,359m remained authorised at 10 May 2026, and the FY2026 pace implies most of it will be spent before the January-2027 expiry.
- Membership-fee dependence: fees are roughly half of operating profit (Q3 FY2026: $1,373m of $2,815m); renewal-rate erosion would hit OI disproportionately.
- Multiple risk is extreme — at ~54× FCF, a de-rating to a still-rich 25× halves the stock with no business deterioration.
- E-commerce substitution is the one force that could strengthen; the warehouse cost advantage does not fully transfer online.
- International build-out consumes capex with less proven unit economics.
- The share count is *rising* despite $903m/yr of repurchase — SBC is outrunning the programme.

To BUY from there: price into ≈$141–175, roughly an 85% decline; historically only 2008–09-type dislocations put COST near band. **Next review:** FY2026 10-K (~early October 2026) — the completed programme year plus the authorisation expiry make this an unusually informative touch.
