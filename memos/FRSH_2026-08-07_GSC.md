# FRSH — Freshworks Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past WEAK; Future WEAK; SF **FAIL** (P17 hard strike); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID (four gates)**.

Freshworks hit the milestones a growth-software company is supposed to hit — FY2025 delivered a first positive operating income ($13.2m) and $236.7m of free cash flow — and the framework still declines it. Cumulative operating income across FY2021–25 is **−$733.7m**, so every normalized ROCE variant is negative; the free cash flow that looks so much better is **62% stock-compensation add-back**, which is why FCFROCE scrapes the 8% floor at 8.06% while ROCE sits at −11% to −13%. The company then bought back **$443m of stock in FY2025** — 1.9× that year's free cash flow, funded from the IPO pile — at prices well above anything this framework's arithmetic calls worth. And the CEO was paid **$15.62m** against ceilings of $9.19m and $7.87m: a breach on both reads, a hard strike. The turn is real; the terms are not.

## 2. Understanding

Freshworks sells cloud software subscriptions — IT service management, customer support and CRM/sales tools — to small and mid-sized businesses and departmental buyers in larger ones, who pay per seat on annual contracts sold through a low-touch, product-led motion; a Nasdaq-listed San Mateo corporation with its engineering base in Chennai, founded 2010, listed September 2021, small-cap, global. **PASS.**
