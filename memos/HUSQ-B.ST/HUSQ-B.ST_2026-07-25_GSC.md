Husqvarna is a 337-year-old brand attached to a business that has not earned its cost of capital for most of a decade. Revenue grew 29% between FY2015 and FY2025; operating income grew from SEK 2,827m to SEK 2,898m — 0.28% per share per annum, against a 3% nominal floor. Capital employed nearly doubled over the same span, largely through the Orbit and Blastrac acquisitions, so returns fell rather than rose: worst-of-four normalized ROCE of 9.1% sits below the 10% ADEQUATE line, and the last four years read 7.9 / 7.8 / 5.5 / 6.2%. This is a double-gate failure, not a valuation problem. The governance is genuinely clean — that is what makes the name instructive rather than merely bad. Nothing here flips on price.

## 2. Understanding

**PASS.** Husqvarna Group designs, manufactures and sells outdoor power equipment — chainsaws, robotic and ride-on mowers, trimmers, blowers — plus consumer garden watering systems and diamond tools and concrete cutting/surface-preparation machines, to landscaping, forestry and construction professionals and to households, through independent servicing dealers, big-box and DIY retail chains, garden centres and e-commerce; it is a Swedish-listed, dual-class, vertically integrated manufacturer in three divisions (Forest & Garden 60% of sales, Gardena 25%, Construction 15%) selling mostly in Europe and North America.

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized 10y ROCE **9.1%** (range 9.1–10.2% across the four beginning/end × with/without-cash bases) and FCFROCE **5.7%** (5.7–6.4%). Both miss P3; the ROCE series also shows sustained decline — a 13–14% opening pair against 5.5–7.9% in FY2022–25 — which forces a downgrade independently of level. Per-share growth is the same story: ΔOI/sh +0.28% p.a. and ΔFCF/sh +1.19% p.a. geometric over ten years, both under the 3% inflation floor; ΔBV/sh +6.1% and ΔTBV/sh +5.4% are retained-earnings arithmetic, not returns. Two years of the ten had negative FCF (FY2018, FY2022 on a SEK 3.8bn inventory build). Leverage is the one comfortable reading: L/E 121.3%, well inside P5. **WEAK** — the returns are below the ADEQUATE threshold and falling.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth **PASSES** both legs: the AR states no individual customer reaches 10% of net sales, and no supplier concentration is disclosed against SEK 43.7bn of total costs. Forces, binary: customer bargaining power **STRONG** (the FY2022–24 build-and-destock cycle was dictated by the retail channel and absorbed entirely on Husqvarna's balance sheet; SEK 861m of accrued customer rebates; Gardena Q4 margins of −38% and −37%); supplier power **WEAK** (fragmented inputs, in-house engines and robotics); substitutes **WEAK** (no way to mow without a mower); new entrants **STRONG** (the battery transition dissolved the engine-manufacturing barrier, and low-cost wire-free robotic mowers from Segway, Mammotion and Dreame are attacking the one product category Husqvarna created). Tally **2 strong** — the book's stop signal. Moat: **brand**, real among professional arborists and in German-speaking Gardena markets, but **eroding** — a decade of flat operating income and an impairment test in which Gardena's recoverable amount sits close to carrying value. Market growth **MIXED**. **WEAK** on either limb alone.

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

**PASS**, with one flag. P17: highest-paid employee SEK 22.3m (the departing CEO, including SEK 13.3m severance) = 1.26% of the lower of FY2025 FCF (1,944) and net income (1,768), against the 5% strike line, and ≈USD 2.3m against the ≈USD 40m CPI-indexed scrutiny trigger; chairman's fee SEK 2.30m is ordinary for a Swedish large cap. No share repurchase has been executed in the ten-year window — the only equity-swap activity is a third-party bank holding 4.57m B-shares (0.8%) to settle LTI awards, which is not a company repurchase and does not meet the dilution-sterilisation predicate. RPT clean and explicitly disclosed. The BV ≫ TBV gap triggered the acquisition audit: **Orbit Irrigation (Nov-2021) at EV USD 480m against USD 28m of adjusted pre-deal operating income = 17.1×** — above the 15× flag, below the 20× strike. One flag, no pair, so no FAIL; the Investor AB / Lundberg dual-class control is a logged caveat, not a second negative.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Low-cost wire-free robotic mowers commoditising Automower, the group's highest-margin franchise and its principal claim to a moat.
- Gardena goodwill and intangibles carried close to recoverable amount — an impairment would cut book value and reset the returns base downward again.
- Structural exposure to retailer inventory cycles, which turn a modest demand swing into a violent working-capital swing (FY2022 CFO −SEK 1,709m).
- SEK 13.2bn of debt and leases against SEK 2.6bn of LTM operating income leaves little room if the cycle does not turn.
- Organic sales −1% LTM and −4% in Q2 2026: the destocking recovery has not become growth.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

To WATCH requires **both** quality gates to move. Past cannot flip on one good year: with FY2016 rolling out of the window, FY2026 operating income of roughly **SEK 8.1bn** — a 17.7% group margin against 6.2% reported — would be needed to lift the rolling worst-of-four to 10%. Realistically this is a multi-year repair, and the earliest credible re-rating of the Past gate is FY2028–29. Future flips only if the entrant threat is demonstrably contained (robotic-mower share and price stabilising) and the brand moat stops eroding. A Gardena impairment or a further leg down in returns would confirm rather than change the verdict. **Next review: FY2026 year-end report, ~early February 2027**; earlier on an announced Gardena impairment or a divisional divestment.
