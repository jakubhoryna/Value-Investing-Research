## 2. Understanding

Group sentence: Beijer Alma AB is a Stockholm-listed industrial group (listed since 1987, net revenue SEK 7.75bn in 2025, about 3,700 employees, controlled by the Anders Wall family through class A shares) that manufactures customised springs, wire forms and stampings in 19 countries for industrial manufacturers and spare-parts distributors in Europe, North America and Asia, and, through Nordic companies, distributes and services hoses, valves, compressors, industrial rubber and machinery and makes niche acoustic, vehicle-scale and windscreen products for Nordic process, engineering, public-sector and real-estate customers.

- **Beijer Components** (64% of 2025 revenue): customised industrial springs, wire and flat-strip components and gas springs (Industry, 81% of the unit) sold to manufacturers in many industries, and chassis springs (19%) sold to vehicle spare-parts distributors in the UK, Germany and the Nordics.
- **Beijer Tech** (36%): Industrial Products (crusher parts, sheet-metal machinery, industrial rubber), Fluid Technology (hoses, couplings, valves, compressed air) and Niche Technologies (acoustics, waste-truck scales, windscreens, microwave systems), sold mainly to Nordic industrial companies, municipalities and emergency services.
