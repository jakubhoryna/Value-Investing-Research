# ORCL — Oracle Corporation: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | SF | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | WEAK | WEAK | FAIL | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** (triple gate failure) |

Oracle is two businesses wearing one ticker: a moaty software annuity that compounded OI/share at 9.2% for a decade, and — where all incremental capital now goes — a debt-financed AI datacenter landlord renting commodity GPU capacity to a few AI labs. The second business fails every gate the first built its record on: FY2026 capex of $55.7B drove FCF to −$23.7B, net debt (incl. leases) to ~$128B, and the $638B backlog is dominated by concentrated, cash-burning counterparties. Add a $250M co-CEO option grant against a $30M cap and a founder exempted from his own anti-pledging policy, and no gate survives. A 58% fall from $345.72 still leaves EV/OI at 25–28× versus 7. The flip needs years: FCF inflection, deleveraging toward the P5 flag, comp reform.

## 2. Understanding

Oracle sells differentiated enterprise database and application software (Oracle Database, Java, Fusion/NetSuite cloud ERP-HCM-SCM, Oracle Health) on subscription and license-support terms, and increasingly rents AI/cloud datacenter capacity (OCI), to corporate and government customers worldwide (HQ Austin; ~62% Americas revenue); a 49-year-old mega-cap incumbent mid-transformation from a high-margin software annuity into a leverage-financed, capital-intensive AI infrastructure provider whose $638B contracted backlog is concentrated in a few AI labs. Multi-unit: dominant by revenue = software/support; dominant by capital allocation = OCI — the split is carried through Gate 1B. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four 10y normalized ROCE is 11.6% (end-of-period, cash-in) — above the 10% ADEQUATE line, below 15% STRONG, and in sustained decline: cash-out ROCE ran 24–30% in FY2017–21 versus 15–16% since. Worst-of-four FCFROCE is 5.2%, with FY2025–26 FCF negative outright. OI/share grew 9.2% geo-mean (beats the 3% floor); FCF/share n.m. Leverage decides: L/E breached the 700% hard cap for six straight years (FY2020–25, n.m. on negative equity FY2022) and sits at 514% now — 2.6× the flag — while P6 fails (FCFROCE not great; today's borrowing funds capex, not under-priced buybacks). Substitutes don't rescue it: net debt/FCF n.m., interest cover 4.5×. **WEAK** — leverage breach plus sustained returns decline.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth fails both sides for the capital-receiving unit. Customer: no >10% customer in trailing revenue, but the $638B RPO (up from $137.8B in one year, reportedly ~$300B OpenAI) makes consolidation of the revenue base certain; the captive-customer waiver is unavailable — these customers backward-integrate (Stargate self-builds) and multi-source. Supplier: NVIDIA is the effective single supplier; no ready-equivalents. Forces, OCI segment: customer power STRONG, supplier power STRONG, substitutes STRONG (identical rival capacity, low switching costs), new entrants STRONG — four of four, the book's stop signal. Legacy software: 0 strong, switching-cost/ingrainedness moat, stable — but the moat guards the annuity, not the increment. Market growth YES, attracting torrential capital. **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Strike — **compensation (P17)**: new co-CEO Magouyrk's Sept 2025 promotion grant is $250M of options (80% merely time-based; Sicilia $100M) versus the $30M cap — 8.3× over, extending the FY2018 mega-PSO pattern (~$100M+ each to Ellison/Catz/Hurd) that produced a decade of failed say-on-pay votes through 2021 (2024: 78% support, celebrated as improvement). Control caveat compounds it: Ellison (~40% owner, 81) is the sole exemption from Oracle's anti-pledging policy, with 346M shares pledged for personal credit lines. RPT history: the $9.3B NetSuite purchase (FY2017) bought a company its chairman ~40%-owned. Buybacks were the bright spot — $92B at $45–90 in FY2019–22, halted entirely once the price ran. One strike suffices. **FAIL.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Counterparty concentration: a material share of the $638B RPO depends on OpenAI, a loss-maker reliant on continuous external funding.
- Balance sheet: $129.5B gross debt, $30.2B lease liabilities, ~$250B undiscounted lease commitments — against negative FCF.
- Commodity economics: GPU rental lacks the switching costs protecting the database annuity; returns on the new capital are unproven.
- NVIDIA dependence prices Oracle's cost structure at its supplier's discretion.
- Key-man/control: an 81-year-old controlling shareholder with 12% of the company pledged against personal debt.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

AVOID is triple-anchored, so the flip is multi-year: Past needs L/E under the 200% flag and positive FCFROCE (earliest visible FY2028+); Future needs RPO diversification or pricing power OCI structurally lacks; SF needs restraint the Sept 2026 proxy is unlikely to show. Next review: FY2027 results (June 2027) — academic unless leverage and FCF inflect together. Trigger band recorded for completeness only.
