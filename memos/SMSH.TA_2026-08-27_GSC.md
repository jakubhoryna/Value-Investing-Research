# SMSH.TA — Smart Shooter Ltd.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS · Past WEAK · Future WEAK · SF PASS (one flag) · Price EXPENSIVE → **AVOID**, failing on two gates (Past and Future).

The attraction is real: a differentiated AI fire-control niche riding a defense super-cycle, first operating profit in FY2025 ($6.1M OI on $36.8M revenue), a ~$49M backlog, ~$80M net cash post-IPO, and counter-drone optionality. A flip needs several more FY2025-quality years, materially broader customer mix, and a very deep derating — none imminent.

## 2. Understanding

Smart Shooter designs, manufactures and sells differentiated electro-optical, AI-assisted fire-control systems (the SMASH family) that attach to small arms and remote weapon stations for military, homeland-security and law-enforcement organisations — paid by government defence ministries (Israeli MoD ≈36% of revenue; Europe the largest region; customers in 25+ countries) — an Israeli-headquartered (Yagur), vertically integrated developer-manufacturer with US, German and Australian subsidiaries, founded 2011, 96 employees, newly TASE-listed (March 2026), ~$37M revenue, first operating profit in FY2025 amid wartime demand. **PASS.**
