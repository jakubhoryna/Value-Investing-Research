## 1. Verdict

Lemonade is growing fast: revenue rose from $22.5m in FY2018 to $737.9m in FY2025, and the loss ratio has fallen into the 60s. But it has lost money in every year it has reported. Operating income before investment income and financing cost was −$181.4m in FY2025, and free cash flow, once the insurance float is taken out, was −$379.5m over the twelve months to June 2026. No price makes this cheap until operating income and free cash flow turn positive. That is the first flip condition, and nothing else can move before it.

## 2. Understanding

Lemonade, Inc. (New York; founded 2015, NYSE-listed since 2020, past 3 million customers) is a licensed insurance group: Lemonade Insurance Company and Metromile Insurance Company in the US, Lemonade Insurance N.V. in the EU and UK, and a Cayman captive, Lemonade Re SPC. It sells renters, homeowners, car and pet insurance direct to individual consumers through its app and website, and places term life with partner insurers for commission. It cedes part of its premium to reinsurers (Hannover Re and MAPFRE from July 2025), which pay it ceding commissions. In FY2025, 96% of gross written premium was in the US (California 21.9%, Texas 12.7%, New York 9.9%). It has one reportable segment. **PASS.**

The lens is **STD**. Lemonade is a licensed insurance group, but it publishes no group capital ratio covering 90% of consolidated assets. It prints entity RBC levels only (LIC 586%, MIC 625%), so FI does not fit. The insurers are read on STD under rule (a).
