# LMND — Lemonade, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past WEAK; Future WEAK; shareholder-friendliness **FAIL (P17 hard strike — more-than-token pay against a year in which both owner-earnings legs are negative)**; cheapness EXPENSIVE (7.5× book vs a 1.2× substitution anchor) — **AVOID on three gates**.

The bull case is real growth: revenue is compounding at 40–60%+, loss ratios have reached target territory, and management guides to EBITDA breakeven in 2026. None of that is testable under this framework yet: the company has never earned a profit in eight reported years, book value per share has halved since 2021 ($16.14 → $7.22) while the share count grew, and derived FCF is still negative once the externally financed customer-acquisition spend is counted as what it is. At 7.5× a melting book the price already assumes the underwriting machine works at scale. A flip requires positive owner earnings first — nothing else in the framework can move until then.

## 2. Understanding

Lemonade sells direct-to-consumer property-casualty and life insurance — renters, homeowners, car, pet, and term-life policies bought and serviced through AI chat agents — as a licensed US carrier (plus EU/UK entities) with a captive Cayman reinsurer and heavy quota-share reinsurance, HQ New York, founded 2015, IPO 2020, ~2.9m mostly young customers, in-force premium ≈ $1.2bn, loss-making in every year of its existence. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Substitution rule (insurer): ROE replaces ROCE, FCFROCE n.m. Net income is negative in all eight reported years FY2018–FY2025 (worst −$297.8m in FY2022; −$165.5m in FY2025). Normalized average ROE FY2021–25: **−31.0%** (end-equity) / −30.9% (beginning) — worst-of-set deeply under any floor. EPS improves (−$4.59 → −$2.24) but BV/sh fell −18%/yr (FY2021–25) as losses consumed the IPO capital. Non-deposit interest-bearing debt/equity 29.6% (FY2025, the growing "synthetic agents" CAC facility). **WEAK** — categorical.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASSES (millions of small policyholders; reinsurance capacity concentrated but multiple carriers stand ready — logged). Forces: customer bargaining power **STRONG** (commodity quotes, aggregator comparison, trivial switching); supplier power WEAK; threat of substitutes **STRONG** (every incumbent insurer and agent channel sells the same promise); new entrants WEAK (licensing and capital are real permit barriers). Tally 2 STRONG — the book's stop signal. Moat: none demonstrated — the claimed AI cost advantage is not yet visible as sustained underwriting profit, and brand is nascent. Market growth YES. **WEAK.**
