# PTON — Peloton Interactive, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past **WEAK** (Σ window OI −$4.3bn; equity negative three years running); Future **WEAK** (2-strong tally on an eroding subscriber base); SF **FAIL** (P17 hard strike — $18.2m vs a $16.2m year ceiling with no normalized acquittal available); cheapness EXPENSIVE (the FCF optics dissolve on the EV/OI gate) — **AVOID (four gates)**.

FY2026 was Peloton's first profitable year, and the $2.5bn question is whether a shrinking subscription base can be harvested indefinitely. The answer is arithmetic: one positive year against −$4.3bn of window operating income is not a record; free cash flow of $378m is 55% stock-compensation add-back on a subscriber count falling 9% a year; equity is still negative under $1.7bn of debt; and the CEO's first-year package breached the ceiling in a year with no positive net income. The MCap/FCF leg alone would pass.

## 2. Understanding

Peloton sells connected-fitness hardware (bikes, treadmills, rowers) and, principally, a $44/month all-access subscription to streamed and on-demand classes consumed on that hardware, plus a cheaper app tier — 2.55m connected-fitness subscribers, mostly North American households, pay; a Delaware corporation, IPO 2019, HQ New York, hardware manufactured by Asian partners (post-Tonic divestiture), Precor-branded commercial equipment retained; the category's brand-defining name, shrunk from its pandemic peak to a subscription-harvest story under a new (2025) CEO. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Not close on any limb. Σ window operating income **−$4,335m** (−2,734 → −1,197 → −529 → −36 → **+161**); every normalized ROCE/FCFROCE variant is deeply negative. FY2026 is the first positive year for both OI and NI. FCF turned positive in FY2025 ($324m) and FY2026 ($378m) — but stock compensation ($230m/$209m) is 55–70% of it (the FRSH pattern), and the count grew ~5%/yr against it. **Equity is negative** (−$242m TTM; −$0.32/sh) — L/E n.m.; judged instead: net debt ≈ $0.5bn against $378m FCF (manageable), interest covered. ΔBV/sh, ΔTBV/sh n.m. (negative base). **WEAK** — the one good year is the start of a record, not a record.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASS (millions of household payers; app-store share of billings is modest for hardware-attached subs — platform-payer look-through noted, not decisive). Customer power WEAK individually, though churn is the collective vote; supplier power WEAK (contract manufacturing diversified); substitutes **STRONG** — gyms, free and cheap fitness apps, YouTube, and doing-without: a subscriber base shrinking 8.8% in FY2026 with FY2027 guided to −9.8% *is* substitution in the data; new entrants **STRONG** (Apple Fitness+, Lululemon Studio-class bundles, zero-marginal-cost content). Tally **2 STRONG**. Moat **switching costs** (owned hardware, workout history, community) — real for the remaining base, **eroding** at a measured 9%/yr. Market growth MIXED. **WEAK.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- SBC at ~$209m/yr against a $2.5bn MCap is ~8%/yr of quiet dilution funding the "free" cash flow.
- Negative equity under $1.7bn of lease-inclusive debt leaves no cushion for a churn shock.
- Hardware demand is the funnel; every quarter of declining engagement thins the future sub base.
- Connected-fitness content is a zero-marginal-cost battleground for competitors who need no profit from it.
