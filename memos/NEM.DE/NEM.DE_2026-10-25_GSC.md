Nemetschek sells design, construction-collaboration and 3D-content software, mostly by subscription, to architects, engineers, contractors and media studios. Its record is excellent: 17–26% returns on capital every year. It fails on capital allocation: in 2024 it paid EUR 733m for GoCanvas, a business with EUR 2.7m of full-year EBITDA, and in July 2026 it closed HCSS, its largest deal, without yet publishing the terms.
