# 005930.KS — Samsung Electronics: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | ADEQUATE | **WEAK** | PASS | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID (Future)** |

Samsung is a competently run, conservatively financed national champion whose earnings engine is a commodity — memory prices it does not set. Three of four competitive forces read STRONG against it and its scale moat is eroding at both ends (HBM leadership ceded to SK Hynix above, state-funded Chinese entrants below), so Future fails the book's ≥2-strong stop signal regardless of the current AI-memory boom. Price compounds the case: at ₩309,500 the stock trades at 59× FCF and 43–46× EV/OI — roughly 7× the framework's limit price, and still 32× EV/OI even backvalued against the FY2018 cycle-peak OI. A flip to WATCH requires the forces picture to change (memory consolidation, HBM switching costs proving structural, Chinese entry contained) — not a price move.

## 2. Understanding

Samsung Electronics designs and manufactures commodity memory semiconductors (DRAM/NAND/HBM — ₩104tn, 31% of FY2025 revenue, and the earnings swing factor), contract logic chips (foundry, System LSI), smartphones and consumer devices (₩126tn), TVs/appliances, OLED display panels (SDC), and automotive audio (Harman); its paying customers are global OEMs, hyperscalers, carriers, and retailers; it is a vertically integrated, Lee-family-influenced chaebol flagship, ~57 years old, Korea's largest company (FY2025 revenue ₩333.6tn), headquartered in Suwon with manufacturing across Korea, the US, China, and Vietnam. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four 10y normalized ROCE is **10.6%** (wc, end-of-period) — above the 10% ADEQUATE floor but far from 15%; worst-of-four FCFROCE is **4.9%**, killed by structurally heavy capex (₩52tn in FY2025; FY2023 FCF was negative). Per-share growth clears the floor: OI/sh +6.2%/yr, FCF/sh +11.2%/yr, BV/sh +10.5%/yr geometric (FY2015→25). L/E 31% — pristine. The concern is trend: half-decade average ROCE_wc fell 15.6% → 9.2% with successively lower peaks (2018: 21.9%; 2021: 15.3%; 2025: 9.4%) as capital employed doubled; two consecutive recovery years keep this cyclical rather than "sustained decline" (alternate WEAK reading logged, App. C). **ADEQUATE.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: **PASS both sides** — no IFRS 8 single-customer ≥10% disclosure (silence is the affirmative under K-IFRS; call logged) and no supplier near 10% of COGS+opex (ASML's EUV monopoly sits on the capex side — logged look-through note). Forces: customer power **STRONG** (commodity memory, zero switching costs — FY2023 OI −85%); supplier power **STRONG** (single-supplier EUV ecosystem; 2019 Japan materials embargo demonstrated chokepoints); substitutes **WEAK**; new entrants **STRONG** (state-funded CXMT/YMTC have already breached the scale barrier at the trailing edge). Tally **3 STRONG** — the stop signal. Moat: scale/cost in memory manufacturing — **eroding** (HBM lead lost, foundry loss-making vs TSMC, Chinese legacy entry). Market growth YES (AI memory) — doesn't rescue. **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Top-paid executive ₩13.47bn (~US$9.5m; sourced, FY2025 business report) vs the US$30m and ₩1,658bn (5%) ceilings — passes by an order of magnitude. Outside-director fees not fetched (data gap; Korean norms sit far below any calibrated stop). RPT (note 31) immaterial against group scale; chaebol structure and the controlling family's legal history are documented caveats, not strikes. Buybacks ₩8.2tn (FY2025) + ₩1.8tn (FY2024) with cancellation, executed at ~9–12× EV/OI — above conservative worth (caveat), but not dilution-sterilisation (118.3M shares bought vs 6.0M granted). Dividends steady at ~₩9.9tn. **PASS.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Memory cycle reversal from record FY2025–26 pricing would collapse OI as in 2019 and 2023.
- Chinese legacy-DRAM/NAND capacity (CXMT/YMTC) floods commodity tiers and permanently caps trough pricing.
- HBM qualification lag vs SK Hynix/Micron persists, keeping Samsung out of the highest-margin tier.
- Foundry share loss to TSMC continues to consume capex without acceptable returns.
- US–China export controls hit China fabs and equipment access; Korea governance/succession discount persists.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

AVOID → WATCH requires the Future verdict to move, not the price: forces tally below 2 STRONG — e.g., memory consolidating around HBM with demonstrated customer lock-in (switching costs becoming structural), Chinese entry contained by sanctions or failure, and the moat stabilising. Cheapness alone cannot flip an AVOID. Band ≈ ₩41,700 kept for reference. Next review: FY2026 audited FS (~March 2027) or a structural forces event.
