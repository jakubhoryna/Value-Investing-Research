**The verdict flips on the same evidence that flipped Alphabet six names ago: Meta has stopped buying.** The Q2 2026 10-Q, filed yesterday, states it plainly — **"We did not repurchase any shares of Class A common stock during the six months ended June 30, 2026"** — and confirms that **$25.03 billion remained available and authorized at both 31 December 2025 and 30 June 2026**, an unchanged balance that independently corroborates a zero draw. This ends a repurchase programme that had run continuously since January 2017. It is not exhaustion; it is a deliberate redirection of capital into the AI build-out, with H1 2026 capex of **$49.1bn against revenue of $117.1bn — 41.9% of sales**.

v2's FAIL rested on the retired P19 20× ladder, applied to executions in 2021, 2022, 2024 and 2025. Those remain the flag: 22.1–24.3× MCap/FCF, with 2025 also at 20.3× EV/OI on the maintenance basis, and no worth-referenced criterion ever published. And the halt is **capex-driven, not price-driven** — nothing has been said about worth, and $25.03bn sits authorised with no expiration date. That combination is CAUTION, not SOUND. One flag; P17 is clean on the FY2025 proxy and the dual-class caveat stays unpaired, consistent with the treatment given GOOGL today. **SF PASSES.**

**The caveat is the same one Alphabet carries, and it is large.** Q2 operating income fell to $18,775m from $20,441m a year earlier and net income to $15,848m from $18,337m, even as revenue grew 28% — costs rose 55%. Long-term debt went $58.7bn → $83.7bn in six months, cash and marketable securities fell from a combined $81.6bn to $90.3bn only because $75.6bn of securities were bought with borrowed money, and H1 free cash flow on an all-capex basis was **$14,975m against $49.1bn of capex**. The returns engine has not broken — but this is a company spending 42% of revenue on a bet whose returns are entirely prospective. WATCH here means the name re-enters the watchlist at **+204%** above band, not that it has become safer.

Band rebuilt on the Q2 balance sheet: **≈ $183–218**.
