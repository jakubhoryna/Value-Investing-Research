# COST — Costco Wholesale: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | STRONG | STRONG | **FAIL** (P19 hard strikes) | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** |

Costco is one of the finest business records in the register: worst-of-four ROCE 19.3%, FCFROCE 14.1%, double-digit per-share compounding across every metric, a cost moat that is strengthening, and zero strong forces. It fails on one gate, and decisively: buyback discipline. Every repurchase year FY2021–FY2025 executed at implied EV/OI between 23.9× and 40.5× — hard strikes above the 20× line on both metrics, in record-earnings years with the price far above any conservative worth — while the flat share count against $0.7–0.9B annual SBC marks the programme as dilution-sterilisation, the book's strike-at-any-price. The special dividends (FY2021, FY2024) are genuinely shareholder-friendly and are noted, but strikes are never averaged away. For the verdict to flip, buybacks must stop or move below conservative worth *and* the price must fall ~85% to the ≈$141 band — the SF strike keeps COST AVOID even inside the band.

## 2. Understanding

Costco sells a deliberately narrow assortment (~4,000 SKUs) of quality goods and services at near-cost prices through ~900 owned membership warehouses, earning its profit primarily from annual membership fees paid by ~80M consumer and small-business member households; it is a vertically-lean, single-format retailer, US-dominant with growing Canadian, Mexican, and Asian operations, HQ Issaquah WA — a mature, prominent, still-expanding giant whose true product is the membership, not the merchandise. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized averages (FY2016–25): ROCE 19.3% (end-of-period, with-cash), FCFROCE 14.1% — both clear P2 comfortably; the ex-cash variants run 28–30%. ΔOI/sh geometric mean +11.0%, ΔFCF/sh +15.2%, ΔBV/sh +10.6% despite ~$25.7B of special and regular dividends over the window. No decline anywhere — returns rose through the decade. L/E 164% latest, under the 200% flag (mid-window years above 200% were current-liability float, not term debt). **STRONG.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: PASS both sides — tens of millions of member households, no customer near 10%; supplier base broad and Costco holds the whip hand. Forces: customer power WEAK (fragmented members, renewal rates ~90%+), supplier power WEAK (scale, Kirkland dual-sourcing), substitutes WEAK (competitors cannot match the cost structure without destroying their margin model), new entrants WEAK (scale and price reputation are decades to build). Tally 0 strong. Moat: **cost**, sourced in scale purchasing plus the membership model that lets merchandise run at near-zero margin — stable-to-strengthening. Market growth YES, modest. **STRONG.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Comp: CEO FY2025 total $13.9M — 0.18% of lower(FCF $7.8B, NI $8.1B), far under the 5% strike and the ~$40M indexed trigger; outside-director fees not independently verified this pass (no breach indication; verdict does not turn on it). RPT: nothing material found. Buybacks: **hard strike** — FY2021–25 implied EV/OI 23.9× / 28.8× / 26.8× / 33.3× / 40.5×, all above 20×, all non-trough years; share count flat vs ~$0.8B/yr SBC = sterilisation. Dividends: regular plus $10/sh (FY2021) and $15/sh (FY2024) specials — rational and positive. **FAIL — P19 hard strikes, multiple.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Membership-fee dependence: fee income ≈ over half of operating profit; renewal-rate erosion would hit OI disproportionately.
- E-commerce economics: the warehouse cost advantage does not fully transfer online; Amazon-style substitution is the one force that could strengthen.
- Multiple risk is extreme: at ~52× FCF, a de-rating to a still-rich 25× halves the stock with no business deterioration.
- International execution (China build-out) consumes capex with less proven unit economics.
- Buyback discipline could worsen: authorisations have grown; every dollar spent near current prices destroys per-share value under the framework.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

To WATCH: buybacks cease or demonstrably move below conservative worth for a full FY, clearing the sterilisation pattern (comp and RPT already clean). Cheapness is a separate, distant hurdle (≈$141). To BUY: both, simultaneously — historically only 2008–09-type dislocations put COST near band. Next review: FY2026 results (~late Sep 2026) re-tests the P19 execution table on the new year's repurchases; earlier on price < ≈$210 (~1.5× band) or a stated change in repurchase policy.
