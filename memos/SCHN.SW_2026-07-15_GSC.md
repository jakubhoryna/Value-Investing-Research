# SCHN.SW — Schindler Holding AG: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | ADEQUATE | STRONG | **FAIL** (P19 buyback hard strike) | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** |

Schindler is one of the best businesses in this register — a top-three global elevator/escalator oligopolist whose profit engine is a decades-long, high-switching-cost service-and-modernization annuity on an installed base it largely built. Returns are excellent (worst-of-four ROCE ~18–20%, FCFROCE ~18% on almost no capital — FY2025 capex was CHF 107m on CHF 10.9bn revenue), leverage is light (L/E 130%), the moat is durable, and capital allocation is otherwise rational. It is stopped at the shareholder-friendliness gate — not on comp or related-party dealing, both clean — but on **P19**: through FY2025 the company repurchased CHF 231m of its own already-fully-valued stock at a weighted-average CHF 281 (reg. 271.10 / PC 283.63), an implied **MCap/FCF of 21.8×** (>20× hard-strike line; EV/OI leg 19.3× flags), for cancellation. Buying back at ~2.7× the framework's CHEAP line is a value-destructive repurchase under P19, and a single hard strike fails the gate regardless of quality. Cheapness is independently no help: at ~CHF 259 blended the stock trades at ~20× FCF and ~18–21× EV/OI, deeply above the ≈ CHF 103 trigger band (+151%). **To flip to WATCH the buyback would have to stop or move to a genuine discount to worth; nothing about price alone rescues it.**

## 2. Understanding

Schindler manufactures, installs, maintains, and modernizes elevators, escalators, and moving walks (one global Elevators & Escalators segment; a tiny Finance/BuildingMinds unit), selling to building owners, developers, and contractors across 100+ countries, as a family-controlled (Schindler/Bonnard, 69.5% of votes) Swiss-listed industrial with a dual registered-share/non-voting-participation-certificate structure. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized-average ROCE is 20.5% (10y 2016–25) / 18.0% (6y detailed 2020–25); FCFROCE 17.8% / 18.7% — comfortably STRONG-calibre returns on a capital-light, cash-rich balance sheet. But the STRONG growth test fails: operating profit is barely above its decade-ago level (2014 CHF 1,138m → 2018 1,269m → 2025 1,384m; ΔOI/sh geo-mean ~1.8% over 2014–25, ~2.2% over 2016–25, clearing 3% only on a trough-2015 base), and there was a clear multi-year decline (OI 1,258m in 2019 → 904m in 2022, China new-installs + inflation) that only reversed in 2023–25. Either condition caps the rating. L/E 130% (well within P5). **ADEQUATE** (STRONG unavailable on growth + mid-decade decline; no P12).

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASS both sides (millions of fragmented building-owner customers; diversified component supply — steel, electronics, motors — none >10%). Four forces, all **WEAK** (0 strong → favourable): customer power weak (fragmented base, high switching costs on the sticky service annuity); supplier power weak (many vendors, scale buyer); substitutes weak (no substitute for vertical transport in mid/high-rise); new entrants weak (global oligopoly — Otis/KONE/Schindler/TK/Mitsubishi — protected by installed-base service density, safety certification, and the razor-and-blade install→service model). Moat source: **switching costs / installed-base service lock-in**, stable-to-strengthening via connected ("Schindler Ahead") units improving retention. Market MIXED (new-install cyclical downcycle led by China; service + modernization structurally growing). **STRONG.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Comp clean: highest-paid is Silvio Napoli (dual Chairman+CEO) at ~CHF 4–5.5m — ~0.5% of net income, far below the 5%-of-lower(FCF,NI) line (~CHF 51m) and the CPI-indexed ~CHF 35–40m absolute scrutiny trigger; outside directors ≤ CHF 371k (below 2× Swiss norm). RPT minimal and arm's-length. Dividends rational (CHF 6.00; 68% payout; mature high-FCF business). Family/dual-class control is a documented caveat, not a strike (allocation is not value-destructive elsewhere). **The gate fails on P19 alone:** the FY2025 buyback (CHF 231m @ wavg CHF 281, for cancellation) implies **MCap/FCF 21.8×** — a hard strike (>20×); the EV/OI leg (19.3×) is a flag. FY2024's smaller tranche (~CHF 253) was only a flag; the FY2025 execution at higher prices tips it over. **FAIL.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Buyback continues to Nov 2026 at ≥ CHF 270; each tranche above ~CHF 232 (20× FCF) re-strikes P19 — the gating issue, not the business.
- China new-installation demand (>20% down) and local competition could keep group revenue flat and pressure the modernization pipeline.
- FX translation (CHF strength) chronically clips reported growth (~CHF 350m headwind in 2024).
- Family control via non-voting PCs limits outside-holder influence on capital allocation (i.e., on the buyback itself).
- Margin recovery (12.6% EBIT, mid-term 13% target) is real but leaves the stock priced for more growth than a decade of ~2% OI supports.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

Flips toward BUY only on a ~60% de-rating to the ≈ CHF 103 band — not in prospect. **Next review: FY2026 results (~Feb 2027)** to re-test buyback pace and price; earlier if the programme is terminated or a new authorization is announced.
