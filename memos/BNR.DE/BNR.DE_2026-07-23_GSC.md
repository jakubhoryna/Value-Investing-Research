# BNR.DE — Brenntag SE: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |

| PASS | **WEAK** | ADEQUATE | PASS | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** (Past WEAK) |

The world's #1 chemical distributor — a genuine scale leader running a broad, low-concentration book on both sides. But the deciding gate is Past: reported operating income has fallen for three straight years, from €1,382m (2022 peak) to €618m (2025), roughly halving ROCE from ~16% to ~7%, with per-share OI growth negative over the window. The worst-of-four normalized ROCE (11.5%) sits at ADEQUATE level, but the sustained multi-year decline in returns and negative per-share OI growth force WEAK under P4 — the same treatment applied to the register's cyclical-industrial cohort (CON, BASF, AD). SF is clean and Future is a solid ADEQUATE, so this is not a broken business — it is a good business at a cyclical trough, mispriced. Even so, the price is nowhere near range: EV/OI is 18× on trough OI and still 8× on the 2022 peak. A verdict flip needs an earnings inflection (Past → ADEQUATE) **and** a price roughly a third of today's; the former is plausible on the next up-cycle, the latter is not on the table at €59.

## 2. Understanding

Brenntag SE is the global market-leading full-line distributor of industrial and specialty chemicals and ingredients (Essentials + Specialties divisions), buying in bulk from ~10,000 producers and selling formulated, small-lot, regulatory-and-logistics-wrapped baskets to ~195,000 industrial and specialty customers across 77 countries from an ~670-site network, HQ Essen, listed as a German SE with no controlling shareholder. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized ROCE (2021–25) **11.5%** (best 13.5%); worst FCFROCE 8.3% (WC-noise-driven, not a clean signal). Per-year ROCE traces the cycle down: 16.1% → 14.2% → 10.0% → **7.3% (2025)** — three consecutive declines. Per-share OI growth **−2.8%/yr** over the window (below the 3% floor and below flat); FCF/sh growth is positive but WC-distorted. Leverage benign (L/E 146%, well inside the 200% flag). Level alone would read ADEQUATE, but the sustained returns decline + negative per-share OI growth are the P4 WEAK conditions. Reported-EBIT history is 5 clean years (<P1) — STRONG is unavailable regardless. **WEAK** — sustained multi-year decline in returns.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth **PASS** both sides — no customer >10% of revenue (~195k customers), no supplier >10% of purchases (fragmented producer base; ready-alternatives condition holds). Forces: customer power **WEAK** (atomised base, high switching/embedded logistics, no backward integration); supplier power **STRONG** (chemical majors hold real terms leverage and can forward-integrate/bypass on large accounts — the distributor's structural squeeze); substitutes **WEAK** (small-lot, multi-product, regulatory full-service is sticky; disintermediation contained); new entrants **WEAK** (scale, hazmat permits, supplier access). Tally **1 strong (contained)**. Moat: **cost/scale** (density + supplier terms) with switching-cost support — real, **stable**, but not dominant in a fragmented, low-margin, ~5–6%-share industry. Market: modest GDP-plus outsourcing growth. **ADEQUATE.**

- Serial bolt-on model requires continued disciplined multiples to avoid a future P19 acquisition strike.

## C. Calculation notes

- **OI basis:** GSC uses reported EBIT (after PPA-amortization of acquired intangibles **and** special items). Company "operating EBITA" (€929.3m FY25) adds back ~€312m of both — **not used** (GSC forbids one-off add-backs; the amortization of acquired customer relationships is a real serial-acquirer cost).
- **NIBCL** = accounts payable + other current liabilities (accruals/tax/provisions). Current debt and current lease portions retained in CE. Deducting only AP (leaving "other" in CE, the stricter reading) would lower ROCE ~1pt further — does not change the WEAK verdict.
- **Leases** IFRS-16 capitalized on the reported balance sheet; lease liabilities carried as debt-equivalent in CE, EV, L/E.
- **Goodwill** retained in CE (serial acquirer).
- **CE build FY25 (wc):** 10,625.8 − 2,198.7 = **8,427.1**; (xc): −797.9 = **7,629.2**.
- **TBV build FY25:** equity ex-NCI 4,314.1 − goodwill+intang ~3,666 = **647.7**.
