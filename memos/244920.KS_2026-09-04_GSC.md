# 244920.KS — A Plus Asset Advisor: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS; Past WEAK; Future WEAK; Shareholder-friendliness FAIL; Cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID on three gates**.

A Plus is Korea's second-largest listed insurance general agency: a commission platform of ~8,900 agents whose revenue grew 2.6× in four years as regulation pushed smaller agencies into it. The activist-forced turn — first repurchase, raised dividend, board-independence amendments — is worth watching, but nothing in the record yet shows a *good* business by this framework: single-digit returns on consolidated capital (near zero in FY2022), FCF negative in two of five years, margins set by carriers and the regulator, and founder pay at eight to ten times the proportionality cap. A flip needs a decade of primary statements showing ROCE this window does not, a completed repurchase cycle plus disposal of the return-diluting non-core units, and a compensation reset. Price is the least of it: ₩13,960 is ~1.8× the loose bound of a band whose width says cash and minority interests dominate.

## 2. Understanding
A Plus Asset Advisor sells other companies' life, health and property insurance to Korean households through ~8,900 commissioned agents, is paid commissions by some 34 insurers (its paying customers), operates as a regulated independent general agency (GA) headquartered in Seoul, and consolidates two non-core units — a prepaid funeral-services business and a controlled listed bio-diagnostics equipment maker — that dominate its minority interest and much of its asset base. **PASS**; the brokerage is ≈88% of revenue but not of capital.

## 3. Quality — past record
Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **4.4%** (best variant 8.6%); worst-of-four FCFROCE **1.1%**. No variant clears the 10% floor, and the result survives the one contestable call — ₩145bn of "other" liabilities kept in CE — which excluded lifts the cash-inclusive read only to 8.9%. OI rose ₩5.7bn → ₩29.2bn (+50%/yr) from a ₩1.2bn trough in FY2022; FCF negative in FY2021 and FY2023. BV/sh +14.7%/yr, flattered by an ₩82bn FY2022 disposal gain. L/E 150%, within P5. Window record-limited to 5y. **WEAK** — level, not trend.
