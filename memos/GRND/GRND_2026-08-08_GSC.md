# GRND — Grindr Inc.: GSC Memo

The operating business is exceptional — 27% ROCE on FY2025, 30% FCFROCE, a near-monopoly network in its niche — and the financial conduct around it is the problem. Equity has been stripped to $47m (negative two of five years) by a debt-funded $196m pre-listing distribution and a $524m FY2025 repurchase executed at roughly 23–31× the framework's own cash-flow yardsticks, with the programme still running and no published price discipline. ## 2. Understanding

Grindr operates the leading dating and social-networking app for the gay, bi, trans and queer community, monetised through subscriptions and in-app purchases (the large majority of revenue) plus advertising; paying customers are individual users worldwide who transact almost entirely through the Apple App Store and Google Play, which collect the cash and take their fee; a Delaware corporation listed November 2022 via the Tiga SPAC after CFIUS forced its 2020 divestiture from China's Kunlun, HQ in West Hollywood; the category's prominent, decades-old franchise, mid-cap, US-weighted. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Returns are high and rising: worst-of-four normalized ROCE **14.71%** (end-wc; the other variants 16.2–18.7%), worst FCFROCE **16.78%**; FY2025 alone 27.1%/30.2%. ## 4. Quality — future

Tally **2 STRONG**. Moat: **Network** — genuine, category-defining, stable. Market growth YES. **WEAK** — breadth FAIL and the tally each suffice.
