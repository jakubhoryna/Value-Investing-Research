## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS (three material units); Past WEAK (4.5% ROCE); Future ADEQUATE (unit customer conc.); Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (iM3 bought clearly above worth); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID (Past + SF)**.

Vimian is a Stockholm-based roll-up of veterinary businesses: allergy and dermatology products, orthopaedic and dental equipment for animal surgeons, and a purchasing club for independent clinics. The niches grow and two of the three units hold their margins, but the record is assembled from acquisitions paid for with goodwill: capital employed is over EUR 1bn against EUR 67m of operating income, and the 2024 iM3 dental deal cost more than fifty times its pro-forma profit. At SEK 40.90 the stock trades at 23.0× maintenance free cash flow and 34.4× EV/OI. A WATCH would need returns on that capital to reach 10% and deals priced below worth.

## 2. Understanding

Group sentence: Vimian is a Stockholm-headquartered animal-health group, assembled since 2018 by its controlling owner Fidelio Capital and listed in 2021, that sells allergy tests and immunotherapy, dermatology and clinical-nutrition products, orthopaedic implants, surgical instruments and dental imaging equipment, and purchasing, marketing and business services, mainly to veterinary clinics and specialist surgeons, and to the animal-health manufacturers that pay it fees on its member clinics' purchases, across Europe, North America and Australia (EUR 425m of FY2025 revenue).

- **Specialty Pharma** (42.9% of FY2025 revenue; Nextmune): allergy diagnostics and allergen immunotherapy, dermatology, ear and skin care and clinical nutrition, paid by veterinary clinics and distributors.
- **MedTech** (36.6%; Movora): orthopaedic implants and instruments (Kyon, BioMedtrix, VOI), dental equipment and imaging (iM3, Dental Focus) and allografts, paid by veterinary surgeons, clinics and distributors.
- **Veterinary Services** (15.1%; VetFamily): a membership platform of 10,948 independent clinics; revenue comes mainly from centrally negotiated purchasing agreements paid by suppliers on members' purchases, plus membership, business-development and pet-care-plan fees.

Diagnostics (5.4% of revenue, 1.4% of segment results, 4.8% of segment assets; livestock and companion-animal lab tests) is immaterial. **PASS.** Lens STD.
