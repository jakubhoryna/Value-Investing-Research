## 2. Understanding

Badger Meter designs and makes differentiated water meters (mechanical and ultrasonic), ORION cellular and fixed-network radio endpoints, water-quality and sewer-monitoring sensors (SmartCover, UDlive) and BEACON SaaS (89% of FY2025 sales are utility water), plus flow-measurement instruments (11%). Municipal and investor-owned water utilities pay, reached through a direct salesforce and regional distributors, with industrial buyers of flow instruments. It is a vertically integrated Milwaukee manufacturer with a software layer and plants in Wisconsin, Mexico, the Czech Republic and Switzerland. Ninety per cent of sales are in the US. It was founded in 1905, is NYSE-listed and had $917m of FY2025 sales, reported as one segment. **PASS.** Lens STD.

## 4. Quality — future

The 10-K states that no customer exceeds 10% of sales, so there is no customer flag. Brass castings, resins and electronic subassemblies are single-sourced by choice. No supplier's share of costs is disclosed, so a supplier above 10% is not established; that is a named re-test. All four forces are WEAK: thousands of fragmented utilities buy on 15–20-year cycles; brass and resin are commodities; metering is required for billing; standards and installed-base compatibility deter entrants. The moat is switching costs (installed endpoints plus BEACON) and stable: gross margin held at 41% in H1 2026 while sales fell 7.8%. Market growth YES. **STRONG.**
