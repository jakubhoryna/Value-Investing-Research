# ENB — Enbridge Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | **WEAK** | ADEQUATE | PASS (2 logged flags) | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** |

Enbridge is a well-moated collection of irreplaceable energy infrastructure that is nonetheless a structurally poor fit for this framework. The deciding gate is **Past WEAK**: a ~CAD $210B capital base throws off only ~CAD $11B of operating income, so worst-of-four normalized ROCE is **4.2%** — less than half the 10% ADEQUATE floor and a third of the STRONG bar — while total-liabilities-to-equity sits at **245%**, breaching the P5 200% flag with none of the P6 rescue conditions (Enbridge issues equity to fund growth, it does not shrink it). A decade of relentless dilution (918M → 2,186M diluted shares, +138%, via the 2017 Spectra and 2024 Dominion-utilities deals) has held per-share book-value growth to **+2.3%** (below the inflation floor) and driven tangible book per share **down 7.6%/yr**. Nothing about the moat or the future record can lift a WEAK past. Cheapness only compounds it: at CAD $78.60 the stock trades at **52× levered FCF and 26× operating income**, and because net debt + preferred + NCI (~CAD $115B) already exceeds 7× OI, **no positive share price ever satisfies EV/OI ≤ 7** — there is no computable buy trigger. A verdict flip would require a structural halving of leverage and a doubling of returns on capital, not a price move.

## 2. Understanding

Enbridge transports and distributes hydrocarbons and, increasingly, delivers regulated gas and renewable power: it earns tolls and regulated/contracted fees from oil producers and refiners (Liquids Pipelines, incl. the Mainline), from gas shippers (Gas Transmission), from ~7m retail utility customers across Ontario, Quebec and the U.S. (Gas Distribution & Storage, enlarged by the 2024 Dominion utilities), and from power off-takers (Renewables) — a capital-intensive, heavily regulated, acquisition-built North American energy-infrastructure group headquartered in Calgary. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized-average **ROCE = 4.2%**, **FCFROCE = 2.3%** (FY2016–25; Σ OI ÷ Σ CE, four beg/end × cash variants — all cluster 4.2–4.6% / 2.3–2.5%, so no variant rescues it). Returns are stable but structurally low: a subtractive ~CAD $210B capital base against ~CAD $11B OI cannot clear the P3 10% bar. Per-share growth is hollowed out by dilution — OI/sh +6.6% geo (flattered by a depressed 2016 base), but **BV/sh only +2.3%**, **FCF/sh −4.9%**, **TBV/sh −7.6%** as goodwill from Spectra/Dominion swamps tangible equity. **L/E 245%** breaches P5; P6 does not apply. **WEAK** — returns far below floor and a leverage breach.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth **PASS** (no single shipper >10% of revenue; millions of utility customers; commoditised construction supply base). Four forces, near-term: customer power **WEAK** (regulated tolls, immovable assets, high switching costs); supplier power **WEAK**; new entrants **WEAK** (near-impossible permitting/rights-of-way — the strongest favourable force); substitutes **WEAK near-term but the live long-term risk** (economy-wide energy transition is a wholly-different-function substitute, slowed only by enormous adoption switching costs). Tally **0 strong** → favourable. Moat: **Cost + Government** (irreplaceable corridors, regulatory barriers), stable for existing assets but with genuine terminal-value overhang. Market **MIXED**. Capped at **ADEQUATE** — the transition overhang keeps it below STRONG. (Moot given Past.)

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Compensation is clean: CEO total (~CAD $13–16m range) is a rounding error against 5% of lower(FCF CAD 3.3B, NI CAD 7.5B) ≈ CAD $165m, and far below the ~US$40m indexed absolute flag; outside-director fees within Canadian norms — **no P17 strike**. Buybacks are essentially absent (Enbridge issues shares, doesn't repurchase) — **no P19 buyback strike**. Two logged **scrutiny flags**, not strikes: (i) acquisition multiples — Spectra (2017) and the Dominion gas utilities (2024, ~US$14B EV, ~16.5× EBITDA) sit at rich pre-deal OI multiples that plausibly approach the P19 acquisition line; (ii) capital allocation — the common dividend (~CAD $8.2B) materially exceeds levered FCF (~CAD $3.3B), the gap plugged with debt and equity. **PASS** on the letter (comp clean, no buyback strike), but borderline; a full circular read could tip it. Immaterial — Past already forces AVOID.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Leverage is structural, not cyclical: the ~CAD $115B net obligation stack is the binding reason both the leverage flag and the cheapness gate can't clear.
- Returns are unlikely to re-rate: regulated/contracted tolls cap ROCE near the current mid-single digits by design.
- Energy-transition demand erosion is the one force that could turn the moat's terminal value against it over a 10–20yr horizon.
- Continued equity funding of growth and distributions keeps per-share compounding weak even as absolute OI grows.
- A rich acquisition pipeline (Dominion) risks converting further into goodwill rather than per-share value.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

The verdict flips only on a structural change, never on price: worst-of-four ROCE sustained above ~10% **and** L/E brought under 200% **and** the net-debt stack falling below ~7× OI. None is plausible on the current asset/funding model. **Next review:** FY2026 10-K (~Feb 2027) re-anchors the OI/CE and leverage reads; no price alert — cheapness is structurally unreachable, so there is no downturn shopping-list entry here.
