# TRI.PA — Trigano S.A.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past ADEQUATE (worst-of-four ROCE 18.24%, FCFROCE 9.70%, capped by a record-limited five-year window); Future **WEAK** on a two-strong-force tally; SF PASS (one unpaired cap-allocation CAUTION); cheapness FAIR at 9.33×/7.65× EV/OI — verdict **AVOID on the Future gate**.

On the retrievable record Trigano is a good business: returns clear even the STRONG thresholds, it holds net cash of €366m against liabilities of 57% of book, and the family owning 57.9% of the capital has begun buying stock back at multiples cheaper than any execution in this register. What stands between here and a BUY is not price — the stock sits about 8% above the looser bound of its own band, closer than any WATCH name in the file — but the Future gate. Two forces are strong against it and neither is cyclical noise: over 40% of what it buys is cab-chassis from OEMs that dwarf it, and a €60–100k discretionary vehicle competes with not buying one, which is what European buyers did in FY2025. Flipping the verdict needs the supplier look-through to resolve benignly *and* the substitutes call defeated; the second is a property of the product.

## 2. Understanding

Trigano S.A. is a French-incorporated, Feuillet-family-controlled (57.9%), Euronext Paris-listed manufacturer that assembles branded but substitutable big-ticket discretionary leisure durables — motorhomes, campervans, caravans and mobile homes at 87.3% of revenue, plus trailers and leisure equipment — onto cab-chassis bought from automotive OEMs, and sells them wholesale to ~1,300 independent licensed distributors (who pay, and carry the floorplanned inventory) and 70 owned Libertium outlets, mainly in France, Germany, the UK and Italy, as a mature, profitable, net-cash leader of a definitively cyclical European recreational-vehicle industry. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst of the four normalized averages: ROCE **18.24%** (end-period, with cash) and FCFROCE **9.70%** (beginning-period, with cash) — both above the P2 STRONG lines, but STRONG is unavailable because only FY2021–FY2025 could be built: **record-limited**. ROCE_wc ran 22.0 → 17.9 → 21.0 → 20.8 → 12.1% — two consecutive declines, not P4's sustained decline. Growth is split: ΔOI/sh **−1.72%** geometric against ΔFCF/sh **+10.71%**, ΔBV/sh +15.29%, ΔTBV/sh +16.09%. L/E **57.4%** is the French block's lowest. **ADEQUATE** — returns clear comfortably; the negative OI/sh leg and the short window cap it.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: customer leg PASS (no >10% disclosure; ~1,300 distributors); supplier leg **UNRESOLVED** — chassis suppliers are >40% of supplies (>€925m against a €318m threshold) but no single-supplier share is disclosed. Forces: customer power **WEAK**, supplier power **STRONG**, substitutes **STRONG** (deferral, rental, hotels, close direct rivals), new entrants **WEAK**. Tally **2 strong → Future WEAK**, independently of the unresolved breadth leg. Moat: cost, from procurement scale and a 1,300-dealer network — real, stable, insufficient. Market growth MIXED. **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

## C. Calculation notes

**Latest-year capital employed (FY2025, 31 Aug 2025).** Total assets €3,275.3m − NIBCL €512.3m = **CE_wc €2,763.0m**; less cash and equivalents €650.8m (no separate short-term investments line is disclosed) = **CE_xc €2,112.2m**.

**NIBCL composition and the burden-of-proof call.** NIBCL = *Fournisseurs et autres créditeurs* €505.0m + *Dettes d'impôt* €7.3m = €512.3m. Deliberately **left inside capital employed**: *Autres passifs courants* €188.4m (unlabelled aggregate; French filers commonly mix customer deposits and deferred income — which would qualify — with social and payroll-tax liabilities and accrued charges), *Provisions courantes* €26.9m, and the current portions of financial and lease liabilities (€100.5m + €19.2m), which always stay in CE. Per §2·1A the burden rests with the item, so the ambiguous line stays in CE even though this *depresses* ROCE. **Sensitivity:** moving the whole *Autres passifs courants* line into NIBCL for every window year lifts CE_wc to €1,506.4 / €1,759.6 / €1,864.3 / €2,230.0 / €2,574.6m and the four normalized ROCE reads to 19.68% / 25.66% / 21.70% / 28.05%, i.e. worst-of-four rises 18.24% → **19.68%**.
