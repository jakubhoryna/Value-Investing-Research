# CDR.WA — CD Projekt S.A.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS · Past ADEQUATE (worst-of-four ROCE 19.3%, FCFROCE 10.4% — the project cycle honestly averaged) · Future **WEAK — customer breadth FAIL** (platform payers each >10% of revenue; the captive-customer waiver tested and denied limb-by-limb) · SF PASS (1 flag — capital-allocation CAUTION) · Cheapness EXPENSIVE (54× EV/OI; FCF negative in the build year) → **AVOID (Future gate)**.

The EA/TTWO finding in Polish form, with better economics: a debt-free two-franchise studio whose decade averages 19% returns on capital *through* a catastrophic launch and a five-year drought, compounding book value 17%/yr. But it sells through three-to-four gateway payers — Valve, Sony, Microsoft — each above the 10% line in its own disclosures, and the waiver fails where it failed for MHI and EA: the customers demonstrably backward-integrate, and a two-IP studio one bad launch from reputational crisis holds no *dominant* moat. The market meanwhile prices Witcher 4 at 54× current-year EV/OI. A flip needs either the precedent overturned (a MODEL question) or a distribution mix where no gateway exceeds a tenth — GOG is nowhere near that.

## 2. Understanding
CD Projekt develops and publishes premium role-playing games from two owned franchises — The Witcher and Cyberpunk — monetised at launch spikes and long catalogue tails through Steam, PlayStation, Xbox and its own GOG storefront, with revenue recognised from platform payers rather than end gamers; Warsaw-HQ'd, founder-led (~30% insider block), debt-free, currently mid-build on Witcher 4 with capitalised development at record levels. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

## 4. Quality — future
**Customer breadth FAIL.** The paying customers are the platforms, not the gamers: CD Projekt's own reports disclose trading partners above 10% of revenue (Valve and Sony foremost). Waiver tested limb-by-limb (App D): (a) cannot backward-integrate + prohibitive switching — **fails**, Sony and Microsoft own first-party studios and buy content continuously; (b) dominant, stable moat — **fails**, two franchises with a 2020 launch crisis in living memory is a real moat, not a dominant one; (c) structural concentration holds alone. **Waiver denied → Future WEAK** (EA, TTWO, 7011.T precedents). For the record forces would tally 1 strong (the 30% platform take); moat **Brand**, stable-strengthening post-Phantom-Liberty; market growth YES. None survives breadth.
