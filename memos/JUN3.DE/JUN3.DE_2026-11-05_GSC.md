## 2. Understanding

Jungheinrich AG, a Hamburg company founded in 1953 whose voting ordinary shares are held equally by the two founding families (listed preference shares in the SDAX), develops, makes, sells, rents and services electric forklifts and warehouse trucks, automated warehouse systems, racking and lithium-ion batteries for manufacturers, retailers, logistics operators and other businesses, about 80% outside Germany (mainly Europe, with US racking and automation since 2023), and leases about a quarter of its business to those customers through a captive finance arm. PASS.

- **Intralogistics:** makes, sells and rents new and used forklifts, warehouse trucks and automation systems (stacker cranes, AGVs, US racking through Storage Solutions), and services its installed base, for businesses of every size; new business is the largest part.
- **Financial Services:** the captive arm that finances and leases Jungheinrich trucks to end customers on long-term contracts (directly and through partner banks and leasing companies), refinanced with matching maturities through banks and securitisation; €1,473m of 2025 revenue including intra-group.
