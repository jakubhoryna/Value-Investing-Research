# LYFT — Lyft, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past WEAK; Future WEAK; shareholder-friendliness PASS (one flag — capital-allocation CAUTION); cheapness EXPENSIVE (EV/OI n.m. on negative OI) — **AVOID on two gates (Past and Future)**.

The business finally generates cash — FY2024 was the first positive-FCF and positive-NI year in its history, and FY2025 FCF reached $1.12bn against a $6.19bn market cap (MCap/FCF 5.5×, under the 8× line) — but ten consecutive years of operating losses mean every ROCE read in the window is negative, and the duopoly's structural position (zero rider switching costs, price parity forced by Uber, live robotaxi entry) fails the forces test outright. The FCF itself is float-assisted: insurance reserves and accruals added ~$0.8–1.1bn to CFO across FY2024–25 while operating income stayed negative. What would flip the verdict is sustained positive operating income (Past re-read would still take years) plus evidence the AV transition treats Lyft as a demand partner rather than roadkill; neither is testable before the FY2026 report.

## 2. Understanding

Lyft operates a two-sided ridesharing marketplace — matching riders (consumers who pay per ride) with independent drivers across the US and Canada, plus bike/scooter rental and, since July 2025, the FreeNow multi-mobility platform in Europe — an asset-light network-intermediary corporation, HQ San Francisco, founded 2012, IPO 2019, the #2 player behind Uber, with its first profitable year only in FY2024. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Operating income is negative in all ten years FY2016–FY2025 (worst −$2,702.5m in FY2019; still −$188.4m in FY2025). Worst-of-four normalized average ROCE (FY2020–25 tabulated window) is −38.7% (beginning-CE, with-cash); FCFROCE −2.8%; the ex-cash variants are n.m.-deep-negative on near-zero CE. FCF turned positive only in FY2024 ($766m) and FY2025 ($1,116m), partly on insurance-reserve float. L/E is 175.9% at FY2025 — inside the 200% flag — but ran 609–1,072% across FY2022–24 before the deferred-tax-asset release rebuilt equity. Per-share record: BV/sh $12.55 (2019) → $7.84 (2025). **WEAK** — the level test fails categorically despite the FY2024–25 inflection.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASSES: millions of riders, millions of drivers, no 10% concentration on either side. Forces: customer bargaining power STRONG (zero switching costs, Uber on the same phone forces price parity); supplier power WEAK (drivers multihome but are atomised); threat of substitutes STRONG (Uber, transit, own car, and Waymo robotaxi expansion); threat of new entrants STRONG (AV players enter with fleets, not driver networks — the two-sided-liquidity barrier does not bind them). Tally ≥ 2 STRONG — the book's stop signal. Moat: second-place network effect, eroding at the autonomous edge. Market growth YES. **WEAK.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Autonomous-vehicle networks (Waymo, others) supply rides without drivers, dissolving Lyft's core asset — the driver network — while partnerships remain revocable.
- FCF quality: FY2024–25 CFO leans on insurance-reserve and accrual build; a reserve unwind or claims shock reverses it.
- Uber's scale advantage compounds — pricing, engineering amortisation, and cross-sell (Eats) that Lyft cannot match.
- Driver reclassification (employment status) remains a standing regulatory tail risk in core states.
- FY2025 equity tripling is a deferred-tax accounting event, not earned capital.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

Deterioration needs no test — the verdict already floors.
