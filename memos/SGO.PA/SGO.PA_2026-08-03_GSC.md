# SGO.PA — Compagnie de Saint-Gobain: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS · Past ADEQUATE (knife-edge) · Future ADEQUATE · SF PASS (capital allocation CAUTION, 1 flag) · price FAIR → **WATCH**.

Saint-Gobain is worth owning as the scale leader in light construction materials — regional oligopolies in plasterboard, insulation and construction chemicals, with a decade of genuinely counter-cyclical buybacks and improving portfolio quality. Two things stand between here and a BUY. First, price: at €81.28 both primaries fail (EV/OI 10.8×/9.7×, MCap/FCF 11.5×) and the band sits ≈ 37–51% below. Second, fragile quality: the worst-of-four ROCE clears the ADEQUATE floor by seven basis points (10.07% vs 10%), and on the Business-income basis — which absorbs SGO's every-year restructuring, impairment and litigation charges — the same read is 7.7%, i.e. WEAK. The verdict rests on the as-reported Operating-income line (App C); a price inside the band would still require conviction that the recent 11–13% ROCE regime, not the decade average, is the durable one. Flip up needs price < ≈ €52; flip down needs FY2026 ROCE mean-reverting toward the 2016–2020 8–9% level.

## 2. Understanding

Saint-Gobain manufactures light construction materials — plasterboard, insulation, mortars and construction chemicals, glass — at dense local scale in ~75 countries and sells them, alongside its builders'-distribution arm, to distributors, contractors and specifiers, earning a spread on regional manufacturing oligopolies where low value-to-weight keeps competition local. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized ROCE 10.07% (end/wc; best read 12.11% beg/xc) — over the P3 10% floor by 7bp, far from the 15% STRONG line; worst-of-four FCFROCE 6.43% also fails STRONG's 8%. The trend is the redeeming feature: end/wc ROCE ran 7–9% in FY2016–2020 and 11–12.5% in FY2021–2025 as portfolio rotation cut low-margin distribution and added chemicals. Per-share growth clears easily — OI/sh +8.6%/yr, FCF/sh +26.5%/yr (2016 base depressed by a €732m one-off pension contribution); BV/sh +4.3%; TBV/sh flat (serial acquirer). Leverage clean: L/E 130–170% across the window, always under the 200% flag. **ADEQUATE** — floor-level returns, improving, honestly flagged as measurement-sensitive (App C).

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASS both sides: no customer ≥10% of sales (FY25 statements); inputs are commodities plus own gypsum quarries. Forces, all four WEAK: customers — thousands of distributors/contractors, no backward integration; suppliers — commodity inputs, energy hedged, no forward integration; substitutes — code-mandated products, and heavy-to-light substitution favours SGO; entrants — capital intensity, freight economics, quarry permits and specification relationships bar both entry and success. Tally 0 strong. Moat: cost/scale in regional oligopolies plus brands (Placo, Isover, Weber, CertainTeed) — plausible and stable, but thin in flat glass and distribution, so not dominant across the portfolio. Market grows ~GDP with renovation/energy-code tailwinds. **ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Comp: CEO Bazin ≈ €7m total (FY24, secondary) against strikes of €144m (FY25 read) and €141m (3y read); the entire Board-plus-senior-management aggregate is €42.4m (FY25 statements) — both reads clear by an order of magnitude, absolute trigger untouched. Directors average ≈ €95k vs the ≈ $666k stop. RPT immaterial (an architecture-award partnership). Capital allocation **CAUTION (1 flag)**: the buyback record is SOUND — a decade of execution at 6.4–10.9× EV/OI, heaviest in the cheapest years — but GCP (2022, $2.3bn) was bought at ~17× trailing actual EBITDA, ≈30×-class pre-deal EBIT, with synergies assumed at half of standalone EBITDA and no published acquisition-price criterion. Not live, not serial → **PASS.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Recurring "non-recurring" charges: €0.9–1.0bn/yr average gap between Operating income and Business income (restructuring, impairments, asbestos/PFOA) is a permanent cash-adjacent drag the OI basis ignores.
- FCF conversion is structurally modest (worst-of-four FCFROCE 6.4%) against ~€2bn/yr capex plus €12bn Lead & Grow growth spend.
- M&A execution: €3bn of sales rotated in H1-26 alone; a second GCP-class multiple would pair the flag and strike SF.
- Europe exposure (~55% of sales) to rate-driven new-construction cycles and energy costs.

Justification: SGO discloses the line inside the note "Trade and other accounts payable and accrued expenses"; sub-components (customer deposits/downpayments, payables to suppliers of fixed assets, grants received, accrued personnel, accrued taxes other than income, misc. operating/non-operating payables) map onto the framework default (AP + accrued expenses + income taxes payable + deferred income).
