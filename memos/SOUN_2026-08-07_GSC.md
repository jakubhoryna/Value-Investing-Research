# SOUN — SoundHound AI, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past WEAK; Future WEAK; SF **FAIL** (P17 hard strike — both fiscal-year legs negative against $9.33m of CEO pay); cheapness EXPENSIVE, band n.m. — **AVOID (four gates)**.

One number frames this memo: across FY2021–25 SoundHound billed **$352m of revenue in total and lost $604m at the operating line**. The second is how the gap was funded: **$840m of stock issued** in five years, taking the count from roughly 12.5m to 435.8m. Revenue growth is real and fast (+64% in FY2025 to $169m, TTM $203m) and the FY2025 operating loss narrowed to $23m — the genuine bull point, and the reason a dated review is worth scheduling. But the answers to the framework's two questions are a negative return on capital in every variant tested and a $3.3bn market capitalisation against $198m of tangible book.

## 2. Understanding

SoundHound AI licenses conversational voice-AI software — speech recognition, natural-language understanding and an agentic assistant layer — to automotive manufacturers, restaurant chains and enterprise contact centres, who pay under multi-year royalty and subscription contracts; a Nasdaq-listed Santa Clara corporation, founded 2005 and public since an April 2022 SPAC merger, small-cap, US-centred (57.5% of H1-2025 revenue) with Korea, China and Europe exposure. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Operating income is negative in all five years; Σ OI **−$604.2m** against Σ revenue of $351.8m. Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **−161.7%** (range −41.5% to −161.7% across both NIBCL bases); worst FCFROCE **−111.8%**; Σ FCF −$439.5m. Per-share growth is **n.m. on all four metrics** — OI and FCF negative throughout, book and tangible book crossing sign mid-window on recapitalisation rather than earnings. Leverage is the one clean line: L/E **48.4%** after the June 2024 term-loan prepayment, against 436.5% in FY2023. FY2025 cut the operating loss to $23.3m; that is one year. **WEAK.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: customers **FAIL**. The H1-2025 filing states no customer exceeded 10% of six-month revenue, but the same document shows three at 16%, 13% and 13% of Q2-2025 revenue, two at 27% and 35% a year earlier, and unbilled receivables at 39%/22%/18% — a base lumpy by construction, since one automotive programme moves the line. Suppliers **FAIL**: an **exclusive** seven-year cloud agreement with $71.0m of minimum payments through 2028 is a single-sourced input the ready-alternatives exception cannot cover. Forces: customer power **STRONG**, supplier power **STRONG**, substitutes **STRONG** (general-purpose assistants, OEM in-house builds), new entrants **STRONG** (foundation models collapsed the barrier that protected specialist speech vendors). Tally **4 STRONG**. Moat: none identified. Market growth **YES**. **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

**P17 hard strike.** On the FY2025 fiscal-year read **both legs are negative** — net income −$14.0m, free cash flow −$99.1m — so under the loss-leg rule any more-than-token pay is the strike regardless of the normalized read.
