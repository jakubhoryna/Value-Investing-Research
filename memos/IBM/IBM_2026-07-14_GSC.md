# IBM — International Business Machines Corp.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | **WEAK** | ADEQUATE | PASS (1 flag) | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** |

IBM is a real business with a genuine switching-cost moat — the mainframe still clears a large share of the world's transaction volume, and Red Hat/OpenShift anchors a growing hybrid-cloud franchise — and it throws off strong cash (FCFROCE ~11% worst-of-four, ~$12bn levered FCF). But the model flags it on two independent grounds. First, **returns on the real capital base are sub-threshold**: worst-of-four average ROCE is 6.6–8.4% across every window versus a 10% ADEQUATE floor, because ~$79bn of goodwill + acquired intangibles (deeply negative tangible book, −$46bn) sits in capital employed against thin, pension- and amortisation-depressed operating income. Second, total-liabilities-to-equity is **365%**, well past the 200% flag, with no P6 rescue (the leverage is structural — Red Hat debt plus a captive-finance arm — not cheap borrowing to buy back under-priced stock). Per-share cash generation has gone **backwards** (FCF/sh geo −1.5% since the Kyndryl spin) as the share count crept up ~6%. On top of quality, the stock is not remotely cheap on the framework: 17× FCF and ~21× EV/OI, the debt load pinning the EV/OI-implied entry near $37. Nothing here is close to a BUY or a live WATCH trigger. What would flip it: a materially lower ROCE-relevant capital base (large goodwill impairment or divestment) plus a price collapse — neither in view.

## 2. Understanding

IBM sells enterprise software (Red Hat hybrid-cloud, automation, data/AI — watsonx — transaction middleware), IT consulting, and infrastructure (z-series mainframes, storage), with a captive financing arm, to large enterprises and governments worldwide; a ~114-year-old, US-HQ'd, vertically-integrated technology group reshaped by the 2019 Red Hat acquisition and the 2021 Kyndryl spin-off. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalised-average **ROCE = 8.4%** over FY2016–25 (Kyndryl-broken), **6.6%** over the clean continuing-ops window FY2019–25, **7.6%** post-spin FY2022–25 — all **below the 10% ADEQUATE floor** (P3), far below 15% STRONG. FCFROCE worst-of-four ~11% clears its 8% leg (cash conversion is genuinely strong; OI is depressed by ~$2.7bn/yr intangible amortisation and pension charges). Per-share growth fails: OI/sh geo −0.5% (10y), FCF/sh geo **−1.1% to −1.5%**, both under the 3% inflation floor, with diluted shares rising 893m→949m. Leverage **L/E 365%** breaches P5 (200%) with no P6 condition; net-debt/FCF 3.9× and interest cover 6.3× are moderate, but the rule reads all liabilities. **Verdict WEAK** — sub-floor ROCE and a leverage breach, either sufficient.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth **PASS** both sides (no customer >10% of revenue; suppliers are talent + components, many sourced). Four forces: customer power **WEAK** (mainframe/Red Hat lock-in dominates a competitive consulting book), supplier power **WEAK**, threat of substitutes **STRONG** (public cloud, SaaS and open-source displace on-prem infra and licensed middleware; long-run "doing-without" pressure on the mainframe), new entrants **WEAK** (high barriers to entrenched positions). Tally **1 strong → judgment/ADEQUATE zone**. Moat source = **switching costs** (z-systems installed base, OpenShift ecosystem, embedded middleware), direction **stable** on the core, pressured at the edges. Market growth **MIXED** (AI/hybrid-cloud up, consulting flat, infrastructure cyclical). **Verdict ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Comp clears both P17 gates: CEO Krishna FY2024 total **$25.1m** (FY2025 ~$38m per aggregator, DEF 14A 2026) — under the ~$40m indexed scrutiny flag and far under the 5%-of-lower(FCF $12.1bn, NI $10.6bn) hard strike (≈$530m); outside-director fees ~$335k < $500k. RPT immaterial. No current buyback strike — repurchases were essentially halted 2020–25 to de-lever (share count rose, so no dilution-sterilisation). Dividend rational for a mature, lower-ROCE name (~$6.7/sh, aristocrat; ~55% of FCF). **One logged flag:** serial premium-priced M&A building $68bn goodwill — Red Hat historically ~68× pre-deal OI, HashiCorp (2025, $6.4bn) and Apptio (2023) pre-/low-profit (judgmental) — plus steady dilution. One flag, not two independent negatives → **PASS**, heightened capital-allocation scrutiny logged.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Secular substitution: hyperscaler cloud and SaaS erode infrastructure and middleware demand faster than software/AI offsets.
- Goodwill concentration: a large impairment would hit book value and confirm the low ROCE-on-capital read.
- Capital allocation: further premium, stock-funded M&A compounds dilution and goodwill rather than lifting per-share cash.
- OI volatility: pension de-risking and currency swing pretax income (2020, 2022 troughs), muddying any single-year multiple.
- Consulting cyclicality and enterprise-IT budget sensitivity to macro.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

Flips toward WATCH only if worst-of-four ROCE clears 10% on a durable basis (a materially lighter goodwill-laden capital base and/or sustained OI recovery) **and** leverage returns within P5 — and, separately, price would have to fall roughly to the ~$103 MCap/FCF leg to even enter the cheapness conversation (the EV/OI leg at ~$37 is the binding constraint). Next review: FY2026 10-K (~Feb 2027), or price < ≈ $103. AVOID is a database entry, not a call to trade against.
