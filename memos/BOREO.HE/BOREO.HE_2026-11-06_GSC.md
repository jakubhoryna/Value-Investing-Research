# BOREO.HE — Boreo Oyj: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS, Past WEAK, Future WEAK, shareholder-friendliness FAIL, price EXPENSIVE: **AVOID**, on all three quality gates.

Boreo is a Finnish buy-and-build owner of small technical distributors. Before 2020 it was Yleiselektroniikka, a small components distributor that earned over 20% on its capital. Since then it has bought about twenty businesses with bank debt and hybrid bonds, and returns have fallen to 4–9%. The worst ROCE is 6.6%. Debt plus hybrid capital is about five times tangible-free book equity, and tangible book is negative. Free cash flow after the hybrid coupons is thin. On that free cash flow, the CEO's pay breaches the proportionality strike line. No price shows: debt-equivalents exceed seven times operating income. A flip needs operating income near EUR 10.6m on today's balance sheet, returns back above 10%, and pay inside the line.

## 2. Understanding

Boreo Oyj (Helsinki; listed for decades as Yleiselektroniikka, renamed and turned into an acquisition platform from 2019–2020) is a group of about twenty small, independently run companies that resell other manufacturers' products. Through Electronics it distributes electronic components, test and measurement instruments, antennas and some own-brand devices to industrial companies, OEMs, defence and research customers in the Nordics, the Baltics, Poland and the US. Through Technical Trade it sells, rents and services machinery, filtration, welding and concrete-industry equipment, spare parts and courier and transport services to construction, process, forestry, mechanical-engineering and concrete companies in Finland, Sweden and Estonia. Revenue in 2025 was EUR 153.3m: 70% in Finland and 360 staff. **PASS.**
