# NOLA-B — Nolato AB: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | ADEQUATE | ADEQUATE | **FAIL** (P17 hard strike) | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** |

Nolato is a competent, conservatively financed Swedish polymer contract manufacturer whose center of gravity has shifted decisively toward medical devices — the part of the business with genuine switching-cost protection. The verdict fails on shareholder-friendliness: CEO total compensation of SEK 14.76m in FY2025 exceeds 5% of levered FCF (SEK 13.65m line), a mechanical P17 hard strike created by a capex-heavy year compressing FCF to SEK 273m. The strike is circumstantial rather than a portrait of greed (comp is 1.9% of net income; FY2024 was clean by a wide margin), but the framework tests the worst, and the P20 maintenance-capex rescue fails its own activation conditions. A GW Plastics acquisition flag (~15–16× disclosed pre-deal EBITA) sits alongside. Flip path: FY2026 FCF recovery re-clears P17 arithmetically, which would move the name to WATCH — the price is nowhere near the band regardless (EV/OI 14.0×, MCap/FCF 46.6×).

## 2. Understanding

Nolato develops and injection-molds polymer components and subsystems (plastic, silicone, TPE — customer-owned designs, differentiated by qualification rather than IP) for paying OEM customers in medical devices, pharma, diagnostics (Medical Solutions, 57% of sales) and consumer electronics, telecom/data-center EMC, automotive and hygiene (Engineered Solutions, 43%); a 1938-founded, family-anchored, Torekov-HQ'd Swedish Large Cap operating plants across Sweden, Hungary, the US (via 2020 GW Plastics acquisition), Switzerland, China and Poland. PASS.

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four 10y normalized ROCE 15.1% (end-of-period, with-cash), FCFROCE 8.0% — the level clears P3 comfortably and grazes P2. STRONG is denied twice: ΔFCF/sh geo ≈ +1.0%/yr (below the 3% floor; FY2022 FCF was −49m) and a five-year return compression, yearly ROCE 29.7% (2018) → 8.2% (2023), recovering to ~13% by 2025 as the GW Plastics and capacity capital digests. ΔOI/sh +9.5%/yr; ΔBV/sh +12.6%. L/E 67.8%, latest year — clean. **ADEQUATE**: solid economics, permanently diluted by a tripled capital base that operating income has not yet caught up with.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: customer PASS (no IFRS 8 major-customer disclosure in AR2025 → no ≥10% customer; fragmented multi-industry base), supplier PASS (commodity resins, 5–10% of selling price). Forces (group, Medical-weighted): customer bargaining power STRONG — customers own designs, commit to no volumes, and the 2021–23 VHP unwind halved a business area at one customer group's discretion; supplier power WEAK; substitutes WEAK; new entrants WEAK for the dominant Medical unit (cleanroom qualification, validated processes) though STRONG within Engineered. Tally: 1 strong, contained. Moat: switching costs in Medical (designed-in, validated production) — stable-to-strengthening as mix shifts. Market growth YES (drug delivery, IVD, data-center EMC). **ADEQUATE**.

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

**FAIL — P17 hard strike.** CEO Wahlquist FY2025 total SEK 14.757m > 5% × lower(FCF 273, NI 777) = SEK 13.65m. P20 maintenance basis tested and denied (Appendix C), so the all-capex leg governs. Director fees far below the 2× local-norm stop (chair SEK 1.18m). RPT clean (Note 24). Buybacks: none in eleven years — P19 buyback leg untested, dividends the sole channel (one pandemic skip 2020, one cut after the 2023 trough). GW Plastics audit: deal EV ≈ SEK 1.7–2.1bn vs disclosed ~SEK 126m pre-deal EBITA → ~13.5–16.7×, central ~15–16× — **P19 acquisition flag**, logged; the 2021 SEK 115m contingent write-down pulls the realized multiple toward the 15× line.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Contract-manufacturing customer power: designs are customer-owned, volumes uncommitted — the VHP boom-bust is the template for how fast a "diversified" book concentrates.
- Capex intensity (8.3% of sales FY2025) with no capex-down guidance; FCF conversion structurally hostage to the growth program.
- US/Hungary/China footprint: tariff and geopolitical exposure on both the Medical and EMC books.
- Thin margins (EBITA 11.3% at the recovery high) leave little cushion in a downturn.
- Contingent-consideration and acquisition accounting have already produced one SEK 115m earnings-quality artifact (2021).

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

AVOID → WATCH requires the P17 strike to clear: FY2026 FCF above ≈ SEK 295m (5% > 14.76m CEO total, comp held flat) restores the test arithmetically — plausible if capex normalizes toward the ~4.7% pre-cycle ratio. A disclosed maintenance-capex figure or capex-down guidance would additionally open the P20 path. Past upgrade to STRONG needs sustained ROCE recovery above 15% and FCF/sh growth clearing 3%. Adverse: a new ≥10% customer disclosure or Engineered re-concentration flips Future WEAK. Next review: FY2026 annual report (~Apr 2027); no price alert — the band sits ~80% below the market.
