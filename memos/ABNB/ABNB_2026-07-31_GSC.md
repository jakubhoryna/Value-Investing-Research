This REVIEW is the MODEL v4.0.0 judgment re-test of the v2 SF FAIL, which rested on the retired P19 mechanical strike ladder (buybacks > 20× EV/OI). Under the replacement protocol the mechanical strike no longer exists: an overpriced history is a yardstick, never a conviction, and SF fails only where the behaviour is live or expected to continue. **ABNB fails on the forward limb, not the historical one.** The $6.0bn programme authorised 6 Aug 2025 has $4.5bn left, executed $1.1bn in Q1 2026 at an average $129.26 — 27.1× EV/OI on FY2025 income and 3.0–5.1× the framework's own conservative worth — with no published price criterion and the stock at $151.52, roughly 3.5× the top of its band. Verdict unchanged at AVOID; the reasoning is now a named judgment rather than a threshold breach, and it is falsifiable on evidence the company can produce.
