## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past WEAK (9.7% ROCE); Future ADEQUATE; Shareholder-friendliness PASS with one caution (compensation); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID (Past)**.

Corticeira Amorim is the world's largest cork processor and the leading maker of cork wine stoppers, integrated back into cork buying in Portugal. The franchise is real: about 25,000 customers, none above 2% of sales, and a cost lead in a scarce raw material. It is off the watchlist because the returns are not good enough: worst-of-four ROCE over the latest five years is 9.7%, under the 10% floor, and it has fallen three years running as wine demand shrinks and capital built up. Per-share operating income fell over the decade. Price is cheap on free cash flow (MCap/FCF 6.1) but not on operating income: EV/OI is 17.6, and the trigger is €1.65 against €6.86.

## 2. Understanding

Corticeira Amorim is a Portuguese cork group, founded in 1870 and headquartered in Mozelos, controlled by the Amorim family (71% through five holding companies) and listed on Euronext Lisbon. It buys and processes raw cork, mostly from Portuguese and Spanish forest owners, into natural and technical cork stoppers, bar-top closures, wire hoods and capsules, sold to wineries, sparkling-wine and spirits producers worldwide (EU 67% of sales, United States 16%), and turns by-product cork into flooring, composite materials for automotive, construction and footwear makers, and insulation boards sold to industrial customers and distributors. **PASS.**

Group: two material units (P22). Amorim Florestal (raw materials) sells 97% of its output to the other units and is merged with its main internal customer, Amorim Cork; the holding column is not a unit.
