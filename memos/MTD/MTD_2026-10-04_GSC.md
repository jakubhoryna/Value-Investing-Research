# MTD — Mettler-Toledo International Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS, Past STRONG, Future STRONG, shareholder-friendliness FAIL, cheapness EXPENSIVE: **AVOID** on the shareholder-friendliness gate alone.

Mettler-Toledo is an exceptional operating business. Worst-of-four ROCE is 39.9%, operating income and FCF per share compounded about 12% a year, no end-customer exceeds 1% of sales, and a large installed base feeds a service annuity. The failing lever is the only one management uses at scale: the buyback. Every year of the decade bought stock at 29–40× FCF and 20–34× EV/OI. Repurchases exceeded FCF, part of them on borrowed money, and that drove equity below zero. A fresh $2.75bn authorisation and a 2026 dollar target of $825–875m are executing at about 30× FCF with no worth criterion. Price is far off as well: 29× EV/OI against a band of $272–274. Flipping the verdict needs repurchases tied to a published worth discipline, or a pause until price meets worth.

## 2. Understanding
Mettler-Toledo makes precision weighing and analytical instruments, and sells their service. Its products are lab balances, pipettes, titrators and analytical instruments with LabX software (about 56% of sales); industrial scales, terminals, metal-detection and x-ray product inspection (39%); and retail food scales (about 5%). Service is about 25% of sales. Pharmaceutical, biotech, food, chemical and academic labs and manufacturers pay for them, mostly through a direct sales force. Sales are 42% Americas, 29% Europe and 29% Asia/rest of world (China 16%). Production is in China, Switzerland, the US and Germany, with offices in Columbus, Ohio and Greifensee. It is a mature, roughly $4bn-revenue global leader, not in transformation. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
**STRONG.** Worst-of-four normalized ROCE is **39.9%** and FCFROCE **29.5%**. Returns rose across the window: ROCE was 34.2% in FY2016–20 and 44.4% in FY2021–25. OI/share compounded 11.9% a year and FCF/share 12.5%. Equity turned negative in FY2023 after $7.6bn of buybacks, so L/E is n.m. and leverage is judged on net debt of $2.2bn at 2.6× FCF, with operating income covering interest 16×. That reading passes; the logged alternate, which fails it, is in Appendix C.

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth **PASS**: "no single end-customer accounting for more than 1%" of 2025 sales, and a "wide range of suppliers" with only component-level single sourcing. All four forces are WEAK: customers are fragmented; suppliers are replaceable; weighing and titration have no functional substitute; and entrants face a 9,300-strong sales and service network plus compliance software customers validate against FDA rules. China's domestic-preference rules are the contained exception, and China sales fell 11% from FY2023 to FY2025. The moat is switching costs, from the installed base, service and validated software. It is stable. Market growth is YES, modest. **STRONG.**
