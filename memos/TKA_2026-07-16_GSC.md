# TKA — thyssenkrupp AG: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

A 200-year-old conglomerate dismantling itself is not a business; it is a liquidation with a listing. The decade record is a cumulative operating loss of −€2.0bn and cumulative free cash flow of −€10.0bn — the elevator sale (FY2020, ~€15bn gain) funded years of cash burn. The stock trades below book (0.76×) yet is among the most expensive names in the register on the metrics that matter, because there is almost no OI or FCF to price. Nothing here flips on a cheaper price; the entity itself is being disassembled (TKMS spun off Oct 2025, Materials Services spin announced Jul 2026, steel headed to a 50:50 JV or sale). AVOID on all three gates.

## 2. Understanding

thyssenkrupp AG is a Duisburg/Essen-headquartered German industrial conglomerate in deliberate break-up: it makes flat carbon steel (Steel Europe; EPCG owns 20%, a 50:50 JV or sale pending, Jindal bid received), distributes materials (Materials Services; spin-off announced Jul 2026), makes automotive components, builds submarines and frigates through majority-held, separately listed TKMS (49% spun to shareholders Oct 2025), and holds plant-engineering assets plus ~50% of listed electrolyser maker nucera — customers are industrial OEMs, distributors, navies and governments, mostly European; management has stated the destination is a financial holding company. **PASS** — complex but comprehensible.

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four 10y normalized ROCE is **−1.2%** and FCFROCE **−5.6%** (FY2016–FY2025, as-reported continuing ops): Σ OI −€2,042m, Σ FCF −€10,001m on ~€230bn of summed CE. Only three of ten years produced positive FCF, none above €0.5bn/yr against a ~€20bn CE base. OI/sh collapsed from €2.08 (FY2016) to €0.04 (FY2025); BV/sh growth is an artefact of the FY2020 elevator gain, not operations. L/E ran 1,000–1,956% through FY2019 (P5 breach, no P6 conditions), repaired to 188% only by selling the crown jewel. **WEAK** — fails P3 on every leg; no rubric reading gets this above WEAK.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: customer PASS on the group disclosure ("no individual customers material to consolidated net sales", AR 2024/25); supplier PASS (iron-ore/coking-coal oligopoly, but several vendors stand ready). Forces: customer bargaining power **STRONG** (auto OEMs; price-driven steel and distribution buyers); new entrants/rivalry **STRONG** (global steel overcapacity, Asian imports; contested auto components); substitutes WEAK; supplier power WEAK. Tally = 2 strong → the book's stop signal. Moat: none at group level — the one genuine moat (TKMS's naval ingrainedness, €18.2bn backlog) is now a separately listed company. Market growth MIXED (defence up; European steel and auto structurally down). **WEAK**.

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

P17 **hard strike**: CEO Miguel López's FY2024/25 total (§162 AktG) of **€3.44m** is 11.5% of levered FCF (€30m) — the cap at 5% of lower(FCF €30m, NI €465m) is €1.5m; NI itself is flattered by a €902m non-cash Elevator-stake remeasurement and is negative without it, so no reading clears. Supervisory chair €297k — within German norms. RPT: nothing glaring. P19: no buybacks in the window (share count flat since FY2018) — no strike. Dividend a token €0.15 (suspended FY2019–FY2021). One positive logged: López bought 238,000 shares open-market (May 2023, €1.5m). **FAIL** (single hard strike; never averaged away).

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- The rally (+250% in a year) is a sum-of-the-parts/defence narrative, not an earnings recovery — H1 FY2026 LTM OI is −€292m.
- Steel Europe exit terms (EPCG 50:50 vs Jindal) can move book value materially in either direction.
- Pension obligations (€5.5bn) remain senior to shareholders in any wind-down of the perimeter.
- Break-up costs and stranded holding overheads routinely consume conglomerate-discount closures.
- TKMS (51% retained, consolidated) now dominates group NCI and sentiment; its order book is not attributable cash flow.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

No quality flip is plausible for this perimeter: Past WEAK is a decade of arithmetic, and the perimeter itself will not exist in current form by FY2027. The framework action is a **fresh ANALYSE of the residual financial holdco once the Materials Services spin and steel transaction complete** — and, separately, TKMS as its own name if naval exposure is wanted. No re-test scheduled; FY2026 AR (~Dec 2026) is informational only.

## C. Calculation notes

**NIBCL (uniform across all 11 balance-sheet years)** = trade accounts payable + current income tax liabilities + current other provisions + provisions for current employee benefits + deferred-revenue analog. Deferred-revenue analog = "Contract liabilities" (current) from FY2018 (IFRS 15); for FY2015–FY2017 = amounts due to customers for construction work + advance payments + deferred income from the other-non-financial-liabilities note (FY2015: 4,131+1,104+66 = 5,301; FY2016: 3,638+1,087+44 = 4,769; FY2017: 3,359+991+45 = 4,395). Burden-of-proof calls: current *other financial liabilities* and the residual *other non-financial liabilities* (employee/social-security/non-income-tax items, ~€1.3–2.4bn/yr) left in CE in every year for cross-year consistency — conservative (higher CE, lower ROCE; direction does not affect the verdict). Non-current provisions and pensions left in CE.

**FY2025 CE build:** total assets 28,885 − NIBCL 9,245 (AP 4,314 + tax 162 + other prov. 1,178 + curr. empl. prov. 186 + contract liab. 3,405) = **CE_wc 19,640**; − cash 5,725 = **CE_xc 13,915**.

**FY2025 TBV build:** equity attr. 9,767 − intangible assets 1,793 (goodwill 1,361 + other 432 per S&P split) = **7,974** (€12.81/sh).
