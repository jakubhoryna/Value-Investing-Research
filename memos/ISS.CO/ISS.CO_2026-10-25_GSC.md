ISS is one of the world's largest facility-service employers, cleaning, catering and maintaining customers' buildings with its own staff. Its ten-year record is poor: a 2020 operating loss of DKK 4.7bn, goodwill near DKK 24bn inside capital employed, and a worst-of-four ROCE of 6.1%.
