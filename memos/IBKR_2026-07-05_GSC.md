## 2. Understanding

Interactive Brokers is an automated, self-clearing global electronic broker earning commissions and — dominantly now — net interest income from margin lending and customer cash across 150+ markets in 30+ countries, selling to fee-paying active individual traders, hedge funds, RIAs, prop groups and introducing brokers, via a founder-controlled US holding company (IBKR Inc.) that owns a ~26% economic stake in the operating partnership IBG LLC (the remainder a noncontrolling interest held by Peterffy and employees). **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Run classically, the raw machinery misfires: with the $203B balance sheet (≈all customer float) counted as capital, ROCE reads ~2.4% and L/E ~893%/3,408% — a spurious WEAK/AVOID driven entirely by customers' ring-fenced money. The burden-of-proof CE rule *mandates* stripping the provably-segregated customer book from both sides (regulatorily trust-held, matched dollar-for-dollar — proven not to be firm capital). Firm capital ≈ equity + own debt ≈ $20.5B. On that basis: **normalized ROCE 19.0% (worst-of-four)** — STRONG and rising (~12%→21%); OI CAGR ~22%; per-share book growth ≫ 3% floor; **real leverage ≈ 0.1%** (own debt $19M). **FCFROCE n.m.** (broker CFO swings ±tens of billions on customer flows). **STRONG.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASS both sides: millions of accounts (no customer >10%); suppliers are many exchanges/venues and IBKR is vertically integrated in tech and clearing. Four forces, all **WEAK** (tally 0): customer power weak (atomised base, no backward integration); supplier power weak (many venues, self-clearing); substitutes weak for the professional/global core (no peer matches cost + 150-market reach), retail contestable; new entrants weak (a global, self-clearing, multi-asset automated broker licensed across dozens of jurisdictions is a decades-and-billions barrier). Moat source: **Cost** (extreme automation → lowest marginal cost, funding both cheapest commissions and highest interest paid), scale-reinforced, **stable-to-strengthening**. Market growth YES (~25–30% account growth). **STRONG.**
