# GSK — GSK plc: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | ADEQUATE | ADEQUATE | PASS | FAIR | **WATCH** |

GSK is a global research-based biopharma — vaccines (Shingrix, Arexvy), HIV (ViiV), respiratory/immunology and oncology — that since the July 2022 Haleon demerger is a focused drugs-and-vaccines company throwing off ~£4.9bn of levered FCF on ~14–16% returns on a goodwill-and-intangible-laden capital base. It is a genuinely *good, cash-generative, investment-grade* business, but not a STRONG-quality compounder: the comparable record is only ~4 years, as-reported operating profit is lumpy (2024 collapsed to £4.0bn on Zantac litigation), total-liabilities leverage is high (L/E 276%), and the whole sector runs on a patent treadmill — GSK's own ViiV HIV franchise faces a dolutegravir cliff around 2028. At £19.2 it trades at ~13.8× FCF and ~12× EV/OI: **FAIR, not cheap**. Two calls decide the verdict and are the honest swing factors: I hold the leverage breach as a flag (not a WEAK-fail) and count substitutes as the single strong force (not two). Flip either and GSK is AVOID. What flips it to BUY is price: ≈ £9 buys both primaries.

## 2. Understanding

GSK plc is a UK-headquartered, globally-operating research-based **biopharmaceutical** company that discovers, manufactures and sells patented prescription medicines and vaccines (HIV via the 78%-owned ViiV subsidiary, shingles/RSV vaccines, respiratory, immunology, oncology) to healthcare **payers and patients** reached through a concentrated set of wholesalers/distributors (esp. the three big US wholesalers) and national health systems, operating as a vertically integrated innovator-manufacturer across ~75 countries with the US at ~52% of turnover. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Clean "new GSK" continuing-ops window = FY2022–25 (Haleon demerged Jul 2022; pre-2022 is a different company, so **STRONG is unavailable on thin comparable history**, P1). Worst-of-four pooled ROCE ≈ **14.5%** (with-cash) to 15.7%; FCFROCE ≈ **11.3%**, comfortably above the 8% floor. Per-share growth over the window is positive (OI/sh & FCF/sh ~+6.9% geo, BV/sh +15.3%), though 2024 OI fell to £4.0bn on litigation before rebounding to £7.9bn in 2025. **L/E 275.8% breaches the 200% flag** (down from 472% in 2022) — the reason this isn't STRONG. Held at **ADEQUATE**: returns clear the 10% bar, leverage is deleveraging and is non-debt-heavy (net-debt/FCF ~2.5×, interest cover ~11×). (Strict P4 reading → WEAK; logged, App. C.)

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: customer base broad in economic substance (outside US no customer >5%; US three-wholesaler ~77%-of-US concentration is a distribution-channel/credit-risk feature GSK calls "standard market practice", not demand concentration) → **PASS** with channel flag logged; supplier base broad → PASS. Four forces: customer power **WEAK** (on-patent pricing power; IRA/payer pressure the erosion vector), supplier power **WEAK**, threat of substitutes **STRONG** (generics/biosimilars post-patent — the ViiV/dolutegravir cliff ~2028 is the live case), new entrants **WEAK** (high IP/regulatory/scale barriers). Tally = **1 strong force → judgment zone → ADEQUATE**. Moat: patents (time-limited, government-granted) plus a durable vaccines brand (Shingrix/Arexvy) and R&D/regulatory scale — plausible but **stable-to-eroding**. Market growth YES. **Future ADEQUATE.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- ViiV HIV franchise (~40%+ of the concentration in the pipeline) faces dolutegravir generic entry ~2028; long-acting injectables must replace it.
- US IRA Medicare price negotiation and payer power structurally lift customer bargaining power over time — could flip Future to WEAK (2 strong forces).
- As-reported OI is litigation-lumpy (Zantac largely settled 2024–25, but tail/other litigation recurs); a second weak OI year pressures the ROCE read.
- L/E 276% leaves little balance-sheet slack if returns soften; a stricter reading already reads Past as WEAK.
- Pipeline execution risk (respiratory/oncology/RSV competition from Pfizer, Moderna, AZ) as Shingrix/Arexvy mature.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

To **BUY**: price into the ≈ £9 band (both primaries) with gates intact. To **AVOID**: leverage read hardening to WEAK on a weaker-returns year, **or** payer/IRA pressure pushing customer power to STRONG (→ 2 strong forces → Future WEAK), **or** a P19/comp strike emerging (2025 pay-policy uplift; future buyback multiples). Upward re-rate risk (away from the band): successful HIV cliff mitigation + vaccines momentum. **Next review:** FY2026 results (~Feb 2027) to reset the litigation-trough OI basis and extend the clean record toward P1; earlier if price < ≈ £12 (NEAR) or on a material ViiV/IRA development.
