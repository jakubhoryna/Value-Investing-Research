BICO, formerly CELLINK, makes laboratory instruments, bioprinters and lab-automation systems for drug developers and research labs. It bought at least thirteen companies, largely with new shares, between 2019 and 2022, then wrote much of the price down: operating income was negative in nine of ten fiscal periods, SEK −5.0bn in total, and FY2025 carried SEK 1.04bn of impairments.
