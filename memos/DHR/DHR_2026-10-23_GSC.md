## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS (three material units); Past WEAK (7.2% ROCE); Future ADEQUATE (unit customer concentration); Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (buybacks); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID** (Past + SF).

Danaher sells the tools, consumables and tests that drug makers, laboratories and hospitals depend on, and much of it is locked in by validation and installed instruments. The capital record is the problem: two decades of acquisitions have left $61bn of goodwill and intangibles, so the company earns about 7% on the capital it employs, and returns have fallen three years running since the pandemic peak. It bought Abcam at a pre-deal operating loss and Masimo at 32× its operating income, and repurchased $10bn of stock since 2024 at prices clearly above worth, with the programme still open. At $229.75 the stock trades at 40.8× EV/OI against a $5.51 trigger. A flip needs returns above 10% and capital spent at or below worth.

## 2. Understanding

Group sentence: Danaher is a Washington, D.C.-based US group, assembled since the 1980s by acquisition, that makes equipment, consumables, reagents and software sold to biopharmaceutical manufacturers, contract manufacturers, research laboratories and universities, hospitals and clinical laboratories, and industrial filtration users, about 59% of revenue outside the US; it spun off Fortive (2016), split off Envista (2019) and spun off Veralto (2023), and bought Masimo (2026).

- **Biotechnology** (29.7% of 2025 revenue): Cytiva and Pall bioprocessing — cell-culture media, chromatography resins, filters, single-use bags and fill-finish systems that biopharmaceutical companies and contract manufacturers buy to make biologic drugs — plus lab filtration and medical filters bought by research labs and hospitals.
- **Life Sciences** (29.9%): research instruments (Beckman Coulter centrifuges and flow cytometers, SCIEX mass spectrometers, Leica microscopes, Molecular Devices), consumables (IDT DNA and RNA oligonucleotides, Aldevron plasmid DNA, Abcam antibodies) bought by pharma, biotech and academic labs, and Pall industrial filtration bought by microelectronics, aerospace, energy and food plants.
- **Diagnostics** (40.5%): clinical analysers and their reagents (Beckman Coulter, Cepheid molecular tests, Radiometer blood gas, Leica Biosystems pathology, HemoCue) bought by hospitals, reference laboratories and physicians' offices, and from June 2026 Masimo patient monitors.
