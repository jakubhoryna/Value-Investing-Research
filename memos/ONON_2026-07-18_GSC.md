## 2. Understanding

On Holding AG is a Zurich-headquartered, founder-controlled (dual-class, 57.1% insider voting on 16.8% economics) designer and marketer of premium performance running shoes, apparel and accessories — differentiated by CloudTec cushioning and LightSpray manufacturing — produced entirely by fewer than 30 third-party manufacturers (90% of footwear in Vietnam), and sold worldwide (Americas 57.7%, EMEA 25.3%, APAC 17.0%) to consumers via ~42% direct-to-consumer and to sporting-goods retailers, a 16-year-old company scaled globally since its 2021 NYSE IPO. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Record available: FY2019–2025 income (7 years; CE computable from FY2020) — thin-history flag, STRONG unavailable. Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **8.2%** (with-cash, end-basis; best leg 20.2% ex-cash beginning-basis); worst-of-four FCFROCE **5.9%**. The level failure is history-weighted: yearly beginning-basis ROCE runs −44.7% (2021) → 7.5% → 14.6% → 14.9% → **19.2% (2025)** — a steep, unbroken ascent, no sustained decline. FCF (interest reclassified from financing into CFO, IFRS) was negative four of seven years, then CHF 178→430→259m in 2023–25. L/E 73.7%, debt = leases only. **WEAK** — worst-of-four 8.2% < P3's 10% floor; the rubric tests the record that exists, and the record is short and scarred.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Vietnam concentration (90% of footwear) exposes COGS to US tariff policy on a 57.7%-Americas revenue base.
- Athletic-footwear brand cycles turn fast (Allbirds precedent); a fashion-cycle rollover would hit the premium-price pillar of the moat.
- SBC-heavy comp culture: dilution ~2%/yr and a CEO package within 4% of the P17 strike line.
- 2022 showed working-capital fragility: inventory build swung CFO to −CHF 227m in one year.
- Founder control (57% voting, 17% economics) removes minority influence over allocation.
