# AMZN — Amazon.com, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | ADEQUATE | ADEQUATE | PASS | EXPENSIVE | **WATCH** |

Amazon is a genuinely good business trading at a price the framework cannot call cheap. Three high-return engines — a scale-advantaged retail/logistics network, the #1 cloud platform (AWS), and a fast-growing advertising arm riding the retail flywheel — compound on capital at 22–29% in normal years behind one of the widest, most multi-sourced moats in the market. The bar to BUY is purely price: every cheapness multiple fails, EV/OI (30.8×) sits above the framework's own sell signal, and reported free cash flow has been crushed to $7.7B by an AI/AWS capex super-cycle. Nothing about quality blocks a BUY; only valuation does. The verdict flips only on a ~75%+ drawdown into the mid-$50s, or — far less likely — a step-change in cash conversion as capex normalises against a much larger operating-income base.

## 2. Understanding

Amazon is the world's leading online retailer and third-party marketplace, paired with AWS (the leading public-cloud provider) and a top-three digital-advertising business; it sells to hundreds of millions of consumers, millions of sellers, and millions of cloud customers, as a single-class US-listed corporation earning ~74% retail / ~17% AWS / ~9% ads, ~22% international, and is the growing leader in each core market. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Returns on ex-cash capital are strong and consistent: ROCE_xc ≥ 15% in 9 of 10 years (typically 22–29%), the lone miss being the 2022 overbuild/inflation trough (7%). OI/share compounded 0.43→7.39 and tangible book/share 1.51→34.97 over the decade. Leverage is conservative — net cash, with elevated early-year liabilities reflecting a negative-working-capital model (suppliers fund growth), not financial risk. The one real blemish: FCF/share went *nowhere* (1.08→0.71, negative in 2021–22), and FCFROCE_xc has stepped down from 30–90% to ~2% as capex absorbed the cash. Strong returns and equity compounding, but the failure of per-share cash flow to grow caps this below STRONG. **ADEQUATE.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASSES decisively — no customer or supplier can hold Amazon hostage on either side. Forces are MIXED: customer power and new-entrant threat are LOW (immense scale barriers), but retail rivalry (Walmart, Temu, Shopify-DTC) and cloud competition (Azure, Google Cloud) are intense and substitutes are credible. The moat is exceptional and multi-sourced — cost/scale in logistics and data centres, two-sided marketplace and Prime network effects, AWS switching costs and data gravity, brand, and habitual ingrainedness — and is stable-to-strengthening on the AWS/ads side. All three markets are growing. Moat strength floors this at ADEQUATE; forces are not clean enough for STRONG. **ADEQUATE** (top of band).

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Alignment is strong: founder Bezos still owns ~8% (largest holder), the company is one-share-one-vote (no dual-class), and all NEOs including CEO Jassy take a $365k base with comp delivered in time-vested RSUs explicitly tied to long-term value. Buybacks are de minimis and were timed into the 2022 trough; no dividend, with earnings retained and reinvested at high ROCE — rational under the rubric, no empire-building M&A. One flag: ~$1.8B/yr of Project Kuiper launch payments to Bezos's Blue Origin — a disclosed, committee-reviewed related-party flow, immaterial in scale but a real founder-conflict to monitor. No strike. **PASS.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- AI/AWS capex may not earn its cost of capital — the entire FCF-recovery thesis rests on the latest data-centre buildout generating AWS returns matching the historic franchise.
- Cloud is a genuine three-way contest; sustained Azure/Google Cloud share gains would erode AWS's pricing power and the profit centre.
- Retail is structurally low-margin and now contested by a resurgent Walmart and price-led Temu/Shein, capping the consolidated return profile.
- Regulatory/antitrust pressure (US, EU) on marketplace practices, bundling, and labour could force costly structural changes.
- Founder influence + the Blue Origin RPT is a low-probability governance tail if related-party flows scale.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

To **BUY**: price into the ~$55 EV/OI band (catastrophic drawdown) — the dominant path — or, secondarily, a durable collapse in capex intensity that lifts FCF an order of magnitude against today's OI base. To **AVOID**: evidence the AI capex is structurally value-destructive (AWS growth/margin rolling over while spend stays elevated) would pull Future toward WEAK. **Next review:** FY2026 10-K (~Feb 2027), the cleanest read on whether capex is moderating and FCF recovering; sooner if AMZN trades below ~$90 or AWS/AI capex guidance is cut materially.
