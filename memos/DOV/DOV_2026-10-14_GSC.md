# DOV — Dover Corporation: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding **FAIL** (one-level decomposition: the material Climate & Sustainability Technologies and Engineered Products units are irreducible) → **DO NOT ANALYSE FURTHER**. Past, Future, shareholder-friendliness and cheapness were not graded.

Dover owns many niche industrial operating companies grouped into five reportable segments. Some of those segments can be described in one sentence each; two cannot. Climate & Sustainability Technologies, a fifth of sales, combines a supermarket-refrigeration business, a brazed-plate heat-exchanger business selling to heat-pump and HVAC makers, and a can-making machinery business selling to beverage-can makers. Engineered Products, an eighth of sales, combines vehicle-service equipment sold to repair shops with aerospace and defence electronics sold to defence contractors, plus winches and hoists. In each case the filer names separately managed businesses that differ in industry and in who pays, and it reports revenue only by segment and geography, so their shares are not printed. Under MODEL v4.5.0 that makes both units irreducible; both are material, so Gate 0 fails. This is a parameter-driven result of the v4.5.0 rule, not a finding of incomprehension or a change in the business. It flips back to a full analysis if the segments stop mixing such businesses, or if a MODEL ruling changes how a multi-business industrial segment is tested.

## 2. Understanding
**Group sentence.** Dover Corporation designs and makes differentiated industrial equipment, components, consumables and aftermarket parts: pumps and fluid connectors, fuel dispensers and cryogenic components, marking and coding printers with their inks, commercial refrigeration and heat exchangers, can-making machinery, vehicle lifts and defence electronics. It sells them directly and through distributors to thousands of industrial, retail and process customers, none above 10% of revenue. It is a decentralised US manufacturer headquartered in Downers Grove, Illinois, with 54% of 2025 revenue from US customers. It has been listed on the NYSE since 1955, with $8.1bn of 2025 revenue after the 2018 Apergy spin-off and the 2024 sale of its waste-truck business.

**Gate 0 test.**
- **CST is irreducible.** The 10-K names three businesses: "Our refrigeration business" (refrigeration systems, display cases and glass doors for food retail; Dover Food Retail, "an operating company within our Climate & Sustainability Technologies segment"), "Our heat exchanger business" (brazed plate heat exchangers for residential heat pumps, industrial heating and cooling and data-centre cooling), and "the other business in this segment", which makes aluminium can-making machinery and turnkey can lines. The filer's own segment description names three end-markets (commercial refrigeration; heating and cooling; beverage can-making equipment), and its customer text names three paying groups: supermarkets and other food retailers, the refrigeration and HVAC industry, and beverage can-makers. Revenue is disaggregated only by segment and geography (Note 2), so no sub-business share is printed. Sub-businesses named without their shares that differ in industry and paying customer make the unit irreducible (J13).
- **EP is irreducible.** The 10-K names "our vehicle service business" (lifts, wheel service, diagnostics and collision repair for vehicle repair and maintenance), "our industrial winch and hoist business" and "our aerospace and defense business" (RF and microwave filters and switches and signals-intelligence systems), plus soldering and fluid dispensing. Repair shops and their distributors pay for the first; defence and aerospace contractors pay for the third. The industries are vehicle-service equipment and defence electronics. No shares are printed; MD&A discusses vehicle service and aerospace and defence as separate demand drivers.
- **No exception applies.** These are not sides of one marketplace and not one product sold through different channels.

Both units are above the P22 line on revenue and segment earnings, CST also on assets. **FAIL → DNAF.** Where a reading was open, the harsher governs (J13); here the filer's own segment and business descriptions decide it. Pumps & Process Solutions, Imaging & Identification and Clean Energy & Fueling were not tested to a finding; the verdict does not depend on them. The lens (STD on the evidence: no bank or insurer part) and the group dependency check (no listed subsidiary or listed holding) were not needed for the verdict.
