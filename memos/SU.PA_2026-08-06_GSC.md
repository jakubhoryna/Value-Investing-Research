# SU.PA — Schneider Electric SE: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding **PASS**, Past **ADEQUATE**, Future **STRONG**, shareholder-friendliness **FAIL** (capital allocation **ADVERSE** under §2·1C·3), cheapness **EXPENSIVE** — final verdict **AVOID**, unchanged from v1 but on a re-derived basis.

The re-test that put this name in the queue went against it. v1's SF FAIL rested on mechanical P19 strikes that v4.0.0 abolished, and on the old evidence alone Schneider would have flipped: the FY2025 tranche at €226.78 reads 21.5–23.4× EV/OI, short of the ≈25× calibration, which is exactly the price limb that rescued SCHN, SAP and ESSITY-B. What defeats the flip is new. On 11 December 2025 the board committed €2.5–3.5bn to repurchases through 2030; in March 2026 it handed Natixis an **irrevocable** €600m mandate to November 2026; by 27 July it had bought 1.2m shares at €262 average, and on 29 July it paid €283.75 — the highest price in the record. That execution is 25×-class on all four defined reads while the stock trades at 5.8× the trigger band, no shares are cancelled, and no price criterion is published anywhere. A flip now needs a completed programme-year at prices the framework can defend, not a cheaper quote.

## 2. Understanding

Schneider sells differentiated electrical-distribution and industrial-automation hardware plus connected software (EcoStruxure, AVEVA) mostly through distributors to building, data-centre, industrial and infrastructure customers worldwide. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Unchanged — no new fiscal year has closed. Worst-of-four normalized 10y ROCE **10.4%** clears P3's 10% but not P2's 15%; worst-of-four FCFROCE **7.7%** misses the 8% STRONG bar. Trend redeems rather than convicts: ROCE_wc 8.3% (2016) → 13.5% (2025), no sustained decline. Per-share growth clears the 3% floor comfortably (ΔOI/sh +11.7%, ΔFCF/sh +9.1% geo-mean). TBV negative throughout on €31.4bn of acquired goodwill and intangibles. Leverage at 30 June 2026 L/E **166%**, inside P5. H1-2026 points the right way — revenue +14% organic, OI margin 17.0% — but an interim does not roll the window. **ADEQUATE.**

## 4. Quality — future

Unchanged and re-affirmed. Breadth PASS both sides. Four forces all WEAK — customer power (fragmented, distributor-mediated, spec-in switching costs), supplier power, substitutes (no functional substitute for electrical distribution), new entrants (certification regimes, channel ingrainedness). Tally **0 strong**. Moat: ingrainedness with the electrical channel and now data-centre designers, stable-to-strengthening; Cognite and AiDASH deepen the software layer whatever they cost. H1-2026 confirmed it — record revenue and adjusted EBITA, full-year guidance upgraded to +10–13% organic revenue. **STRONG.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

P17 **PASS** on both reads: CEO Blum's total ≈ €6–7m against 5% of lower(FCF, NI) = €208m and the ≈ $40m indexed absolute trigger. RPT **PASS**. Capital allocation **ADVERSE**, and therefore SF FAIL on its own. All three ADVERSE conjuncts are met by named forward evidence: the live 2026 tranche prices at 32.5×/33.7× MCap/FCF and 24.8–26.9× EV/OI (year and 3y-normalized, both cash bases); the stock is EXPENSIVE at 27.8–28.5× EV/OI against a €44–52 band; and the programme is executing weekly under an irrevocable mandate, freshly re-authorised to 2030, with **no published price criterion** — while €310m of spending has reduced the issued count by nothing at all. **FAIL.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Allocation is the thesis-breaker, not a risk: €2.5–3.5bn of committed repurchases to 2030 and $3.45bn of announced all-cash software M&A, all priced off a 28× stock.
