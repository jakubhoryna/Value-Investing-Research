# 6645.T — OMRON: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS; Past WEAK; Future WEAK; Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (flag pair — P17 normalized read and capital-allocation CAUTION); Cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID** (Past, Future and SF).

Omron is a Kyoto maker of factory-automation controls and home blood-pressure monitors, with a social-infrastructure arm and, since 2023, a majority stake in the medical-data company JMDC. Its record has deteriorated on every axis the model reads. Operating income per share is lower in FY2025 than in FY2015. Free cash flow fell from ¥67bn in FY2020 to ¥8bn in FY2025. The JMDC purchase added ¥299bn of goodwill and ¥160bn of minority interest at about 56× the target's operating income. At ¥6,396 the shares trade at 161× free cash flow. The verdict could only change after several years of rebuilt returns in automation and a capital record that earns back the JMDC price.

## 2. Understanding
Omron designs, makes and sells factory-automation components and systems (programmable controllers, motion control, sensors, vision and inspection equipment, safety devices and robots), bought mainly by machine builders and manufacturers through distributors (53% of FY2025 continuing sales). It also sells home blood-pressure monitors and other personal health devices to consumers through retail and online channels (19%), and railway ticketing, traffic and energy-storage systems with maintenance to rail operators, public bodies and businesses (19%). Through 54%-owned JMDC it sells medical-claims data and health-management services to health-insurance societies, pharmaceutical companies and employers (7%). It is vertically integrated and Kyoto-headquartered, founded in 1933, with 45% of sales in Japan and 15% in China. It is a mature, ¥767bn-revenue group mid-restructuring, whose electronic-components unit is being sold to Carlyle. **PASS**.

## 3. Quality — past record
The worst-of-four normalized ROCE is **7.2%** and FCFROCE **3.6%**, both end-of-period with cash; ex-cash reads are 8.8–9.7% and 4.3–4.8%. Returns fell by half between the window's halves: ROCE from 10.2% to 5.5%, FCFROCE from 7.7% to 1.1%. FY2023–25 ROCE was 1.4–4.2%, as JMDC goodwill entered capital and automation demand fell. Operating income per share compounded at **−0.6%** a year, FCF per share at **−15.5%** and TBV per share at −0.8%. BV per share grew 7.5%. L/E is 62%. **WEAK**: returns sit below the ADEQUATE floor, decline steadily, and per-share earnings did not grow.

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth: PASS on customers (the yūhō states that no single customer is material). No supplier concentration is disclosed. Forces:
- Customer power WEAK: fragmented machine builders and consumers.
- Supplier power WEAK: standard components from many vendors.
- Substitutes WEAK: no alternative to control or measurement.
- New entrants **STRONG**: the yūhō cites intensifying price competition in blood-pressure monitors and low-price pressure in emerging markets. In automation, management's new plan sets out to "re-forge" competitive advantage in its device businesses.

Tally: 1 strong force. Moat: switching costs in the installed controller base. It is **eroding**: automation margin fell from 16.7% in FY2020 to 5.4% in FY2023 and 10.4% in FY2025, against flat sales. Market growth MIXED. **WEAK**.

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness
P17: the CEO's ¥369m clears the fiscal-year cap (¥391m) but breaches the 3y-normalized cap (¥235m), which is one flag. Outside directors average ≈ ¥22m. No shareholder controls the company, and no related-party transactions are disclosed. Capital allocation is **CAUTION**. FY2021 repurchases ran at ≈ 29–37× EV/OI and 50–75× FCF. The JMDC deal cost ≈ 56× pre-deal operating income, was bank-funded, and was followed by an ¥11.7bn goodwill impairment. Dividends exceeded FCF in each of FY2023–25 while borrowings rose. There is no live repurchase programme, and the DMB sale is a course change. **FAIL** (flag pair).
