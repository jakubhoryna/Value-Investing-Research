## C. Calculation notes

**C1 — J1 operating-income line.** Hitachi's IFRS statement has no line labelled operating income: revenues − cost of sales − SG&A = "Adjusted operating income", then other income, other expenses, financial income and expenses and the equity-method share to EBIT. Adjusted operating income excludes impairments, restructuring and other operating charges booked in other expenses, so it is not J1. The lowest operating subtotal is the derived line AOI + other income − other expenses, which carries every operating charge. FY3/2026: 1,199,275 + 133,520 − 200,802 = 1,131,993. EBIT is not used: it adds financial income and the equity-method share, which J3 takes out. Gains on sale of PP&E stay (operating); impairment losses (FY3/2026 ¥151,502m) stay.

**C2 — NIBCL calls.** MODEL NIBCL = trade payables + accrued expenses (income taxes payable is reported inside accrued expenses, confirmed in the H1 FY3/2026 semi-annual report) + contract liabilities, from FY3/2019. For FY3/2016–FY3/2018 the releases print one aggregated "Other current liabilities" line (¥1,739,315m / ¥1,657,766m / ¥1,765,046m) mixing accrued expenses, advances and provisions; the burden of proof is not met, so it stays in CE and NIBCL is trade payables only for those three years. Supplier-finance payables inside trade payables (¥48,238m FY3/2024, ¥77,820m FY3/2025, ¥92,065m FY3/2026; note on supplier finance arrangements, FY3/2026 securities report) leave NIBCL and enter the debt-equivalents. Other current liabilities, other financial liabilities and all non-current liabilities stay in CE. Goodwill and acquired intangibles stay in CE.

**C3 — Financial income (J3) and the G6 classes (P31).** Removed from OI: the net gain on business reorganisation and others (the cash-flow reconciliation line; it sits in other income), every year a gain, ¥1,425,267m over the window including the FY3/2017 bound (B1, gaps in A). Financial income, interest income and the equity-method share sit below the J1 line and are out. Removed from CFO: interest received and dividends received (both printed in CFO from FY3/2018; FY3/2017 derived from the income statement). Classes: equity-method investments (associates and JVs; carrying value 3.0%–7.5% of total assets, above 5% at six of eleven dates) and the non-current securities line (equity securities mixed with lease receivables, loans and derivatives). Summed, they are 7.2%–16.0% of total assets at every balance-sheet date, and the equity-method result alone is 4.4%–35.3% of exclude-reading OI: **above the P31 line**, both readings built. Exclude: income out of OI and FCF, carrying value out of CE (both cash variants). Include: the equity-method result as reported (after the investees' interest and tax) in OI; dividends received stay in CFO (all dividends come from equity holdings); interest received stays out on both readings (Hitachi does not attribute it to a class, J13). No dividend income is added to OI on the include reading, because the income statement does not show it apart from other financial income (J13). Fair-value changes and disposal gains stay out on both readings; there was no net disposal loss in any year.
