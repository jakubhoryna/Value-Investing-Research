Analysed on the eve of its own irrelevance as a standalone record: the all-stock Udemy merger closed mid-2026, the share count is 60% bigger, and a $500m repurchase programme launched days after closing — every gate re-bases at the FY2026 10-K (HOG perimeter treatment). The equity is two-thirds cash.

## 2. Understanding

Coursera operates an online learning marketplace — university and corporate-branded courses, certificates and degrees (Google/IBM/Meta career certificates prominent), sold to consumers, enterprises and governments, revenue-shared with content partners — now combined with Udemy's instructor-marketplace and enterprise-learning business via the 2026 all-stock merger; Delaware-incorporated, 2021 IPO, CEO Greg Hart (ex-Amazon, from Feb 2025). **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Pre-merger record, five years: reported OI −143.1 → −177.4 → −145.6 → −113.2 → −77.4 — improving, never positive; normalized ROCE **−15.3%** (end-wc; all variants deeply negative). FCFROCE worst-of-four **1.85%**; the FY24–25 FCF ($59m, $79m) is $100m+/yr SBC added back plus deferred-revenue build — owner earnings in the Marshall sense are negative throughout. Share count 113m → 165m (+9.9%/yr dilution) *before* the merger's +60%. L/E 29–57%, debt zero — solvency is the IPO cash pile ($793m). **WEAK.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: customers — millions of learners + thousands of enterprises: PASS. Suppliers — **the content partners are the concentration**: a handful of brand-name universities and Google/IBM-class certificate issuers drive disproportionate catalog demand; revenue-share terms are theirs to move; no single supplier >10% of COGS verified — logged as a structural negative, not a clean FAIL. Forces: customer power WEAK; supplier power **STRONG** (partner brands own the credential value; forward integration live — partners can and do sell direct); substitutes **STRONG** — free content and AI tutors/assistants attack the paid-credential premise itself (doing-without, demonstrably live in the −54% de-rating and slowing consumer segment); new entrants MODERATE (marketplace scale matters, but Udemy itself proves entry succeeded once already). Tally **2 strong → WEAK**. Moat: Brand/network (partner catalog + 175m registered learners) — **eroding** at the demand line. Market growth MIXED (enterprise upskilling up; consumer certificates questioned). **WEAK.**
