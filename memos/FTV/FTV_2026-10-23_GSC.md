Fortive sells Fluke test tools, safety and facilities software, and hospital sterilisation and clinical software, much of it to customers who stay with an installed product or a workflow. The capital record is the problem: three separations (Automation & Specialty in 2018, Vontier in 2020, Ralliant in 2025) and a decade of acquisitions leave goodwill and intangibles at 81% of assets, and returns on that capital have run near 7% since 2020. At $60.05 the stock trades at 30.6× EV/OI against a $4.54 trigger. A flip needs ROCE above 10% and repurchases at or below worth.

## 2. Understanding

Group sentence: Fortive is an Everett, Washington-based US group, spun off from Danaher in 2016, that makes professional test and measurement instruments and gas detectors and sells facilities, maintenance, construction-cost and safety-compliance software and services to industrial, utility, commercial-property and public-sector organisations (Intelligent Operating Solutions), and sterilisers, their consumables, instrument-tracking, biomedical test and radiation-dosimetry products and clinical documentation software to hospitals and surgery centres (Advanced Healthcare Solutions), about 44% of sales outside the US; it sold Automation & Specialty (2018) and spun off Vontier (2020) and Ralliant (2025).
