# NVDA — NVIDIA Corporation: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

|Understanding|Past|Future|Shareholder-friendliness|Cheapness|FINAL|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|PASS|STRONG|WEAK|FAIL (comp; buybacks; Groq allocation)|EXPENSIVE|**AVOID (Future WEAK + SF FAIL)**|

The past record is the best in the register — worst-of-four 10-year average ROCE 58.2%, FCFROCE 45.0%, per-share compounding near 60% a year on 31.5% L/E. But the model asks whether the future is protected and whether the people deserve partnership, and NVIDIA fails both. Revenue now concentrates in a handful of hyperscalers (one direct customer 22%, another 14% in FY2026) that are demonstrably backward-integrating into custom silicon, while the effective supply chain runs through one leading-edge foundry ecosystem — two strong forces, breadth FAIL, no waiver available. Governance adds three strikes: CEO pay above the $30M cap for a third straight year, $40B of buybacks at ~50× FCF, and $17B paid to Groq for a non-exclusive license and staff. A flip to WATCH requires customer deconcentration back under 10%, comp reform, and allocation discipline — none imminent. At 49× FCF, cheapness is academic.

## 2. Understanding

NVIDIA is a 33-year-old, Santa Clara-based, fabless US semiconductor company that designs differentiated AI-accelerator systems — data-centre GPUs, networking, and the CUDA software stack — plus gaming graphics processors, sold worldwide to a concentrated set of organisational buyers (cloud hyperscalers, AI labs, OEMs/ODMs, system integrators, distributors), with wafer fabrication outsourced chiefly to TSMC and assembly to Asian subcontractors, having transformed from a PC-graphics vendor into the dominant supplier of AI data-centre infrastructure. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized averages: ROCE 58.2% (wc/end), FCFROCE 45.0% (wc/end) — multiples of the 15%/8% bars. ΔOI/sh and ΔFCF/sh geometric means ≈ 60% over nine years; ΔBV/sh ≈ 45% with modest distributions. L/E 31.5%, no P5 issue. The shape is cyclical-spiky, not smooth: OI/sh fell 24% in FY2020 (crypto hangover) and 58% in FY2023 (gaming/crypto correction) before the AI super-cycle drove FY2024–26; averages are dominated by the last three years, but even the FY2017–23 sub-period clears P2 comfortably. No sustained decline. **STRONG.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: customer-side **FAIL** — one direct customer 22% of FY2026 revenue, another 14%, with indirect customers estimated at ≥10% individually. The captive-customer waiver is unavailable: the concentrated buyers (hyperscalers) demonstrably *can* and *do* backward-integrate (TPU, Trainium, Maia, MTIA, ByteDance custom silicon). Supplier-side also fails on the effective-supplier test: TSMC leading-edge wafers plus CoWoS packaging is a single ecosystem; Samsung is not a ready alternative at the frontier. Forces: customer power STRONG, supplier power STRONG, substitutes WEAK, new entrants WEAK — tally 2 = the book's stop signal. Moat: real and singularly sourced (switching costs via CUDA ingrainedness), dominant today but eroding at the margins as customers fund alternatives and frameworks abstract the hardware. Market growth: YES, explosively — which is precisely what attracts the capital now arrayed against it. **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Strike 1 — comp: CEO total $36.3M FY2026 (>$30M cap; third consecutive breach: $34.2M, $49.9M prior). Strike 2 — buybacks: $40.1B FY2026 at ~35–50× FCF, far above conservative worth, partly sterilising ~$6B/yr SBC dilution. Strike 3 — allocation: $17B to Groq (Dec 2025) for a non-exclusive license and employees — no products, contracts, or equity acquired; unsanctionable at any framework multiple. Positives noted: Huang holds 3.58% outright; top outside director $364k (<$500k norm); RPT trivial. One strike disqualifies; there are three. **FAIL.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Hyperscaler AI-capex growth decelerates or reverses, compressing the FY2026 FCF base on which the band is computed.
- Customer custom silicon reaches performance parity for inference at scale, converting concentration into direct substitution.
- Taiwan-Strait or export-control escalation severs the single-ecosystem supply chain.
- The FY2024–26 earnings level proves a demand super-cycle peak; averages and the band would fall materially at REVIEW.
- Upside risk to the AVOID: CUDA lock-in proves durable for another decade and concentration disclosures recede below 10% — the Future gate would need re-testing.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

AVOID → WATCH requires all three: no customer ≥10% of revenue (or waiver conditions genuinely met, capping Future at ADEQUATE), CEO comp brought under P17, and evidence of price-disciplined allocation (no repeat of Groq-style deals; buybacks only below conservative worth). Cheapness alone can never flip an AVOID. Next review: FY2027 10-K (~Feb 2027); earlier only on structural governance change.
