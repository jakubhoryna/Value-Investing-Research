## 2. Understanding

Cohu supplies the back end of semiconductor manufacturing: test handlers, contactors, interface boards and inspection/metrology systems, plus a growing data-analytics layer (DI-Core, Tignis), sold to semiconductor IDMs, fabless firms and OSAT subcontractors worldwide who pay; a Delaware corporation, NASDAQ-listed since 1957, HQ Poway, California, manufacturing in Asia and Europe; a niche top-two supplier in handlers/contactors, small-cap, revenue ~half Asia. **PASS.**
