## 2. Understanding

Group sentence: Bufab AB is a Värnamo-based distributor listed on Nasdaq Stockholm (net sales SEK 8.07bn in 2025, about 1,900 employees, 29% owned by the Liljedahl family's industrial group) that sources C-parts — fasteners, fixings, springs, turned and pressed parts, about half of sales made to customers' own drawings — from Asian and European factories and supplies them, with quality assurance and stock-keeping on the customer's line, to roughly 20,000 manufacturers in machinery, electrification, defence, rail, vehicles and construction, through more than 60 companies in 31 countries.

- **Europe North & East** (35% of 2025 sales): trading companies in the Nordics, Poland, Hungary, the Baltics, Slovakia and Romania, plus some manufacturing of demanding components, selling C-parts to industrial manufacturers.

- **UK/Ireland** (18%): trading companies — industrial fastener distributors, TIMCO's fixings sold to builders' merchants and the construction trade, and D.C. Iron's ironwork parts (July 2026).
- **Americas** (12%): trading companies in the US and Mexico selling C-parts to vehicle, recreational-vehicle and industrial manufacturers.
- Immaterial: Asia-Pacific (5.5% of sales); "Other" holds two sourcing companies, parent costs and eliminations.
