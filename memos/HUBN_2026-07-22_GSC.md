## 2. Understanding

HUBER+SUHNER designs and manufactures differentiated electrical and optical connectivity hardware — cables, connectors, antennas, RF-over-fibre and optical switching — combining radio-frequency, fibre-optic and low-frequency technologies, sold to 4,000–5,000 industrial, telecom, transport and defence organisations across EMEA, the Americas and APAC, as a vertically integrated, family-anchored Swiss listed company reporting under Swiss GAAP FER. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized ROCE 12.3% (beginning-of-period, with-cash) against 16.4% on the flattering ex-cash end-period read — comfortably clearing P3's 10% but well short of P2's 15%. Worst-of-four FCFROCE 7.5% misses the 8% STRONG floor. Returns dipped in 2023 and have recovered gradually since; no sustained decline. Per-share growth is mixed off a peak-2021 base: OI/share −2.2% geometric, FCF/share +10.4%, BV/share +2.6%. Leverage is exceptional — L/E 28.5% against a 200% flag, with zero financial debt. **ADEQUATE** — solid but unremarkable returns, and STRONG is unavailable regardless on five years of machine-verified history against P1's ten.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth **PASS** both sides: 4,000–5,000 buying customers with segments split 38/32/30, no disclosed concentration; inputs are copper, polymers and commodity components with ready alternatives. Four forces all **WEAK** — customer power (fragmented base, multi-year requalification lock-in, backward integration implausible), supplier power (commodity inputs, many vendors), substitutes (wired and wireless both served in-house; doing-without infeasible in critical applications), new entrants (qualification cycles in rail, aerospace and defence dull entry). Tally 0 strong — favourable. Moat: switching costs via qualification lock-in, stable but narrow — 10.5% EBIT margins and 12.3% ROCE are not moat-grade economics. Market growth YES: order intake +13.7%, backlog +48.5%, 2026 guidance ≥10% organic. **ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Compensation is clean: CEO total CHF 1.705m = 2.4% of the lower of FCF and net income, far inside the 5% strike and the ~CHF 40m scrutiny trigger; chairman CHF 570k, under the indexed stop. Ownership is anchored — EGS 9.74%, Hoffmann-Suhner 6.50%, Huwa 3.42%. Related-party dealing is trivial (JV trade CHF 4.0m, 0.5% of sales). Dividends are rational at ~50% payout and the 2021–23 shares were genuinely cancelled. But **P19 strikes**: that programme's CHF 80.61 average implies MCap/FCF above 20× on both the year and normalized reads across every tranche year, with no rescue (lowest EV/OI read 12.9×, above the 7× bargain line) and no redemption (no published worth-referenced repurchase policy). **FAIL.**
