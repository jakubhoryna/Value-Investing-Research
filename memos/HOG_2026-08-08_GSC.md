# HOG — Harley-Davidson, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past **WEAK** (bound-proof on both treatments — group w-o-4 ROCE 5.83%, and the ROE-substitution read fires P4 on three straight declines); Future ADEQUATE; SF **PASS** (clean P17, P19 SOUND — the run's first); cheapness EXPENSIVE on the coherent post-perimeter basis — **AVOID (Past gate)**.

The most interesting AVOID of this run. An iconic brand trading **below tangible book** (0.87×/0.89×), pay passing the comp test with headroom, buybacks executed at 5–7× free cash flow — and a demand problem the record cannot hide: revenue −33% TTM, operating income down three consecutive years, and a core-market demographic decline that is secular, not cyclical. The 2025 KKR/PIMCO deal moved ~$4bn of finance receivables off the balance sheet, so FY2026 is the first year the motorcycle economics print clean. If that year shows a stabilised operating line while the price stays under book, this name re-tests better than anything else in the US block.

## 2. Understanding

Harley-Davidson designs and manufactures heavyweight cruiser and touring motorcycles, parts, accessories and apparel, sold wholesale to a global network of independent dealers who pay, alongside Harley-Davidson Financial Services, whose consumer-receivables book was majority-transferred in 2025 to a KKR/PIMCO partnership, leaving a capital-light originate-to-sell model. Wisconsin-headquartered, listed 1986; 2025 also brought a CEO transition (Zeitz → Starrs) and a survived activist proxy contest. The definitive American motorcycle brand, in secular volume decline at home. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Two treatments, one answer. **Group-consistent basis** (HDFS operating result in the numerator, its assets in CE): worst-of-four normalized ROCE **5.83%** (beg-wc), FCFROCE worst ≈ 6.3% — the finance book structurally dilutes motorcycle returns. **Substitution alternate** (ROE analog): normalized ROE ≈ 19%, but the series runs 25.5 → 25.5 → 21.7 → 14.4 → 10.7 — **three consecutive declines, P4 fires regardless of level**. Reported OI fell three straight years (779 → 417 → 387) and TTM halves again ($213m). L/E 153% post-deconsolidation, inside P5. ΔOI/sh −12.1%/yr, ΔFCF/sh −11.3%/yr, ΔBV/sh +12.1%/yr. **WEAK — bound-proof on either treatment.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASS: thousands of independent dealers, none plausibly over 10% (sentence unverified — flagged); supply chain broad. Customer power WEAK (dealers carry the brand, not the reverse); supplier power WEAK; substitutes **STRONG** — a discretionary purchase whose deferral is costless, with doing-without the live form in a shrinking rider demographic; new entrants WEAK (the brand is a hundred-year barrier, demonstrated by every failed cruiser challenger). Tally **1 STRONG, contained**. Moat **Brand** — genuine, **stable as a moat but sitting on a shrinking market**: pricing power within the niche persists even as the niche contracts. Market growth **NO** — US heavyweight registrations have declined for a decade, and that is the thesis problem. **ADEQUATE.**
