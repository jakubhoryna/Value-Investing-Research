# DSV.CO — DSV A/S: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past WEAK (ROCE decline); Future ADEQUATE (unit customer concentration in Road and Contract Logistics); Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (flag pair: buybacks, acquisitions, executive pay); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID**.

DSV is one of the world's largest freight forwarders, a global leader in transport and logistics after buying DB Schenker in 2025. Its ten-year worst-of-four ROCE is 10.2%, carried by serial acquisitions whose goodwill now dominates capital employed. Returns have fallen three years running since the 2022 freight peak, to 6.0% on year-end capital in FY2025, so the decline rule binds while the level sits below the STRONG bar. Capital allocation adds three distinct flags. Price is a fourth barrier: DKK 1,372.50 against a DKK 35.2 trigger. A return to WATCH needs two clean ROCE years (FY2027 at the earliest) and two of the three SF flags gone.

## 2. Understanding

DSV buys air, ocean, road and rail capacity from airlines, shipping lines and hauliers and resells it shipment by shipment, with customs and documentation, to corporate shippers worldwide, and runs contract warehouses and fulfilment for them; it is a Danish, widely held, asset-light listed group of about 150,000 people, built by serial acquisition (UTi 2016, Panalpina 2019, GIL 2021, Schenker 2025). **PASS.** Lens STD. Three material units; no listed subsidiary or holding, no finance arm.

## 4. Quality — future

The group says no customer exceeds 5% of group revenue. That rules out a customer above 10% of Air & Sea, but not of Road or Contract Logistics, where silence is concentration. Neither unit is dominant, so each carries a strong customer-power force. Road adds easy entry: two strong forces, WEAK. Contract Logistics holds its one force through embedded operations, ADEQUATE. Air & Sea has one strong force, ocean carriers' power and forward integration, contained by network density and multi-carrier buying; unit EBIT held at DKK 11.9–13.4bn. Moat: cost and scale, stable; market growth YES. S = 1.88. **ADEQUATE** (unit customer conc.).
