Brockhaus Technologies bought three German technology companies after 2017, sold two of them (Palas in 2022, its 52% of Bikeleasing in June 2026) at gains, returned €90m through a tender buyback in October 2026, and now owns only IHSE, a maker of high-end KVM switching equipment, plus a large cash balance. ## 2. Understanding

Brockhaus Technologies AG, a Frankfurt-based holding company founded in 2017 as Brockhaus Capital Management, listed on the Frankfurt Prime Standard since July 2020 and 22.4% held through its founder-CEO's family vehicle, owns 100% of IHSE GmbH (Oberteuringen, Germany, with kvm-tec in Austria and sales subsidiaries in the US, Singapore and China), which designs and makes high-end KVM extenders, matrix switches and KVM-over-IP equipment that let operators in control rooms reach many computers securely and without lag, sold mainly through global sales partners and systems integrators to government security and defence users (about 43% of FY2025 IHSE revenue), broadcasters, air-traffic control, healthcare, industry and other organisations worldwide (FY2025 revenue €30.3m: EMEA, Americas, APAC), and holds most of the proceeds of its Palas (2022) and Bikeleasing (June 2026) disposals in cash. PASS.

One material unit remains (Security Technologies); Bikeleasing (bicycle leasing as an employee benefit) was discontinued in FY2025 and deconsolidated on 30 June 2026. Not a group.
