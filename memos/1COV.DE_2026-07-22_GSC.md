# 1COV.DE — Covestro AG: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | SF | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | **WEAK** | **WEAK** | **FAIL** | **EXPENSIVE** | **AVOID — triple gate** |

A competent, asset-heavy commodity-polymer producer that earned a real cost-and-scale return through 2016–18 and again in 2021, then was overwhelmed by Chinese isocyanate and polycarbonate capacity and a European energy base it cannot fix. Eight years average 7.4% return on capital against a 10% floor, falling four years running into an FY2025 operating loss. That decides it. Two facts close the file: EUR 4.4m for the top-paid board member against negative net income *and* negative free cash flow is a mechanical P17 strike; and a EUR 59.46 squeeze-out by XRG leaves no investable minority position.

## 2. Understanding

Covestro AG is a Leverkusen-headquartered German chemicals manufacturer selling commodity and semi-specialty polymer intermediates by the tonne — MDI/TDI isocyanates and polyols for polyurethane foams, plus polycarbonate resins, coating and adhesive raw materials and engineering plastics — to industrial B2B buyers in automotive, construction, furniture, electronics and coatings, from 46 sites with a European core, as a German stock corporation 95.1%-controlled by Abu Dhabi's XRG P.J.S.C. since December 2025 and now under a EUR 59.46 statutory squeeze-out. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Window FY2018–FY2025 (8 years; FY2015–16 balance sheets unavailable, so P1 is unmet and STRONG unavailable). Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **7.4%**, FCFROCE **3.8%** — both under the 10% P3 floor. Shape matters more than level: 27.6% (2018), 19.9% (2021), then 2.1%, 1.5%, 0.8%, −3.1%; a sustained decline downgrades on its own. OI/share fell EUR 13.39 → −EUR 1.83 and FCF/share EUR 8.28 → −EUR 2.19, both n.m. against the 3% floor. BV/share compounded 4.3%, but only through two equity injections. Leverage is clean at 92.2%. **WEAK** — returns, not balance sheet.

## 4. Quality — future

Customer breadth **PASS**: note 4 states no customer reached 10% of sales. Supplier breadth **PASS, gap logged** — feedstocks are traded commodities, but Chempark site services are single-sourced and unquantified. Forces: customer power **STRONG** (tonne-priced grades, low switching costs); supplier power WEAK; substitutes WEAK; new entrants **STRONG** (Chinese capacity at world scale, utilisation at historic lows). Tally 2 strong — the stop signal. Moat: cost, from world-scale integrated plants, **eroding** as entrants match that scale on cheaper energy. Market growth MIXED. **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

**P17 hard strike:** FY2025 net income −EUR 644m and levered FCF −EUR 415m are both negative, and the highest-paid board member took EUR 4,441k including service cost — a third consecutive loss year with multi-million board pay. Absolute quantum (~US$5m vs the ~US$40m indexed trigger) and the chair's EUR 355k add nothing. **One P19 flag:** the small 2022–23 tranches price at 34–74x year-read EV/OI, but normalized reads of 8.5–15.3x clear the 20x line, and the 2017–18 EUR 1,456m programme rescues at 5–7x. **FAIL.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Structural global overcapacity in MDI, TDI and polycarbonate is the dominant fact; nothing in Covestro's control fixes it.
- European energy and CO₂ costs are a permanent disadvantage against Chinese and US Gulf Coast producers.
- EUR 5.5bn of capex went in FY2019–FY2025 against cumulative operating income of EUR 4.0bn — reinvestment below the cost of capital.
- XRG control removes public-register discipline; related-party purchases run under the IAS 24.25 disclosure exemption.
- The security is being extinguished at EUR 59.46 — below the EUR 62.00 offer and subscription price six weeks earlier.
