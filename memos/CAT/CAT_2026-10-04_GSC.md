# CAT — Caterpillar Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS · Past WEAK · Future ADEQUATE · Shareholder-friendliness FAIL · Cheapness EXPENSIVE → **AVOID** (Past WEAK on leverage; SF FAIL on an ADVERSE capital-allocation judgment).

Caterpillar is a business worth owning at the right price: the largest dealer-and-parts network in heavy equipment, a mining franchise with few rivals, and a Power & Energy segment now out-earning Construction. Two things stand between here and a BUY, and price is the smaller of them. First, total liabilities run 3.6× equity on the default consolidated basis; stripping Cat Financial's debt still leaves 2.1×, above P5, with no P6 relief condition met. Second, management is executing a $20bn authorisation at prices around 40× operating income with a stated policy of returning "substantially all" MP&E free cash flow regardless of price. The verdict flips to WATCH only if the captive-finance basis is settled in favour of an adjusted reading (IDEAS A2) and repurchases acquire a price discipline, or stop. CHEAP sits ≈ 90% below the current price.

## 2. Understanding

Caterpillar designs, manufactures and finances construction and mining machines, reciprocating engines, gas turbines and locomotives, sold through ~150 independent exclusive-territory dealers (its paying customers) to contractors, miners, oil-and-gas, power and marine operators in 190 countries, with a captive finance arm (Cat Financial, 42% of assets) funding dealer and end-user purchases; a 100-year-old, Texas-headquartered, $67.6bn-revenue cyclical incumbent. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **11.7%** (end-of-period, with cash) and FCFROCE **8.0%** over FY2016–FY2025 — the finance book, kept in capital employed on the group basis, dilutes a machinery business that earns ≈ 27% on its own capital. Returns are cyclical (ROCE 1.7% FY2016, 22% FY2023), not in sustained decline. Per-share compounding is strong: ΔOI/sh +14.1%/yr, ΔFCF/sh +10.7%/yr, with the diluted count down 21.5%. Leverage decides: **L/E 362%** consolidated, 244% on the MP&E-only statements, 211% excluding Cat Financial debt — every reading above P5, and P6 is unavailable because the default-basis returns are not great and the equity shrinkage came from buybacks at 13–25× FCF, not from under-priced stock. **WEAK** on the P4 leverage limb; ADEQUATE on every other metric.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASS on both sides: no customer reaches the 10% disclosure threshold (Item 101 names none; the largest dealer is a fraction of that), and inputs are steel, castings and multi-sourced components. Forces: customer power WEAK (fragmented end users, dealer-service dependence, fleet switching costs); supplier power WEAK; substitutes WEAK (mechanised earthmoving and prime power have none; electrification is a product transition CAT leads in mining); new entrants **STRONG** — Chinese OEMs price 20–40% below incumbents and are taking emerging-market construction share (Komatsu precedent, same call). Tally 1 strong, contained. Moat: **switching costs** via the dealer parts-and-service network and installed-base annuity — stable. Market growth MIXED: construction cyclical, mining capex and data-centre/prime power rising. **ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Compensation: executive chairman Umpleby's $22.2m (FY2025) is 0.30% of the lower leg against the 5% ceiling, both reads pass, below the ≈ $40m absolute trigger; directors ≈ $330–400k vs stops of $672k/$1.02m. Insider ownership 0.3%; RPT immaterial (two relatives' employment). **Capital allocation ADVERSE:** H1 2026 repurchases of $6.5bn at $706–880/share read 33–55× on all four yardsticks, the stock remains at 39× EV/OI, $8.4bn of authorisation is live, and the published criterion is cash-return volume, not price. The decade pattern — zero buybacks in the FY2016–17 trough, record $7.7bn at the FY2024 peak — confirms no price discipline. **FAIL.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- A construction or mining down-cycle on the FY2016 pattern cuts operating income by two thirds and exposes the finance book's credit quality.
- Cat Financial's $34bn of debt is self-funding only while residual values and dealer credit hold; a residual-value shock lands on the consolidated equity.
- Chinese OEM share gains in emerging markets compress the installed base that feeds the parts annuity.
- Repurchases at 40× operating income transfer value from continuing holders to sellers every quarter the programme runs.
- Tariff and trade-policy cost ($1.5–1.8bn guided for 2026 in the 10-Q) is a recurring margin risk outside management's control.
