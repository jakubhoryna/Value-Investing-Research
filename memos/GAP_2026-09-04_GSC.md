## 1. Verdict

Stack it on the $417m tariff credit already known and half-year operating income of $1,121m becomes **≈$391m underlying, against $552m a year earlier — down 29%** on flat sales. The funding, however, reads worse than v2 could see: strip the settlement receipt out of operating cash flow and half-year free cash flow is roughly **negative $52m**, against $726m returned. ## 2. Understanding

Unchanged. A specialty-apparel company designing and marketing casual clothing, accessories and personal-care goods under four brands — Old Navy (>50% of sales, value), Gap, Banana Republic, Athleta — sold to individual retail consumers through company-operated and franchised stores plus e-commerce, sourcing finished product from roughly 200 independent third-party factories in about 30 countries, San Francisco-headquartered and concentrated in North America. **PASS.**
