Continental is mid-metamorphosis: Vitesco spun 2021, Aumovio (Automotive) spun Sep 2025, OESL sold Feb 2026, and the ContiTech sale to Lone Star (€4.0bn + up to €250m earn-out) signed 4 Jul 2026 will leave a pure-play premium tire maker. The tire business inside is genuinely good — segment ROCE 23.3% (2025) / 24.9% (2024), 13.6% adjusted margin, 76% replacement mix — but the framework buys entities, not segments, and the entity's record is WEAK: worst-of-four normalized ROCE 6.6% on 2021–2025, FY2025 as-reported continuing EBIT of €272m (2.0–2.6% ROCE) after €1.7bn of spin/disposal special effects, and post-spin L/E of 347% above P5 without P6 cover. At ~10× EV/adjusted-EBIT and 15.3× FCF the price prices the good segment, not the reported entity. The flip path is not price but record: the first pure-play fiscal year (FY2026, restated) could establish a clean tires-only entity history — a full re-analysis is queued for the FY2026 AR, with the thin-history cap (no STRONG) governing until the record lengthens.

## 2. Understanding

Continental AG is a 150-year-old, Hanover-headquartered, Schaeffler/IHO-controlled (dependency-report regime) DAX manufacturer completing its dismantling from auto-parts conglomerate to pure-play tire maker: it sells differentiated premium passenger, truck and specialty tires (70% of continuing sales; 76% of tire sales into the fragmented replacement channel via ~6,000-outlet dealer/franchise networks, 24% to global vehicle OEMs) plus — until the signed €4.0bn ContiTech sale closes in 2026 — industrial rubber products (hoses, belts, surfaces), produced at scale (~80% of tire capacity in megafactories) in Europe, the USA and China and sold worldwide. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

WEAK. Worst-of-four normalized averages, FY2021–25 (flattering S&P-standardized OI; as-reported would read lower): ROCE 6.6% (beginning, with-cash — worst) to 8.0%; FCFROCE 3.8–4.8% — all below the 10% P3 floor. Company-reported ROCE (own, more generous definition): 10.0/3.7/8.9/11.4/2.6% — below cost of capital (~10%) in four of five years; CVC negative three of the last four. FY2025 as-reported continuing EBIT €272m → framework ROCE 2.0–2.3% (company 2.6%). Latest L/E 347% (> P5 200%, P6 conditions absent — leverage strike). Per-share growth n.m. across three perimeter breaks. The 23–25%-ROCE Tires segment has no entity-level record; even a charitable continuing-perimeter read is thin-history capped. **Verdict: WEAK** — sub-hurdle returns on every computable read plus a leverage breach.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASS both sides: 53% of continuing sales is replacement business sold to dealers/end users (fragmented); OE spread across all major OEMs, none plausibly >10% of RemainCo revenue; inputs are traded commodities (natural/synthetic rubber, carbon black, steel cord — 62% of production-material spend) from a global supplier base. Forces: customer power WEAK; supplier power WEAK; substitutes WEAK (no substitute for tires); **new entrants STRONG** — Asian budget capacity is visibly entering (EU replacement growth "driven primarily by imports"), contained by Continental's premium/UHP position (62% UHP share of core brand, top test rankings). Tally 1 strong, contained → judgment zone. Moat: **Brand** (test-ranking-fed, OE-homologation-reinforced) — stable. Market growth: YES, slight (+1% replacement; larger-tire mix). **Verdict: ADEQUATE.**
