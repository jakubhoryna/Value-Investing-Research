# RUSTA.ST — Rusta AB: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | **WEAK** | ADEQUATE | **FAIL** (P19 sterilisation) | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** |

Rusta is a well-run Nordic discount retailer with a genuine cost-side franchise — direct Asian sourcing, 240+ leased stores, countercyclical trade-down traffic, and improving margins under a fresh growth program. It fails two gates independently. Past is WEAK on leverage: total liabilities have run 344–595% of equity across the whole public record (P5 cap 200%), driven by SEK 5.4bn of capitalised store leases against a SEK 2.2bn equity base — the company is bank-debt-free and net cash ex-leases, but the framework prices lease commitments as debt and the coverage (EBIT ≈ 3.8× finance expense) is genuinely modest. SF fails on a P19 hard strike: the FY23/24 and FY24/25 repurchases exist, by the company's own stated goal, "to hedge the delivery of performance and matching shares" under LTIP 2023/2024 — textbook dilution-sterilisation, which strikes at any price (MYCR precedent). At SEK 22–24m per tranche it is also the second de-minimis sterilisation case in the register, strengthening the standing MODEL-queue item. Price is EXPENSIVE regardless (EV/OI 19.3×). A three-year IPO history additionally caps everything. Flip path: equity compounding toward L/E < 200% is a ~2029+ story at current retention; the sterilisation strike is structural while the LTIP-hedge mechanism persists.

## 2. Understanding

Rusta develops, directly sources (offices in China, India, Vietnam, Türkiye) and retails a rotating low-price assortment of home, garden and leisure products to Nordic and German consumer households through 240+ wholly-owned stores in leased premises plus online in Sweden and Finland; founded 1986 in Gävle, founder-family-controlled (Aforber Invest / Öngal i Uppsala), HQ Upplands Väsby, IPO'd on Nasdaq Stockholm October 2023, ~SEK 12.6bn sales, new CEO from June 2026. PASS.

## 3. Quality — past record

Only five fiscal years exist (April-ending; Apr-2021 opening balance unavailable) — STRONG is structurally unavailable. On the available window, worst normalized ROCE 10.6% and FCFROCE 11.9% (end-of-period, with-cash) — clean P3 levels, improving each year since the FY22/23 trough. ΔOI/sh +3.5%/yr; ΔBV/sh +13.9%. But L/E: 549% → 595% → 471% → 437% → 344% — a P5 breach (200%) in every year of the record, without the P6 conditions; EKTA (280%) and MICC precedents govern. **WEAK** — the returns are adequate; the balance-sheet shape and three-year public history are not.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: customers PASS (millions of consumers; 6.4m Club Rusta members); suppliers PASS (direct sourcing from "a large number of leading suppliers", four sourcing offices, no concentration disclosed). Forces: customer power WEAK; supplier power WEAK; substitutes WEAK (online low-price is same-function rivalry; trade-down demand is countercyclical) with an e-commerce caveat; new entrants **STRONG** — Normal A/S's rapid Nordic rollout is live proof that leased-store discount retail has low entry barriers. Tally 1 strong, contained. Moat: cost — scale sourcing plus network density; stable, margins rising (gross 43.5%, EBITA 7.6%). Market growth YES (low-price share gains; 50–80 store pipeline). **ADEQUATE**.

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

**FAIL — P19 hard strike: dilution-sterilisation.** FY23/24 (SEK 22m) and FY24/25 (SEK 24m, 310,000 shares) repurchases were executed, per the directors' report verbatim, to "hedge the delivery of performance and matching shares to participants and hedge social security costs" under LTIP 2023/2024 — treasury holding (577,333) sized to outstanding share rights (577,772). Sterilisation strikes at any price; the tranches' size (~0.2% of shares) makes this the second de-minimis exhibit after MYCR — logged to the MODEL queue, applied as written. RPT minor and disclosed (founder consultancy SEK 0.1m; family-member employment; market terms). Dividends rising four consecutive years (1.00 → 1.80 proposed).

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Fixed-lease leverage: SEK 5.4bn of lease debt against SEK 2.2bn equity leaves little equity cushion in a sustained downturn despite zero bank debt.
- Normal A/S and online low-price (Temu et al.) compress the discount niche from both directions.
- CEO transition (Wigzell from June 2026) during the largest expansion program in company history (50–80 stores, automation ramp).
- Three-year public record: pre-IPO economics rest on prospectus-era disclosure not independently rebuilt here.
- Germany remains sub-scale within "Other markets" — expansion capital at uncertain returns.
