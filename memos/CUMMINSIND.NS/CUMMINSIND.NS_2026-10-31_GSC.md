# CUMMINSIND.NS — Cummins India Limited: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS, Past STRONG, Future WEAK, shareholder-friendliness FAIL, price EXPENSIVE: **AVOID**, on two gates (Future and SF).

Cummins India is the Indian arm of Cummins Inc., which owns 51%. It makes diesel and gas engines, generator sets and their parts, and services the installed base. Its record is strong: worst-of-four ROCE of 21.5% over ten years, no debt, and operating income per share up about 14% a year. Two things fail. The parent sits on both sides of the business: Cummins group companies buy 12% of revenue, supply the engines and know-how through sister companies, and in FY2026 charged INR 2,130m for "support services" and INR 779m in royalty. The support-services fee to the controller is a glaring related-party payment, a strike. And three outside customers took 31% of FY2026 revenue. The trigger is INR 445.0 against INR 4,452.60.

## 2. Understanding

Cummins India Limited, founded in 1962 and based in Pune, a listed subsidiary of Cummins Inc. (USA, 51%), is a group that designs, makes in its own plants in Maharashtra (Pune, Phaltan) and sells diesel and gas engines, generator-set engines and power-generation systems, and the parts, service and upgrade kits for them, to generator-set assemblers, data-centre, commercial-property, infrastructure and industrial buyers, equipment makers in construction, mining, rail, marine, defence and compressors, and Cummins companies abroad, mainly in India (83% of revenue) with exports to Asia, Africa, Europe and the Americas; through Valvoline Cummins, a 50:50 joint venture with Valvoline, it also sells lubricants in India. **PASS.**

## 4. Quality — future

Engines (90% of the weight): three outside customers took INR 37,347m, 31% of FY2026 revenue, and Cummins group companies another 12%. No strong moat carries them: the filings show no pricing power over these buyers, and the parent sets the terms of its own purchases. Customer power is STRONG. Supplier power is STRONG: Tata Cummins and Cummins Technologies India each supply more than 10% of costs, the technology is licensed from the parent, and the parent charges royalty and support fees that doubled in FY2026. Substitutes and entrants are WEAK. The moat is switching costs in the installed base and service network: stable. Market growth YES. Lubes: the filer is silent on its customers. Group **WEAK** (S 1.0; unit customer conc.).

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Payments to the parent (support services, royalty, the know-how and other assets bought from it for INR 1,355m in FY2024–FY2025) are set by the controller and rose sharply in FY2026.
- Three outside buyers took 31% of FY2026 revenue (two took 21% in FY2024 and FY2025), with data-centre demand behind the recent growth.
- The engines come from sister companies (Tata Cummins, Cummins Technologies India), on terms the minority cannot check.
- The filer reports intensifying competition and pricing pressure in power generation, and rail electrification shrinks a diesel market.
- The Managing Director resigned with effect from 31 August 2026 (Q1 FY2027 results, secondary report).

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

SF would pass only if the support-services and royalty payments to Cummins Inc. were described and shown to buy something management is not already paid to do, at a checkable price. Future needs the customer concentration to fall below 10% per customer or a strong moat with pricing power over those customers to be evidenced. Next review: FY2027 results, May 2027.
