## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past ADEQUATE (21.7% ROCE, capped by a sustained decline); Future ADEQUATE; Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (buybacks); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID** (SF).

Builders FirstSource is the largest US supplier of structural building products to professional homebuilders, turning commodity lumber into trusses, wall panels and framing packages. The ten-year record is strong (worst-of-four ROCE 21.7%, FCFROCE 17.4%), but operating income has fallen four years running, from $3.77bn in 2022 to $436m on the latest twelve months, as housing starts weakened. Capital allocation is what fails: the company bought back 33.3m shares since 2023, the last 3.3m in early 2026 at $92.25, 35× trailing operating income on enterprise value, and a fresh $500m authorisation with no price criterion followed in April. At $56.85, debt of $5.28bn exceeds seven times trailing operating income, so no trigger exists below $755m of operating income. A flip needs repurchases held to worth, acquisition earnings disclosed and the decline arrested.

## 2. Understanding

Builders FirstSource, Inc. is an Irving, Texas-based, NYSE-listed supplier, formed by the 2015 ProBuild acquisition and the 2021 all-stock BMC merger, that manufactures roof and floor trusses, wall panels, Ready-Frame pre-cut framing packages, pre-hung doors, vinyl windows, millwork and factory-built homes (value-added products, 48% of 2025 sales of $15.19bn), distributes dimensional lumber, plywood and OSB (26%) and sells siding, roofing, insulation, cabinets and other specialty products with installation and turnkey framing services and Paradigm design software (27%), from about 585 locations in 43 states, to production and custom homebuilders, chiefly in new single-family construction, plus multi-family builders and repair-and-remodel contractors, its largest customer 4% and its top ten 14% of sales.

One reportable segment (three geographic operating divisions aggregated); no listed subsidiary or holding. **PASS.** Lens STD.

## 3. Quality — past record

STD lens, ten fiscal years, leases capitalised in FY2017–18. Worst-of-four ROCE is 21.7% over ten years and 23.8% over five; FCFROCE is 17.4% and 19.9%, on maintenance-capex FCF (constructed; all-capex 17.4% and 19.9%). Per-share growth clears the floors: ΔOI/sh 23.1% and ΔFCF/sh 19.4% on the harsher reads. Liabilities are 158% of equity. But ROCE has fallen four years in a row (43.4% in 2022 to 8.8% in 2025 on beginning capital), and FCFROCE with it: a sustained decline on the first limb. Because the level, growth and leverage conditions hold on both windows, the decline caps Past at **ADEQUATE** (`21.7% ROCE`).

## 4. Quality — future

No customer exceeds 4% of sales and the top ten are 14%; the largest supplier is 8% of materials purchases. No concentration flag. Three forces are weak: many builders with none dominant, thousands of suppliers of commodity inputs, and no substitute for framing and finishing a house. New entrants are strong (the filer itself cites a low barrier to entry), contained because local lumberyards cannot reach its scale in purchasing and component manufacturing, and returns held at 21.7% across the window. The moat is cost: purchasing scale and a manufacturing network for value-added components, stable (gross margin 25.1% in 2016, 30.4% in 2025). Market growth is MIXED: core organic sales fell 10.3% in 2025 and 7.5% in the first half of 2026. **ADEQUATE.**
