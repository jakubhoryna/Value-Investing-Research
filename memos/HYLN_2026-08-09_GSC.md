# HYLN — Hyliion Holdings Corp.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Past **WEAK** (no positive operating income or free cash flow; returns on capital −29%, cash returns −32%) · Future **WEAK** (no moat exists yet — a product that has not been sold commercially cannot have one) · SF **FAIL** (P17: both legs negative, and $2.66m of chief-executive pay against them) · EXPENSIVE (no denominator on either primary; the equity is 4.5× the cash) → **AVOID (four gates)**.

Hyliion is a venture investment wearing a listing. It abandoned its electrified-powertrain business in 2023, pivoted to the KARNO linear generator, and now has five units running at its own facility, ten early-adopter deliveries planned, and $3.5m of research-service revenue against a $65.6m operating loss. It is honestly financed — $152m of cash and investments, no debt, about three years of runway — and the technology may prove out. None of that is what this framework measures: there is no record of earning anything on capital, no demonstrated moat, and the market already pays $690m, or 4.5× the cash, for the option. The register's job is to file it and date when it becomes analysable.

## 2. Understanding
Hyliion is commercialising the KARNO generator — a fuel-agnostic linear generator built on additive-manufactured heat-exchanger cores, aimed at distributed power for data centres, industrial sites and military applications — after exiting its hybrid and electric truck-powertrain business in 2023; Cedar Park, Texas-HQ'd, founder-led, from a 2020 SPAC merger. **PASS** — product and market are legible; whether it works at scale is an evidence problem, not a comprehension one.

## 3. Quality — past record
There is no record to assess. Operating losses were **$64.3m (2024) and $65.6m (2025)** on revenue of $1.5m and $3.5m, all research services rather than product; free cash flow **−$73.2m and −$70.2m**. On capital employed of roughly $197–251m returns are **−29%**, cash returns **−32%**. Total assets fell $263m → $204m funding the burn and equity $244m → $192m. No debt (L/E 6.0%). The one favourable number is the cash: $152.3m against a guided 2026 use of about $50m. **WEAK** — by absence, not decline.

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth is untestable: at $3.5m of revenue there is no customer base to be broad, and the pipeline — roughly 500 cores under **non-binding** letters of intent, $40–50m of hoped-for military contracts — is intention, not concentration data. Forces: customer power WATCH (early adopters of an unproven generator hold the leverage) · supplier power WATCH (additive capacity is the constraint capex is chasing) · **substitutes STRONG** — reciprocating gensets, turbines, fuel cells and grid supply do this job today at known cost with service networks · new entrants WATCH (Caterpillar, Cummins, Generac and the turbine makers can enter once the category is proven). Moat: **none identified** — patents and a prototype are an intellectual-property position, not a moat, and prospective ones get no credit. **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness
Comp: **hard strike.** Both legs are negative in FY2025 — net income −$57.2m, free cash flow −$70.2m — and against no owner earnings at all, the most recent disclosed chief-executive total of **$2.656m** is categorically more than token; the trailing read is identical. RPT: none surfaced. Capital allocation: **SOUND-leaning** — the money goes to the stated programme, there is no debt, no dividends, no repurchases, and abandoning a failing powertrain business in 2023 rather than funding it further was the right call taken promptly. The gate fails on the strike alone. **FAIL.**

## 6. Price
Neither primary metric has a denominator: operating income and free cash flow are both negative in every year built, so no trigger band exists and none can be constructed — and unlike the leveraged cases in this run, no level of operating income "restores" a bound, because there is no debt to be covered; what is missing is the numerator itself. What can be said: the equity is **$690m against $152.3m of cash and investments — 4.5×** — and **3.6× book**. The market is paying roughly $540m for a pre-commercial product line. **EXPENSIVE.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers (context for the AVOID)
The bull case is specific and dated: certification mid-2026, initial customer and military deliveries during 2026, the full 200 kW rating by late 2026, and a data-centre power market desperate for electrons. If KARNO works and the letters convert, this becomes a growth company and the framework will pick it up on the record it then has. The bear case is balance-sheet arithmetic: two more years like the last two consume the cash.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review
The name becomes analysable when there is a positive operating-income year to divide by. Before that, the sequence to watch is: certification, first paid deliveries, conversion of non-binding letters into orders, and unit economics disclosed at any scale. Next review: **FY2026 results (~Feb 2027)** — revenue against the ~$10m guide and cash use against the ~$50m plan. Alert: n/a — no band exists to alert against.
