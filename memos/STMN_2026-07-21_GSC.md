Straumann is the rare STRONG/STRONG name — worst-of-four ROCE 19.0%, a stable ingrainedness moat in a growing under-penetrated market — but the verdict is AVOID on shareholder-friendliness. Two negatives decide it: treasury-share repurchases whose sole function is delivering SBC (share capital frozen; every plan share bought in the market at 23–65× the framework's primaries — the P19 dilution-sterilisation strike, SAP/ENR precedent), and the DrSmile/PlusDental direct-to-consumer aligner adventure, ~CHF 0.3bn invested in pre-profit targets, ~CHF 272m written off, exited 2024. Even under the lenient reading (plan-servicing = flag, not strike), the flag pair fails SF. Price is nowhere near a decision anyway: 28.8–29.8× EV/OI against a ≤7× threshold. A flip requires the FY2026+ record to show repurchase discipline referenced to worth and a clean allocation slate; the quality gates need no help.

## 2. Understanding

Straumann sells premium and challenger-brand dental implant systems, clear aligners, biomaterials and digital dentistry equipment (differentiated, clinically evidenced products) to dentists, dental labs and dental service organizations who pay for them — not the patients who wear them — in the global tooth-replacement and orthodontics industry; a vertically integrated Swiss manufacturer-distributor headquartered in Basel with plants in Switzerland, Brazil, the US, Germany and China and revenue across ~100 countries; a prominent, 70-year-old, founder-anchored market leader (~30% implant share) with CHF 2.6bn revenue. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized averages (end-of-period, with-cash): **ROCE 19.0%**, **FCFROCE 11.5%** — both clear P2 (best variants 27.0%/16.4%). Per-share growth 2016→2025 geometric: ΔOI/sh +10.0%, ΔFCF/sh +6.3%, ΔBV/sh +14.4%, ΔTBV/sh +16.6% — the latter two despite a rising dividend. 2020 is a single COVID trough (ROCE 7.7%), not a sustained decline; 2025 dips on a CHF 30.8m restructuring impairment and CHF 100m+ FX headwind. L/E 73.8%. FY2016 leases capitalized per P18 (marginal 10.3% breach; Appendix C). **STRONG** — high returns, double-digit compounding, conservative balance sheet.

## 4. Quality — future

Forces: customer power WEAK (millions of fragmented clinicians; China VBP state procurement noted, contained at ~15% of revenue); supplier power WEAK; substitutes WEAK (bridges/dentures inferior, doing-without declining with penetration); new entrants WEAK (regulatory approvals, clinical evidence base, clinician training). Tally: 0 strong. Moat: **ingrainedness** — clinicians train on Straumann workflows through the ITI education network and stay — stable.
