# CALX — Calix, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past **WEAK** (w-o-4 ROCE 1.94%); Future ADEQUATE; SF **FAIL twice** (P17 hard strike 12×/37×, and P19 **ADVERSE** — the run's second, the register's third); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID (three gates)**.

Calix has a genuinely improving franchise — a sticky cloud-plus-systems platform for small broadband providers, with TTM operating income at a record — wrapped in financial conduct the framework scores twice. Five years of operating income sum to $130m against a $2.35bn price (EV/OI 38× even on the record TTM), the CEO took $10.8m from a company whose smoothed three-year net income averages $5.8m, and the buyback is running at $260m a year — three times free cash flow — at roughly 27–35× cash-flow multiples with no published price discipline. Owner earnings this small cannot fund pay and repurchases this large; that mismatch is the memo.

## 2. Understanding

Calix sells broadband access platforms — systems (access equipment for fiber networks) and a growing cloud/managed-services layer (marketing, operations and subscriber-experience software) — to hundreds of small and regional broadband service providers, mostly North American telcos, co-ops and municipal fiber builders, who pay; a Delaware corporation, IPO 2010, HQ San Jose, fab-less with contract manufacturing; a mid-cap niche leader positioned as the platform for BEAD-funded rural fiber. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **1.94%** (beg-wc; end variants 3.4%/5.3%) — Σ window OI is $130m on ~$760m of average capital, with FY2024 a loss year (−$43.0m, inventory correction). FCFROCE worst ≈ **1.9%** (beg-basis; end-wc 7.0%) — and FY2025's $115.5m FCF carries an $87.9m SBC add-back (76%). FY2021's $238m net income was a deferred-tax valuation-allowance release, not earnings (logged; OI line used). Growth: ΔOI/sh n.m. through the FY2024 loss; revenue +10%/yr. Leverage: none (debt $13m; L/E 23%). **WEAK — level, with the FY2024 hole in the middle.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: hundreds of BSP customers, none disclosed above 10% in recent filings (sentence not retrieved — flagged); suppliers: fab-less with semiconductor dependence — the 2021–22 shortage demonstrated the chokepoint. Forces: customer power WEAK (fragmented small ISPs — the business model's point); supplier power **STRONG** (semiconductor single-sourcing, demonstrated); substitutes WEAK (fiber access gear has no doing-without; rivals are competitors, not substitutes); entrants WEAK (platform/cloud attach makes displacement slow — Nokia/Adtran compete on boxes, not the stack). Tally **1 STRONG, contained**. Moat: **Switching costs** — the cloud layer converts equipment sales into an operating system for small ISPs; **strengthening** (cloud/services mix rising). Market growth **YES** (BEAD/rural fiber funding). **ADEQUATE.**
