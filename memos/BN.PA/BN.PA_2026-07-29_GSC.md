# BN.PA — Danone SA: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS · Past WEAK · Future WEAK · SF FAIL · Cheapness EXPENSIVE → **AVOID**, disqualified independently at three gates.

The ownable core is Specialized Nutrition — infant formula and medical nutrition at a 21.7% recurring margin, 55% of FY2025 recurring OI on 34% of sales, protected by regulation and parental switching costs. But a shareholder buys the whole group, and the whole group has earned 7.5–9.1% normalized ROCE across a decade on a capital base permanently bloated by WhiteWave goodwill, while two-thirds of sales (dairy/plant-based and water) face concentrated retailers holding a credible private-label alternative. The SF record seals it: both repurchase years in the window executed far above the framework's lines, the 2025 tranche bought stock above the flag band to feed employee share plans, and the defining capital allocation of the era — WhiteWave at ~37× pre-deal operating income — is the textbook overpriced acquisition. A verdict flip requires a multi-year return re-basing (structurally hard with €17.3bn of goodwill in CE) plus a demonstrated repurchase discipline; neither is in evidence.

## 2. Understanding

Danone is a 107-year-old, Paris-headquartered and Euronext-listed packaged-food multinational that manufactures and sells branded fresh dairy and plant-based products (48% of FY2025 sales), infant-formula and medical nutrition (34%), and bottled water (18%) — differentiated, repeat-purchase consumer goods sold mainly to mass-market retailers (largest customer 6.4% of sales) and healthcare/pharmacy channels across ~120 countries, as a vertically integrated producer running its own factories, farms-to-plant milk sourcing and water springs, mid-way through its "Renew" reset after a decade of portfolio churn. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **7.5%** (range 7.5–9.1% — every variant below the 10% ADEQUATE floor); worst-of-four FCFROCE **6.1%** (6.1–7.5%). The shape is mediocrity, not accident: yearly ROCE_wc never exceeded ~11% (FY2016) and sat at 5.5–8% through FY2018–FY2023; the FY2024 rebound (9.1%) partially retraced in FY2025 (8.1%) on a €725m impairment year. OI/share geometric mean **−0.4%** over nine years — below the 3% nominal floor and negative; FCF/share +4.7% is genuine but sits on returns too thin to matter. L/E 166%, within the 200% flag. Verdict **WEAK** — fails P3 outright on level.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASS on both sides: largest customer 6.4% of consolidated sales (disclosure present); supply is atomised (thousands of milk producers, commodity packaging, owned springs). Forces, weighted across the three units: customer bargaining power **STRONG** (concentrated grocery retail, zero shelf-switching cost, private label credible in dairy and water — the EDP+Waters two-thirds of sales; weak only in Specialized Nutrition); supplier power WEAK; substitutes **STRONG** (private label as direct substitute in the same two units); new entrants WEAK (scale, brand, infant-nutrition regulation). Tally 2 strong → stop signal. Moat: brand — durable in Specialized Nutrition (Aptamil, Nutricia), eroding in dairy/waters. Market growth MIXED. Verdict **WEAK**.

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Compensation clean: CEO FY2025 total €6.3m vs a €91m 5%-of-lower(FCF, NI) bar — passes both P17 reads by ~14×; chair €650k fixed, directors €0.9m in aggregate; RPT immaterial and disclosed. Buybacks fail: the window's only two repurchase years both flagged on both reads — FY2021 (€801m at €60.87: 22.2×/18.2× EV/OI, 16.2×/17.0× MCap/FCF) and FY2025 (€486m at €74.46: 19.3×/20.3×, 17.6×/17.5×) — P19 repetition **hard strike**; FY2025 was moreover allocated wholly to employee share plans with the year breaching the flag lines — sterilisation **hard strike**. Acquisition audit: WhiteWave at $12.5bn EV = **37.5×** pre-deal normalized OI (31.1× on the best pre-deal year) — third hard strike. Redemption limbs all fail. Verdict **FAIL**.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- The AVOID rests on a decade average; a sustained Renew-era re-basing of returns (FY2024–25 ROCE_xc touched ~9.5–11%) could lift the worst-of-four above 10% as weak years roll out — earliest plausible ~FY2028.
- Specialized Nutrition is the hidden asset: any structural separation of EDP would change the analysable entity entirely and void this memo.
- China/North Asia (29% recurring margin, €4.0bn sales) concentrates the group's best economics in one regulatory-political geography.
- FY2025's €725m other-operating-expense line continues a ten-year pattern (impairments/restructuring in 8 of 10 years) — "recurring one-offs" are the business, and as-reported OI is the honest base.
- A repeat large acquisition funded by the €6.6bn cash pile would re-test P19 immediately.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

Flip to WATCH requires all three failed gates to move: worst-of-four ROCE ≥ 10% (window arithmetic makes FY2028 the earliest realistic date), a forces re-read showing private-label pressure contained (evidence: EDP margin durably ≥ 12% with volume growth), and P19 rehabilitation (the FY2021 strike-pair ages out after FY2031, or the v3.5.0 redemption limbs are satisfied — none is today). Next review: FY2026 results (~Feb 2027) only if requested; no standing re-test — AVOID rows save time.
