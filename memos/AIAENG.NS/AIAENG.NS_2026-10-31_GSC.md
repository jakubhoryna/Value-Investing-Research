# AIAENG.NS — AIA Engineering Limited: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS, Past STRONG, Future ADEQUATE, shareholder-friendliness FAIL, price EXPENSIVE: **AVOID**, on one gate (SF).

AIA Engineering makes high-chromium grinding media and mill liners that wear out inside the grinding mills of cement plants, mines and coal power stations, and it has earned about 15% on all its capital and far more on its operating capital for ten years while building a cash and bond portfolio of almost INR 39bn. Two flags fail it together. In September 2024 it bought back 1.07% of its shares at INR 5,000, about 100 times the free cash flow of the three years before. And it sells INR 2.9bn a year to AB Tradelink, a company the promoter's family controls, with nothing disclosed beyond the amount, the balance and a statement that the terms are arm's length. The trigger is INR 387.8 against INR 3,595.90.

## 2. Understanding

AIA Engineering, an Ahmedabad company founded in 1991 by its managing director Bhadresh Shah, who owns 58.5%, is a foundry group that makes high-chromium wear parts for grinding mills (grinding balls and other media, mill liners, diaphragms and discharge systems), about 436,000 tonnes a year of capacity in Gujarat, and sells them, mostly through its Dubai distribution subsidiaries, to mining companies (gold, copper and iron ore), cement producers and thermal power utilities in India (36% of revenue) and around the world (64%), where it is converting mines from forged-steel to high-chromium media. One reportable segment. **PASS.**

## 4. Quality — future

Forged-steel media still hold 70–75% of the mining market the company targets, and the filer itself names local competitors, anti-dumping duties and "general resistance to change". It is contained: the substitute slows conversion but does not reach the company's cost and metallurgy advantage, and returns held across the window. Customer power and new entrants are WEAK. The moat is cost, stable. Market growth is MIXED: sales volume has been flat near 255,000 tonnes for three years. **ADEQUATE.**
