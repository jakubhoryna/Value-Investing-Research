Nabtesco owns real niche franchises — the RV reducer in robot joints (company-estimated ~60% global share), rail brakes and doors, marine engine controls — but the group has not turned them into returns. End-of-period ROCE fell from 13.5% (FY2016) to 4.1% (FY2024), OI per share shrank over nine years, and three straight years of negative or near-zero FCF (FY2022–24) came with a capex surge. The verdict flips only after several years of restored returns.

## 2. Understanding
Nabtesco makes precision motion-control components and systems — RV reduction gears for industrial-robot joints and machine tools, railway brake and door systems, aircraft flight-control actuation, marine main-engine remote controls, and automatic doors for buildings and platforms, plus packaging machinery — sold mainly to robot makers, rolling-stock builders and railways, airframers, shipyards and building owners, from a Tokyo-headquartered IFRS group with plants in Japan, China and Europe; a 2003 merger of Nabco and Teijin Seiki, now pruning (HDS stake sold 2021–22, 70% of hydraulics sold to Comer Industries effective 1 Jan 2026). **PASS.**
