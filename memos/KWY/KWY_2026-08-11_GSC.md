Kingsway is the self-styled "only publicly-traded US search fund": a micro-cap holdco compounding small services acquisitions on top of a legacy extended-warranty book and a large NOL shield. The story is momentum — KSX adjusted EBITDA tripled in eight quarters, Q2 2026 swung to a small net profit — but the framework prices records, not stories. ## 2. Understanding

Kingsway is a Chicago-headquartered NYSE micro-cap holding company — a former Canadian trucking insurer transformed through 2018–2023 divestitures — that (a) sells and administers extended-warranty/vehicle-service agreements to US consumers through credit unions and dealers (IWS, Penn/PWI; Trinity sold May 2026), and (b) through its Kingsway Search Xcelerator acquires and operates roughly a dozen small US B2B services businesses (outsourced accounting/CFO, HR consulting, staffing, cardiac telemetry, shared-ownership software, IT managed services) run by hired operators-in-residence, earning fees and service revenue. **PASS.**
