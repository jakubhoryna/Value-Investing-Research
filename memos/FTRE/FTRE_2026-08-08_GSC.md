The 2023 Labcorp spin has consumed itself: $797.9m of goodwill impaired in FY2025 on top of FY2024 charges, equity down from $1.7bn to $526m in nine quarters, and divestitures funding debt paydown. Everything here waits on the first clean operating year.

## 2. Understanding

Fortrea is a contract research organisation (CRO) running Phase I–IV clinical trials and (post-divestiture) enabling services for pharmaceutical and biotech sponsors — one sponsor 17.2% of revenues — on a ~$7.7bn backlog, globally delivered; a Delaware-incorporated June-2023 Labcorp spin-off, on its second CEO (Thakral, ex-EVERSANA, from June 2025), mid-restructuring. **PASS.**

Moat: switching costs within a running trial are high but expire trial-by-trial — narrow, **eroding** at the relationship level (the FY24–25 revenue decline is the receipt). Market growth MIXED (biotech funding cyclical; pharma R&D outsourcing mature). **WEAK.**
