## 2. Understanding
SEMITEC makes temperature sensors — bulk NTC thermistor elements and, for the higher-value fifth of sales, thin-film thermistors — plus infrared sensors and surge absorbers, sold as custom-specified components to automotive (39%: HEV/EV battery and motor), appliance (16%), office-equipment (14%), medical-device (14%: glucose meters) and industrial makers, with elements made in Chiba and assembly in the Philippines, China, Vietnam and Korea; Tokyo-headquartered, founded 1958, 46% Ishizuka-family owned, 82% of sales outside Japan. **PASS**.
