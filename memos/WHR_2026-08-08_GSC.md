A 115-year-old appliance franchise in structural retreat: Europe sold, India sold down, revenue $22.0bn → $15.5bn in four years, and the balance sheet arriving where that road ends — dividend suspended after the Q1 2026 loss, and a February 2026 rescue financing of $475m common at $69 (the stock now $43) plus $525m of 8.50% mandatory convertible preferred. ## 2. Understanding

Whirlpool manufactures and sells major domestic appliances (refrigeration, laundry, cooking, dishwashing — brands Whirlpool, KitchenAid, Maytag, Consul, Brastemp) plus a global small-appliance line (KitchenAid), sold predominantly wholesale to retailers (Lowe's alone 15% of sales) and builders across an Americas-centred footprint (~90% of sales) after exiting Europe into a minority JV stake and reducing India to ~25%; a Michigan-headquartered manufacturer, NYSE-listed for a century, now mid-restructuring under leverage pressure. **PASS.**

L/E **489%** (FY25) — past the 200% flag, and the P6 let-out fails on every limb (metrics not great; borrowing funded dividends and working capital, not under-priced repurchases). **WEAK** on returns, trajectory, and leverage independently.

## 4. Quality — future

Supplier side passes. Forces: customer power **STRONG** (concentrated retail channel, price-led category); supplier power WEAK; substitutes WEAK (replacement-driven necessity category); new entrants **STRONG** — the Asian majors' 20-year share capture is the demonstrated case that barriers to success are low.
