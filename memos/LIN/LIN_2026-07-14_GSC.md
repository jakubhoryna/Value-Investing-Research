# LIN — Linde plc: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | **WEAK** | STRONG | **FAIL** | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** |

Linde is the highest-quality business the framework will reject this quarter — a wide-moat, oligopolistic industrial-gas compounder whose merged capital base and share price both defeat it. Two independent gates fail. **Past is WEAK**: worst-of-four normalized-average ROCE is ~8.1% (FY2019–25 combined), below the 10% ADEQUATE floor, because the 2018 Praxair/Linde AG merger loaded ~$40bn of goodwill + intangibles onto capital employed while ~$1.3bn/yr of purchase-price amortization depresses as-reported operating income. Strip the intangibles and operating ROCE is ~23% — but goodwill stays in for an acquisitive company, and the all-in return on the capital a buyer actually pays for is single-digit. This is the **IBM precedent** exactly (GW-laden CE → ROCE <10% → Past WEAK). **SF also FAILs** on a P19 hard strike: FY2025 buybacks of $4.6bn were executed at an implied ~27× EV/OI and ~42× MCap/FCF, far above the 20× line. Future is genuinely STRONG and comp is clean, but neither can rescue the verdict. Nothing flips this short of a multi-year re-rating of returns on the post-merger base **and** a collapse in price toward the low-$80s.

## 2. Understanding

Linde plc produces and distributes atmospheric gases (oxygen, nitrogen, argon) and process gases (hydrogen, CO₂, helium, electronic/specialty gases), and builds gas-processing plants, selling under long-term on-site/pipeline take-or-pay contracts plus merchant, bulk and packaged supply to chemicals, energy, healthcare, electronics, metals, manufacturing and food customers worldwide; it is the Ireland-incorporated, USD-reporting, NASDAQ/XETRA-listed global #1 formed by the Oct-2018 merger of Praxair (accounting acquirer) and Linde AG. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized-average ROCE ~8.1% and FCFROCE ~6.6% over the combined FY2019–25 window — both below the P3 ADEQUATE floor (ROCE ≥10%) and the P2 STRONG floors. The cause is structural, not operational: merger goodwill ($27.9bn) + intangibles ($11.9bn) inflate CE, and PPA amortization suppresses reported OI; ex-intangibles operating ROCE ≈ 23%. Per-share growth is strong — OI/sh +23%/yr, FCF/sh +16%/yr (flattered by a depressed 2019 base) — and BV/sh −1.7%/yr reflects heavy capital return, not decay. L/E 123% (P5-clean). **WEAK** — returns on the goodwill-laden employed capital sit below the floor. Pre-2019 years are Praxair-standalone; flagged, not blended into the verdict.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASS both sides: tens of thousands of customers, none >10% of revenue; power and feedstock sourced from many utilities/suppliers with pass-through clauses, none >10%. Four forces all WEAK → **0 strong** (favourable): customers locked by 15-yr take-or-pay and switching costs; suppliers fragmented against a scale buyer; no substitute for industrial gases; entry barred by density economics, capital intensity and the three-player global oligopoly. Moat: **cost/density + switching costs** (local network density, pipeline monopolies, on-site contracts), stable-to-strengthening. Market growing (decarbonization, hydrogen, electronics, healthcare). **STRONG.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Comp clean: CEO Lamba FY2024 total $20.7m — inside the 5%-of-lower(FCF,NI) hard-strike line (~$246m) and the ~$40m absolute scrutiny flag; modest outright ownership; RPT negligible. The strike is **P19**: FY2025 repurchases of $4.6bn at an implied ~25–28× EV/OI and ~40–45× MCap/FCF (net-reducing, not sterilization) — a hard strike at any of these bases (>20×). One hard strike ⇒ **FAIL**. Dividends ($2.8bn) are rational for a mature capital-returner; the buybacks are simply overpriced acquisitions of own stock.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- The Past-WEAK call rests on keeping merger goodwill in CE; an analyst deducting it (Linde is arguably now organically run) would see ~23% ROCE and STRONG — but that flatters away the price paid, and FCFROCE is only 6.6% even so.
- Continued mid-teens OI/sh growth could lift ROCE-on-goodwill toward 10% within a few years, reopening the ADEQUATE question at the next 10-K.
- P19 flips only if a future year's buybacks run below ~14× EV/OI — implausible unless the stock halves.
- Cyclicality (electronics, metals, chemicals end-markets) can compress OI and worsen the returns math.
- Currency: ~2/3 of sales are non-USD; translation swings OI and per-share figures.

Cheapness is a separate, distant hurdle (price → low-$80s).
