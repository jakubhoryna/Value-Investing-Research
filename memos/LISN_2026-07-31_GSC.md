## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- **Capital allocation is the binding defect and it is live and enlarged.** Roughly CHF 1bn is authorised to April 2029 with no price gate; on the predecessor's cadence most of it will be spent well above worth.
- **Cocoa cost super-cycle:** record bean prices pressure gross margin industry-wide; Lindt offsets via price, but −6.6% volume in 2025 shows the ceiling on pass-through.
- Discretionary premium demand is the first thing consumers trade down.
- FX: >90% of sales outside CHF; a strong franc is a persistent reported-growth drag.
- At ~24× EV/OI the multiple carries the entire return case.
- **Governance:** foundation control plus non-voting PCs means the buyback pricing policy is effectively unreachable from outside — which is why this defect is structural rather than cyclical.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

AVOID → WATCH needs the capital-allocation judgment at CAUTION or SOUND: a completed programme year executed at or below conservative worth, a **published** valuation criterion, or a suspension while the stock trades above band. Given that the last programme was concluded *early* and doubled, a suspension would be a genuine reversal and strong evidence. Redirecting the cash to a special dividend would also do it — the ordinary dividend has risen 30 years running, so the mechanism plainly exists. To BUY from there: price into ≈CHF 16,700/reg, roughly −82%. **Next review:** FY2026 results (~March 2027) — the first full year of the 2026–2029 programme, with the tranche price disclosed. Earlier only on a formal change to buyback pricing policy or a price collapse toward band.
