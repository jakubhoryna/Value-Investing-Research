# EMBRAC-B.ST — Embracer Group AB (publ): GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS (three material units); Past WEAK (losses); Future WEAK (unit customer conc.); Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (compensation, with buybacks and acquisitions ADVERSE); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID** (Past + Future + SF).

Embracer owns game studios and publishers, a mobile-games group and an entertainment arm built around The Lord of the Rings and Tomb Raider rights. It grew from SEK 0.3bn of sales in 2016 to SEK 42bn in FY2024 by buying about everything it could with new shares and debt, then wrote off most of what it bought: goodwill and acquired intangibles fell from SEK 94bn to SEK 15bn in three years, partly through write-downs and partly through selling or spinning off Gearbox, Saber, Asmodee and Coffee Stain. Operating income on the J1 line has been negative in every year since FY2022. A BUY would need years of positive operating income and cash flow on the remaining business, and a board that buys back stock only below a stated worth.

## 2. Understanding

Group sentence: Embracer Group AB is a Karlstad-based holding company, listed in Stockholm since 2016 (as THQ Nordic until 2019), with founder Lars Wingefors controlling 40.7% of the votes, that develops, publishes and licenses video games and entertainment rights worldwide and sells them mainly through Steam, PlayStation, Xbox, Epic and the Apple and Google app stores, which pay it a share of each sale (FY2026 sales SEK 15.9bn after the Asmodee and Coffee Stain spin-offs) — **PASS**.

- **PC/Console Games** (42% of FY2026 sales): PLAION, THQ Nordic and Crystal Dynamics–Eidos develop and publish premium games (Kingdom Come, Tomb Raider, Killing Floor, Titan Quest), sold to the digital storefronts and to retailers.
- **Mobile Games** (15%): DECA Games and CrazyLabs publish free-to-play mobile games, paid by app-store purchases collected through Apple and Google, and by advertising networks.
- **Entertainment & Services** (44%): Dark Horse Media (comics, films), Middle-earth Enterprises (licensing of The Lord of the Rings and The Hobbit rights to licensees), and PLAION's partner publishing and physical distribution of other publishers' games and films to retailers.
