# PTC — PTC Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS, Past WEAK, Future STRONG, shareholder-friendliness FAIL (flag pair), cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID (Past WEAK + SF FAIL).**

PTC is a good business that the ten-year record does not yet show. Its CAD and PLM franchises carry real switching costs and 95% recurring revenue, and returns have climbed from losses in FY2016 to an 18% ROCE in FY2025. But the window still holds the subscription-transition years, and ≈ $3.3bn of acquisitions (FY2016–25 cash paid) sit in capital employed, so the worst-of-four normalized ROCE is 9.5%, below the 10% line. Shareholder-friendliness fails on two independent flags. CEO pay breaches the trailing-three-year read. Buybacks have been sized up at prices well above conservative worth, and in July 2026 part of that was funded with new debt. The stock is also EXPENSIVE: EV/OI is 17.5–17.9× against a band of $45.66–48.89. To flip, the returns record has to clear the line as the early years roll off, *and* both flags have to clear.

## 2. Understanding
PTC sells engineering software — Creo and Onshape CAD, Windchill/Arena PLM, Codebeamer ALM and ServiceMax/Servigistics service software — almost entirely as recurring subscriptions, to manufacturers in industrials, aerospace and defense, automotive, electronics and medtech (organisations pay; >30,000 customers, ~75% direct). It is a Boston-headquartered, global, four-decade incumbent ranked behind Dassault and Siemens, and it narrowed its focus in 2026 by selling Kepware/ThingWorx. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
**WEAK.** Worst-of-four normalized ROCE is **9.5%** (end-period, cash in), with the band running 9.5–11.9%. That is below the 10% line, so the ADEQUATE floor is missed. FCFROCE is 11.5–14.3%. The yearly path is rising, not falling: −2.7% in FY2016 and 3–6% through FY2019, then 9–12% for FY2021–24 and 18.7% in FY2025. The worst read stays capped because acquired goodwill and intangibles of $4.3bn are larger than equity. Per-share growth is strong: FCF/sh +20.0%/yr and OI/sh +42.4%/yr (FY2017 base). L/E is 72.9%. The failure comes from the average level, not from the trend or leverage.

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth: **PASS.** The 10-K states that no customer exceeded 10% of revenue in FY2023–25. Suppliers are hosting and channel partners with ready alternatives. All four forces are WEAK: customers face years of embedded product data, suppliers are commodity, substitutes are other CAD/PLM vendors with no doing-without option, and entrants face installed-base and certification barriers. Tally 0. Moat: **switching costs**, with the CAD model and PLM record forming the system of record for a product's life. It is stable, and 95% recurring revenue plus ARR growth of 8.5% (ex-divestiture, Q2'26) evidence it. Market growth YES. **STRONG.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness
