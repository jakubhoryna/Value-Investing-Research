## 2. Understanding

STIF S.A., a family-controlled French industrial group founded in 1984 near Angers and listed on Euronext Growth Paris since December 2023, designs and mass-produces explosion-protection products. These are vent panels (Vigilex), flame-arresting vents (Vigiflam), isolation valves and active suppression systems (FLASH, from StuvEx). They sell to makers of battery energy-storage containers such as Tesla, Sungrow, Fluence and CATL (Explosion Energy, 45.8% of 2025 revenue), and to food, grain, wood and chemical plants and their equipment builders (Explosion Industry, 25.9%). Its older line is elevator buckets, compression couplings and other bulk-handling components sold mostly through exclusive country distributors (Material Handling, 21.6%). Plants are in France, Belgium, Suzhou (China) and Texas, and 91% of sales are outside France. One business, reported by product family and by location of the selling entity. **PASS.**
