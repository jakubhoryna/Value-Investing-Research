Understanding PASS (three material units); Past WEAK (ROCE decline); Future WEAK (unit customer conc.); Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (compensation); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID** (Past + Future + SF).

XANO is a Jönköping group of about 30 small engineering companies: line builders for can-makers and food plants, plastic moulders and precision machinists. It roughly tripled revenue over the decade by acquisition, but returns peaked in 2021 and fell three years running, and per-share operating income grew under 3% a year on the harsher read. Every unit sells to industrial customers without unit-level breadth disclosure, and the CEO's 2024 pay was 17% of that year's free cash flow. Fixing the returns record, customer disclosure and pay discipline would all be needed before price mattered; the trigger sits near SEK 12.

## 2. Understanding

Group sentence: XANO Industri AB is a Nasdaq Stockholm-listed Swedish industrial group (net revenue SEK 3.40bn in 2025, about 1,340 employees, controlled through class A shares by Viem Invest, the Benjamin family's company, and Pomona-gruppen) that designs, builds and services conveyor, packaging and automation lines for can-makers, food, pharmaceutical and other manufacturers in Europe, the US and Asia; moulds polymer tanks and customer-specific plastic components for automotive, infrastructure and agricultural manufacturers in the Nordics and northern Europe; and machines precision metal and polymer parts for medtech, defence and aerospace manufacturers, mostly in Sweden.

- **Industrial Solutions** (58% of 2025 revenue): conveyors, packaging machines, robot cells and complete lines with digital controls (55% of revenue recognised as projects over time), plus after-sales service, sold to can-makers and food, pharmaceutical and infrastructure manufacturers.
- **Industrial Products** (27%): rotational, blow and injection moulded polymer products, 70% made to customer specification and 30% own products (tanks, containers), sold to automotive, infrastructure and environment, agriculture and forestry manufacturers.
- **Precision Technology** (14%): CNC machining, 3D printing and laser welding of complex components to customer drawings for medtech, defence, aerospace, automotive and energy manufacturers.
