# 6506.T — Yaskawa Electric: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS; Past WEAK; Future ADEQUATE; Shareholder-friendliness PASS (one flag); Cheapness EXPENSIVE. Final verdict: **AVOID (Past)**.

Yaskawa makes servo motors, drives and industrial robots, sold in near-equal halves. Operating income per share barely kept pace with the 3% floor over ten years (+2.8% a year). Free cash flow per share shrank 6.0% a year, because capital spending doubled while operating cash flow stalled. Returns have fallen three years running, from 13.4% to 6.9% on capital employed. The price, ¥4,806, is 210× FY2025 free cash flow and 29× operating income. The verdict would move only if free cash flow per share rebuilds above the FY2015 base and returns recover across a full cycle; even then the price is far from any band.

## 2. Understanding
Yaskawa designs and manufactures AC servo motors and controllers, inverter drives, industrial robots (welding, handling, painting, semiconductor-wafer transfer) and electrical systems for water, steel and port cranes. Its customers are machine builders, semiconductor-equipment makers, car makers and their suppliers, electronics manufacturers and system integrators; no customer reaches 10% of revenue. Motion control (44% of FY2025 revenue) and robotics (46%) are near-equal units, built and sold through its own subsidiaries in Japan (27% of revenue), the Americas (24%), China (21%), the rest of Asia (15%) and EMEA (13%). Founded in 1915 and headquartered in Kitakyushu, it had ¥542bn of FY2025 revenue. **PASS**.

## 3. Quality — past record
The worst-of-four normalized ROCE is **10.6%** (end-of-period, with cash), above the ADEQUATE level. The worst FCFROCE is only **3.1%**. Year by year, ROCE_wc ran 10.5% → 16.6% → 15.2% → 6.7% → 7.1% → 12.7% → 13.4% → 11.9% → 8.3% → **6.9%**, falling for three straight years since FY2022. Operating income per share grew **2.8%** a year, under the 3% floor. FCF per share fell **6.0%** a year: capex rose from 4% to 8.5% of sales while CFO barely grew. FY2022 CFO was negative after an inventory build. Book value per share compounded 10.6%. FY2025 liabilities-to-equity was 65.9%. **WEAK** (P4: FCF per share shrinking, below P3; returns declining).

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth PASS. The yūhō states that no customer exceeds 10% of revenue. Parts come from many suppliers worldwide, key components are made in-house, and rare-earth magnet supply is being localised.

Forces:
- Customer power WEAK: fragmented machine builders design servos into their equipment.
- Supplier power WEAK: diversified purchasing.
- Substitutes WEAK: automation has no functional alternative.
- **New entrants STRONG**: Chinese servo and robot makers are winning peripheral axes and price-led share in China (21% of revenue). Management concedes this on the FY2026 Q1 call.

Tally: 1 strong, contained. Moat: **switching costs**. Servo design-ins and robot programs are costly to re-qualify. Stable in the core, eroding at the China edge. Market growth YES. **ADEQUATE**.
