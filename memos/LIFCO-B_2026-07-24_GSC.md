Lifco converts retained earnings into controlling stakes in small, market-leading niche businesses at roughly seven times their operating income, then leaves them alone. Four sampled cohorts priced at 6.6×–8.1× pre-deal EBITA with no drift, in a decade when every Nordic compounder chased the same targets. ## 2. Understanding

Lifco is a Swedish-listed, Bennet-family-controlled decentralised holding company that buys and permanently holds controlling stakes in ~275 small market-leading niche businesses in 37 countries — dental consumables and equipment sold to clinics, demolition robots and excavator attachments sold to contractors and rental fleets, and environmental-technology, transportation, contract-manufacturing and systems businesses sold to European industrial customers — earning its return by buying family-owned companies at high-single-digit multiples of operating income and leaving management autonomous. **PASS**, with the Appendix C caveat that the object understood is the acquisition-and-hold system, not 275 knowable units.

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **14.4%** (the four run 14.4/15.2/16.7/17.7%); worst-of-four FCFROCE **12.0%**. Goodwill stays in capital employed — mandatory for a serial acquirer, and the whole story: Lifco's own headline ROCE-excluding-goodwill of 132% measures the businesses, not the price paid for them. Per-share growth is emphatic and self-funded: ΔOI/sh 16.7%, ΔFCF/sh 18.5%, ΔBV/sh 17.1% geometric over ten years against a 3% floor, on an unchanged share count. L/E 114.1%, well inside P5. No sustained decline — ROCE_wc oscillates 13.1–15.8% without trend. **ADEQUATE**: clears P3 easily, misses P2 only on the level of returns.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth passes both sides — largest customer ~6% of 2025 sales; supplier independence is an explicit acquisition screen. Forces: customer power WEAK (atomised base, small-ticket items), supplier power WEAK (275 units sourcing separately), substitutes WEAK (no single wave reaches a group this diversified), **new entrants STRONG** — barriers into the niches are modest and barriers into the Nordic serial-acquirer trade visibly low, with Indutrade, Addtech, Lagercrantz, Sdiptech and Storskogen courting the same sellers. Tally 1 strong, contained. Moat: **brand**, held with sellers not buyers — standing as a permanent home for family firms yields proprietary deal flow at seven times earnings; **stable**, evidenced by flat entry multiples through rising competition. Market growth MIXED. **ADEQUATE**.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Entry multiples are the thesis: if competition pushes the cohort multiple through 15×, P19 flags and the compounding arithmetic break at the same moment.
- Returns are already only adequate on an all-in capital base, so 1–2pp of further ROCE erosion from paying up takes Past toward WEAK.
- Reported OI carries ~SEK 1.1bn of annual acquisition-intangible amortisation that grows with deal volume, widening the gap against the EBITA the market quotes.
- Demolition & Tools stayed weak through H1 2026 while the rest recovered; a prolonged European construction downturn lands in a high-margin area.
- The deal-access moat is partly personal to a controlling family and long-tenured management, making succession a genuine thesis risk.

## C. Calculation notes

**FY2025 capital-employed build.** CE_wc = total assets 41,106 − NIBCL 3,987 = **37,119**. NIBCL = accounts payable 1,829 + advance payments from customers 418 + current tax liabilities 482 + accrued expenses and deferred income 1,258. CE_xc = 37,119 − cash 1,878 = **35,241**.

**NIBCL burden-of-proof calls.** "Other current liabilities" (SEK 1,124m) is **left in CE**: it houses the current portion of the put/call options over minority stakes, a financing instrument, not trade credit. "Other short-term provisions" (SEK 60m) is likewise left in CE as ambiguous. Both calls raise CE and therefore lower ROCE — the conservative direction the framework prescribes.

**Goodwill treatment.** Goodwill and acquisition intangibles (SEK 26,817m) **stay in capital employed**. Lifco is the archetypal acquisitive company; the goodwill deduction available to non-acquisitive names is inapplicable. This single choice is the difference between the 14.4% computed here and Lifco's own 132% headline.

**Leases.** IFRS 16 in force throughout the reported window; lease liabilities (SEK 989m non-current + 327m current = 1,316m) are already on balance sheet and are treated as debt-equivalent in CE, EV and L/E. P18 pre-adoption capitalisation is not triggered for the in-window years. FCF is CFO − capex per the framework's literal rule; note that SEK 338m of lease principal repayment sits in financing, so CFO is flattered by that amount — deducting it would give FCF of SEK 4,244m and MCap/FCF of 33.3×, worsening an already-failing metric.

**TBV build (FY2025).** BV 19,137 − goodwill and other intangibles 26,817 = **−7,680**.

**EV builds (FY2025 balance sheet, price SEK 311.60).** MCap = 454.2163m × 311.60 = **141,534**. Debt = total interest-bearing liabilities 10,964 (including lease liabilities 1,316) + interest-bearing pension provisions 31 = **10,995**. EV conservative = 141,534 + 10,995 + NCI 140 = **152,669**. EV all-cash-excess = 152,669 − 1,878 = **150,791**.

*Stricter alternative logged:* the put/call option liabilities over minority stakes sitting in other non-current (2,507) and other current (1,124) liabilities are arguably deferred acquisition consideration and therefore debt-equivalent. Including them lifts EV to ≈156,300 and EV/OI to 30.2×. Excluded from the headline for definitional fidelity; the direction is adverse and does not touch the verdict.

**Gate 0 caveat.** Understanding passes on the acquisition-and-hold system rather than on 275 individually-modelled subsidiaries. This follows the treatment already applied to BRK.B in this register. The caveat is real: an investor here is underwriting an allocator, not a set of enumerable moats.

**Bias check.** Two live and handled. *Cleverness* — the serial-acquirer compounding narrative is intellectually attractive and was deliberately tested by keeping goodwill in capital employed, which cut the headline return by an order of magnitude and moved Past from the STRONG most commentary assumes to ADEQUATE. *Hotness* — the shares sit near the top of their 52-week range after a well-received recovery quarter; the band was computed mechanically from FY2025 audited figures and is unaffected. Neither judgment is born of a bias.
