## 2. Understanding

Pool Corporation is a Covington, Louisiana-based, Nasdaq-listed wholesale distributor (listed since October 1995, revenue $5.29bn in 2025, about 95% North America, 4% Europe) that buys pool equipment, chemicals, replacement parts, building materials and irrigation products, mostly from manufacturers such as Pentair (20% of product cost), Zodiac (12%) and Hayward (11%), plus its own chemical and equipment brands, and resells more than 200,000 products from 456 sales centres to about 125,000 trade customers: pool builders and remodelers, pool service and repair businesses, specialty retailers (including franchised Pinch A Penny stores), irrigation and landscape contractors and commercial pool operators, with California, Florida, Texas and Arizona 53% of sales.

One reportable segment; no listed subsidiary or holding. **PASS.** Lens STD.

## 3. Quality — past record

## 4. Quality — future

No customer reaches 10% of sales. Pentair supplies 20% of the cost of products sold, about 16% of costs: `supplier conc.`. Dominance is not evidenced by a printed share, so the concentration is a STRONG supplier-power force. Its containment: Zodiac and Hayward, at 12% and 11%, sell the same equipment categories through Pool, which steers volume through a preferred-vendor programme and its own brands, and returns on capital stayed at or above 19.9% every year with Pentair the largest supplier. The other forces are weak. The moat is cost from scale and local density, stable (gross margin 28.7–31.3%). Market growth MIXED (sales fell 2023–25; +4% in 2026, much of it price). **ADEQUATE** (`supplier conc.`).
