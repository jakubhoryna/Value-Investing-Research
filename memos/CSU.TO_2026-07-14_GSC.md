A world-class serial acquirer of mission-critical vertical-market software (VMS), compounding capital at very high returns through a decentralised buy-and-hold-forever machine. ## 2. Understanding

Constellation Software is a Toronto-listed (single-class common) holding company that acquires and permanently operates hundreds of small vertical-market software businesses — mission-critical software sold to narrow industry niches (municipalities, healthcare, transit, dealerships, financial services, utilities) — run through decentralised operating groups (Volaris, Harris, Jonas, Perseus, Vela, plus listed affiliates Topicus and Lumine), earning recurring licence/maintenance and SaaS revenue from tens of thousands of organisational customers globally, funded largely by customer-prepaid maintenance. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Returns are excellent and stable. Over FY2021–25 (USD), worst-of-four normalized-average **ROCE ≈17.2%** (goodwill-in CE; range 17–27% across the four cash/timing variants) and **FCFROCE ≈24.7%** — both above the STRONG thresholds (15% / 8%). Per-share growth is high and well clear of the 3% floor: OI +21%/yr, FCF +20%/yr, BV +36%/yr (shares flat at 21.19m — no dilution). TBV is deeply negative and widening (acquired goodwill/intangibles), read here as capital deployed into M&A, not buyback-driven erosion. Leverage optically trips the P5 flag — L/E 333% — but ~37% of liabilities are deferred revenue + payables (customer float); real leverage is minimal (net debt/FCF **0.66×**, high interest coverage), a logged deferred-revenue artifact. **ADEQUATE** — economics are STRONG-tier; the rating is capped by the sub-10-year audited window (P1).

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth **PASS** both sides: no customer >10% (tens of thousands across 100+ verticals), no supplier >10% (principal input is developer labour). Four forces, all **WEAK** (favourable), tally 0 strong: customers hold little power (mission-critical, tiny share of their budgets, high switching costs, fragmented); suppliers none concentrated; substitutes low (deeply embedded workflows, costly to replace); new entrants deterred by barriers to *success* (niches too small to be worth attacking, plus CSU's non-replicable acquisition/allocation model). Moat source: **switching costs** at the operating-company level, reinforced by a structural capital-allocation/deal-sourcing advantage; direction **stable-to-strengthening**. Market growth **YES** (VMS spend growing; acquisition runway vast). **STRONG.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Dividend US$4/yr (~US$85m, ~3% of FCF) — rational retention at high ROCE. RPT minimal (listed-affiliate consolidation is subsidiary, not insider, activity). Acquisition audit: BV≫TBV from many small tuck-ins under a documented high-IRR hurdle, no single transformative deal trips the per-deal 15×/20× lines. **PASS** — no strike, no flag pair.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- **Reinvestment runway**: the model needs a continuous stream of VMS targets at disciplined IRRs; if capital deployed grows faster than good deals, incremental ROIC compresses (the central long-term risk).
- **Law of large numbers**: at >US$40bn, moving the needle requires ever-larger deals (Large Acquisition Group), where competition and prices are higher and the hurdle harder to hold.
- **Key-person / succession**: Leonard's departure for health reasons (Sep 2025) removes the architect; Miller and the decentralised culture are the mitigant, but capital-allocation discipline is the whole thesis.
- **AI disruption** to embedded VMS niches over the long run (mitigated by switching costs and CSU's own AI adoption).
- **Valuation**: a premium-priced compounder; multiple compression alone can produce years of flat returns even if the business performs.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

To **BUY**: price into the band (≈ CAD 513 binding; ≈ CAD 718 on the flattering EV variant) — i.e., a ~70–80% de-rating, realistic only in a severe market drawdown. To **AVOID**: a durable ROCE/FCFROCE decline, a leverage or governance deterioration, or a shift to value-destructive large-deal M&A. **Next review:** FY2026 annual results (~Mar 2027), which reset the USD FY25 basis and extend the record toward the 10-year window (potentially lifting Past to STRONG and unlocking the P12 edge); earlier if price < ≈ CAD 900 or on a material capital-allocation/succession development.
