# RSW.L — Renishaw plc: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding **FAIL** (one-level decomposition: a material residual unit that mixes industries) → **DO NOT ANALYSE FURTHER**. Past, Future, shareholder-friendliness and cheapness were not run.

Renishaw's core is easy to understand: probes, gauges and encoders designed into machine tools, measuring machines and motion systems. The problem is the third of the three segments it reports for the first time in FY2026. Specialised Technologies is, in the filer's words, "all other product lines within the Group": metal additive-manufacturing machines, Raman spectroscopy instruments and neurosurgical products. It carried 13.2% of FY2026 revenue, above the 10% materiality line. Its parts sell to different industries and different paying customers, and the filer gives no revenue share for any of them. Until FY2025 the filer itself reported spectroscopy and neurology in a separate segment whose customers "tend to be end users … working in healthcare and academia", while additive manufacturing sat with the industrial products. MODEL v4.5.x fails Gate 0 for such a unit, whether it is read as a material "Other" or as an irreducible portfolio unit. The DNAF is a parameter-driven result, not a finding of incomprehension or obfuscation. It flips back to a full analysis if the unit drops below the line or is re-segmented, or if a MODEL ruling changes how a filer-defined residual is tested.

## 2. Understanding
**Group sentence.** Renishaw designs and makes, mostly in its own UK plants (with major sites in Ireland and India), precision measurement and manufacturing technology: machine-tool probes, tool setters, CMM sensors, gauging systems and metrology software; optical and magnetic encoders; and, in a smaller residual segment, metal additive-manufacturing machines, Raman spectrometers and neurosurgical products. Its paying customers are machine-tool and motion-system builders and manufacturers in semiconductors, electronics, automotive, aerospace and precision engineering, plus research laboratories and hospitals for the residual lines. It sells through its own subsidiaries in APAC, EMEA and the Americas (China and the USA are its largest markets). It is a long-established, family-controlled UK mid-cap headquartered in Gloucestershire.

**Units (P22; FY2026 segment note, first year of the new segments).**

| Unit | Filer's description | FY2026 revenue (£m) | Revenue share | Statutory OI share | Material |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Industrial Metrology | sensors, measurement systems and software to measure machines and machined parts and control production machines | 447.4 | 54.8% | 49.0% | yes |
| Position Measurement | encoders that improve control and precision in customers' machines | 260.9 | 32.0% | 49.7% | yes |
| Specialised Technologies | "all other product lines within the Group, which consists of additive manufacturing (AM), spectroscopy and neurological product lines" | 107.4 | **13.2%** | 1.3% | **yes** |

The filer states that Industrial Metrology and Position Measurement each "aggregate product offerings with similar economic characteristics, similar production processes and similar customer bases". It makes no such statement for Specialised Technologies.

**Gate 0 test.** Industrial Metrology and Position Measurement can each be described in one six-parameter sentence. Specialised Technologies cannot:
- **Its sub-businesses differ in industry and paying customer.** AM machines are industrial capital equipment sold to manufacturers, especially in aerospace and defence in FY2026. Raman spectrometers are analytical instruments whose key markets the filer names as academia, healthcare, high-tech manufacturing and materials science. Neurosurgical robots and surgical-planning software are medical devices for healthcare end users (hospitals: inference from the product, logged). Each is a product line with its own goodwill cash-generating unit (AM, SPD, NPD), the level at which management monitors goodwill.
- **Shares.** The filer gives no FY2026 revenue split inside the unit, and under J13 that alone makes the unit irreducible. The FY2025 figures confirm it on size as well: spectroscopy and neurology together took £41.6m (the old Analytical instruments and medical devices segment), and AM the remaining £33.4m of the £75.0m restated unit. AM is therefore about 45% of the unit, and the healthcare-and-academia lines about 55%. Both sides are far above 10% and differ in industry.
- **"Other".** The filer defines the unit as the residual of all other product lines. It carries operating businesses, so it is a unit, and it is material.

The unit is above the P22 line on revenue (107.4 of 815.8). Neither route needs the harsher reading: the filer's own definition and the FY2025 split decide it. **FAIL → DNAF.**
