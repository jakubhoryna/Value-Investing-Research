Every display manufacturer on earth uses photomasks written on Mycronic's machines, and Pattern Generators earned SEK 1,623m of the group's SEK 2,194m divisional profit on 41% of sales at a 68% gross margin — 24.5% returns on capital through a mask-writer drought, on 54% liabilities-to-equity and no financial debt. It fails anyway, and not on price. Every krona spent on its own shares since 2021 has gone to deliver stock to LTIP participants: the issued count has stood at 195,833,018 all decade, 562,876 shares sit in treasury, nothing cancelled, no capital returned. P19 strikes that at any price, and the FY2025 tranche separately breaches 20× on both dual-read legs of implied MCap/FCF. The verdict flips on how incentive plans are settled, not on a cheaper stock.

## 2. Understanding

**PASS.** Mycronic is a Swedish-listed, Täby-headquartered capital-equipment maker building four families of high-precision production machines — laser mask writers for display and semiconductor photomasks, flexible PCB-assembly lines, high-volume dispensing and coating systems, and acquired niche electronics-test and die-bonding equipment — sold with a large recurring aftermarket to merchant photomask shops, display and semiconductor manufacturers, and electronics OEMs and subcontractors, almost entirely outside Sweden.

## 3. Quality — past record

Ten full years. Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **24.5%** and FCFROCE **19.4%** (21.6%/17.1% on the alternate NIBCL basis) — both clear P2 with room. Per-share growth compounds at 12.2% (OI) and 12.9% (FCF) against the 3% floor, on a count that *fell* from 195.8m to 195.3m. Leverage 54%, and the SEK 420m of interest-bearing liabilities is entirely leases. One blemish: returns stepped down from ~45% (2016–18) to ~25% as goodwill and intangibles rose from SEK 1,085m to SEK 3,524m. That is a level shift from deliberate diversification, not erosion — the last four years read 18.4%, 22.1%, 33.1%, 25.6%. **STRONG.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth passes both sides: FY2025 and FY2024 state **no customer exceeds 10% of group sales** (FY2019 had two at 21.9% — mask-writer lumpiness re-concentrates the base), and no supplier is disclosed above a tenth of COGS plus opex. Forces: customers **WEAK** (68% divisional gross margin shows buyers cannot extract price), substitutes **WEAK**, entrants **WEAK**, suppliers **STRONG** — sole-source dependency is named a principal risk and left unquantified. Tally 1, contained. Moat: **Cost** — minimum efficient scale in a market too small to amortise a second supplier's optics and laser R&D, plus installed-base switching costs. Stable within Pattern Generators, narrowing in group mix. Markets growing, violently lumpy. **ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Three tests are clean: the CEO's SEK 24.3m is 1.96% of the lower leg (FCF SEK 1,242m) against the 5% strike and ≈USD 2.6m against the ≈USD 40m trigger; the chair's SEK 1.2m is far under any norm; related-party dealings are nil; the 41% payout is rational and atg L&M (2021), the only deal large enough to audit, went at **11.5×** pre-deal operating income. The failure is P19, twice. Repurchases exist solely to hand shares to LTIP participants — **dilution sterilisation, a hard strike at any price**. Independently the FY2025 tranche implies **30.0× year / 26.1× normalized** MCap/FCF, above 20× on both reads. The redemption clause fails all three limbs. **FAIL.**

## D. Future-performance worksheet

**Breadth — customers: PASS.** Note 7 states for both FY2025 and FY2024 that "0 customers represent more than 10 percent of the Group's total sales" — the disclosure sentence §2·1B names as the test. **Logged caveat:** the same disclosure read "2 (0) customers" in FY2019 (SEK 944m, 21.9% of sales), one customer at SEK 396m (10.2%) in FY2020 and one at SEK 647m (14.0%) in FY2021. A single Prexision order re-concentrates the base; the current pass reflects a revenue mix now spread across four divisions, not a structurally broad customer base in Pattern Generators. No waiver used or needed.

**Breadth — suppliers: PASS on the spend-share test, caveat logged.** No supplier is disclosed above 10% of COGS plus operating expenses (SEK ~5,998m in 2025, so the line sits near SEK 600m). Against that, the AR2025 risk table names "Dependency on single/sole source suppliers" as a principal risk with no quantification. The distinction applied: a sole-source supplier of a critical optical or laser component need not be a tenth of total spend, and the §2·1B test is a spend-share test. Treated as PASS with the criticality carried into the supplier force call and a named re-test. Precedent: EPI-A battery cells (2026-07-24).

**Forces (binary; §2·1B, P16).**

| Force | Call | One-line reason |
|---|---|---|
| Customer bargaining power | **WEAK** | No customer over 10%; backward integration into laser mask writing is implausible; switching costs on calibrated installed systems are high; a 68% Pattern Generators gross margin is direct evidence buyers cannot extract price. |
| Supplier bargaining power | **STRONG** | The company itself discloses dependency on single/sole-source suppliers as a principal risk and quantifies nothing; precision optics, lasers and motion systems have no ready alternative vendors. |
| Threat of substitutes | **WEAK** | Photomasks remain the production route for essentially all displays and most semiconductors; maskless direct imaging has not displaced them at volume, and the aftermarket (35% of PCB Assembly sales) is not substitutable. |
| Threat of new entrants | **WEAK** | Decades of accumulated optics and laser IP amortised over a handful of machines a year; the display mask-writer market is too small to repay a challenger's development cost. Chinese competition in High Volume dispensing is real but that division is 22% of sales. |

**Tally: 1 strong force** — the judgment zone, contained by the fact that no supplier has been shown to hold pricing power over a company earning 24% margins.

**Moat: Cost — stable within its domain, narrowing in group mix.** Mechanism: the world market for display photomask writers absorbs a few systems a year, so the fixed cost of the optics, laser and precision-mechanics platform can only be amortised by the incumbent that has already paid it. AR2025: "All manufacturers in the display industry use photomasks produced with Mycronic's laser-based mask writers." Reinforced by switching costs on the installed base and an aftermarket that is a third of divisional revenue. Direction: **stable** for Pattern Generators — the FY2025 report describes orders won to replace competitors' legacy installed systems. The qualification that matters: Pattern Generators is 41% of sales and 74% of divisional profit; the 42% of revenue in High Volume and Global Technologies carries no comparable protection and competes on ordinary terms.

**Market growth: YES, lumpy.** Advanced OLED/LCD displays require more and more complex photomasks; semiconductor photomask demand grew on AI and advanced memory; AI-server PCBs drive the PCB Test line; EV battery assembly drives dispensing. Against that, SLX order intake slowed in 2025 on reduced Chinese semiconductor capacity investment, and group order intake has swung between SEK 2,455m (2016) and SEK 7,757m (2025). FY2026 guidance was raised in July 2026 from SEK 8.75bn to SEK 9.25bn.

**Verdict: ADEQUATE** — breadth PASS both sides, one strong force contained, a genuine moat that is stable where it exists. STRONG is withheld on the supplier force and on the moat's narrowing share of the group.
