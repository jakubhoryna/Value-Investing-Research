## 2. Understanding

Trigano S.A. is a Feuillet-family-controlled (57.9%), Euronext Paris-listed French group that builds branded but substitutable motorhomes, campervans and caravans on cab-chassis bought mainly from Stellantis (87.3% of FY2025 revenue, with accessories), and mobile homes, trailers and garden and camping equipment (12.7%), selling them wholesale to about 1,300 independent distributors (none above 5% of revenue), through its own Libertium dealerships to consumers, and to campsite operators, mainly in France, Germany, the UK and Italy, as a mature, net-cash leader of a cyclical European leisure-vehicle industry. **PASS.**
