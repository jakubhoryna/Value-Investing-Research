## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS (three material units); Past WEAK (9.5% ROCE); Future WEAK (unit customer conc.); Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (RPT); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID** (Past + Future + SF).

Addnode is a Stockholm group that resells and services Autodesk and Dassault Systèmes design software and sells its own case-management and GIS software to Nordic municipalities. Returns on capital have hovered around 10%, leverage has climbed with debt-funded acquisitions, the two largest units depend on one vendor each, and the chairman has billed the company about SEK 2.5m a year for strategy and acquisition advice since at least 2020. A flip would need all three: returns above 15% on the full capital base, unit-level breadth that no longer rests on Autodesk and Dassault, and the consulting arrangement ended.

## 2. Understanding

Group sentence: Addnode Group AB is a Nasdaq Stockholm-listed group (listed since 1999, net sales SEK 5.8bn in 2025, about 3,000 employees in some 20 companies in 20 countries) that earns commissions and services revenue on Autodesk and Dassault Systèmes design and product-lifecycle software it sells as a partner, and licenses and supports its own software, to architects, engineers, construction firms, manufacturers, property owners and Swedish and Norwegian public authorities, mainly in Sweden, the USA, the UK, Germany and Norway.

- **Design Management** (44% of 2025 revenue): Symetri and sister companies sell Autodesk design software as an agent (Autodesk invoices the user and pays a commission since late 2024), plus own add-on and facility-management software and services, to architects, engineers, builders, manufacturers and property owners in the Nordics, the UK, the USA and Canada.
- **Product Lifecycle Management** (31%): Technia resells and implements Dassault Systèmes PLM and simulation software (about 60% of the unit's pro forma 2024 revenue) with own software and services, for manufacturers in automotive, aerospace, defence and industry, mainly in Sweden, Germany, the UK and the USA.
- **Process Management** (26%): Sokigo and fifteen sister companies license own case-management, urban-planning, GIS and project-collaboration software, with services, to Swedish and Norwegian municipalities, government agencies and construction clients.
