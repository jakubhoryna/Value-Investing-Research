TOTO is a dominant consumer franchise — the Washlet, ~60% of Japanese sanitary ware, and a semiconductor-ceramics arm now contributing roughly half of segment operating profit on 9% of sales. None of that survives the arithmetic. FY3/2026 operating income of ¥53.8bn is barely above FY3/2018's ¥52.6bn, earned on a balance sheet ~40% larger: worst-of-four normalized ROCE is 8.0%, below the 10% ADEQUATE floor; FCFROCE is 2.5%. On top of the quality failure, the FY3/2026 ¥20.0bn buyback at ≈¥3,812/share implies 22.5× MCap/FCF — a P19 hard strike. At ¥8,059 the stock trades at 47× FCF and 23.5× EV/OI after a +108% twelve-month run on the ceramics narrative. Flipping the verdict requires structurally higher returns on the whole capital base plus disciplined repurchase pricing — a multi-year proof, not an event.

## 2. Understanding

TOTO Ltd. is a 109-year-old, Kitakyushu-based, TSE-Prime-listed, vertically integrated manufacturer (plants in nine countries) of differentiated bathroom plumbing products — sanitary ware, Washlet shower-toilet seats, faucets, unit bathrooms — sold to wholesalers, homebuilders and remodelers, ~65% of revenue in remodel-led Japan, ~26% overseas (Americas/Asia growing, Mainland China restructuring), plus high-precision ceramic components (electrostatic chucks) sold to semiconductor-equipment makers: 9% of revenue, ~51% of segment operating-profit composition in FY3/2026. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

On the verified window (FY3/2022–26; §A gap flag), worst-of-four normalized ROCE is **8.0%** (best variant 9.7% — all four under the 10% floor); worst-of-four FCFROCE **2.5%**. ΔOI/share +1.3%/yr; FCF volatile (negative FY3/2023 on a ¥31bn inventory build); ΔBV/TBV +7.6%/+7.2%/yr flattered by retained low-return capital; L/E 55.6%. Presentations extend the picture: OI ¥47.4bn (FY3/17) → ¥40.1bn (FY3/19) → ¥53.8bn (FY3/26) — a decade of sideways profit on a growing asset base — a sustained returns decline. J-GAAP flatters OI: the ¥34.1bn FY3/25 China impairment and ¥15.2bn FY3/26 restructuring sit below the operating line. **WEAK** on every variant, the trend clause independently confirming.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: PASS both sides — no single customer near 10% of revenue (broad wholesale/remodel channel; ceramics customers concentrated but only 9% of revenue); supplier base broad. Forces, binary: customer power WEAK · supplier power WEAK · substitutes WEAK · new entrants WEAK (brand, installed base, channel ingrainedness, ceramics qualification barriers) — tally 0 strong. Moat: brand + ingrainedness — dominant and stable in Japan, eroding in China (share loss forced the restructuring), strengthening in ceramics. Market growth MIXED (Japan flat, China negative, ceramics growing). **ADEQUATE** — China erosion and the profit-mix shift onto a semiconductor cycle bar STRONG.
