A good operating business inside a capital structure that has absorbed the returns it generates. Adjusted margins near 27–30%, an atomised customer base, a sticky installed base — but two decades of acquisition have loaded EUR 12.1bn of intangibles against EUR 10.2bn of equity, and returns on capital employed fell from 11.4% to 6.4% across the window. It fails the returns test and, independently, shareholder-friendliness: every share repurchased since 2020 fed the incentive plan rather than retiring capital. Two gate failures, price three to five times above any level that would matter.

## 2. Understanding

**PASS.** Hexagon AB is a long-established (Leica heritage), EUR-reporting Swedish listed company with dual-class shares and a decentralised, highly acquisitive form — ~17 profit centres in three business areas after the 28 May 2026 Octave spin-off and the February 2026 sale of Design & Engineering — selling differentiated precision-measurement and positioning hardware with attached software (coordinate measuring machines, laser trackers, total stations and GNSS receivers, mine- and machine-control systems, CAD/CAM software, and non-destructive testing once Waygate closes) in the industrial-metrology and geospatial-positioning industry to organisations that pay — manufacturers, construction contractors, surveyors, miners and autonomy OEMs — spread evenly across EMEA, the Americas and Asia, no customer above 0.7% of sales.

## 3. Quality — past record

**WEAK.** Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **9.25%** (end-of-period, cash-inclusive) against P3's 10% floor; worst-of-four FCFROCE **7.50%** against P2's 8%. Both use Hexagon's *own* flattering capital-employed definition and a cash-flow line already excluding items affecting comparability — the framework-strict treatment is lower still. The trend decides anyway: ROCE trends from 11.4% (2016) to 6.4% (2025) while capital employed more than doubled, a sustained decline that forces a downgrade regardless of level. ΔOI/share compounds at **+1.78%**, under the 3% floor. Leverage is fine: L/E 67%.

## 4. Quality — future

**ADEQUATE.** Breadth passes both legs — largest customer 0.7% of net sales (disclosed), no supplier concentration disclosed. Forces: customer power **WEAK** (atomised base, backward integration implausible, requalification costly); supplier power **WEAK** (broad component base, optics in-house); substitutes **STRONG** — drone photogrammetry, SLAM scanning and statistical sampling absorb the lower-precision half of reality capture and inspection; new entrants **WEAK** (scale, calibration traceability, installed base). Tally 1 strong, contained. Moat: **switching costs** — inspection routines, calibration histories and CAM toolpaths sit inside customers' quality documentation; direction **stable**. Market growth **MIXED**.

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

**FAIL — P19 hard strike.** All 21,100,000 Class B shares repurchased 2020–2023 are held for one disclosed purpose: meeting Hexagon's long-term incentive-programme undertakings. That is dilution sterilisation, which strikes at any price; redemption fails at limb (a) — the AGM mandate cites capital structure, acquisition financing and incentive hedging, never worth. Compensation is clean: top employee EUR 6.30m = 1.0% of the lower of FCF and net income (strike EUR 30.9m), ≈US$7.3m against the ≈US$40m trigger; chair fee EUR 254k. No material related-party transactions. Acquisition audit triggered (TBV negative) but **INDETERMINATE** — target profitability undisclosed.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Waygate (USD 1.45bn on ~USD 630m revenue) is the go-forward company's largest allocation decision, at an undisclosed multiple.
- Post-separation Hexagon is ~EUR 3.7bn of revenue at ~1.5× net debt/EBITDA guided — smaller and less diversified than the ten-year record shows.
- Geosystems faces low-end substitution and Chinese competition; FY2025's EUR 206.1m impairment of "overlapping technology" from Septentrio and Geomagic is an early signal.
- AEON humanoids (BMW Leipzig, seven-year Schaeffler deal) are an option with no disclosed economics — upside and spend both unpriced.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

Upward flip needs worst-of-four ROCE above 10% on a clean post-separation capital base — arithmetically plausible, since the distributed businesses carried a disproportionate share of the goodwill, but it needs two to three audited years, not one restated quarter. The P19 strike is the harder lock: it stands until Hexagon publishes a worth-referenced repurchase policy, executes a programme year inside the bargain lines or refuses to buy above them, *and* turns over the strike-era allocators — all three. Price alone changes nothing while two gates fail.
