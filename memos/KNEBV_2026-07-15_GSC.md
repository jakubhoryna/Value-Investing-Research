# KNEBV — KONE Oyj: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | ADEQUATE | STRONG | **FAIL** | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID (SF)** |

KONE is a genuinely elite business — a capital-light "big-four" elevator oligopolist whose profit engine is a recurring, high-retention maintenance annuity on a >1.4m-unit installed base, throwing off ~30% ROCE with negative working capital funded by customer advances. It is not, however, a name the framework can hold: the pending **€29.4bn acquisition of rival TK Elevator (TKE)** — signed 29 Apr 2026 — is a shareholder-friendliness strike. Deal EV ÷ TKE's ~€1.36bn pre-deal EBIT ≈ **21.6×**, above the P19 20× hard-strike line, and it is funded by ~€5bn cash, ~€9.2bn refinanced debt, and **270m newly issued B shares (~52% dilution)** engineered so Antti Herlin keeps >50% of votes. Even absent the strike, the shares are EXPENSIVE (≈19× EV/OI at a 52-week low). What flips the verdict back to WATCH is regulators blocking or killing the deal — a live possibility, with a competitor challenge already signaled and a review running to ~mid-2027.

## 2. Understanding

KONE Oyj is a Finnish-HQ, Herlin-family-controlled (≈62% of votes via dual-class shares) global manufacturer and servicer of elevators, escalators and automatic building doors, selling new equipment to building developers/owners (~30% of sales in China) and — its profit engine — recurring maintenance and modernisation to a >1.4m-unit installed base across ~70 countries, as one of the global "big four" now pursuing a transformative €29.4bn takeover of rival TK Elevator. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Returns are STRONG-caliber: worst-of-four normalized ROCE ≈ **29.5%** and FCFROCE ≈ **24.8%** (FY2021–25, CE_wc/end-of-period the binding variant), corroborated by KONE's own published ROCE of 25–34% and ROE of 28–38% across FY2016–20. But per-share operating income is **essentially flat over the decade** — OI €1,293m (2016) → €1,336m (2025), ΔOI/sh geo-mean ≈ 0.3%/yr — as EBIT margin compressed from ~15% to ~12% while revenue grew ~€8.8bn→€11.2bn. That fails the 3% nominal STRONG floor (P2), capping Past at ADEQUATE. Leverage looks high (L/E 223% > P5 200%) but is benign under P6: near-zero net interest-bearing debt, equity thinned by full-payout distributions, and a negative-working-capital model funded by ~€2bn customer advances. **ADEQUATE** — elite returns, no growth.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth passes both sides (~550k customers, no single one >10%; ~2,000 component suppliers, dual-sourced — the China supply/demand concentration is a geographic risk, not a breadth fail). Four forces all WEAK: customers fragmented with high post-install switching costs (>90% maintenance retention); suppliers many and forward-integration-proof; no substitute for vertical transport; entry barred by installed-base density, safety permits and the razor/razorblade service annuity. Tally 0 strong → favourable. Moat: **switching costs / installed-base ingrainedness**, stable (mild China service-competition erosion). Market growth MIXED — resilient service/modernisation vs. structurally weak China new-equipment. **STRONG.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

**FAIL.** The P19 acquisition audit strikes: TKE deal EV €29.4bn ÷ pre-deal EBIT ~€1.36bn ≈ **21.6×** (>20× hard strike; ~21–22× even on adjusted EBIT). The consideration — €5bn cash + ~€9.2bn refinanced TKE debt + up to 270m new KONE B shares (33.8% of post-deal shares, only 18.3% of votes) — is structured so the Herlin family's >50% voting control survives ~52% economic dilution: a documented governance flag that, paired with the pricey deal, independently satisfies the dual-class value-destructive-allocation strike. Comp is a non-issue (CEO Delorme base €844k; whole Exec Board €5.0m base + €2.0m STI + €9.6m LTI in 2025 — far under 5% of NI €980m and the ~€40m absolute flag; board fees €110k ≪ €500k). Historic allocation was disciplined (they walked from the overpriced TKE bid in 2020); this deal reverses that. **FAIL — P19 acquisition strike, reinforced by entrenchment flag.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- **TKE integration & leverage:** near-net-cash today → heavily levered post-close; execution risk on a €20.5bn, 100k-employee combination.
- **Antitrust:** combining the #3 and #4 global players faces 12–18-month multi-jurisdiction review to ~mid-2027; competitor challenge signaled; heavy divestiture remedies or a block are plausible.
- **China:** ~30% of sales; structural new-equipment decline pressures the cyclical half of the business for years.
- **Payout cut:** dividend policy drops to "≥50% of net income through the cycle" from ~95–124% — a real income-thesis change.
- **Entrenchment:** family voting control preserved through the dilution limits outside-holder influence over allocation.

It would move toward **AVOID on Future/leverage grounds** if the deal closes and integration/debt impairs the combined moat.
