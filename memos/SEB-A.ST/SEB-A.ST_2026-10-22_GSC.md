## 2. Understanding

Skandinaviska Enskilda Banken, a Stockholm-headquartered universal bank founded in 1856 and 21.8%-owned by Investor AB, earns interest, fees and trading income from lending, deposits, payments, cards, capital-markets, custody, asset-management and life-pension services sold to large Nordic and German corporates and financial institutions, Swedish households and small businesses, wealthy families and foundations, and Baltic households and companies; it is one of the four large Swedish banks, with SEK 76.9bn of 2025 income and SEK 3.7trn of assets. **PASS.**

A group of four material divisions (the filer's 2025 segmentation):
- **Corporate & Investment Banking**: lending, cash management, trade finance, debt and equity capital markets, advisory, trading and custody for large corporates and institutions in the Nordics, Germany and the UK.
- **Business & Retail Banking**: mortgages, deposits, cards and advisory for Swedish households and small and medium-sized companies, plus corporate card and travel-payment services (SEB Kort, AirPlus) across Europe and North America.
- **Wealth & Asset Management**: banking, lending and advice for wealthy individuals, foundations and family offices, SEB's fund management, and unit-linked and traditional life and pension insurance (SEB Life & Pension).
- **Baltic**: retail and corporate banking in Estonia, Latvia and Lithuania.

Group Functions (treasury, staff units) is not a unit. No listed subsidiary or listed holding is material.
