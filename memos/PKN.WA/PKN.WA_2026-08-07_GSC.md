# PKN.WA — ORLEN S.A.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS · Past **WEAK** (per-share growth limb: ΔOI/sh −1.1%/yr and ΔEPS −3.7%/yr across the window — P3's flat-to-positive condition fails; FCFROCE 4.4%) · Future ADEQUATE · SF **FAIL (flag pair: state-as-controlling-party value extraction, documented + capital-allocation CAUTION)** · Cheapness FAIR → **AVOID (Past and SF gates)**.

Orlen is Central Europe's energy monopolist-of-necessity — refining, fuel retail, gas and power fused by state-directed mergers into a PLN 265bn-asset colossus. The framework's two objections are structural: a decade in which owners' per-share operating profit *shrank* while the share count nearly tripled (the 2022 Lotos/PGNiG stock mergers issued equity into a windfall the incumbents never got back per share), and a controlling shareholder — the Polish state — with a documented record of extracting value through pre-election fuel pricing, below-value remedy disposals and politically-cycled boards. The stock is only FAIR-priced (+62% from its band, the closest AVOID in this run), which is precisely the trap the SF gate exists to flag: cheap multiples on capital you don't fully own the returns to.

## 2. Understanding
Orlen is a state-controlled (49.9% Treasury) integrated energy group: crude refining and petrochemicals, ~3,500 CEE fuel stations, Poland's dominant gas import/trading position (ex-PGNiG), regulated power distribution and growing renewables/offshore — selling fuels, energy and chemicals to consumers and industry across Poland, Czechia, Lithuania and Germany; Płock-HQ'd, product of the 2022 triple-merger (Orlen–Lotos–PGNiG). **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **11.4%** (end-of-period, with-cash) — the level clears the 10% floor; FCFROCE **4.4%** — a decade's cash flow eaten by a capex programme that peaked at PLN 36bn/yr with no maintenance split disclosed (P20 not active: capex is not guided down). The gate fails on the **per-share growth limb**: ΔOI/sh **−1.1%/yr** (17.6 → 15.9 PLN over nine years), ΔEPS **−3.7%/yr**, against ΔFCF/sh +4.5% (capex-light 2016 base — stated) — shares outstanding went 427.7m → 1,161m via merger issuance, and the acquired earnings did not keep per-share pace. Year ROCE path: 18.2 → 18.7 → 14.1 → 9.6 → 6.2 → 20.9 → 17.8 → 12.6 → 4.1 → 7.6%. L/E 82.5%; year-end 2025 net debt ≈ zero. **WEAK** (P4 — below P3 on growth; the judgment that −1.1%/yr is a decline, not "flat", is logged in App C).

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth PASS both sides. Forces: customer power WEAK (fragmented fuel/energy demand) · supplier power WEAK (post-Russia crude/LNG diversified) · **substitutes STRONG** — EV adoption and heating electrification structurally shrink fuel and gas volumes (doing-without form) · entrants WEAK (nobody builds CEE refineries). Tally **1 strong, contained** near-term by the infrastructure position. Moat: **Cost/Government** — single identified source taken as **Government** (concessions, regulated tariffs, strategic-asset protection); stable as a barrier, but the same state that grants it prices it (see §5). Market growth MIXED-to-declining in the core. **ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness
Comp: trivial pass (Polish SOE pay caps; limb PLN 556m vs single-digit-million packages). **Governance/RPT — FLAG 1:** the controlling state is a related party with a documented extraction record: the Q3-2023 pre-election retail-fuel price suppression (margin collapse quantified by NIK and sell-side alike), the Lotos remedy disposals assessed by NIK as ~PLN 5bn below value (2022), and board turnover synchronised with each government change. **Capital allocation — FLAG 2 (CAUTION):** the Olefins III petrochemical mega-project's multi-billion writedowns (2023–24) are a documented value-destructive reinvestment episode; the capex programme (PLN ~30bn/yr) continues at returns visibly below the historical record, criterion-free; dividends (progressive DPS policy) are the redeeming rational lever; course change under the 2024 board is claimed, not yet demonstrated — which is what CAUTION is for. **Two independent flags → SF FAIL (§2·1C pair rule).**
