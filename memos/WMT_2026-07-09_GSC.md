# WMT — Walmart Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | SF | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | ADEQUATE | STRONG | **FAIL** | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID (SF gate)** |

Walmart is what the future gate says it is: the strongest cost moat in physical retail, now compounding through a profitable e-commerce arm, with breadth on both sides of the business that few companies anywhere can match. But the shareholder-friendliness gate fails outright, and not marginally: every buyback tranche of the last five fiscal years was executed above the P19 hard-strike line — 27–51× implied MCap/FCF, and in FY2023/25/26 above 20× on implied EV/OI as well — in record earnings years, at prices 3–7× the framework's worth line. These are systematic overpriced acquisitions of its own stock, escalating with the share price (FY2026: $8.1bn at avg $95.13, ≈28× EV/OI), with a fresh $30bn authorization now running at ~30×+ levels. A flip requires a demonstrated regime change in repurchase discipline, which the 44–46% family owner has shown no intent to impose. Price is academic: at 60× FCF and 32× EV/OI the stock sits ~650% above the band.

## 2. Understanding

Walmart sells groceries (~60% of US sales) and general merchandise at everyday low prices to hundreds of millions of mass-market consumer households — plus, increasingly, advertising and marketplace services to sellers — through ~10,600 owned-and-leased discount stores, supercenters, and Sam's Club warehouses and a top-two US e-commerce operation, as a vertically integrated, US-HQ'd (Bentonville) retailer earning ~82% of revenue domestically, the world's largest company by revenue ($713bn FY2026), 60+ years old and mid-transformation into an automated, omnichannel operator. PASS.

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized 10y averages (FY2017–26): ROCE 15.0%, FCFROCE 10.1% — the ROCE leg touches the STRONG bar and FCFROCE clears it comfortably. Growth is where STRONG fails: ΔOI/sh geo-mean +4.1% clears the 3% floor, but ΔFCF/sh only +1.1%, depressed by the capex ramp to 3.7% of sales (all-capex basis retained; overly-dark bias noted). BV/sh +4.1% alongside ~45% of FCF+ paid out. No sustained returns decline — ROCE ended the decade at its highs (17.3% wc FY2026). L/E 179% latest, within P5 (breached 200% in FY20/21/23; latest-year test governs). **ADEQUATE** — returns near-STRONG, FCF/sh growth below the floor.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: PASS both sides — customers are atomized households; no supplier nears 10% of COGS+opex (largest ~2–3%, alternatives abundant). Forces, binary: customer power WEAK (no individual buyer matters); supplier power WEAK (Walmart is the channel gorilla); substitutes WEAK (pure-online is the live threat, but the ~60% grocery anchor plus Walmart's own #2 e-commerce position blunts it — judgment logged); new entrants WEAK (scale barriers are effectively absolute). Tally 0 strong. Moat: **Cost** — procurement and distribution scale no rival can replicate, stable-to-strengthening as automation lowers unit cost. Market growth: YES, modestly (retail ≈ nominal GDP). **STRONG**.

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

P17: top-paid FY2026 employee Danker $44.1m (one-time inducement grant) — 0.30% of FCF, far under the 5% cap, but above the ≈$40m indexed absolute trigger: one flag. Directors below the $500k stop. RPT: Walton-entity payments $25.8m + ~$60m land transaction — trivial in scale, dynasty noted; family ~44–46% ownership is a genuine alignment positive. P19: **hard strike** — FY2022–26 repurchases ($35.1bn total) at implied MCap/FCF 27.3–51.1× every year, EV/OI 20.8–27.7× in three of five, non-trough years, below-worth inapplicable, not sterilisation (buybacks ~5× SBC, count −4.7%). **FAIL** (P19 hard strike; the P17 flag is a redundant second negative).

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- The P19 pattern is board policy, not accident: the new $30bn authorization at ~30×+ multiples entrenches the strike indefinitely.
- Family control (44–46%) makes external pressure on capital allocation effectively impossible.
- CEO transition (Furner, Feb 2026) is untested through a downturn; McMillon-era momentum is the consensus assumption.
- E-commerce/advertising mix shift is the margin story; a stall would compress the quality trajectory the market is paying 32× OI for.
- Grocery deflation or wage-cost shocks squeeze the thin-margin core faster than the multiple implies.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

SF flips only on a demonstrated repurchase regime change — a full program year executed below the 14×/16× flag lines, or suspension of buybacks at these prices; realistically structural, not event-driven. Past could reach STRONG if FCF/sh growth clears 3% as capex intensity normalizes. Cheapness needs ~−85% or a decade of compounding into a flat price. Next review: FY2027 10-K + 2027 proxy (~Mar–Apr 2027) re-test the execution table; no earlier trigger plausible.
