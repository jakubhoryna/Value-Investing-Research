# COPN.SW — Cosmo N.V.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | WEAK | WEAK | FAIL (P19 hard strikes) | EXPENSIVE (primaries n.m.) | **AVOID** |

Cosmo is a licensing-and-milestones pharma/medtech whose decade record is losses punctuated by one-offs: worst-of-four normalized ROCE is 2.9% against a 10% ADEQUATE floor, and the only strong year (2024, €148.9m OI) was €185m of non-recurring Medtronic payments. Revenue depends on a handful of giant licensees — the top three customers were 58.9% of 2025 revenue — and the company repurchased shares through loss years at triple-digit implied EV/OI while paying dividends it did not earn and recycling treasury stock into option exercises. Three gates fail independently; price (n.m. on negative FY2025 OI and FCF, ~12× revenue) is moot. A flip would require several consecutive years of recurring operating profit and FCF at double-digit returns on capital plus a reformed buyback discipline — nothing shorter.

## 2. Understanding

Cosmo N.V. is a Netherlands-incorporated, Dublin-headquartered, SIX-listed life-sciences licensor (~325 employees, founded 1997, repeatedly transformed) that develops gastrointestinal and dermatology drugs (MMX colonic delivery: Lialda/Mezavant, Uceris/Cortiment, Aemcolo; clascoterone: Winlevi for acne, Breezula for hair loss) and the GI Genius AI colonoscopy module, manufactures in Lainate, Italy (own products plus CDMO), and earns most revenue as royalties, milestones, upfronts and supply fees from large pharma/medtech licensees — Medtronic, Sun Pharma, Takeda, Bausch, Ferring, Glenmark — rather than selling to patients itself. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Nine fiscal years available (FY2017–25; FY2016 not in company disclosures — flagged; STRONG unavailable regardless). Worst-of-four normalized ROCE 2.9% (end/wc; best variant 4.9%) — far below the P3 10% floor. FCFROCE (2019–25, gaps flagged) worst-of-four 4.0% vs the 8% bar. Operating losses in 2017, 2018, 2019 and 2025; 2024's €148.9m OI was €185m of non-recurring Medtronic recognition. ΔOI/sh and ΔFCF/sh geo-means n.m. (negative endpoints); BV/sh €31.7 → €30.2 over nine years despite retained one-offs. L/E 25.6%, debt-free — the balance sheet is the only clean line. **WEAK** — returns never reached adequacy in any normal year.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: customer side **FAIL** — top three customers 25.0% / 18.3% / 15.6% of 2025 revenue (2024: largest 72.4%); captive-customer waiver unavailable (Medtronic can backward-integrate in AI, switching costs not prohibitive, moat not dominant). Supplier side passes (own plant; NVIDIA IGX dependency for Module 300 noted, alternatives exist). Forces: customer bargaining power STRONG (few giant licensees set the economics); supplier power WEAK; threat of substitutes STRONG (competing CADe systems from Fujifilm, Olympus, others; legacy mesalamine/budesonide genericised); new entrants WEAK (FDA/CE permits, patents). Tally 2 strong = stop signal. Moat: product-specific patents/exclusivity mediated through licensees — narrow, stable-to-eroding. Market growth YES (AI endoscopy, dermatology) — attracts capital and competition. **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Dividends of ~€33m/yr paid against −€16.7m FY2025 FCF while re-issuing equity (Feb 2026 placement of 937k treasury shares). Founder Ajani holds ~38% — genuine alignment, the lone positive. RPT clean. **FAIL — P19 hard strikes, multiple.**

- Conversely for any future WATCH case: Medtronic building or buying its own CADe would hole the largest revenue line.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

AVOID rests on three independent gates; the earliest plausible mover is Past/Future evidence, not price. Flip requires: (a) ≥3 consecutive years of recurring (non-milestone) operating profit and positive FCF at ≥10% normalized ROCE; (b) customer concentration falling below the 10% test or qualifying for a waiver it currently fails; (c) buyback discipline — no repurchases above 14× EV/OI and no treasury-to-options recycling. Next review: FY2026 results (~Mar 2027); earlier only if the AGA readout or a structural licensing deal changes the revenue mix materially.
