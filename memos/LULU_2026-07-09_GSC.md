## 2. Understanding

lululemon sells premium technical athletic and lifestyle apparel, footwear, and accessories (differentiated branded goods) directly to individual retail "guests" via ~770+ company-operated stores and e-commerce plus limited wholesale — a vertically integrated, single-share-class Delaware retailer HQ'd in Vancouver, sourcing from Asia (~34% of fabric from Taiwan), revenue ~71% Americas / 16% China / 13% RoW; a ~$13B, ~19-year public company past its US growth peak. **Since v1:** permanent CEO **Heidi O'Neill** (ex-Nike President, Consumer/Product/Brand) named, effective 8 Sep 2026 (interim co-CEOs bridge); founder proxy contest **settled** (AGM 25 Jun 2026). **PASS.**
