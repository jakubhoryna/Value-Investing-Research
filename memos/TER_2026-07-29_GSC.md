TER is a good business buying its own stock at prices its economics do not support; the AI cycle does not change that, and the verdict flips only on demonstrated repurchase discipline.

## 2. Understanding

Teradyne designs and sells automated test equipment — chiefly semiconductor SoC and memory testers (Semiconductor Test, the dominant unit, >$1.0bn of Q1-2026 revenue), plus Product Test (board, wireless/LitePoint, photonics, defence) and Robotics (Universal Robots, MiR) — to semiconductor makers, foundries and OSATs, and to industrial manufacturers for robotics; a ~65-year-old Massachusetts-HQ'd global leader in an Asia-weighted, deeply cyclical capital-equipment industry. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Restated, not re-derived — no new fiscal year has closed. Worst-of-four normalized averages over FY2016–25 remain **21.5% ROCE** and **17.7% FCFROCE** (both the end-of-period, cash-inclusive cut), against 15%/8% floors; all four cuts clear. Forces: customer power **STRONG**, the other three WEAK — one strong, contained. Moat (switching costs, scale, duopoly ingrainedness) stable-to-strengthening. Market growth YES. **ADEQUATE.**
