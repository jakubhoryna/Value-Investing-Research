# 5908.KL — DKSH Holdings (Malaysia) Berhad: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS (two material units: Consumer Goods and Healthcare); Past WEAK (ROCE decline); Future WEAK (unit customer conc.); Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (RPT); cheapness FAIR — **AVOID (Past + Future + SF)**.

DKSH Malaysia is the 74%-owned Malaysian arm of the Swiss distributor DKSH: it buys consumer goods and medicines from brand owners and resells them to Malaysian shops, hospitals, clinics and pharmacies, earning under 3% of sales. Operating returns on capital are near 11–12%, but working capital absorbs most of the profit: free cash flow averaged 4% of capital over ten years and fell to 2.5% in the last three. Liabilities are well over twice equity, and the company pays its parent group royalties for the DKSH name and management fees. A move to WATCH would need cash conversion to recover, leverage under 2×, and the parent's fees to stop.
