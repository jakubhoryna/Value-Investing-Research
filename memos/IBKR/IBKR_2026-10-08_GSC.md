Interactive Brokers is a low-cost, highly automated global broker whose profits now come mostly from interest on customer cash and margin loans. The v4.5.0 re-test removes the customer-float adjustment the earlier version relied on. Customer liabilities now count in capital employed and in leverage. Margin lending is a lending activity funded by customer credit balances, so that funding enters EV. On that reading no positive price makes the stock cheap.

## 2. Understanding

Interactive Brokers runs an automated, self-clearing electronic brokerage. It executes, clears and custodies trades in stocks, options, futures, currencies and bonds on more than 170 venues, and it earns commissions plus interest on margin loans, securities lending and segregated customer cash. The payers are about 4.4 million individual and institutional accounts (active traders, hedge funds, advisers, proprietary firms, introducing brokers) in over 200 countries. The company is a US Up-C holding company that controls and consolidates IBG LLC, whose broker-dealer and futures subsidiaries operate globally. It is a large, founder-controlled business with one reportable segment. **PASS.** Lens STD: the regulated entities are broker-dealers and futures commission merchants, and none is a licensed bank or insurer.
