# TRNS — Transcat, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Past **WEAK** (worst-of-four ROCE 4.83%, FCFROCE 6.14%, and a return path falling 7.6 → 5.1 → 3.0% as the balance sheet doubled) · Future ADEQUATE · SF **FAIL** (P17 hard strike, both reads: $5.86m against ceilings of $0.27m and $0.56m) · EXPENSIVE (75× EV/OI; both band bounds negative) → **AVOID (three gates)**.

Transcat owns a genuinely good business and is burying it under acquisitions. Accredited instrument calibration is recurring, regulated and sticky — pharma and aerospace customers cannot skip it. But over three fiscal years the company spent **$183m on acquisitions**, took debt from $1.8m to $99.9m, grew total assets $196m → $480m, and watched operating income fall from $19.8m to $13.3m; returns on capital went 7.6% → 3.0%. The market pays 75× that operating income. The compensation strike — $5.86m to a departing chief executive in a year the company earned $5.4m — is what happens when the denominator collapses and the package does not.

## 2. Understanding
Transcat provides accredited calibration and laboratory-instrument services to regulated industries — life sciences, pharmaceutical, aerospace, energy — through a network of labs and on-site programmes, and separately distributes test and measurement instruments made by third parties; Rochester, New York-HQ'd, no controlling holder. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **4.83%** (end-basis, with-cash; best variant 6.71%) and worst FCFROCE **6.14%** — both well under their floors. The path is the story: **7.6 → 5.1 → 3.0%**, two consecutive declines, driven by a denominator growing faster than anything else. Capital employed went $170m → $441m on $183m of acquisitions with the share count barely moving, while operating income went $19.8m → $17.9m → $13.3m and operating income per share fell 33%. Free cash flow held at roughly $19–26m a year — the encouraging part, and the measure of how little the acquired assets have contributed. Leverage is not yet the problem at 59.8%, but debt went from nothing to $99.9m in two years. **WEAK.**

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth PASS both sides. Forces at segment level: in Service, customer power WEAK (fragmented regulated buyers), substitutes WEAK (calibration is mandated), entrants WATCH (accreditation and lab density are slow to build) — a good structure; in Distribution, **supplier power STRONG**, since the segment resells instruments from a concentrated set of manufacturers on their terms. Tally 1 strong, contained by mix. Moat: **Switching costs** — calibration records, validated procedures and audit trails follow the provider, and regulated customers change vendors reluctantly; stable. Market growth YES. **ADEQUATE** — the service franchise would support more; the distribution half does not.

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness
Comp: **P17 hard strike.** FY2026 ceiling **$0.269m** (net income is the lower leg) against a highest-paid total of **$5.862m — 21.8×**; trailing-3-year ceiling **$0.559m — 10.5×**. Both reads. The package belongs to the departing chief executive and reflects a transition award, which the test does not weigh: the total is the total, paid against a year in which the company earned $5.4m. Secondary-sourced with a parsing caveat, which a 10–22× cushion makes immaterial. Capital allocation: **CAUTION** — $183m of acquisitions over three fiscal years at undisclosed multiples, debt-funded from a standing start, with operating income lower at the end than the beginning; no dividends, no repurchases. Pricing evidence is INDETERMINATE; the aggregate is not — more capital, less profit. **FAIL** on the strike, with the caution alongside.

## 6. Price
At $93.11 the equity is $871m against $13.3m of operating income — **EV/OI 75.4× / 75.1×**, **MCap/FCF 44.6×**. Both band bounds are negative because $128.9m of debt and leases exceed seven times operating income; the restoring condition is **operating income above ≈ $18.4m**, roughly the FY2025 level, so a single decent year makes the band computable again. The free-cash-flow leg, which does not bind, sits at $16.71. **EXPENSIVE.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers (context for the AVOID)
The bull case is an integration trough: acquired labs carry cost before margin, service organic growth is high single-digit, and $332m of revenue at historic service margins would produce far more than $13m of operating income. The FY2027 result tests it directly. The bear case is that the acquisitions were priced on the synergies, and the operating-income decline is the answer rather than the delay.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review
Past needs operating income near $45m on today's capital for the return level, and the decline broken first. Price needs operating income above ~$18.4m merely for a positive bound. The comp strike should clear naturally once the transition award is out of the comparison — the new chief executive's first full-year package is the test.
