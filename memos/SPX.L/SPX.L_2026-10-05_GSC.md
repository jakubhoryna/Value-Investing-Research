# SPX.L — Spirax Group plc: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS; Past WEAK; Future ADEQUATE; Shareholder-friendliness PASS (one flag: capital-allocation CAUTION); Cheapness EXPENSIVE. **AVOID (Past)**.

Spirax sells steam-system components, electric process heaters and peristaltic pumps. Most of the revenue is maintenance spending by many small customers. Steam Thermal Solutions and Watson-Marlow each earn about 24% statutory operating profit on their segment assets. The problem is the capital added around them. Since 2017 about £1.0bn has gone into four electric-heating businesses: Chromalox, Thermocoax, Vulcanic and Durex. That segment now earns 3.7% statutory (6.5% adjusted) on £1.1bn of assets. Group ROCE has fallen from 22% to 11% over the decade, and FY2022–25 are the four weakest years in the window. The level (worst-of-four 14.5%) would be ADEQUATE; the decline makes it WEAK. The verdict flips if FY2026 returns break above the FY2022–25 band. At 7,275p the stock is in any case 29× FCF and 24×/23× EV/OI.

## 2. Understanding
Spirax designs, makes and sells, mostly through its own engineers, three product families. Steam traps, valves, controls and condensate-recovery systems (Spirax Sarco, Gestra; 50% of FY2025 revenue). Electric process heaters, heat tracing and high-precision thermal components (Chromalox, Vulcanic, Thermocoax, Durex; 26%). Peristaltic pumps, tubing and single-use fluid paths, largely for biopharma (Watson-Marlow; 24%). Customers are operators and OEMs in food and beverage, pharma, chemicals, power, healthcare and semiconductors worldwide, and about 85% of sales come out of their operating budgets. It is a Cheltenham-headquartered FTSE 100 company dating from 1888, renamed Spirax Group in 2024. **PASS**.

## 3. Quality — past record
Worst-of-four normalized ROCE is **14.5%** and FCFROCE **8.5%**, both on end-of-period capital including cash. That is short of the 15% STRONG line and above ADEQUATE. Year by year, ROCE fell from 22.4% (FY2016) to 14.4% (FY2019), recovered to 19.3% (FY2021), then dropped to 12.6%, 11.5%, 12.7% and 11.1%. Statutory operating income fell 17% from FY2021 to FY2025 while revenue rose 27%. Per-share growth is above the floor: OI/sh +6.5% and FCF/sh +6.6% a year (FY2015–25). L/E is 118%. **WEAK** — a sustained decline in returns (P4) overrides the ADEQUATE level.

## 4. Quality — future

Steam, electric heat and pump tubing are all Spirax products, so the decarbonisation shift from steam to electric stays largely in-house. Entrants are WEAK for steam and Watson-Marlow: the direct engineer network and biopharma process validation keep them out. For the electric-heating unit, entrants are STRONG: margins are thin and the field is fragmented. Tally: one strong force, contained to a segment. The moat is switching costs, stable.
