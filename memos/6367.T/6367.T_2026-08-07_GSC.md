# 6367.T — Daikin Industries, Ltd.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS · Past **WEAK** (worst-of-four ROCE 9.1%, three straight declines — P4 on both limbs) · Future STRONG · SF PASS (1 flag — capital-allocation CAUTION) · Cheapness EXPENSIVE → **AVOID (Past gate)**.

Daikin is the world's #1 HVAC maker with a genuinely strong franchise — installer-channel ingrainedness, heat-pump secular demand, 0 strong forces. The framework's problem is arithmetic, not narrative: a ¥5.3tn capital base now earns 7.8% with-cash returns, down from 11%+ pre-Covid, FCFROCE has averaged 4.9% through a capex super-ramp, and returns have declined three consecutive years. The verdict flips only if the US/heat-pump reinvestment wave starts earning its keep — a visible w-o-4 recovery through 10% — and even then the price is 3× the band.

## 2. Understanding
Daikin designs, manufactures and sells air conditioners, heat pumps, applied HVAC systems and the refrigerants and chemicals behind them — differentiated hardware plus an installer/dealer service network — to consumers, businesses and building owners worldwide (84% of sales overseas; #1 globally), from Osaka, a 101-year-old maker that tripled revenue in a decade partly via the 2012 Goodman (US) acquisition and a post-2021 US/European heat-pump build-out. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **9.1%** (end-of-period, with-cash; best variant 12.5%) — **below the 10% ADEQUATE floor**; worst FCFROCE **4.9%**, depressed by a capex ramp from ¥86bn to ¥246bn/yr without a disclosed maintenance split. End-basis ROCE has fallen **FY23 9.6% → 8.7% → 8.5% → 7.8%** — three consecutive declines, the P4 trigger, on top of the sub-floor average. Growth is fine (ΔOI/sh +6.7%, ΔEPS +6.6%, ΔBV/sh +12.2% geo) and leverage modest (L/E 76.7%) — the business grows; the capital pile grows faster. **WEAK** (P4: below P3 *and* sustained decline). Window record-limited at 9y — cap moot at this level.

## 4. Quality — future

Tally **0 strong — favourable**. Moat: **Ingrainedness** — the installer/dealer and applied-service channel that specifies Daikin by default; stable.
