Upstart sells an underwriting model, and the five-year record is what happens when the buyer of that model can walk. Cumulative operating income across FY2021–25 is **−$360m**; all four normalized ROCE variants are negative (worst −9.7%), and the one clean year, FY2021, was a funding-market accident that reversed inside twelve months. One fact drives both quality gates: **the top three lending partners originate 83% of volume and pay 61% of revenue** — a breadth failure on the arithmetic, waiver denied at limb one, because a bank can and does bring underwriting back in house.
