## 2. Understanding

Indutrade AB is a Stockholm-listed, Lundberg-anchored Swedish holding company — a decentralised serial acquirer of ~220 autonomous subsidiaries across ~30 countries — whose companies manufacture and distribute niche technical components, instruments and systems (flow, sensors, medical and laboratory equipment, industrial and vehicle components) to European industrial, infrastructure, life-science and water/energy organisations, none above 3% of sales, growing chiefly by buying founder-owned businesses at single-digit EBITA multiples and leaving their managements in place. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Returns are consistent but unspectacular: worst-of-four normalized ROCE 14.1%, FCFROCE 11.7%. Cash conversion is genuine — FCFROCE tracks ROCE across the window, unusual for an acquisitive group. Per-share growth is strong: OI/share compounded 12.7% and FCF/share 16.3% over nine periods, far above the 3% floor, on a share count up 1.0% in a decade. Leverage is conservative at 94% liabilities-to-equity. The 15% bar is missed and the last two years are the weakest since 2017. **ADEQUATE** — clears 10%, not 15%, and the direction is wrong.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth passes on both legs, explicitly disclosed: no customer above 3% of sales, no group-level supplier reliance. Forces — customer power WEAK (atomised base, specified-in components); supplier power WEAK at group level (no material principal, ~half of sales own-manufactured); substitutes WEAK (certified components, installed base); new entrants **STRONG**, because barriers protect each niche subsidiary but not the acquisition engine that creates the value, where Lifco, Lagercrantz, Addtech, Röko, Teqnion and private equity bid for the same assets. Tally 1 strong, contained to capital allocation. Moat: switching costs, subsidiary level, stable. Market MIXED — 2025 sales −1%, H1 2026 +11%. **ADEQUATE.**

## C. Calculation notes

**NIBCL build (FY2025).** Trade payables 1,819 + contract liabilities 352 + current tax liabilities 364 + accrued expenses 1,299 = **3,834**. "Other current liabilities" SEK 856m is **left in capital employed** under the burden-of-proof rule — the line is unbroken in the notes and cannot be shown to be non-interest-bearing operating float. Same treatment applied consistently to all eleven years. Alternate reading (sweeping the residual into NIBCL) lifts worst-of-four ROCE from 14.05% to approximately 14.4% — still short of 15%, so the verdict is insensitive to the call. Current interest-bearing liabilities stay in CE throughout.

**CE build (FY2025, ending).** CE_wc = total assets 33,229 − NIBCL 3,834 = **29,395**. CE_xc = 29,395 − cash 2,393 = **27,002**. No short-term investments are reported separately. Goodwill retained in CE — Indutrade is a serial acquirer by design and the framework's non-acquisitive exception cannot apply.

**TBV build (FY2025).** Equity attributable to owners 17,114 − goodwill 9,930 − other intangible assets 4,913 = **SEK 2,271m** (SEK 6.23/share). At 30 Jun 2026: 17,880 − 11,142 − 5,483 = **SEK 1,255m**.

**EV build (24 Jul 2026 price, 30 Jun 2026 balance sheet).** Market cap = SEK 242.40 × 364,323k = **88,312**. Debt = non-current interest-bearing and pension liabilities 7,060 + current interest-bearing 4,123 = **11,183** (includes IFRS 16 lease liabilities as debt-equivalent and pension obligations). NCI 6. No preferred. Cash 1,362. **EV (all cash excess, lower) = 98,139. EV (no cash excess, conservative) = 99,501.**

**IFRS interest reclassification — not required.** Indutrade reports interest paid (SEK −447m FY2025) and interest received inside cash flow from operating activities. No adjustment made; verified against the FY2025 consolidated cash-flow statement.

**Leases.** IFRS 16 applied from FY2019; comparatives not restated. P18 tested and not triggered for FY2016–FY2018 (see Appendix A note 2). Lease liabilities are inside interest-bearing liabilities from FY2019 and therefore inside CE, EV and L/E throughout the post-adoption window; the FY2019 adoption-year step-up in both PP&E and debt is the visible artifact in the B2 and B3 series.

**Maintenance-capex basis (P20) — not active.** Capex runs 1.4–1.9% of sales across the whole window with no ramp, no expansion-labelled programme and no guided step-down. All-capex FCF used throughout, on both gates.

**Operating income as reported — no add-backs.** FY2025 OI includes SEK 208m of contingent-consideration remeasurement gains recognised in other operating income (FY2024: SEK 186m). These are recurring for this business model and are neither added back nor stripped out; multi-year averaging is the framework's only normalisation. Flagged in the body because the quantum is 5.5% of FY2025 OI.

**Past verdict judgment call — the decline clause.** ROCE_wc has fallen two consecutive years (15.2% → 13.3% → 12.8%). P4 turns Past WEAK on a *sustained* decline. Called ADEQUATE, not WEAK, because: the ten-year series is range-bound between 12.8% and 15.4% with no trend; the FY2025 level matches FY2016–17; and the compression is traceable to the FY2022–25 acquisition wave loading capital employed ahead of earnings plus a soft European industrial year (sales −1%). This is the one judgment in the memo that decides the verdict. A third consecutive decline at FY2026 removes the defence and the call becomes WEAK. Registered as a named re-test.

**Bias check.** Live: **cleverness** — the decentralised-compounder story is intellectually attractive and the acquisition-multiple discipline is genuinely impressive, which creates pressure to promote Past to STRONG on narrative. Handled by holding the arithmetic: 14.05% is below 15%, so STRONG is unavailable and P12 is not. Also noted: **consistency** with the LIFCO-B verdict reached earlier today on a near-identical business model — checked by computing INDT's gates independently before comparing. No other bias live.
