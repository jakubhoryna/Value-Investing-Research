# META — Meta Platforms, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | STRONG | ADEQUATE | PASS (dual-class flag) | EXPENSIVE | **WATCH** |

Meta is a rare combination of an extraordinary economic engine and a deeply concentrated governance structure, trading at a price that prices in continued success. The Family of Apps segment threw off ~$102B of operating income in 2025 (~51% segment margin) on one of the world's strongest network-effect moats — 3.58B daily users, with ad impressions up 12% **and** price-per-ad up 9%, proof the pricing power is intact and growing. What stands between here and a BUY is purely price: all four valuation gates fail, and the binding constraint (MCap/FCF) is itself depressed by an AI-capex surge that management is *doubling* to $115–135B in 2026. The verdict flips to BUY only on a large drawdown (~$180 or below) **or** a credible signal that the capital pour is converting to free cash flow rather than perpetual reinvestment. The single biggest non-price caveat is structural: Mark Zuckerberg controls ~61% of votes on ~14% of the economics and is sole deciding vote on everything — outside owners are along for the ride, not steering.

## 2. Understanding

Meta Platforms is the global leader in social-media and messaging apps (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp, Messenger, Threads), a US-incorporated company earning ~98% of revenue selling targeted digital advertising to millions of businesses worldwide, alongside a small, heavily loss-making Reality Labs AR/VR-and-AI-hardware segment. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Elite and consistent. Over FY2016–2025, ROCE (ex-cash, beginning-of-period) ran 29.5%–63.8% — above the 15% bar in **10 of 10** years — and FCFROCE (ex-cash) cleared 8% in **10 of 10**. Per-share compounding is dramatic: operating income/share +662% (\$4.25→\$32.35), FCF/share +351%, tangible book/share +468% over the decade. The only blemish was 2022 (metaverse spend + ad recession: OI/sh −34%, ROCE_xc still 29.5%), fully recovered by 2023. Leverage is conservative — total-liabilities/equity peaked at 68% in 2025, far below the 200% flag. **STRONG.**

## 4. Quality — future

Customer breadth is clearly broad (millions of advertisers, no concentration); supplier breadth carries real concentration — Nvidia compute, the Apple/Google distribution duopoly (ATT once cost ~\$10B/yr) — but pricing power is demonstrably undamaged and Meta is vertically integrating (custom silicon). Five forces are **MIXED-to-unfavourable**: rivalry HIGH (TikTok, YouTube, Google, Amazon), substitutes/supplier power elevated, offset by a powerful moat. Moat = **Network + Cost/scale + Ingrainedness**, stable-to-strengthening (DAP +7%; AI deepening ad relevance). Digital-ad market growing; Meta gaining share (rev +22%). Not STRONG (forces aren't favourable); not WEAK (moat demonstrably stable). **ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

CEO pay is exemplary (Zuckerberg: \$1 salary, no bonus, no new equity; "comp" is security/aircraft costs) and insider economic alignment is best-in-class (founder, ~14% stake). Buybacks are value-creating: \$148.6B over five years vs \$72.3B of SBC — net-reducing diluted shares ~12% over the decade, including accretive repurchases during the 2022 trough — not mere option-sterilisation. Capital allocation is rational (reinvest at high ROCE; modest 0.4% dividend; no empire-building external M&A). **Strike risk: the dual-class structure** concentrates ~61% of votes in Zuckerberg on ~14% economics, combined Chair/CEO, all shareholder proposals defeated regardless of Class A support. Scored **PASS** with this as the dominant flag (see Appendix E); a stricter governance reading would FAIL test 1 → AVOID.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- **The AI capital bet**: 2026 capex guided to \$115–135B (~2× 2025) for "personal superintelligence"; if returns don't materialise, FCF stays suppressed and the reinvestment thesis breaks.
- **Reality Labs**: −\$19.2B operating loss in 2025 (cumulative >\$80B); guided to remain similar in 2026 — a persistent, unaccountable cash drain.
- **Governance**: zero outside-owner check on strategy; a single person can commit the company to multi-year, tens-of-billions bets (already demonstrated in the metaverse era).
- **Regulatory/litigation**: FTC monopoly loss is on appeal (residual breakup tail); March-2026 child-safety jury verdicts and a California scam-ads suit raise liability and EU/Less-Personalized-Ads headwinds.
- **Attention competition**: TikTok/YouTube short-video rivalry pressures engagement of younger cohorts and the aging Facebook surface.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

- **To BUY**: price falls below ~$180 (FCF-binding) — or capex normalises such that FCF re-rates and the band lifts toward ~$225 with price below it.
- **Toward AVOID**: a sustained ROCE/FCFROCE decline as the capex base compounds without commensurate OI growth; or a governance/capital-allocation event that one judges crosses the SF line.
- **Next review**: FY2026 full-year results (~late Jan 2027); or each quarter's capex/FCF trajectory update; or price entering the trigger band; or the FTC appeal ruling.

## D. Future-performance worksheet

**Breadth** — Customers: BROAD; ~98% of revenue is advertising from millions of businesses, no single customer material; demand unlikely to consolidate. Suppliers: CONCENTRATED but mitigated — Nvidia (compute), Apple/Google (distribution gatekeepers; ATT precedent) are real chokepoints, yet FY2025 price-per-ad +9% with impressions +12% shows pricing power undamaged, and Meta is integrating vertically (custom silicon). Net breadth: PASS (with supplier caveat).

**Five forces** — Customer (advertiser) power: LOW–MEDIUM (fragmented, but budgets are mobile). Supplier power: MEDIUM–HIGH (GPUs, talent wars, platform gatekeepers). Substitutes: MEDIUM–HIGH (TikTok/YouTube/CTV/retail media). New entrants: LOW–MEDIUM (network barriers high; TikTok proved attention is contestable). Rivalry: HIGH. **Overall: MIXED-to-unfavourable.**

**Moat** — Network effect (3.58B DAP; WhatsApp/Messenger lock-in) + Cost/scale (only a handful can spend \$100B+ on AI infra) + Ingrainedness (habitual daily use). Direction: **stable-to-strengthening** (engagement growing; AI improving ad relevance/recommendations), with mild erosion at the youth/legacy-Facebook margin.

**Market growth** — YES (global digital-ad spend growing; Meta taking share). Per Marshall's caveat, growth attracts the very rivalry above; treated as supporting, not decisive.

The FCF metric binds purely because capex is at a cyclical/strategic peak; on an OI or TBV basis the stock implies ≈$225, so the band is highly sensitive to the capex-to-FCF trajectory.
