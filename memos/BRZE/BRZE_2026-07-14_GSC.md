# BRZE — Braze, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | SF | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | WEAK | WEAK | FAIL | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID (triple gate)** |

Braze is a well-run product in a brutally competitive category, growing revenue 24% into a GAAP operating loss that has not narrowed in dollar terms since FY2023. The framework fails it three independent ways: seven consecutive years of negative operating income (Past WEAK), two strong competitive forces in martech (Future WEAK), and an SF FAIL carrying both a P17 comp hard strike and P19 buyback hard strikes — including a $50m ASR executed in Q1 FY27, while GAAP-loss-making, with management explicitly framing it as offsetting SBC dilution: the textbook sterilisation strike. No price flips a quality-based AVOID; a flip requires sustained GAAP operating profitability, a reformed comp/buyback posture, and a competitive structure that hasn't existed in this category. Structural AVOID — the row's value is saving future time.

## 2. Understanding

Braze sells differentiated subscription software — a customer-engagement platform ingesting first-party data to orchestrate automated cross-channel messaging (email, push, in-app, SMS, WhatsApp) plus newly acquired AI "decisioning" agents — paid for by the marketing departments of ~2,609 consumer-facing organisations (333 large customers ≥ $500k ARR = 64% of ARR) worldwide; martech/CDP industry; Delaware C-corp, dual-class (founder Class B super-votes), vertically integrated software run on rented hyperscaler infrastructure; HQ New York, global operations; founded 2011, IPO Nov 2021, ~$738m revenue — a prominent independent amid suite giants, currently bolting on agentic AI (OfferFit, June 2025). **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

OI negative in all seven available fiscal years (FY20 −$33.5m → FY26 −$144.8m; no dollar improvement since FY23 despite revenue doubling). All four normalized-average ROCE variants negative (most economically meaningful, end-of-period CE_wc: −22.6%; worst tested, beg CE_xc: −590% on tiny deferred-revenue-funded CE — arithmetic artifact, sign is what matters); all four FCFROCE variants negative. FCF turned positive FY24 and reached $58.0m FY26 — but FY26 SBC was $143.7m: FCF is positive only because equity issuance is excluded from the calculation, not because the business earns its capital. L/E 78.5%, no debt — leverage fine, irrelevant. Thin history (7 flow years, 6 balance sheets) — STRONG unavailable regardless. **WEAK** — P4 on level, trivially.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: customer PASS (2,609 customers, none material); supplier PASS with the ready-alternatives exception logged — hosting concentrated on hyperscalers, but AWS/GCP/Azure all stand ready. Forces: customer power WEAK (many customers, embedded workflows); supplier power WEAK (competing hyperscalers); **threat of substitutes STRONG** (Salesforce/Adobe suites, Klaviyo, Iterable, CleverTap, and increasingly in-house AI agents — renewal-time substitution is easy and DBNR has decelerated to 109%); **threat of new entrants STRONG** (AI collapses the cost of building engagement tooling; Klaviyo proved the category admits new winners; scale and integrations are barriers to success, not to entry). Tally 2 strong = the book's stop signal. Moat: switching costs — real but modest, not dominant; no moat-decides floor available. Market growth YES. **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

**P17 hard strike:** CEO Magnuson FY2026 total ≈ $14.2m (DEF 14A 18 May 2026, secondary parse) vs 5% of lower(FCF $58.0m, NI −$131.3m) — the NI leg is negative, so any comp breaches; even the FCF leg caps at $2.9m (4.9× over). OfferFit ($303.2m, June 2025, −$1.9m net tangible assets, $233.7m goodwill) is pre-profit → judgmental: logged as aggressive. RPT: nothing material found (proxy ordinary-course only). Insiders: 0 buys / 18 sells in 6 months — inconclusive per model. Dual-class caveat logged. **FAIL** — multiple independent hard strikes.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- The AVOID rests on structure, not price: martech substitution pressure and the SBC-funded economics are durable features, not cyclical ones.
- A sustained GAAP-OI inflection (guidance implies non-GAAP progress only) would restore a computable band and force a Past re-read — watch the FY27 print.
- Agentic AI cuts both ways: OfferFit/BrazeAI may extend the product, or in-house AI agents may disintermediate the whole category.
- Suite consolidation (Salesforce/Adobe bundling) could compress pricing faster than customer count grows.
- A buyout is plausible at this size and would terminate the row.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

Flip to WATCH requires all of: sustained positive GAAP OI (Past re-read still needs years of positive ROCE — realistically FY2030+), comp brought under the P17 line or NI positive enough to carry it, and a full programme year of buyback discipline (no sterilisation, sub-flag multiples). Cheapness alone can never flip it. **Next review:** FY2027 10-K (~Mar 2027) — first print that could restore a band; re-test P19 on the full FY27 buyback table.
