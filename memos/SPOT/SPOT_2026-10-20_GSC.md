# SPOT — Spotify Technology S.A.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past WEAK; Future WEAK; Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (compensation and buybacks strikes); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID**, unchanged, still on all three quality gates.

Spotify is a great consumer product, profitable only since 2024. Its ten-year record fails on returns: worst-of-eight ROCE 0.3% with the Tencent Music stake and its 2021 loss, 4.8% without. The Future fails structurally, because three major labels and Merlin set the cost of 68% of revenue and identical catalogues sit one tap away. Shareholder-friendliness fails twice over. The board raised a loss-year pay package in FY2023, and it kept buying stock at 39–56 times operating income through August 2026, then topped the programme up by US$1.5bn. A flip needs years of returns above 10%, broken label power and repurchases below worth — none a price event.

## 2. Understanding

**Group sentence.** Spotify sells on-demand audio streaming — music licensed mainly from Universal, Sony, Warner and the independent-label agent Merlin, plus podcasts (some owned) and audiobooks — to individuals who pay for Premium subscriptions (290 million at FY2025 year-end, 89% of revenue) and to advertisers and agencies who buy audio and podcast ads against its free tier (11%), in 184 markets. It is a Luxembourg holding company run from Stockholm, whose two founders control 69% of the votes through non-economic beneficiary certificates; a 20-year-old, NYSE-listed (2018) large-cap, profitable since 2024 and run by co-CEOs since 1 January 2026. **PASS.** Lens STD.

Both reportable segments are material (P22; Ad-Supported is 10.7% of FY2025 revenue), so the group rules apply:
- **Premium** (89.3% of revenue): subscription access to catalogue, podcasts and audiobook hours, paid by individuals directly or via partner bundles.
- **Ad-Supported** (10.7%): free-tier music and podcast ad inventory, paid by advertisers, agencies and automated channels.

## 3. Quality — past record

The record now runs a full ten years, FY2016–FY2025, on eleven balance sheets. The Tencent Music stake is above the P31 line, so both readings apply. The include reading carries the stake's €1,219m fair-value loss of 2021, and its worst-of-eight ROCE is 0.3% over the window and 4.4% over the latest five years; the exclude reading gives 4.8%. FCFROCE on maintenance-capex FCF (constructed) is 15.2%, 14.9% all-capex. ΔOI/sh is n.m.: seven of ten years lost money. ΔFCF/sh is n.m. on the geometric read, because FY2022 FCF was negative. L/E is 0.80. No J6 decline. **WEAK** — 0.3% ROCE, with growth n.m. as a second failure.

## 4. Quality — future

Premium carries the decisive concentration. The majors and Merlin supply about 72% of label-delivered streams, and each major exceeds 10% of costs. Ad-Supported adds a silence finding: organisations pay all of that unit's revenue, and no filer statement rules out a customer above 10%. No moat carries either flag. Supplier power is STRONG in both units and substitutes are STRONG: identical catalogues at Apple, Amazon and YouTube, with YouTube's free tier. Customer power is STRONG in Ad-Supported. Each unit has two or more strong forces. The moat is switching costs (playlists, taste data, ubiquity), stable. Market growth YES. S = 1.00. **WEAK** — unit supplier conc.

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

The hairiest number is Gustav Söderström's FY2024 grant-date total, $18.7m. Proportionality passes on the fiscal-year read but breaches on the trailing read. FY2023 was a loss year in which his total rose from $14.2m to $14.7m, with the trailing leg also negative: an executive-pay **strike**. Martin Lorentzon's $0.75m FY2024 director total is above the ≈ $666k cap, a flag. Management holds 5.9% of shares: ALIGNED, with votes separated. Repurchases in FY2025 and 2026 ran at 32–56× on every read. The programme is live and upsized, and the stock is still above worth: ADVERSE. **FAIL** at step 1, two hard strikes.
