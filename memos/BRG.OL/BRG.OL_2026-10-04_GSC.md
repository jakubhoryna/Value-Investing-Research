## 2. Understanding

Borregaard ASA is an Oslo-listed (since 2012) Norwegian chemicals company that runs one integrated wood biorefinery at Sarpsborg, plus smaller lignin plants beside third-party pulp mills in the US and Europe, selling differentiated lignin-based binders and dispersants (BioSolutions, 57% of 2025 sales), speciality cellulose for ethers and acetate (BioMaterials, 35%) and contrast-agent intermediates and bioethanol (Fine Chemicals, 9%) to about 2,800 industrial customers in construction, agriculture, chemicals, food and pharma across Europe, the Americas and Asia, with costs mainly in NOK and sales mainly in USD and EUR. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized ROCE is 14.4% (closing capital, cash included); the best read is 16.2%. Worst FCFROCE is 5.5% on all capex (9.3% on replacement capex): the 2017–2018 Florida plant build and steady expansion spending absorb cash. ROCE fell to 9–11% in 2018–2020, recovered to 19% in 2022 and has eased each year since to 15.0% in 2025; the second five-year half averages higher than the first. Operating income per share compounded 9.2% a year and book value per share 11.0%. Liabilities-to-equity is 65%. **ADEQUATE** — returns sit just under the 15% STRONG line and cash conversion is weak.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth passes: BioSolutions sells to about 2,800 customers; BioMaterials' three largest customers take 32% of that segment (≈11% of group sales combined), and no single-customer disclosure appears. Wood comes from many forest owners. Customer power is WEAK — specialised grades qualify into customer recipes. Supplier power is WEAK. Substitutes are STRONG — synthetic dispersants, synthetic vanillin and Chinese cellulose grades compete on price.
