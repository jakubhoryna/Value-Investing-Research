## 2. Understanding
Konecranes designs, manufactures and services lifting equipment: overhead and process cranes, hoists and components, and their inspection, maintenance, spare parts and modernisation (Industrial Service, 37% of FY2025 sales); industrial cranes sold direct and through distributors and OEMs under the Konecranes, Demag, SWF, Verlinde, R&M and Donati brands (Industrial Equipment); and container-handling cranes, lift trucks and terminal automation for ports and terminal operators (Port Solutions, 36%). The customers who pay are manufacturing and process plants, shipyards, ports and terminals. It is a Finnish public company headquartered in Hyvinkää; the US and Germany were 36% of FY2025 sales. It is an established global leader, transformed in 2017 by acquiring Terex's MHPS business. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
**ADEQUATE.** Worst-of-four normalized ROCE is **10.0%** (end of period, with cash); the cash-stripped reads are 11.8–12.7%. Worst FCFROCE is 8.8%. Excluding the FY2017 STAHL gain, the worst ROCE read would be about 9.2%. The shape runs upward: 7.2% over FY2016–20 and 12.3% over FY2021–25 (end, with cash), so there is no decline. Over the decade OI/share compounded 20.3% and BV/share 12.9%. Both are flattered by the small pre-MHPS FY2015 base, but FY2023–25 per-share OI is still three and a half to five times FY2016. L/E fell from 223% (FY2020) to 118% (FY2025).

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth **PASSES**. The filing states that "there are no single customers which account for over 10% of the Group's sales." Inputs are steel, fabrications and electrical components from about 22,000 tier-1 suppliers, with dual sourcing. The force calls are by segment. Industrial Service is 0 strong. Industrial Equipment has new entrants **STRONG**, because crane fabrication is contestable, which is why it earns a 9.4% EBITA margin. Port Solutions has customer power **STRONG**, because a few global terminal operators buy by tender. Supplier power and substitutes are WEAK everywhere. That is one strong force per project unit, contained by the service franchise. The moat is **cost**: route density across a global crane-service network of scale, which services other brands too. It is stable. Market growth is MIXED: industrial capex is cyclical, and port orders rose 21% in FY2025 but are lumpy. **ADEQUATE.**
