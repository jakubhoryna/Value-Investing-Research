Understanding PASS; Past STRONG; Future STRONG; SF PASS (one flag — capital-allocation CAUTION); EXPENSIVE — verdict **WATCH**.

Donaldson is worth owning for a replacement-filter annuity: half of group sales are aftermarket filters pulled through by first-fit positions on off-road equipment and engines, and that installed base has carried normalized ROCE above 20% on every read for a decade while per-share operating income compounded near 10% a year. What stands between here and a BUY is price alone: at $88.65 the stock trades at 24.1× FCF and about 19× EV/OI, roughly three times the bargain lines, so the band sits near $24–26. The capital-allocation record is the soft spot — repurchases executed at 16–44× FCF, a $62m bioprocessing write-down in FY2025, then Facet bought for about 20× EBITDA with debt. The verdict flips to AVOID if a second scrutiny-level negative appears (the 2026 proxy, or another deal at Facet's price) or if the aftermarket starts losing ground to electrification; it reaches BUY only through a drawdown of roughly 70%.

## 2. Understanding

Donaldson Company, Inc. is a ~$3.9bn-revenue Minneapolis-based manufacturer, founded in 1915, that makes air, fuel, lube, hydraulic and exhaust filtration systems and — mostly — their replacement filters, selling first-fit systems to construction, mining, agricultural and truck OEMs and replacement filters through OEM dealer networks, distributors and fleets (Mobile Solutions, 62% of sales), dust-collection, compressed-air, power-generation and aerospace filtration equipment and parts to industrial customers (29%), and membrane, disk-drive and bioprocessing filtration (9%), from plants on every continent, with 42.5% of sales in the U.S. and Canada and the rest in EMEA, Asia-Pacific and Latin America. **PASS.**

## D. Future-performance worksheet

**Breadth (P15).**
- *Customer:* "No customer accounted for 10% or more of our net sales in fiscal 2026, 2025 or 2024", and none exceeded 10% of gross receivables (10-K Item 1A, Note 19). **PASS.** The AFSI joint venture with Caterpillar makes Caterpillar's fluid filters, but it is equity-accounted and outside sales.
- *Supplier:* purchased raw materials are ~67% of cost of sales, spread across steel, filter media and petrochemical products (plastic, rubber, adhesives), with "selective supply arrangements" for cost stability. Several vendors stand ready in each category, and Donaldson produces its own synthetic media (Ultra-Web, Synteq). No >10% supplier is disclosed. **PASS** — a logged judgment, since the 10-K carries no supplier-concentration sentence.

**Forces (P16) — group level; segment note where it differs.**

| Force | Call | Reason |
|-------|------|--------|
| Customer bargaining power | WEAK | Aftermarket is $1,936.6m of $3,885.6m (50%) and industrial replacement parts add more. No customer reaches 10%, and replacement filters are low-ticket, high-consequence purchases. OEM first-fit buyers (Off-Road + On-Road, 12%) are powerful, but they are the minority channel. The OEM-dealer share of aftermarket is undisclosed (gap). Life Sciences disk-drive customers are concentrated, but the segment is 9% of sales. |
| Supplier bargaining power | WEAK | Commodity inputs with multiple vendors; forward integration into filters improbable; costs recovered through price. |
| Threat of substitutes | WEAK | No direct substitute for filtration on combustion and hydraulic equipment. Doing-without means unprotected engines and hydraulics (10-K product descriptions). The wholly-different-function threat (battery-electric drivetrains removing fuel, lube and exhaust filtration) faces high adoption costs in mining, construction and agriculture duty cycles. On-Road first-fit (3% of sales, −6.6% in FY2026) is the exposed line. Logged as the main future-performance risk. |
| Threat of new entrants | WEAK | Barriers to success: OEM first-fit specification steers replacement demand, proprietary geometries (PowerCore, Duramax, Synteq media) resist will-fit copies, and global distribution scale matters. "Many regional competitors" exist in the aftermarket, but Donaldson reports share gains there (FY2026). |

**Tally: 0 STRONG → favourable.**

**Moat — switching costs.** A filter designed into an OEM's air-intake or hydraulic system at first fit dictates the replacement part for the machine's life. That turns each equipment sale into a decade-long consumables stream, and fleets and dealers rarely re-qualify a will-fit part against warranty and uptime risk. **Stable:** aftermarket sales grew 6.4% in FY2026 on utilization and share gains. The acquired Facet business brings the same structure, with ~70% of sales consumables on regulated replacement cycles.

**Market growth: MIXED.** Equipment utilization and aerospace, defense and power-generation demand are growing. Truck build and bioprocessing capital spending are weak. Electrification is a slow secular headwind to the engine-filtration base.

**Verdict:** breadth PASS + 0 STRONG forces + stable switching-cost moat → **STRONG**.
