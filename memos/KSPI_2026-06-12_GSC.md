## 1. Verdict & takeaway (≤800 words)

**WATCH.** Kaspi.kz is the dominant Kazakhstan super-app — Payments, Marketplace, and Fintech (consumer lending and deposits via licensed Kaspi Bank) fused into one closed-loop ecosystem with 25M+ consumers and ~900K merchants across Kazakhstan and Türkiye. It clears the Understanding and Quality gates with genuine strength, but at $78.58 it is not GSC-cheap, and two of four valuation metrics fail.

**Understanding: PASS.** The model is legible: merchants pay take-rates on payments and marketplace GMV; the bank funds a consumer loan book with granular retail deposits (₸7.18T of ₸7.53T customer accounts from individuals); each leg feeds the others. The bank-holding structure complicates *measurement* (no classified balance sheet, no separate operating-income line, CFO distorted by deposit/loan flows) but not *comprehension*. All measurement adaptations are logged in Appendix C.

**Past: ADEQUATE.** Levels are excellent in every observable year — ROCE_xc 21.7–30.4%, ROCE_wc 16.1–20.3%, OI/share 4.2× over five years — meeting STRONG thresholds on levels alone. Two things force the one-notch downgrade: (1) the audited public window is only FY2020–2025 with returns observable 2022–2025 (IPO Oct 2020) — 4 of 10 required years; (2) the trend is a sustained decline — ROCE_xc 30.4% (2023) → 28.4% → 21.7% (2025), FCFROCE_xc 31.4% → 8.0% — driven by deposit-cost inflation at an 18% NBK base rate and the consolidation of loss-making Hepsiburada. L/E of 326% (down from 615%) flags above the 200% threshold but stays under the hard cap; the exceptional-returns allowance applies and the funding is granular retail deposits, deleveraging each year.

**Future: ADEQUATE.** Breadth passes decisively — millions of consumers, hundreds of thousands of merchants, no concentration. Critically, and contra the PYPL precedent, Kaspi owns its rails end-to-end: no card-network supplier dependence. The moat is network effects plus ingrainedness (77 monthly transactions per active payments consumer) — stable-to-strengthening in Kazakhstan, unproven in Türkiye. Forces are MIXED, which blocks STRONG: Kazakhstan tailwinds (e-commerce penetration, GDP growth) are offset by Türkiye rivalry against Trendyol from a #2 position, an 18% policy rate with no cuts before mid-2026, ARDFM consumer-lending cooling measures (unsecured issuance −5.9% yoy in 1Q26), the VAT rise to 16%, and recurring Russia-adjacency/sanctions allegations (company denies SDN exposure). Consistent with CROX and TROW: durable moat + unfavourable-to-mixed forces = ADEQUATE.

**Shareholder-friendliness: PASS, flagged.** Insiders own heavily (CEO Lomtadze 22.62%, Chairman Kim 20.78%, officers+directors 46.18%; 66.5% including Baring vehicles — control concentration is itself a risk). Cash comp is trivial (₸654M = 0.049% of OI; SBC 1.2%). RPTs are ordinary-course deposits, immaterial. Buybacks were done at ~7.0× trailing EPS / 3.0× BV — value-accretive prices. The flag is Hepsiburada: $1.2B+ deployed (65.41% Jan 2025 → 85.17% Jan 2026) into a loss-making, hyperinflationary-TRY e-commerce #2, which cost Türkiye-attributable losses of ₸89.6bn in 2025 and forced a dividend suspension. Mitigants: no equity issued, distressed purchase price, and dividends resumed within five quarters — ₸850/share for FY2025 plus ₸850/share for 1Q26 (≈8.9% forward yield if quarterly cadence holds). Per the CROX/HEYDUDE precedent: flag, don't fail.

**Cheapness: FAIR.** At $78.58 (MCap $14.92B = ₸7,288bn): MCap/BV 2.9 **passes**; MCap/TBV 4.4 **fails** (47% above threshold, beyond the 1.25× tolerance — Hepsiburada added ₸422bn goodwill + ₸382bn intangibles); MCap/FCF 14.8 fails but the metric is distorted for a deposit-funded bank; EV/OI is ill-defined — 9.9× treating deposits as debt, 4.3× treating them as operating liabilities. Trailing P/E is 6.9×, which is cheap on earnings but not what the framework prices. With the one-average allowance, CHEAP flips below ≈ **$42.4** (conservative EV build) to ≈ **$52.9** (lenient build, where MCap/TBV ≤ 3.0 binds). Current price sits 34–47% above the band.

**Bottom line:** a genuinely high-quality, founder-run, self-funded ecosystem trading at a single-digit P/E — but the framework's asset-based gates correctly register that you are paying 4.4× tangible book for a business whose returns are compressing and whose second act (Türkiye) is unproven. Onto the watchlist with a wide trigger band; the Türkiye P&L and the NBK rate path decide whether the band is reachable or the quality rating is the next thing to move.

## Appendix C — Judgment calls (logged for repeatability)

1. **OI = PBT.** Kaspi's IFRS P&L has no separate operating-income line; Revenue − Operating expenses = Profit before tax exactly (no material non-operating items). PBT is used as OI throughout.
2. **NIBCL build (unclassified bank B/S).** NIBCL = Total liabilities − customer accounts − due to banks − debt securities issued − subordinated debt. Includes deferred tax liabilities (₸71.4bn in 2025). 2025 NIBCL jump to ₸600,549M reflects Hepsiburada consolidation (trade payables etc.).
3. **Deposits retained in capital employed.** Customer deposits are interest-bearing funding for the loan book, not free working capital; they remain in CE. This is the honest treatment for a deposit-funded financial and depresses ROCE vs. an industrial-style exclusion.
4. **Securities as excess-cash-like.** Investment securities (FVTOCI + FVTPL + amortised cost; predominantly KZ government paper held as liquidity) are deducted alongside cash in CE_xc / ROCE_xc.
5. **Dual EV builds.** EV is ill-defined for a deposit-funded bank. Conservative: all deposits + debt as debt → EV ₸13,194,852M, EV/OI 9.9. Lenient: deposits as operating liabilities, only debt securities + sub debt + due-to-banks as debt → EV/OI 4.3. Both shown; neither privileged.
6. **Owners' equity for BV/TBV.** NCI (₸109,734M, mostly Hepsiburada minorities) excluded from BV and TBV.
7. **Goodwill = 0 for 2021–22.** Not separately disclosed; immaterial pre-2023.
8. **2020 diluted WAS derived** from disclosed diluted EPS ₸1,347 (193.74M shares).
9. **Observable window 4/10 years.** IPO Oct 2020; audited public record FY2020+; returns computable 2022–2025 only. Flagged; contributes to the Past downgrade.
10. **FCF/CFO distorted.** Banking CFO includes deposit/loan flow swings; MCap/FCF and FCFROCE are computed mechanically but labeled unreliable; BV/TBV anchor the cheapness verdict.
11. **Türkiye hyperinflation.** Hepsiburada consolidates under IAS 29; TRY restatement adds noise to consolidated growth rates. KZ-only figures cited where disclosed.
