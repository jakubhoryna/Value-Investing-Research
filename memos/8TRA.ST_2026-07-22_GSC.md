The consolidated arithmetic is what the model judges, and it is poor: worst-of-four normalized ROCE of **4.4%** against a 10% adequacy floor, **negative** FCFROCE, and liabilities at **266%** of book equity in every year of the record. ## 2. Understanding

TRATON SE is the Munich-headquartered, 87.5%-Volkswagen-controlled European stock corporation that builds and sells heavy and medium commercial vehicles — trucks, buses and vans — plus parts, workshop services and long-term service contracts, under four autonomous brands (Scania, MAN, International, Volkswagen Truck & Bus), to professional fleets, hauliers, transport authorities and dealers across Europe and the Americas, and additionally runs a captive finance and leasing arm whose receivables now dominate the group balance sheet — a vertically integrated, deeply cyclical capital-goods manufacturer, listed since 2019 and still consolidated into the Volkswagen Group. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized ROCE is **4.42%** (range 4.42–5.06%) over FY2020–25; worst-of-four FCFROCE is **–1.58%**. The best single year, FY2024, reached 9.0%. The denominator is the point: capital employed of EUR 55.6bn carries EUR 17.9bn of financial-services receivables and EUR 5.3bn of leased-out assets, and the cash those books absorb (–EUR 3.4bn combined in FY2025) is why FCF was negative in five of seven years. OI/share compounded 4.3% from FY2019 but fell from a FY2024 peak; TBV/share **declined 5.7% a year** as Navistar intangibles amortised. L/E is **266%**, through the P5 flag with no P6 relief available. **WEAK.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth **PASSES** both legs: no IFRS 8 major-customer disclosure exists, and the supplier base is broad with ready alternatives. Forces: customer bargaining power **STRONG** (professional buyers purchasing on total cost of ownership, routinely multi-sourcing — visible in International's 0.1% margin and MAN's 6.4%); supplier power **WEAK**; substitutes **WEAK**; new entrants **WEAK** (service networks, homologation, residual-value management and captive finance are formidable barriers, though Chinese OEM entry is a watch item). Tally **1 strong — judgment zone**. Moat: **switching costs**, stable at Scania but narrow — group returns of 4.4% say it does not span the portfolio. Market growth **MIXED** (unit sales –9%). **ADEQUATE.**
