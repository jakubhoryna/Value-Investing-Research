# ZIP — ZipRecruiter, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past WEAK; Future WEAK; shareholder-friendliness FAIL (P17 hard strike on both reads, capital-allocation ADVERSE); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID**, on three gates independently.

The ownable version of this business is a capital-light, top-of-funnel US recruiting brand holding $393m of investments and printing FY2021–FY2023-class free cash flow. That business no longer exists at the fundamentals: owner earnings have fallen from $131m (FY2021) to $3.5m (FY2025), the paid-employer base has shrunk from ~150k to 59k, and the candidate funnel is hostage to Google's search and AI-answer layer — a dependence the company itself discloses. Meanwhile the controller's economics run against the minority: CEO pay at ~26–53× the proportionality limit, $629m of repurchases executed across the window without a single tranche near the bargain lines — funded by $550m of 5% notes and lately providing exit liquidity to a 5%-holder VC while the founder sold 42% of his own stake. Nothing here is one price away from fixable; all three failed gates require multi-year repair.

## 2. Understanding

ZipRecruiter runs a US-centric (foreign <10% of revenue, sourced) two-sided online employment marketplace whose paying customers are ~59k mostly small and mid-sized employers buying subscription job postings and performance-based promotion — job seekers ride free — operating as a founder-controlled dual-class Delaware corporation (Class B 20 votes/share, CEO 79.2% voting power) in the cyclical, intensely competitive online-recruitment industry: founded 2010, direct-listed 2021, now a ~$366m-cap company whose revenue and paid-employer count have roughly halved from the 2021–22 peak. **PASS.**
