SHIFT sells software testing, system development and IT consulting to Japanese companies and to the large system integrators that subcontract to it. ## 2. Understanding

Group sentence: SHIFT is a Tokyo-based software-services group, founded in 2005 by its chief executive, listed in 2014 and grown from ¥5.5bn to ¥161.6bn of revenue by FY2026 through about forty acquisitions, that sells software quality-assurance and testing work, custom system development and package-software implementation, IT and digital-transformation consulting, and smaller help-desk, back-office and PC-set-up services, billed mainly per engineer-month or per project, to Japanese companies that use the systems (two-thirds of revenue) and to system integrators that subcontract the work (one-third), with more than 90% of revenue earned in Japan.

- **Test** (39.7% of FY2026 revenue): test planning, design, execution and automation, increasingly AI-assisted, for business systems, web and mobile apps and games; paid by end-user companies and by integrators.
- **Development** (38.5%): custom development, maintenance and package implementation, including Nissay Com's packages for mid-sized manufacturers, national universities and health-insurance societies; mostly paid by integrators in the predecessor segment.
- **Consulting** (11.7%): strategy, business-process and digital-transformation consulting paid by companies.
