# 1913.HK — Prada S.p.A.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past WEAK; Future ADEQUATE; Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (flag pair); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID**, unchanged, now on two gates.

Prada owns two of the strongest brands in leather goods and has never diluted or repurchased a share. Its decade still fails: ten years of operating income over the capital the stores and factories need average 9.4% at the worst reading, because FY2016–FY2020 earned 0.3–7.1% before the Miu Miu years lifted it to 13–17%. Two separate pay and deal findings now fail the owner test: the best-paid key manager's FY2023 package ran above what three years of profit support, and Versace was bought, with new debt, while it was losing money. At HKD 39.28 the shares trade at 13.4 times operating income against a buy line near HKD 12.45.

## 2. Understanding

Prada S.p.A. is a Milan-based, Hong Kong-listed luxury house, about 80% owned by the Prada–Bertelli family through Prada Holding, that designs, makes largely in its own Italian factories and sells differentiated leather goods, ready-to-wear and footwear under the Prada (66.5% of FY2025 retail sales), Miu Miu (31.3%), Versace (consolidated from 2 December 2025), Church's and Car Shoe brands, plus Marchesi 1824 pastry shops, mainly through about 830 directly operated stores and e-commerce to affluent individual consumers in Asia-Pacific, Europe, the Americas, Japan and the Middle East (89.2% of FY2025 revenue), with wholesale to department stores and franchisees (8.2%) and royalties from eyewear and beauty licensees (2.5%); it is a mature, family-run house in a brand transition, Miu Miu growing fastest and Versace in a loss-making repositioning. The brands are aggregated into one reportable segment, so this is not a group. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

STD lens, one reportable segment. Worst-of-four ROCE is **9.4%** on the ten-year window (end of period, cash in) against 13.4% on the latest five years; the lower governs and sits under the 10% floor. Per-share growth clears every floor on a constant share count: OI/sh +11.5% and maintenance FCF/sh +5.6% a year on the harsher reads. L/E is 137% after the debt-funded Versace purchase. No sustained decline. **WEAK** on the ten-year return level.

## 4. Quality — future

Moat: brand, one source, stable overall — Miu Miu strengthening, Prada flat-to-up, Versace eroding. Market growth MIXED, which caps the grade. **ADEQUATE.**
