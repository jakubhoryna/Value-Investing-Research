## 2. Understanding

Irish Continental Group, a Dublin-based shipping and port group listed since 1988 and run by the same chief executive since then, carries tourists with their cars and haulage companies' lorries and unaccompanied trailers on its Irish Ferries ro-pax ships between Ireland, Britain and France and across the Dover Strait, charters spare ships to other operators, carries shippers' and freight forwarders' containers between Ireland and Rotterdam and Antwerp through Eucon, and runs container terminals in Dublin and Belfast ports that load and unload ships for shipping lines. **PASS.**

The group has two material units:
- **Ferries:** a ro-pax ferry operator. Tourists and road hauliers pay for passage on Dublin–Holyhead, Rosslare–Pembroke, Ireland–Cherbourg and Dover–Calais, and other operators pay to charter its ships.
- **Container & Terminal:** a container shipping line (Eucon, door-to-door and feeder) and stevedoring at Dublin Ferryport Terminals and Belfast Container Terminal. Shippers, forwarders and shipping lines pay.
