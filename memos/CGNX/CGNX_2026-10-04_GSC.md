# CGNX — Cognex Corporation: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS, Past ADEQUATE, Future WEAK, shareholder-friendliness FAIL, cheapness EXPENSIVE: **AVOID** on two independent gates.

Cognex is a high-gross-margin, debt-free machine-vision franchise. Its returns sit only modestly above the floor, though: worst-of-four ROCE is 12.0%, inflated cash sits on the balance sheet, and FY2023–25 returns ran at 6–9%. Two gates fail on primary text. First, one customer took 15% of FY2025 revenue, and concentration of this kind recurs (15% in 2018; 13% and 14% from two customers in 2020). Second, the shareholder-friendliness test fails twice: the highest-paid executive's $8.24m breaches both pay reads, and a decade of buybacks at 25–89× earnings mostly offset option dilution. Price is far away as well: 65.9–68.4× EV/OI against a band of $6.27–8.65. Flipping the verdict needs a breadth disclosure clean for consecutive years, pay inside 5% of owner earnings, and repurchases tied to worth. No price move does it.

## 2. Understanding
Cognex designs and sells machine-vision hardware and software. Its products are smart cameras, vision systems, 2D/3D sensors, barcode readers and deep-learning inspection software. They are sold directly and through integrators and OEMs to manufacturers (consumer electronics, automotive, packaging, semiconductors) and to logistics and e-commerce operators who pay to automate inspection, guidance, gauging and identification. Revenue is 33% US, 25% Europe, 16% Greater China and 26% other. Cognex is a single-segment company headquartered in Natick, Massachusetts, with 40 years in the business, a contract-manufactured product line, roughly $1bn of revenue, and a $500m robotic-perception acquisition (RealSense) pending. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
**ADEQUATE.** Worst-of-four normalized ROCE is **12.0%** (end, with cash); the cash-stripped reads are 15.8–17.2%. FCFROCE is 12.5–17.9%. Returns halved across the window: the first five years averaged 13.9%, the last five 10.6%, and FY2023–25 averaged 7.4%. OI/share compounded 3.3% a year, above the floor only because FY2025 rebounded; FCF/share compounded 8.4%. FY2025 L/E is 35%, with no debt. FY2022–24 declined three years running, which is the P4 question. The FY2025 recovery (8.9%) and the LTM operating income of $238m read as a cyclical trough, so P4 does not convict; the call is logged and re-tested at FY2026.

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth **FAILS**. The 10-K states: "Revenue from a single customer accounted for 15% … of total revenue in 2025". The waiver fails because customers can switch to rival vendors and to "internal engineering departments" (the 10-K's own words), and the business has 30,000 customers, so concentration is not structural. Forces: customer power **STRONG** (a 15% buyer that shifts procurement across regions); supplier power WEAK (contract manufacturing is replaceable); substitutes **STRONG** (open-source and AI tools plus commodity cameras, which the 10-K names as lowering barriers); entrants WEAK. That is 2 strong. The moat is switching costs plus an ingrained installed base, stable but contested; gross margin fell from 72% to 67% between FY2023 and FY2025. Market growth is MIXED. **WEAK.**
