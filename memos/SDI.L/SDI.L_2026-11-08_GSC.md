# SDI.L — SDI Group plc: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past WEAK (ROCE decline); Future ADEQUATE; Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (compensation); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID (Past + SF)**.

SDI buys small British instrument makers and runs them as separate businesses. Returns peaked in FY2022, helped by a pandemic-era camera contract that ended in FY2024, and acquired goodwill has since pulled them down to about 10% of capital. Executive pay is large next to the group's cash: the CEO's FY2026 package is over a fifth of the year's maintenance-capex free cash flow. The FY2025 deals were bought, in aggregate, at about 26 times the targets' profit as printed. At 96p the stock is also far above the trigger. The verdict would flip only with returns recovering for two clean years, pay inside the P17 lines, and deals bought at or below worth.

## 2. Understanding

SDI Group plc is a Cambridge-based, AIM-listed buy-and-build group (formerly Scientific Digital Imaging plc) of about eighteen small UK designers and manufacturers that sell scientific and industrial instruments, spare parts and service (89% instruments and parts) to laboratories, schools and universities, hospitals, OEMs and industrial plants, half in the UK and the rest mainly in Europe, the US and Asia, with no customer above 10% of revenue. **PASS.**

It is a group: three reportable divisions are each material (P22).

- **Laboratory Equipment** (36% of FY2026 revenue): fume cupboards (Safelab, the largest UK supplier, focused on education), clean-air cabinets and cleanrooms (Monmouth), autoclave sterilisers for labs and hospitals (LTE), high-temperature furnaces and environmental chambers (Severn), and gel-imaging and colony-counting instruments for life-science labs (Synoptics).
- **Industrial & Scientific Sensors** (28%): pressure, vacuum and gas-flow instruments (Chell), chemical dosing and control systems for metal-processing manufacturers (Astles), OEM sensors (Sentek), flowmeters (MPB), microLEDs and LED light engines for avionics and defence (PRP), and industrial process sensors (Peak).
- **Industrial & Scientific Products** (36%): scientific CMOS cameras (Atik), anti-static equipment for manufacturing lines (Fraser), chillers for instruments and processes (ATC), photolithographic graticules and optics (Graticules), sputter coaters and vacuum systems (SVS), 2D/3D metrology machines (InspecVision) and industrial electric boilers (Collins Walker).
