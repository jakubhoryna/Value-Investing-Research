# SUN.SW — Sulzer AG: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past WEAK (worst-of-four ROCE 6.8%, L/E 252%); Future ADEQUATE; SF PASS; cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID (Past)**.

Sulzer is a pump, rotating-equipment-service and separation-technology group that has repaired itself since 2022. Its record still fails. Over FY2016–FY2025 operating income averaged under 7% of working-capital-inclusive capital employed, and liabilities are 2.5× book equity. Returns improved sharply in FY2023–FY2025 (ROCE_wc 9.9% → 13.1%, ROCE_xc 15.5% → 19.4%), but a decade of 3–7% years is the record the framework tests, and price is far away anyway: CHF 159.70 against a CHF 43–50 bargain line, at 25.8× FY2025 FCF. To reach WATCH, the post-2022 return level has to persist long enough to lift the ten-year average above 10%, with leverage back under 200%.

## 2. Understanding

Sulzer designs, makes and services centrifugal pumps, mixers and agitators (Flow, 44% of FY2025 sales), repairs and maintains pumps, turbines, compressors and motors of its own and other makes (Services, 37%), and supplies column internals, separation and polymer-process technology and licences (Chemtech, 19%). It sells to oil, gas and chemical producers, EPC contractors, water utilities and general industry, through project tenders and installed-base service contracts. It is an engineered-equipment manufacturer plus a global repair network, headquartered in Winterthur, selling worldwide, founded in 1834, mid-sized (CHF 3.6bn sales), 48.8% owned by Tiwel Holding (Viktor Vekselberg, OFAC-listed), and slimmed by the 2021 medmix spin-off. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized ROCE is 6.8% (working-capital-inclusive, beginning and end of period alike) and FCFROCE 5.6%. Both are below the ADEQUATE floor. Cash-out ROCE is 9.8%, which also fails. The decade splits: FY2016–FY2022 ran ROCE_wc at 3–7%, including a CHF 111m FY2022 after the Russia exit; FY2023–FY2025 ran at 10–13%. OI per share compounded at 13.6% from a depressed FY2015 base, and FCF per share at 3.5%. BV per share fell 5.3% a year, because of a CHF 618m 2016 distribution and the 2021 spin-off. L/E is 252% (259% at June 2026), above the 200% flag, and the P6 exception needs great metrics. **WEAK.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth passes. The group states that no individual customer is a significant portion of sales, and castings, steel, motors and machining are available from many vendors. Forces: customer power STRONG in Flow and Chemtech projects, where oil, chemical and EPC buyers run competitive tenders; it is contained, because Services sells to a fragmented installed base. Supplier power WEAK. Substitutes WEAK: the alternative to a centrifugal pump is another centrifugal pump. Entrants WEAK: the installed base, end-user qualification and a global service network deter them. The moat is switching costs: pumps and column internals specified into plants pull decades of repairs and parts. It looks stable, given Services margins of 16.8% → 18.3%. Market growth MIXED. **ADEQUATE.**
