# WISE.L — Wise Group plc: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | ADEQUATE | ADEQUATE | **FAIL** | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** |

Wise is a genuinely good, structurally low-cost cross-border payments business — 19m customers, +31% volume, a 52bps take rate no incumbent bank matches, and a widening moat from direct connections into domestic payment systems. It is also, by the framework, un-ownable here on two independent counts. Gate 1C fails on a **P19 hard strike**: FY26 saw $470m of repurchases into an Employee Share Trust "to eliminate shareholder dilution from historic share options" — textbook dilution-sterilisation, which strikes at any price — reinforced by the July-2025 **bundled** vote that extended founder Käärmann's 9:1 supervoting a decade (to ~2036) as the price of the US listing, over proxy-adviser and co-founder objection. And the price is EXPENSIVE: ~23× EV/OI and ~26× owner-FCF against 7×/8× thresholds. To flip to WATCH the sterilisation policy would have to stop (return capital as buybacks below worth or dividends, not as comp funding) **and** price fall ~70%. Neither is near.

## 2. Understanding

Wise sells low-cost, fast cross-border money movement and multi-currency accounts to consumers, SMBs, and — via Wise Platform — banks/enterprises; it is a UK-founded, USD-reporting, FCA-authorised electronic-money institution (not a lender) that safeguards ~$30bn of customer balances and earns net interest on that float, now dual-listed with a US primary (Nasdaq) and LSE secondary. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Returns are high but the machinery is distorted: the balance sheet is dominated by ~$30bn of pass-through customer money, so classical CE and reported FCF are economically meaningless (§2·1A treatment — ROE substituted, FCFROCE n.m.). ROE (aggregator, currency-consistent): 9.3% → 22.5% → 51.4% → 36.3% → **26.8%** (FY22–26); normalized ~36% — comfortably above the 10% ADEQUATE floor. But only **5 years** of public history (P1 needs 10 → STRONG unavailable), and returns/margins have compressed two years running as the FY24 rate-driven interest peak normalises and reinvestment steps up (admin +40%, marketing +52%). Interest-bearing debt/equity ~0.2× — trivial. Strong volume, customer and book-value growth. **ADEQUATE.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASS both sides (millions of atomised customers; many banking/rail partners, and Wise is disintermediating them via direct connections). Four forces: customer power **WEAK** (atomised, no backward integration), supplier power **WEAK** (many rails; direct-access strategy), threat of substitutes **STRONG** (banks, Revolut, Remitly, Western Union, emerging stablecoin rails — take rate already sliding 0.58%→0.52%), threat of new entrants **WEAK** (dulled by scale, a multi-jurisdiction licence stack, and structural cost lead). Tally: **1 strong (substitutes)** → judgment zone. Moat: **cost** (plus emerging network), stable-to-strengthening via new Brazil/Japan connections. Market: growing (secular cross-border). One strong force + reinvestment + take-rate erosion hold it below STRONG. **ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Comp is a positive: founder-CEO Käärmann's total ~£206k, well under the P17 5% and ~$40m absolute lines; ~18–19% founder ownership is strong alignment. Buybacks are the problem: FY26 allocated **$470m to purchase 35.9m shares into the EST specifically to eliminate option dilution** — a P19 **hard strike at any price** (sterilisation) — and the new $500m+ programme keeps ~40% for that recurring EST purpose; the residual return-of-capital tranche executes near ~20× EV/OI (flag). Independently, the 2025 bundled extension of the 9:1 Class B supervoting to ~2036 is a documented entrenchment negative. **FAIL** — P19 sterilisation strike (dual-class extension a second, corroborating negative).

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Interest-income normalisation: ~50% of net revenue is non-cross-border (mostly float interest); falling central-bank rates or higher customer pass-through compress the margin structurally (guidance already implies 20-25% → 15-20%).
- Take-rate compression: the cost moat is being handed to customers (0.52% and falling); volume must keep outrunning price.
- Stablecoins/big-tech: a credible substitute vector Wise is only now staffing for.
- Governance: extended founder control removes the shareholder check that would normally curb the sterilisation habit.
- Data: FY26 income USD / balance sheet GBP mix in aggregator; OI is aggregator statutory, not primary-verified line — see Appendix A.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

WATCH requires the P19 sterilisation policy to end (capital returned as below-worth buybacks or dividends, not comp funding) — clearing the strike — with cheapness following separately only on a ~70% de-rate. AVOID here is quality-of-capital-allocation plus valuation, not a knock on the operating business, which is strong. Re-review at FY2027 results (~Jun 2027), the 2026 proxy (dual-class/comp), or any structural governance change. Not held → no zone/sell-line.

Handled by holding to the mechanical gates; the sterilisation strike and ~23× EV/OI are not close calls. No judgment born of bias.
