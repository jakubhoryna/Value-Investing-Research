Understanding PASS, Past WEAK, Future WEAK, shareholder-friendliness FAIL, price EXPENSIVE: **AVOID**, on all three quality gates.

Legacy runs four accredited private career colleges in California that train nurses, sonographers and other healthcare workers. Since 2021 it has earned real returns on a light asset base: worst-case ROCE is 15.5%, and revenue grew from USD 11.4m to USD 80.1m in eight years. Three things fail. AVOID would need the loss years out of the window, a contained payer, and pay and related-party practice changed.

## 2. Understanding

Legacy Education, a Lancaster, California company founded in 2009 and listed on NYSE American since September 2024, is a small, fast-growing for-profit operator of four accredited, state-approved private career colleges in California: High Desert Medical College (Lancaster, Bakersfield, Temecula), Central Coast College (Salinas), Integrity College of Health (Pasadena) and Contra Costa Medical Career College (Antioch, bought December 2024). It sells diploma, certificate and associate-degree programs in vocational and registered nursing, sonography, MRI, medical and dental assisting, pharmacy technician and a few business subjects to adult students. Those students pay mostly with U.S. federal Title IV grants and loans and other federal aid (59.8%–86.8% of each school's FY2025 90/10 revenue), and the rest with cash, private loans and the company's own TuitionFlex payment plans of up to six years. One reportable segment. **PASS.**
