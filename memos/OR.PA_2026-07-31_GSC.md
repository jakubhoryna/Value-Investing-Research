v1's FAIL rested on the retired P19 strike ladder. Re-derived as a judgment it survives comfortably, and the delta since 29 July settles the liveness question that decided most of this queue: **on 5 March 2026 L'Oréal gave a mandate to an investment services provider to buy up to €500m of its own stock, a maximum of 2 million shares, by the end of June 2026, for cancellation.** That is the fifth consecutive annual ~€500m programme — FY2022, 2023, 2024, 2025 and now 2026 — executed while the shares traded near record levels. The Aesop acquisition flag stands alongside, independent.

H1 2026 was an excellent operating half — sales €23,776.1m (+6.8% like-for-like), **operating profit €5,063.0m at a record 21.3% margin** — and it changes nothing at this gate. What it does change is the balance sheet: **net debt rose from roughly €2.1bn at FY2025 to €12,663.8m**, on Kering Beauté (Creed, Bottega Veneta, Balenciaga, consolidated from April), the 20% Galderma stake and Innovist. **That alone drops the band from v1's €106 all-cash leg to ≈€86.**
