# SKF-B.ST — AB SKF (publ): GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding **FAIL** (one-level decomposition: the material Specialized Industrial Solutions unit is irreducible) → **DO NOT ANALYSE FURTHER**. Past, Future, shareholder-friendliness and cheapness were not graded.

SKF is easy to describe one business at a time. Bearing Solutions sells rolling bearings to industrial machine builders and through distributors. Automotive, now SKF Vertevo, sells wheel-end and driveline bearings to vehicle makers and leaves the group on 1 December 2026. The difficulty is the third segment, which SKF has reported since the first quarter of 2026. Specialized Industrial Solutions holds four businesses the filer calls stand-alone: Aerospace, Lubrication Lifetime Solutions, Sealing Solutions and Magnetic Solutions. Aerospace sells engine and airframe bearings to aircraft-engine and airframe makers. Sealing Solutions sells seals to vehicle makers, their suppliers and industrial customers. Each is well above 10% of the unit, and they differ in industry and in who pays. Under MODEL v4.5.0 that makes the unit irreducible, and it is about a fifth of sales and a quarter of segment profit, so Gate 0 fails. This is a parameter-driven result on a segment structure the filer adopted in 2026, not a finding of incomprehension. It flips back to a full analysis if the segment stops mixing such businesses, or if a MODEL ruling changes how a specialty-components segment is tested.

## 2. Understanding
**Group sentence.** AB SKF designs, makes and services rolling bearings, seals, lubrication systems, magnetic bearings and condition-monitoring products. It sells them to industrial machine builders, aircraft-engine and airframe makers, vehicle makers and their suppliers, and through about 17,000 distributor locations to end users. It is a vertically integrated Swedish manufacturer headquartered in Gothenburg, selling in about 130 countries (USA 19%, China 16%, Germany 8% of 2025 sales). It is the bearing industry's incumbent since 1907 and is now distributing its Automotive business to shareholders.

**Units (P22; Q2 2026 interim report, segment information with 2024–2025 restated; the 16 March 2026 restatement release).** From 1 January 2026 SKF reports three segments; "Other" holds corporate functions and eliminations and is not a unit.

**Gate 0 test.** Bearing Solutions and Automotive can each be described in one six-parameter sentence. SIS cannot:
- **Its sub-businesses are separately managed operations.** The filer names four "stand-alone" businesses and reports SIS organic growth by business unit (Q2 2026 report). Their revenue shares are not printed. Two are shown above 10% of the unit from the filer's own figures: Aerospace is 9% of FY2025 Industrial net sales of MSEK 65,614 (Annual Report 2025, Note 2, sales by customer industry), about MSEK 5.9bn, roughly a third of SIS (derived); the automotive sealing business moved into SIS is "Sales of BSEK ~2.4 in 2025" (restatement release), 13% of SIS on its own, before any industrial seals (derived).
- **They differ in industry and paying customer.** Aerospace sells bearings for aircraft engines and airframes, paid for by engine and airframe makers and their maintenance chain. Sealing Solutions sells seals for vehicles and industrial machinery, paid for by vehicle makers, their suppliers and industrial customers. The industries are aerospace components and industrial and automotive seals.
- **No exception applies.** These are not different sides of one marketplace, nor the same product sold through different channels, and the filer's own description of SIS names four businesses rather than one industry.

SIS is above the P22 line on all three measures, so it is a material irreducible unit. **FAIL → DNAF.** Where a reading was open, the harsher governs (J13); here the filer's segment description and two of its own figures decide it.

The SKF Vertevo distribution, approved by the Extraordinary General Meeting on 1 October 2026 (one Vertevo share for five SKF shares of the same class; record date 27 November, first trading 1 December 2026), removes Automotive but leaves SIS unchanged. The lens test (STD on the evidence: no bank or insurer part) and the group dependency check (SKF India Ltd and SKF India (Industrial) Ltd are 52.6%-owned listed subsidiaries below the P22 line, with combined total assets of MSEK 5,452 against the group's 106,422 at FY2025; no listed holding) were not needed for the verdict.
