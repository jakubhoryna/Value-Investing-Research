# DKSH.SW — DKSH Holding AG: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS (three material units and one listed subsidiary); Past WEAK (leverage); Future WEAK (unit customer conc.); Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (flag pair); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID (Past + Future + SF)**.

DKSH runs outsourced sales, marketing and distribution in Asia: it takes the products of pharmaceutical, consumer-goods, chemical and instrument makers into Thai, Malaysian, Taiwanese and other Asian markets and earns about 3% of sales for it. Returns on its own capital sit near 11–12%, operating profit per share grew 1.4% a year over ten years, and liabilities are 2.1 times equity. Brand owners can move their lines to rival distributors or go direct, and the filings name no customer limit. A move to WATCH would need leverage back under 2×, per-share profit growth of 6% a year, evidence of pricing power, and cleaner pay and deal records.

## 2. Understanding

Group sentence: DKSH Holding AG is a Zurich-based market-expansion services company, listed on SIX since 2012, 45% held by the Keller family pool through Diethelm Keller Holding and descended from 19th-century Swiss trading houses in Asia, that buys pharmaceuticals, consumer-health and medical products, packaged food, personal-care and household goods, specialty chemicals and food ingredients, and scientific and industrial instruments from brand owners and manufacturers (its "clients") and resells and delivers them, with marketing, field sales, regulatory, warehousing and after-sales services, to hospitals, clinics, pharmacies, supermarkets, convenience stores and other retailers, food-service operators and manufacturing companies, mainly in Thailand (30% of FY2025 net sales of CHF 11.1bn), Malaysia, Taiwan and Hong Kong.
