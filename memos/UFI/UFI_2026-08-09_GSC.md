# UFI — Unifi, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS, Past WEAK, Future WEAK, shareholder-friendliness FAIL (P17 hard strike), cheapness EXPENSIVE (both primaries n.m. on negative denominators) — **AVOID on three gates.**

Unifi is the cheap-asset-that-does-not-earn case at its purest: 0.36× book with a real recycled-fibre brand (REPREVE), no goodwill of consequence and moderate leverage — and a decade in which the whole enterprise generated $96m of operating income, cumulative free cash flow of **minus** $51m, and three consecutive operating-loss years. The equity is priced as an option on a yarn cycle that import economics keep refusing to deliver. A BUY-path does not exist at any price while both primary denominators are negative; the flip is operational (OI restoring toward ~$27m), not a price event.

## 2. Understanding
Unifi manufactures and sells texturized synthetic yarns — polyester and nylon, including the REPREVE recycled-PET branded portfolio — to knitters, weavers and brand supply chains (organisations pay, consumers wear), a vertically integrated producer recycling bottle flake through chip, POY and texturing, operating plants in the Americas (US, Brazil, El Salvador, Colombia) with an Asian sourcing hub, HQ Greensboro NC; a 50-year-old formerly mid-cap incumbent now a ~$90m small-cap in a drawn-out downturn. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
Bound-proof WEAK. On the widest defensible NIBCL the P1-window normalized ROCE is ~2.4–2.7%; on a zero-NIBCL floor ~1.8–2.0% — no read approaches the 10% ADEQUATE floor, and the FY2021–25 sub-window is **negative** (−0.9%). Leverage is the one passing limb (L/E 71.1% latest, ≤78% throughout). **WEAK** — returns, growth and cash conversion all fail independently.

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth: customer PASS on the 10-K's own sentence (no single direct customer ≥10%; top-10 ≈ 28% at last primary read — current-year sentence to re-verify); supplier concentration (NanYa virgin chip/POY) passes on the ready-alternatives exception — commodity petrochemical inputs. Forces: customer power **STRONG** (commodity-adjacent yarn, brands and knitters price-shop globally); supplier power WEAK; substitutes **STRONG** — cheap imported virgin and recycled yarn is the direct substitute crushing REPREVE's premium; entrants **STRONG** (global capacity glut, low switching costs). Tally 3 strong. Moat: REPREVE brand/certification — **eroding**, the margin record is the evidence. Market growth: MIXED (recycled-content demand real but brands trade down). **WEAK.**

- Upside risk to the verdict: a genuine texturing-cycle turn plus tariff protection could restore OI faster than the record implies.
