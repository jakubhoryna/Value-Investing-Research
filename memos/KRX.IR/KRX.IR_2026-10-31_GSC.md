Kingspan is the world's largest maker of insulated metal panels. Around it sits a broad insulation, roofing and daylighting range, plus a fast-growing data-centre airflow business. The franchise is strong: breadth is clean, no force is strong in the core panel business and the markets grow. Two things keep it off the watchlist. ## 2. Understanding

Kingspan Group is a building-products group headquartered in Kingscourt, Ireland, enlarged by a long run of acquisitions. It makes and sells insulated metal wall and roof panels, rigid insulation boards (PIR, phenolic, XPS, mineral fibre, wood fibre), roofing and waterproofing membranes, rooflights and daylighting systems, and data-centre airflow, containment, cooling and lighting systems. Its customers are building contractors, cladding and roofing installers, distributors, developers and data-centre owners and builders, mostly in Europe (70% of revenue) and the Americas (22%). **PASS.**
