## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS (two material units); Past ADEQUATE (11.7% ROCE); Future WEAK (unit customer conc.); Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (compensation); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID (Future + SF)**.

Berner Industrier is a small Nordic group that mostly resells other manufacturers' pumps, water-treatment, vibration-damping, plastics and packaging equipment to industrial and municipal buyers, plus a growing set of its own products (Zetapannan electric boilers, Swedamper dampers, Autofric screw presses). Its record is respectable: about 12% returns on capital and per-share earnings growing well above inflation. But as a distributor squeezed between large suppliers and business customers it shows no defensible position, and the chief executive's pay ran above a tenth of net income in two of the last three years. A move to WATCH would need evidenced pricing power, a customer statement, and pay back within proportion.
