# ROG — Roche Holding AG: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | STRONG | ADEQUATE | PASS (1 P19 flag; dual-class caveat) | EXPENSIVE | **WATCH** |

Roche is a first-quartile compounder — a ~26% worst-of-four ROCE, ~21% FCFROCE, self-funding R&D engine with a growing 38-year dividend — trading at ~14× EV/OI and ~19× MCap/FCF. Every gate that measures the *business* passes; the only thing between here and a BUY is price. It would need to roughly two-thirds off — below ≈ CHF 138/security (vs ~CHF 327 today) — for both primaries to clear. The thesis Roche is worth owning for: an integrated pharma-plus-diagnostics franchise (oncology, neuro, immunology + Genentech + Chugai) with pricing power inside patent windows and a founder-controlled board that returns cash through dividends rather than value-destructive buybacks. What flips it toward a real WATCH-to-BUY is a large drawdown, or an OI/FCF re-rating on the obesity/Alzheimer's pipeline that lowers the entry multiple; what flips it toward AVOID is a second SF negative or a Future downgrade if payer pricing pressure (IRA/MFN) plus biosimilar erosion visibly compress returns.

## 2. Understanding

Roche develops, manufactures and sells patented prescription biopharmaceuticals (Pharmaceuticals ~78% of sales) and in-vitro diagnostics instruments, reagents and tests (Diagnostics ~22%) to healthcare payers, hospitals, labs, wholesalers and governments worldwide; it is a Basel-headquartered, founder-family-controlled (bearer-share pool 64.97% of votes) global healthcare group operating through Genentech (US) and majority-owned Chugai (Japan). **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four 10y (FY2016–25) average ROCE **25.7%** (wc, end-of-period basis binds; xc variants ~30%+) and FCFROCE **20.8%** — both far above the STRONG floors (15% / 8%). Per-share growth: OI/sh +5.4%/yr, FCF/sh +4.6%/yr geo-mean, above the 3% nominal floor (aided by the 2021 cancellation of Novartis's 53.3m bearer shares). Returns show no sustained decline — IFRS OP is volatile (impairment/amortisation-driven: 2016–17 dip, 2024 CHF 3.2bn Flatiron/Spark write-down) but the multi-year normalised average is stable and high. L/E 185.9% (FY2025), under the 200% flag. **STRONG.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: customer base PASS (fragmented global payers/labs; no disclosed >10% customer at group level), supplier base PASS. Four forces: customer power **WEAK** (must-have on-patent drugs, high switching costs, fragmented buyers), supplier power **WEAK**, threat of substitutes **STRONG** (the defining pharma force — biosimilar/generic entry at every patent cliff; Rituxan/Herceptin/Avastin already eroded, Ocrevus/Perjeta/Tecentriq next), new-entrant threat **WEAK** (R&D scale, regulatory, IP, Genentech barriers). Tally: 1 strong force, contained. Moat: R&D scale + oncology/companion-diagnostics ingrainedness, **stable** (product-level erosion offset by a refilling pipeline — Vabysmo, Hemlibra, Phesgo, obesity CT-388/CT-996, trontinemab). Markets growing. **ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Comp (P17): highest-paid = CEO Schinecker CHF 10.2m (2025) — 0.08% of the lower of FCF (13,915) and NI (12,880); the 5% hard-strike line is CHF 644m and the indexed absolute flag ≈ CHF 32.5m — both cleared by a wide margin. Chairman Schwan CHF 5.7m (high but within Swiss mega-cap chair norms; not a >2× strike). Founder pool holds 64.97% of votes — deep alignment. RPT: dynastic bearer/participation control is a logged dual-class **caveat**, not a strike (allocation is not value-destructive). Buybacks (P19): recurring repurchases are trivial (~CHF 1bn/yr, comp-plan treasury); the one-off 2021 CHF 20.4bn Novartis-stake buyback-and-cancellation at ~CHF 356 implies ~16× EV/OI / ~18× MCap/FCF — a **flag** (>14×/16×), below the 20× hard-strike; strategic overhang removal, EPS-accretive. Dividends exemplary (38 straight increases). One flag alone ≠ FAIL. **PASS.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- US drug-pricing: IRA Medicare negotiation and any MFN/Section-232 tariff action compress pricing on Roche's largest US drugs — the single biggest Future risk.
- Biosimilar/patent cliffs (Ocrevus, Perjeta, Tecentriq) erode legacy revenue faster than the pipeline refills.
- Pipeline binary: obesity (CT-388/CT-996) and Alzheimer's (trontinemab) are large but unproven — Phase 3 disappointment removes the growth re-rating.
- FX: a strong CHF mechanically deflates reported (CHF) revenue and OI vs the USD-heavy cost/sales base.
- Dual-class founder control limits external governance leverage (mitigated by a clean allocation record).

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

To BUY: price into the ≈ CHF 138 band (or ≈ CHF 154 P12 edge) with gates intact — a ~58% drawdown or a large OI/FCF step-up. To AVOID: a second independent SF negative (a value-destructive deal or a clean sterilisation-buyback pattern) → SF FAIL; or payer pricing + biosimilar erosion driving a second strong force → Future WEAK. Next review: FY2026 results (~early Feb 2027) reset the OI/FCF basis; earlier if price → ≈ CHF 200 (NEAR) or on obesity Ph3 / IRA-MFN pricing news.
