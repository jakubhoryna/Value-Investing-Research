# 7213.T — LECIP Holdings Corporation: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS · Past **WEAK** · Future **WEAK** · Shareholder-friendliness **PASS** (one flag) · Cheapness **FAIR** → **AVOID** (two gates).

The attraction is real: ¥3.0bn of net cash against a ¥10.3bn market capitalisation, 0.93× book, and niche-top domestic shares in fareboxes, destination signs and forklift chargers. It fails anyway, and not on price. Across a decade the worst-of-four normalized ROCE is 9.4% against the 10% floor, with operating losses in four of eleven years — returns unstable rather than merely low, driven by lumpy replacement cycles rather than anything recurring. Forward, two forces read STRONG: the cash-handling line sits in the path of cashless substitution, and FY3/26's ¥1,274m onerous-contract provision on loss-priced US orders shows customers setting price. Nothing here flips on a lower share price.

## 2. Understanding

LECIP Holdings is a 73-year-old Gifu-based listed holding company whose wholly-owned subsidiaries build, install and service differentiated on-vehicle hardware for public transport — fareboxes, IC-card fare-collection units, LED destination indicators, LCD displays and the LIVU telematics unit — sold to bus and rail operators, transit authorities and vehicle makers in Japan and increasingly to US municipal transit agencies, alongside a smaller industrial arm selling forklift chargers, outdoor UPS units and contract circuit-board assembly to Japanese manufacturers. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized ROCE 9.4% (end-of-period, with cash; range 9.4–12.7%); worst-of-four FCFROCE 5.4% (range 5.4–7.3%). Both sit below the P3/P2 lines, and the shape matters more than the level: operating losses in FY2016, FY2018, FY2021 and FY2023, and single-year ROCE swinging from −4.5% to +38.8%. Per-share OI and FCF growth are not computable on a geometric mean because the endpoints and most intervening years change sign — itself the finding. Book value per share compounded 9.1%, but two equity raises supplied much of it. Leverage repaired from 380% to 98%. **WEAK** — below the floor on a full decade, on returns that are cyclical rather than earned.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASS on both legs: no customer reaches 10% of revenue, and component sourcing is multi-vendor (look-through logged — the domestic IC-card platform runs on a royalty-free Sony FeliCa licence renewing annually). Forces: customer power **STRONG** (tender buyers; the FY3/26 provision is a booked loss taken to hold share), substitutes **STRONG** (contactless and phone ticketing displace the on-board cash layer; in Houston LECIP supplies cash fareboxes as INIT's subcontractor while INIT takes the electronic system), suppliers WEAK, new entrants WEAK domestically. Tally two — the stop signal. Moat: switching costs and ingrainedness in the Japanese installed base, **eroding** as cash leaves the vehicle. Market MIXED. **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

P17: directors' aggregate ¥299m across six is disclosed, no individual is, so the top figure is bounded only below ¥100m against thresholds of ¥58.9m (FY) and ¥76.5m (3y) — **INDETERMINATE**, severity-changing, and unable to complete a pair. Outside directors ¥4m each, far inside any Japanese norm. No related-party transactions; cross-shareholdings ¥831m and a founder-family EVP are caveats, not flags. Capital allocation **CAUTION** (one flag): the live buyback executes at 2.9× FCF and the dividend is rational, but the overseas build-out has cost ~¥3bn with a fully reserved ¥1.59bn tax asset. **PASS.**

## C. Calculation notes

**NIBCL composition (all years, consistent):** 支払手形及び買掛金 + 電子記録債務 + 未払法人税等 + 前受金 + 賞与引当金. Burden-of-proof calls, all resolved *into* capital employed (conservative, i.e. depressing ROCE): 製品保証引当金, 受注損失引当金, 未払消費税等 and 流動負債・その他 are left in CE.

**未払金 judgment call.** Other payables are economically an accrued-expense line and would normally sit in NIBCL, but the FY2026 filing folds them into 流動負債・その他 with no separate figure, so including them would make the last year non-comparable with the other ten. They are therefore left in CE throughout. Sensitivity, running the whole window with 未払金 deducted (FY2026 held at the FY2025 level of ¥455m as a labelled assumption): worst-of-four ROCE 9.4% → **9.8%**, FCFROCE 5.4% → 5.6%. **The Past verdict does not move** — both readings sit below the 10% floor.

**Leases.** No P18 capitalisation. Finance leases (リース資産／リース債務) are on balance sheet throughout and are treated as debt-equivalent in CE, EV and L/E; the US subsidiary adopted ASC 842 in FY2023 (使用権資産 ¥19.8m at adoption — immaterial); the company discloses non-cancellable operating-lease commitments as immaterial and omits the table, so there is nothing to capitalise in the pre-adoption years.

**Goodwill.** None in the window. Goodwill amortisation of ¥33m ran off in FY2016 (Arcontia, acquired 2013); intangibles thereafter are capitalised software. TBV = equity − 無形固定資産.

**Operating income** is taken as reported, with no add-back of the FY2016 ¥605m impairment, the FY2026 ¥1,274m onerous-contract provision, or any other charge. Multi-year averaging is doing the dilution, per §2·1A.

## D. Future-performance worksheet

**Breadth — customers: PASS.** 有報 FY2026, 販売実績注記: no counterparty reaches 10% of total sales, and the segment note repeats it. Concentration risk is nonetheless structural in kind rather than in name — the buyer set is Japanese bus and rail operators whose capital budgets depend on municipal subsidy, plus US transit authorities buying through competitive RFP.

**Breadth — suppliers: PASS with a logged look-through.** Multi-vendor component sourcing is the stated policy and no supplier is disclosed above any threshold. The look-through item is the Sony FeliCa licence (重要な契約, granted 4 Oct 2001, royalty-free, non-exclusive, annually auto-renewing): the domestic IC-card product line depends on one counterparty's technology on a contract that renews rather than runs. Not a breadth failure — the terms are favourable and the counterparty's interest is ecosystem-wide — but it is the one effective-supplier dependency in the group.

| Force | Call | One-line reason |
|---|---|---|
| Customer bargaining power | **STRONG** | Tender/RFP procurement by subsidy-constrained operators; FY3/26 booked a ¥1,274m loss provision on orders taken expressly to defend share — the surplus goes to the buyer |

**Tally: two STRONG → Future WEAK** (P16 stop signal; memo completed per §2).

**Moat.** Single source: **switching costs / ingrainedness** in Japan — fareboxes integrate with depot cash-settlement machines, fare-data workflows and regional IC schemes, so replacing a vendor means replacing a system. Direction: **eroding**. The mechanism depends on cash moving through a machine on the vehicle; as fare payment migrates to the passenger's card or phone, the moat's substrate shrinks, and the company's own VISION2030 "モノ＋コト" pivot is an explicit acknowledgement.

**Market growth: MIXED.** Domestic public transport contracts with population decline and driver shortage (the company says so in its own 経営環境 section). US transit AFC replacement is genuinely growing — nine contract wins to date, US revenue ¥1,890m → ¥2,994m (+58.4%). The growing half is the half where LECIP has no pricing power.
