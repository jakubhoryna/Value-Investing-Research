Dropbox is the PTON/WEN split-primary case in its most instructive form: the equity trades at **8.4× free cash flow — a hair above the bargain line itself** — while the enterprise costs 15.5× operating income, because management has swapped nearly all the equity for $2.8bn of converts and term debt to retire a third of the share count.
