## 2. Understanding

Sygnity S.A., a Warsaw-based IT company founded in 1991 and listed on the Warsaw Stock Exchange since 1995 (originally as ComputerLand), with 860 average staff in 2025, designs, implements and maintains its own and third-party software systems, mainly for Polish organisations that buy through public tenders and framework contracts. In FY2025 public administration and state agencies paid 37% of revenue (social-benefit, payment-processing and registry systems), banks, insurers and capital-market institutions 28% (regulatory reporting, AML and back-office systems, including for the central bank), energy, gas and water utilities 21% (billing, balancing and network-asset systems), retailers and FMCG makers 7% (Sagra sales-analytics software, acquired 2024), and others 7%. By type, implementation was 54%, maintenance 41%, licences 4.5% and hardware 0.4%. About 8% came from outside Poland, mainly Lithuanian document-management and banking software (Edrana, DocLogix).
