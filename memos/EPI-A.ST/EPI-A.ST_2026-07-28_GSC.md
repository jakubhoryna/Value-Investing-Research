The re-test queue asked whether Epiroc survives the v3.9.0 softening of the sterilisation rule, which turned purpose-based repurchasing from an automatic strike into a flag that escalates only on price. It does not survive. The quantum v1 recorded as a data gap is now sourced: SEK 284m in FY2021 and SEK 232m in FY2022, the latter buying 1,384,888 A shares at an average of SEK 167.52. That year reads 17.7× EV/OI and 42.8× MCap/FCF — above both escalation lines, and above them at every price the shares touched in 2021 or 2022. Epiroc bought stock in its own overpriced band to hand to option holders. The strike is unchanged in outcome and better supported than before.

Three things did move. Epiroc has repurchased nothing since FY2022 — FY2023, FY2024 and FY2025 each renewed a ~1.9m-share mandate and used none of it while the stock traded far above the flag lines. That is the first redemption-clause limb this name has ever satisfied on its face, though the honest reading is inventory: the treasury holding was overstocked at 8.17m shares in 2022 and has been drawn down to 4.70m to settle deliveries, which is not price discipline. Limbs (a) and (c) fail outright, so nothing changes. Second, v1 mis-stated the SEK 3.00 per share as a 2020 redemption; it was the FY2021 dividend, SEK 3,618,864,323, approved 25 April 2022 — and the aggregator maps it to a repurchase line, which is how the error would have propagated. **Dual-class MCap in the P19 read.** The framework's implied MCap is average repurchase price × diluted shares. Epiroc repurchases A shares only, and A carried a 15.4% premium to B on 2022 average closes, so applying the A price to the whole register would overstate MCap by roughly 4%. The implied MCap here holds the year's average A/B ratio constant, mirroring the trigger-band arithmetic: SEK 823.766m A at SEK 167.52 plus SEK 389.973m B at SEK 145.17 = SEK 194,628m. At the A/B lows of SEK 150.25 / 139.70 the implied EV is SEK 175,876m against OI of SEK 8,995m — **19.6×**. At the year's average closes it is 24.9×. Every price in the range clears 14× by a wide margin, so the finding is **robust, not INDETERMINATE**. The same test on FY2022 gives 15.7× at the lows against the 17.7× derived read — the derived read is not a lucky corner of the range.

**Annual-range source caveat.** Yahoo daily OHLC is used unadjusted for the range test. Epiroc's only capital event inside the tested years is ordinary dividends, whose adjustment would lower historical prices and therefore lower the implied multiples — i.e. the direction that favours the company. The finding survives the unfavourable direction, so the caveat cannot change it.
