## 2. Understanding

Adobe sells subscription software for creating and editing images, video, design and documents (Creative Cloud, Firefly, Acrobat, Express) and for managing marketing content, search visibility and customer data (Experience Cloud, Semrush). Individual professionals, consumers, businesses and enterprises pay, online and through enterprise agreements and resellers, worldwide. It is a 44-year-old, US-headquartered market leader, reporting one segment since FY2026, in a CEO transition: Anil Chakravarthy takes over on 1 December 2026 and Shantanu Narayen becomes executive chair. **PASS.** Lens STD.
