## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |

Rockwell is the archetypal switching-cost business: its Logix controllers and Allen-Bradley installed base are wired into North American factories and into the muscle memory of the engineers who run them. Returns on capital are excellent in absolute terms (ROCE_xc never below 17% in a decade), cash conversion is real, and governance is clean — single share class, no related-party transactions, performance pay that actually paid $0 bonus in a down year. What stands between here and a BUY is price, by an enormous margin: at ~$490 the market pays ~34× EV/OI against a 7× threshold — above even the framework's 25× sell signal. Both quality verdicts sit at ADEQUATE rather than STRONG for trend reasons: ROCE halved from its 2018 peak as ~$3.5B of software/robotics acquisitions diluted the capital base, and the moat, while stable, faces a credible software-defined-automation substitute and real rivalry from Siemens. The verdict flips to BUY only on a severe dislocation into the $77–96 band with fundamentals intact; it flips to AVOID if ROCE_xc breaks below 15% or the distributor channel or moat visibly cracks.

## 2. Understanding

Rockwell Automation is the world's largest pure-play industrial automation and digital transformation company: a Delaware corporation (NYSE: ROK, HQ Milwaukee, roots to 1903 Allen-Bradley), selling control hardware (programmable controllers, drives, safety, sensing — Intelligent Devices), automation software and control platforms (FactoryTalk, Plex — Software & Control), and consulting/maintenance services (Lifecycle Services) to discrete, hybrid, and process manufacturers worldwide, ~65% of sales flowing through independent distributors and roughly 60% of revenue from North America, where it is the entrenched discrete-automation market leader; a mature leader growing at GDP-plus. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Ten-for-ten on both return floors: ROCE_xc 17.4–42.1% (all years ≥ 15%), FCFROCE_xc 8.9–38.1% (all years ≥ 8%). OI/share roughly doubled ($7.69 → $15.06) and FCF/share nearly did ($6.33 → $12.01), on a diluted count shrunk 14%. But ROCE_xc fell four consecutive years, 42.1% (FY2018) → 17.4% (FY2022), as the Plex/Fiix/Clearpath acquisitions and Sensia JV tripled goodwill+intangibles, then stabilised at 20–25%. The rubric's sustained-decline rule forces a downgrade regardless of absolute level. L/E (205% at FY2025) marginally breaches the 200% flag, but equity is buyback-shrunken; net debt ≈ 2.0× FCF, interest cover ~11×. **ADEQUATE** — exceptional absolute returns, downgraded on trend.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: end customers highly diversified across auto, food & beverage, life sciences, energy, e-commerce; suppliers broad with some single-source components — PASS, flagged for channel concentration (two distributors ≈ 20% of sales). Forces: MIXED — fragmented end-customer power LOW, supplier power LOW-MEDIUM, substitutes MEDIUM (software-defined/virtualised automation, IT entrants), entrants LOW, rivalry MEDIUM-HIGH (Siemens, Schneider, ABB, Emerson). Moat: switching costs + ingrainedness — Logix installed base, engineer training, distributor franchise, spare-parts ecosystem — stable; Allen-Bradley brand supports pricing. Market growth YES (reshoring, labour scarcity, data-centre/semiconductor capex), though cyclical (FY2024 sales −9%). **ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Comp: CEO Moret's FY2025 total $15.4M ≈ 0.9% of OI, heavily PSU/option-weighted; FY2024 annual incentive paid $0 — genuine linkage. Insider ownership small (officers+directors 0.76%) but large in dollars; single class, one share one vote, no pledging. RPT: proxy states none. Buybacks: programmatic (~$0.3–1.5B/yr, count −14% over decade, new $1B authorisation Jun 2026) — executed at full multiples, not value-triggered: caveat, not a strike, since accretion is real and far exceeds dilution sterilisation. Dividends raised consistently; M&A bolt-on but expensive (Plex ~15× revenue; recurring Sensia impairments). **PASS** with buyback-price and M&A-price caveats.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Software-defined/virtualised automation and IT-native entrants could erode hardware-anchored switching costs over a decade.
- Two distributors ≈ 20% of sales; channel disruption or consolidation would pressure terms.
- Deeply cyclical manufacturing capex: FY2024 showed −9% revenue and −46% FCF/share swings.
- Continued rich-multiple M&A (Plex precedent) would keep diluting ROCE toward the downgrade line.
- At ~34× EV/OI, above the framework's own sell signal, multiple compression alone can halve the stock without any business deterioration.

## D. Future-performance worksheet

**Breadth.** Customers: no end customer near 10%; end markets span discrete (auto, semis, e-commerce), hybrid (food & beverage, life sciences), process (energy, mining, chemicals) — broad, unlikely to consolidate. Channel: ~65% of global sales via independent distributors; two largest ≈ 20% combined (FY2023–FY2025, 10-K) — flagged. Suppliers: broad electronics/semiconductor base with some single-source components (10-K risk factor) — no dominant supplier. **PASS (flagged).**

**Five forces.**

| Force | Threat | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Customer power | LOW–MEDIUM | Fragmented end users; concentrated franchised channel |
| Supplier power | LOW–MEDIUM | Broad base; episodic chip-supply exposure (2021–22) |

Overall: **MIXED.**

**Moat.** Switching costs + ingrainedness, reinforced by brand: mission-critical Logix controllers embedded in plant processes where downtime costs dwarf hardware prices; a generation of controls engineers trained on Rockwell/ladder logic; Allen-Bradley brand premium; exclusive North American distributor franchise; parts/service ecosystem. Direction: **stable** — deepening software attach (FactoryTalk, ARR growing) offsets the long-run virtualisation threat. Moat-decides floor not needed (verdict already ADEQUATE on its own terms).

**Market growth.** YES — reshoring, labour scarcity, data-centre/semiconductor/logistics capex; but deeply cyclical (FY2024 −9% sales) and growth attracts the very rivals above. Supporting evidence only.
