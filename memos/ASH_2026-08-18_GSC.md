# ASH — Ashland Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past WEAK; Future WEAK; SF FAIL (P17 flag paired with P19 CAUTION); EXPENSIVE — verdict **AVOID** on three gates.

Ashland owns one good business — pharmaceutical excipients in Life Sciences, where drug-master-file filings and formulation revalidation create real switching costs and a 30%-plus segment EBITDA margin — bolted to a cellulose-ether franchise Chinese capacity is commoditising and a commodity BDO/NMP unit near a cyclical trough. The decade's arithmetic is unambiguous: ~$2.1bn of repurchases and a comparable weight of acquisitions went into a business whose revenue fell 40% and whose ten-year normalized ROCE is negative on all four reads. What stands between here and a BUY is not price — seven times reported OI does not cover the debt and lease stack, so the band has no positive price on either EV variant. Ancora's 28 July 2026 agreement is the first credible governance change here, but a committee is not a return on capital.

## 2. Understanding

Ashland Inc. is a century-old, ~$1.8bn-revenue Delaware corporation headquartered in Wilmington, Delaware — the serially divested residual of a former conglomerate — that manufactures, in owned plants across the Americas, Europe and Asia-Pacific, differentiated cellulose-ether and vinyl-pyrrolidone polymers, biofunctional actives and microbial protectants plus commodity BDO/NMP intermediates, and sells them to pharmaceutical, food, nutraceutical, personal-care, household, architectural-coatings, construction, oilfield and EV-battery formulators in more than 100 countries, while carrying legacy asbestos and environmental obligations. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Reported operating income over FY2016–FY2025 sums to **−$378m** against capital employed averaging ~$4.2–9.3bn: worst-of-four normalized average ROCE **−0.6%** (four reads spanning −0.55% to −0.63%), worst-of-four FCFROCE **1.8%** (1.81%–2.09%). Both sit far below P3, before any trend test. Reported OI was negative in four of ten years, including FY2024 and FY2025. Revenue fell from $3,019m to $1,824m; FCF/share compounded at −12.0% and BV/share at −2.2% against the 3% nominal floor, while ΔOI/sh and ΔTBV/sh are n.m. on sign flips. L/E of 142% (FY2025) is inside P5. P4 fires on level, on trend and on sign. **WEAK.**

## 4. Quality — future

Forces: **3 STRONG of 4.** Customer power STRONG — group pricing fell ~2% in FY2025 and management exited ~$208m of sales rather than defend them. Substitutes STRONG — synthetic associative thickeners and outright reformulation in coatings and construction. New entrants STRONG — Chinese cellulose-ether capacity is already in the market and named by management as the primary earnings headwind, so entry is realised, not threatened. Supplier power WEAK — several global dissolving-pulp producers, forward integration improbable. Breadth **not established** (customer-concentration sentence not retrieved; App A). Moat: switching costs, confined to pharma excipients (~35% of sales) and **eroding** — $706m of goodwill impaired in FY2025 ($375m Life Sciences, $331m Specialty Additives) after $530m in FY2020. Market MIXED. **WEAK.**
