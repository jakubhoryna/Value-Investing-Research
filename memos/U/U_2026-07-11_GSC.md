# U — Unity Software Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | SF | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---------------|------|--------|----|-----------|-------|
| PASS | WEAK | WEAK | FAIL (P17 + P19 hard strikes) | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** |

Unity is the rare register name that fails three gates independently and each one decisively. The company has never produced a positive operating income in eight reported fiscal years; its dominant revenue unit sits in a contested ad-tech fight it has already partly conceded (the ironSource Ads Network it paid $2.9bn for in 2022 was sunset in April 2026, $279m impaired); and its capital-allocation record contains two of the cleanest hard strikes in the register — a $52m CEO new-hire package against $286m of FCF, and a $1.5bn buyback executed at negative operating income and negative free cash flow to blunt merger and SBC dilution. The FY2025 FCF inflection ($404m) and the Vector-driven Q1-26 revenue reacceleration (+17%) are real, but FCF here is SBC-flattered ($385m added back in FY2025 alone) and GAAP OI remains −$479m. Nothing short of a multi-year record of positive operating income, comp normalisation, and clean allocation flips any gate.

## 2. Understanding

Unity sells differentiated software: a real-time 3D engine and editor licensed by subscription to game studios and industrial users (Create, ~31% of Q1-26 revenue), and a performance-advertising business paid by mobile app advertisers largely on revenue-share, now rebuilt around the Vector AI model (Grow, ~69%); customers are game developers, publishers, and app advertisers — organisations, none above 10% of revenue; industry: game-development tools and mobile ad tech; form: Delaware C-corp software platform with a partly-owned Unity China JV (redeemable NCI ~$253m); geography: San Francisco HQ, global revenue, R&D across the US, Denmark, and Israel; status: founded 2004, IPO 2020, prominent (a large share of new mobile games ship on Unity), never GAAP-profitable, and mid-way through unwinding parts of the 2022 ironSource merger. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Seven balance-sheet years exist (FY2019–25; flows from FY2018) — below P1, so STRONG is unavailable and the record is judged as it stands. Operating income is negative in all eight flow years; the worst-of-four normalized ROCE is **−19.0%** (best variant −11.5%) and worst-of-four FCFROCE **1.4%** — both nowhere near the 10% floor. FCF turned positive only in FY2023 and is heavily SBC-assisted. ΔOI/sh is n.m. (negative throughout); ΔFCF/sh crosses zero mid-window (n.m. as a geometric series). Diluted shares nearly quadrupled 2019→2025 (114m → 421m). L/E 103.2% is the lone clean metric. **WEAK.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: customers PASS (sourced 10-K statement — no customer ≥10% of revenue FY2023–25); suppliers PASS on the ready-alternatives exception (hosting concentrated but multi-cloud: GCP, AWS, Azure, Tencent). Forces on the dominant Grow unit: customer power WEAK (fragmented, though the 2023 runtime-fee revolt showed collective teeth); **supplier/platform power STRONG** (Apple and Google control signal and distribution — ATT structurally impaired the business in 2021–22); **substitutes STRONG** (AppLovin's Axon out-executed ironSource to the point Unity killed its own network; Unreal and free Godot press Create). New entrants WEAK on the engine, not on ads. Tally ≥2 strong — the book's stop signal. The Create moat (switching costs/ingrainedness: C# skills, asset store) is real but confined to the minority unit and was dented by the 2023 runtime-fee episode. Market growth YES. **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

P17 **hard strike**: CEO Bromberg's FY2024 total was **$52.1m** — 18.2% of FY2024 FCF ($286m) against a 5% line of $14.3m, with net income (−$664m) negative so the literal lower-leg test is breached at any comp level; it also clears the ~$39m indexed absolute trigger. P19 **hard strike**: FY2022 repurchase of 42.7m shares for $1.5bn (avg $35.13) executed at OI −$882m and FCF −$117m — no positive denominator exists at any price, and the programme was announced alongside the all-stock ironSource close with $550m of SBC; shares outstanding still rose 293m → 374m: dilution-sterilisation. Acquisition audit (BV ≫ TBV): ironSource consideration $2.92bn ≈ 44× its FY2021 GAAP net income of $60m — deal EV/OI ~30–41× on any defensible OI reading (hard-strike territory), and the asset was written off and sunset within 3.5 years. RPT observation: the $1bn 2027 converts funding the buyback were placed with board-affiliated Silver Lake and Sequoia. Director fees fine (top ~$320k). **FAIL** — two independent hard strikes; the audit and RPT corroborate.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Continued GAAP losses depend on SBC ($385m FY2025) staying tolerated by holders — dilution is the business model's funding mechanism.
- Grow's rebuilt Vector stack competes head-on with AppLovin from a losing incumbent position; Apple/Google policy remains an uncontrollable input.
- 2026 convertible maturity ($556m current at FY2025) and the Unity China put are liquidity claims against $2.1bn cash.
- Turnaround optics (adjusted EBITDA $138m in Q1-26) invite hotness bias; GAAP OI was −$328m the same quarter incl. impairments.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

AVOID is structural: a flip requires Past to heal (multi-year positive OI and unflattered FCF — earliest credible test is the FY2027–28 record), Future to clarify (Grow either winning with Vector or shrinking to a Create-led mix where the moat sits), and SF to reset (comp within P17, no sterilising repurchases). None is testable before the FY2026 10-K (~Feb 2027) — the first annual print after the ironSource network sunset and planned Supersonic divestiture; review then, or on any new buyback authorisation while OI remains negative.
