# PAGEIND.NS — Page Industries Limited: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS, Past STRONG, Future WEAK, shareholder-friendliness PASS, price EXPENSIVE: **AVOID**, on one gate (Future).

Page makes and sells Jockey-branded innerwear and leisurewear in India under an exclusive licence, plus Speedo swimwear. Its record is exceptional: worst-case returns of 40.7% on capital and 25.3% in free cash flow, operating profit per share up about 9% a year, no financial debt to speak of, and modest, cleanly disclosed pay. What fails is the franchise's ownership. The brand that earns those returns belongs to Jockey International, the licensor, and every product Page sells rests on that one licence, which runs to 2040. That is a supplier concentration no evidenced moat of Page's own carries. FY2026 sales also show ten dealers taking about a fifth of revenue with no statement ruling out one above 10%. The trigger is INR 4,566.8 against INR 34,150: about 87% lower. AVOID would lift only if the filings evidence the licence's price terms and Page's market position, and the customer flag clears.

## 2. Understanding

Page Industries, a Bengaluru company founded in 1994 and listed in India since 2007, is a large, mature, vertically integrated apparel maker that designs, manufactures in 16 of its own Karnataka and Odisha plants (with subcontracting) and markets Jockey-branded men's and women's innerwear, athleisure, socks and towels under an exclusive Jockey International licence for India and nearby markets, and Speedo swimwear under a Speedo licence; it sells mostly to about 2,000 dealers and distributors who supply some 116,000 multi-brand retail outlets (83% of FY2026 sales), and to 1,579 franchised exclusive stores, department-store chains and e-commerce platforms, almost entirely in India (exports under 1%). One reportable segment. **PASS.**
