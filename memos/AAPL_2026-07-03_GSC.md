# AAPL — Apple Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

|Understanding|Past|Future|Shareholder-friendliness|Cheapness|FINAL|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|PASS|STRONG|ADEQUATE|**FAIL** (comp)|EXPENSIVE|**AVOID (SF FAIL)**|

One of the best businesses this project has examined — worst-of-four 10-year ROCE of 33.2% and FCFROCE of 29.3%, double-digit per-share compounding, an ecosystem switching-cost moat holding hundreds of millions of paying consumers. The verdict is nonetheless AVOID, on a mechanical Gate-1C strike: CEO total compensation of $74.3m is ~2.5× the P17 ceiling, corroborated by the board chair's $557k fee breaching the 2×-norm stop and ~$89bn of FY2025 buybacks executed at ~$222/share — over four times conservative worth. One strike disqualifies. A flip requires comp reform, which nothing in the 92% say-on-pay result suggests is coming; the price question is academic regardless at ~46× FCF.

## 2. Understanding

Apple designs differentiated consumer electronics — iPhone (majority of sales), Mac, iPad, wearables — and monetises the installed base with attached services (App Store commissions, subscriptions, licensing, advertising), paid for overwhelmingly by individual consumers via carriers and resellers plus direct channels, in the consumer-electronics and digital-services industries, as a vertically-integrated-by-design corporation (own silicon, own software) that outsources substantially all manufacturing to Asian partners, headquartered in Cupertino with a majority of sales outside the US, a 50-year-old, ~$4.5-trillion, maximally prominent company transformed since 2007 from computer maker into a phone-anchored ecosystem. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized 10y averages: ROCE 33.2% (end-of-period, with-cash) and FCFROCE 29.3% — both more than double the P2 hurdles, and rising: FY2025 ROCE_xc on beginning CE hit 67.9%. Per-share growth: ΔOI/sh geomean 11.2%, ΔFCF/sh 8.1%, both clearing the 3% floor; ΔBV/sh −0.5% is an artifact of distributing >100% of earnings and is unhelpful here — itself a finding. Leverage: L/E 387% breaches the P5 200% flag; P6 conditions applied — every other metric is exceptional, the borrower is AA-rated with sub-4% coupons, gross debt is shrinking (peak $124.7bn FY2021 → $98.7bn), and equity contracted through distributions, not losses (call logged, Appendix C). **STRONG** — elite, still-rising returns with clean growth.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: customer side PASS (no >10%-of-revenue customer; concentration exists only at the trade-receivables level). Supplier side PASS on the letter, narrowly — Foxconn assembly exceeds 10% of COGS+opex but the ready-alternatives exception applies (Luxshare, Pegatron, Tata already serve); TSMC, the sole leading-edge fab with no ready alternative, is estimated at ~8–10% of COGS+opex [inference], flagged prominently. Forces: customer power WEAK; supplier power **STRONG** (sole-source silicon; memory oligopoly currently forcing Apple device-price rises); substitutes WEAK (Android exists, but retention ~90%+); new entrants WEAK. Tally 1 strong, contained by mutual dependence and prepaid long-term supply agreements. Moat: switching costs — hardware-software-services integration and data gravity — stable-to-strengthening. Market growth MIXED (hardware mature, services growing). **ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Comp: Cook FY2025 total $74,294,811 [sourced, proxy] vs the $30m P17 ceiling — **STRIKE** (the 5% test, $4.9bn, is not binding). Highest-paid outside director Levinson $557,231 vs the $500k US stop — second breach, marginal. Insider ownership: all 12 directors/officers hold 9.1m shares (~0.06%) — negligible. RPT: one trivial disclosed item (relative of former COO employed on standard terms) — clean. Buybacks: $89.3bn at avg ~$222/sh, ~4.2× conservative worth — overpriced acquisitions in the book's terms, though genuinely share-count-reducing (−2.6%/yr). Dividends modest (~14% payout), no empire M&A. **FAIL — strike: executive compensation.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Governance is the gate: nothing in a 92% say-on-pay vote or a founder-less, low-ownership board points toward the comp reform a flip requires.
- TSMC single-fab dependence and Taiwan-Strait exposure sit beneath every hardware line; no ready alternative exists at the leading edge.
- Memory-cost inflation is currently squeezing gross margin and forcing device-price rises — a live test of how much pricing power the moat really holds.
- App Store economics (~25%+ of services) remain under regulatory and litigation attack in the US, EU, and Japan; adverse rulings compress the highest-margin revenue.
- AI lag: agentic interfaces disintermediating the app layer would erode the moat's services-monetisation edge.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

AVOID→WATCH requires the comp strike to clear: top-executive total below $30m (or a defensible local-norm recalibration — none is available for a US mega-cap) plus director fees inside 2× norm. Quality gates would then already support WATCH. WATCH→BUY additionally needs ~$53 (band recomputes each FY). Next review: FY2026 10-K (~Oct/Nov 2026) or the January 2027 proxy, whichever first surfaces; earlier if price approaches ~$60.

## C. Calculation notes

**FY2025 CE build (US$bn).** Total assets 359.241 − NIBCL 91.931 = **CE_wc 267.310**; − cash 35.934 − ST marketable securities 18.763 = **CE_xc 212.613**. NIBCL = AP 69.860 + income taxes payable 13.016 + deferred revenue (current) 9.055. Judgment calls: (i) "Other current liabilities" (beyond taxes payable, undisaggregated in XBRL from FY2018) left in CE under burden-of-proof — accrued expenses are inside it but cannot be cleanly extracted; this depresses ROCE, conservative direction; series NIBCL composition therefore shifts at FY2018 (accrued separately netted 2015–17 only) and FY2022 (taxes payable tagged from then; FY2024 includes the $26.6bn EU state-aid tax payable — disclosed, left as-is). (ii) Long-term marketable securities ($77.7bn FY2025) stay in CE under both variants — only cash+STI are excluded per definition. (iii) Goodwill immaterial and undisclosed post-FY2017; no deduction.

**TBV build.** FY2025 TBV = equity 73.733 − goodwill/intangibles n/a — not disclosed (immaterial) ≈ **73.733**. TBV = BV for FY2018+.

**EV build, FY2025 balance sheet + current MCap (US$bn).** MCap = $308 × 14.687bn shares out = 4,523.6. Debt-equivalent = commercial paper 7.979 + current term debt 12.350 + non-current term debt 78.328 + operating leases 12.490 + finance leases 1.230 = **112.377**. EV (all cash excess) = 4,523.6 + 112.4 − 54.7 = **4,581.3**. EV (no cash excess) = **4,636.0**.

**Leases.** FY2020+ on-balance-sheet (ASC 842, adopted FY2020); liabilities treated as debt-equivalent in CE/EV/L/E. Pre-2020 years: undiscounted operating-lease commitments (~$10bn order of magnitude) were ~3% of total assets — far below the P18 10% trigger even under generous error bars; no capitalisation performed.

**Other.** No maintenance-capex disclosure → full capex subtracted. US GAAP filer — no IFRS interest reclassification. No captive-finance arm. Split adjustment: FY2015–17 diluted shares ×4 (as-filed pre-split values in companyfacts; FY2018+ restated post-split by the FY2020 10-K, latest-filed-wins).

**P6 leverage call.** L/E 387% > P5. Applied P6: all other metrics great ✓; AA-rated issuer, coupons largely 1–4%, gross debt declining since FY2021 ✓; equity shrank via distributions (buybacks + dividends > net income most years), not losses ✓. Logged reservation: FY2025 repurchases at ~$222 were not "clearly under-priced stock" by this framework's arithmetic — the parenthetical rational-distributions branch is the one relied on; the pricing critique is carried in Gate 1C, not double-counted here.

**Bias check.** Live candidates: affinity/product-familiarity (Gate 0 written to what the business is, not the brand halo), consensus and authority (near-universal institutional and Buffett-history endorsement — resisted by letting P17 fire mechanically despite a 92% say-on-pay), hotness (+47% 1-year). No verdict born of a bias; the SF strike is arithmetic.

## D. Future-performance worksheet

**Breadth.**
- Customer: Does any single customer exceed 10% of revenue? **No** — the FY2025 10-K discloses concentration only at the trade-receivables level (one customer ≥10% of receivables; carriers collectively a significant portion); no Reg S-K >10%-of-revenue customer disclosure exists, and its mandatory nature makes absence probative. **PASS.**
- Supplier: Does any single supplier exceed 10% of COGS+opex ($282bn FY2025)? Foxconn (assembly, with pass-through component procurement — vendor non-trade receivables "concentrated among a few individual vendors" per the 10-K) — very likely yes [inference]; **ready-alternatives exception applies**: Luxshare, Pegatron, and Tata already assemble Apple products at scale and the concentration is deliberate, for terms and scale. TSMC (100% of Apple silicon) ≈ $23–30bn ≈ 8–10% of COGS+opex [inference from TSMC revenue mix] — at or below the line, but **no vendor stands ready** at the leading edge; flagged as the structural fragility. **PASS on the letter**, TSMC flag carried into forces.

**Forces (binary).**

|Force|Call|Reason|
|---|---|---|
|Customer bargaining power|WEAK|Hundreds of millions of consumers, none >10% of revenue, ecosystem switching costs|
|Supplier bargaining power|**STRONG**|Sole-source leading-edge silicon (TSMC); memory oligopoly currently extracting price rises Apple is passing to consumers|
|Threat of substitutes|WEAK|Android is a real direct substitute but iOS retention ~90%+; adoption switching costs high|
|Threat of new entrants|WEAK|Barriers to entry and to success both extreme: scale, silicon, brand, ecosystem|

Tally: **1 strong — judgment zone, contained** (mutual dependence: Apple ~quarter of TSMC revenue; prepaid long-term supply agreements per 10-K).

**Moat.** Single source: **switching costs** — integrated hardware-software-services with iCloud/iMessage/App-Store data gravity across a >2bn-device installed base. Direction: **stable-to-strengthening** (services attach still rising). Brand is adjacent but the operative lock is the switching cost.

**Market.** MIXED — global smartphone units mature/flat; services and installed-base monetisation growing; wearables uneven.

**Verdict: ADEQUATE** (breadth PASS + exactly 1 strong force, contained + durable stable moat; STRONG unavailable with a strong force on the board).
