# 7974.T — Nintendo Co., Ltd.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS · Past STRONG · Future ADEQUATE · SF PASS (1 flag — capital-allocation CAUTION) · Cheapness EXPENSIVE → **WATCH**.

Nintendo is worth owning as the only pure platform owner in dedicated gaming — irreplaceable first-party IP monetised across a 150m+ install base, no debt, and worst-of-four returns that clear the STRONG bars across a full console cycle. What stands between here and a BUY is price and only price: at ¥7,641 the stock is ~4.2× its all-pass line, and even the flattering all-cash EV/OI read is 19×. The verdict flips toward the band only on a deep console-cycle drawdown; the quality verdicts would flip only if Switch 2 momentum broke (Future) or the FY22–25 returns slide resumed past the FY26 recovery (Past).

## 2. Understanding
Nintendo designs and sells dedicated video-game platforms — hybrid consoles (Switch, Switch 2 since June 2025) built by contract manufacturers around a single-sourced Nvidia SoC, plus first-party software and expanding IP licensing (film, theme parks) — sold to consumers worldwide (Americas ~45% of sales) through retail/wholesale and its own digital storefront; a 135-year-old Kyoto company, legally a single-segment integrated platform owner, currently one year into a generation transition. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
Worst-of-four normalized average ROCE **16.2%** (end-of-period, with-cash) and FCFROCE **11.0%** — both clear P2 (≥15% / ≥8%); the cash-excluded variants run 30–90%, the with-cash reads are dragged by a ¥2.2tn cash-and-securities pile. Returns fell four straight years FY22→FY25 (30.5%→8.9%, cycle-end plus Switch 2 launch costs) before turning up in FY26 and further in Q1 FY27 (OI +150% y/y) — read as console-cycle shape, not secular decline; judgment logged (App C). **STRONG.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- IP concentration: Mario/Zelda/Pokémon franchise fatigue or a failed film/park extension erodes the moat's single source.
- ¥2.0tn idle cash (22% of MCap) earning ~nothing — a standing drag on with-cash returns and a governance temptation.
- US tariffs on hardware (Q1 FY27 already carries refunds/effects) can compress hardware margin unpredictably.
