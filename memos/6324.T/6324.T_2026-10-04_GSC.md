# 6324.T — Harmonic Drive Systems: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS; Past WEAK; Future WEAK; Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (flag pair — P17 fiscal-year read + capital-allocation CAUTION); Cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID** (Past, Future and SF each fail).

The world's reference maker of strain-wave gears, the zero-backlash reducer in a robot joint, has not turned that position into returns. Across ten audited years it earned 4.8–6.0% on operating capital, with free cash flow of ¥21.7bn against ¥22.0bn of dividends. Operating income per share fell 10.6% a year. The 2017 purchase of Harmonic Drive AG valued the German affiliate at about ¥83bn on ¥11.4bn of sales. The 2021–22 minority buyout added ¥17.7bn, and ¥28.2bn of the deal was written off in FY3/24. At ¥6,570 the stock trades at 240–249× EV/OI and 1,291× FCF. Guided FY3/27 operating income of ¥8.5bn would still leave it above 70× EV/OI. Three gates would have to move before the verdict could change. That would take a full cycle of returns above 10% on a ¥107bn capital base, plus a capital-allocation record that buys below worth.

## 2. Understanding
Harmonic Drive Systems designs and makes strain-wave gears, precision planetary reducers and the actuators built on them. Most sales go through distributors and direct to makers of industrial and collaborative robots, semiconductor and display equipment, machine tools, medical devices, aerospace equipment and one automaker's engine actuator. Production is in Nagano, Germany (Harmonic Drive SE, wholly owned since 2022) and Massachusetts. The company is Tokyo-headquartered, was founded in 1970 and is 35.4% owned by KODEN Holdings. **PASS**.

## 3. Quality — past record
Worst-of-four normalized ROCE is **4.8%** (others 5.0–6.0%) and worst FCFROCE **1.7%**, both far below the 10% floor. ROCE peaked at 12–14% in FY3/19 and has not exceeded 8.1% since. FY3/24–FY3/26 read 0.1%, 0.0% and 2.4% on ¥107–115bn of capital. Per share over ten years, OI fell 10.6% a year and FCF 14.1% a year, while BV rose 7.7% and TBV 5.9%, largely on the 2018 equity raise. L/E is 39%. The result is robust to the NIBCL call and to adding back goodwill amortisation. **WEAK**.

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth passes, narrowly. The FY3/26 yūhō names no customer at 10% or more of sales. In seven of the ten years, though, one customer did: distributor Haneda Shokai, then Nissan at 11.7% and 10.3%. Customer power is **STRONG**: a few cyclical capital-goods industries set volumes, and they dual-source. New entrants are **STRONG**: Leaderdrive and other Chinese makers now supply mid-range and Chinese robots (secondary source). Supplier power and substitutes are WEAK. That is two strong forces. The moat is switching costs in premium design-ins (semiconductor, medical, space), stable there and eroding in the mid-range. Market growth is YES. **WEAK**.

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness
P17: five inside directors shared ¥213.0m, and nobody reached ¥100m. The top earner therefore received at least ¥42.6m, against a FY3/26 cap of ¥24.1m (5% of FCF ¥482m). That is a breach on the fiscal-year read only; the normalized read passes (cap ¥168m) → **flag**. Capital allocation is **CAUTION**. Repurchases of ¥5.0bn (FY3/23) and ¥1.6bn (FY3/24–26) ran at 39× to over 300× EV/OI. Acquisition spending on the German unit totalled ¥49.2bn, of which ¥28.2bn was written off. Dividends have exceeded FCF. The chairman was president through all of it. There is no live programme. No related-party transactions are disclosed. KODEN's 35.4% stake is a control caveat. **FAIL** — flag pair.
