# BABA — Alibaba Group Holding: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | ADEQUATE | ADEQUATE | PASS | FAIR | **WATCH** |

Alibaba is worth owning for two franchises: a still-dominant (if contested) Chinese commerce marketplace throwing off cash, and a China cloud/AI business that is now the group's strategic centre of gravity. The balance sheet is pristine — net cash, L/E ~70%, TBV/ADS compounded ~6× over the decade — and management is buying back stock hard at prices below book. What stands between here and a BUY is price against *normalized* earnings: the stock is cheap on assets (1.4× book, 1.9× tangible book) but the earnings-based screen (EV/OI) fails because a deliberate ~RMB380bn AI/cloud capex programme and a quick-commerce subsidy war have transiently collapsed FY2026 operating income and driven free cash flow negative. The verdict flips to BUY if price falls into the ~$59–74 band (normalized EV/OI), or sooner if the investment cycle proves value-creating and returns re-rate. It flips to AVOID if the return decline continues into FY2027 (ROCE staying sub-10%, FCF staying negative), confirming structural rather than cyclical erosion.

## 2. Understanding

Alibaba is a Cayman-incorporated holding company (NYSE ADS = 8 shares, HK dual-primary listed; China operations run through VIEs under a partnership / weighted-voting-rights governance structure) that earns mainly from merchants and brands paying advertising and commission fees on its Chinese marketplaces (Taobao, Tmall) plus enterprise customers of its leading Chinese cloud/AI business (Alibaba Cloud, Qwen), with smaller contributions from international commerce (AliExpress, Lazada, Trendyol), logistics (Cainiao) and local services — ~85%+ of revenue is China, where it remains the e-commerce and cloud leader but is losing commerce share to PDD and Douyin while cloud/AI grows fast. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

ROCE_xc cleared 10% in 8 of 10 years but only 15% in 4, and the through-cycle trajectory steps down — from mid-20s% (FY17–18) to a regulatory/competitive trough (FY22, 8.2%), a partial recovery (FY23–25), then a fresh collapse to 5.2% in FY26. FCFROCE_xc was ≥8% in 9 of 10 years but turned negative (–5.2%) in FY26. TBV/ADS compounded steadily (~6×); OI/ADS is roughly flat over the decade and FCF/ADS actually *declined* (RMB 28 → –RMB 21). Leverage is conservative throughout (net cash; L/E 46–70%). No per-share cash-flow growth over ten years plus a sustained return decline prevents STRONG; the pristine balance sheet, ≥10% ROCE in most years, and a FY26 trough driven by *deliberate disclosed investment* (not distress) hold it at **ADEQUATE** — flagged as the pivotal judgment call.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASSES (hundreds of millions of consumers, tens of millions of merchants; no concentration). Forces are MIXED-leaning-unfavourable: intense rivalry (PDD, Douyin, JD; quick-commerce subsidy war), real substitute pressure, and chip-supply constraint on the cloud side, against enormous scale. Moat is split-direction — the core marketplace network is **eroding** (proven contestable by PDD/Douyin share gains), while the cloud/AI franchise (scale, Qwen leadership in China, Cainiao logistics) is **strengthening**. Market growth is MIXED (mature commerce, fast cloud/AI). A plausible, in-part-strengthening cost/scale moat floors the rating at **ADEQUATE**; it is not STRONG (forces not favourable, core moat eroding).

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Four tests, one strike disqualifies. **Buybacks (strong positive):** ~RMB250bn repurchased FY23–25 at depressed prices below book — value-accretive, not option-sterilising. **Comp/ownership (flag):** one share class but the Alibaba Partnership holds exclusive rights to nominate a board majority (a WVR control structure subordinating outside owners); insiders ~1.8%. **Related parties (flag):** Ant Group (33% stake) and affiliate dealings, disclosed, historically <1% of revenue. **Capital allocation (flag):** dividend initiated FY24 and growing; but a mixed M&A record (Sun Art bought then divested at a loss) and a giant AI bet carry empire-building risk. No single disqualifying strike. **PASS (with flags).**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Anthropic's June-2026 allegation of industrial-scale illicit Claude distillation by Alibaba's Qwen lab (disputed, unverified) is a legal/reputational/IP-access overhang for the AI thesis.
- February-2026 Pentagon listing linking Alibaba to the Chinese military (Alibaba is suing) risks sanctions, index exclusion, and US-delisting tail risk.
- The ~RMB380bn (potentially ~RMB480bn) three-year AI/cloud capex could keep FCF negative and returns depressed well beyond FY27 if monetization lags.
- Structural commerce share loss to PDD and Douyin could make the FY26 return trough permanent rather than cyclical.
- VIE structure and WVR governance leave outside shareholders with limited control and residual China-regulatory/geopolitical risk.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

To **BUY:** price into the ≈ $59–74 band, or FY27 evidence that AI/cloud investment is re-rating returns (ROCE_xc back toward mid-teens, FCF turning positive) while the discount to book persists. To **AVOID:** Past flips to WEAK if FY27 shows continued decline (ROCE_xc sub-10%, FCF negative) confirming structural erosion, or if the Pentagon/legal overhangs materialize into forced divestiture/delisting. **Next review:** FY2027 Q1 print (~Aug 2026), or price < ≈$74, or a named legal/export-control resolution, or a capex-plan revision.
