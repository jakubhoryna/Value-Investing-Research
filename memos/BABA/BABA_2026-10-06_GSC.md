# BABA — Alibaba Group Holding: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS; Past WEAK; Future WEAK; Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (hard strike: outside-director pay); Cheapness EXPENSIVE. Final verdict: **AVOID** (Past + Future + SF).

v1 called this a WATCH on rules that predate the live framework. Re-tested on current mechanics, three gates fail. Alibaba has a huge balance sheet that has never earned a high return. Through the decade, every RMB of capital employed, including the Ant stake and the investment book, earned 7.5% in operating income on the strictest reading. Operating income per share rose 1.2% a year. Free cash flow has now gone negative, because AI capex rose to 12% of revenue. In August 2026 the company issued 710m new shares (HK$80bn) to fund more of it. The commerce core is under pressure: customer-management revenue fell 7% in the June quarter. Cloud is growing fast but takes the capital. A WATCH needs a decade of returns above 10% and an outside-director pay level back under the line. Neither can change before the FY2027 annual report.

## 2. Understanding
Alibaba is a Cayman-incorporated holding company, listed in New York (ADS = 8 shares) and Hong Kong. Its PRC operations run partly through VIEs, and the Alibaba Partnership nominates a board majority. Merchants and brands pay it advertising and commission fees on Taobao and Tmall. It also sells quick commerce, and enterprises pay for its fast-growing cloud and AI services (Alibaba Cloud, Qwen, T-Head chips). International commerce and other businesses are smaller. Most revenue is from China, where it leads e-commerce and cloud but is losing commerce share to PDD and Douyin. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
The worst-of-four normalized ROCE is **7.5%** (end-of-period, with cash), and the beginning-of-period read is 8.3%. The cash-excluded reads are 11.0–12.7%. The worst reading is below the 10% floor. FY2026 ROCE was 3.6%. The worst-of-four FCFROCE is 9.6%, but FY2026 FCF was −RMB 49.9bn. OI per share grew 1.2% a year, below the inflation floor. FCF per share fell over the decade. Book value per share compounded at 16.9% a year, mostly through retained investment gains and capital that earns little. Leverage is conservative at L/E 70%. **WEAK.** Returns do not reach the ADEQUATE floor on the strictest reading. v1 never measured that reading.

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth passes: there are millions of merchants and consumers, and no customer is concentrated. Customer power is WEAK, because demand is fragmented. Supplier power is STRONG for compute: advanced AI chips come from a few export-constrained sources, and the June-quarter release names higher chip-component prices. The substitute threat is STRONG, because PDD, Douyin and AI shopping agents offer the same discovery. The entrant threat is STRONG: Douyin and PDD entered and won share, which proves the barriers to success are low. That makes two or more strong forces in the dominant commerce unit. The commerce network moat is eroding, and customer-management revenue fell 7% (+1% like-for-like). The cloud and AI scale moat is strengthening, but it is not yet the profit centre. Markets are mixed. **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness
Outside-director pay is a hard strike. Six non-executive directors received RMB 6.1–7.4m each in FY2026, about $0.88–1.07m. The ordinary-seat stop is about $672k, and every one of the six exceeds it. CEO Eddie Wu's RMB 495.4m (about $71.8m) passes the 5% test on both reads but trips the absolute trigger, which is a flag. Capital allocation is CAUTION. Buybacks during FY2024–25 were reasonably priced. Since then the programme has gone near-idle, dividends have run ahead of negative FCF, and the August placing funds capex whose returns have not yet shown up. Related-party dealings are under 1% of revenue. **FAIL** — the director strike, with a flag pair behind it.
