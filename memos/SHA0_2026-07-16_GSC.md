# SHA0 — Schaeffler AG: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | SF | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | WEAK | WEAK | FAIL | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID (triple gate)** |

Schaeffler is a comprehensible, technically respected components maker whose economics fail every quality gate. Ten-year worst-of-four ROCE is 8.0% — below the 10% ADEQUATE floor — with a relentless decline from ~16% (2016–18) to 1.2% (2025) and L/E at 619% with none of the P6 conditions. The Vitesco merger (Oct 2024) doubled revenue into the structurally worst part of the value chain (OEM-facing powertrain electronics) without restoring returns: FY2025 OI was €190m on €23.5bn revenue. The customer side is concentrated OEMs with brutal bargaining power, and the ICE-linked half of the portfolio faces direct substitution — two strong forces, Future WEAK. SF fails on the P17 mechanical strike: CEO total comp €5.73m (+94% YoY, paid at 191% bonus achievement) against a €424m net loss — 5% of lower(FCF, NI) is negative, so any comp strikes; dividends paid through loss years at 619% L/E, partly servicing the controlling family holding's debt, is a logged second negative. No price flips this: even at zero equity value, EV/OI is 29×. Flip requires a multi-year returns recovery (restructuring targets €290m savings from 2029), deleveraging, and a positive-NI P17 re-test — a 2028+ question at best.

## 2. Understanding

Schaeffler AG is a ~€23.5bn-revenue, roughly century-old, Herzogenaurach-headquartered and globally manufacturing German maker of differentiated but competitively-bid motion components — bearings, clutches and transmission systems, e-drives, power electronics and sensors (via the 2024 Vitesco merger) — sold mainly to concentrated automotive OEMs plus industrial customers and the vehicle aftermarket, organised as a family-controlled (IHO ≈70%) listed AG mid-transformation from combustion components to e-mobility. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four 10y normalized ROCE **8.0%** (range 8.0–9.2%), FCFROCE **4.3%** — below the P3 10% floor outright. Trend is a sustained decline: ROCE_wc 14–16% (2016–18) → 7.9% (2019) → ~1% (2025), forcing WEAK regardless of level. Per-share record collapses: ΔOI/sh −23.9%/yr geometric, ΔFCF/sh −10.5%, ΔTBV/sh −7.3% (2016→2025). L/E **619%** (latest), a P5 breach with no P6 cover — the debt funds operations and the Vitesco integration, not cheap repurchases of underpriced stock. **WEAK** — fails level, trend, and leverage simultaneously.

## 4. Quality — future

Customer breadth **FAIL (probable)**: no positive no->10%-customer disclosure; VW Group is estimated ≥10% of merged revenue (Vitesco pre-merger was heavily VW-weighted) — waiver unavailable (OEMs demonstrably backward-integrate e-drives). Supplier breadth passes. Forces: customer bargaining power **STRONG** (concentrated OEMs, annual price-downs); supplier power WEAK; substitutes **STRONG** (ICE-linked components face direct EV substitution across ~half the portfolio); new entrants WEAK (scale/precision barriers). Tally 2 strong — the book's stop signal. Moat: manufacturing scale and co-engineering switching costs in bearings, but **eroding** at group level as powertrain content shifts to contested electronics. Market growth MIXED. **WEAK.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Restructuring (4,700 jobs, €290m savings from 2029) could restore mid-cycle OI toward €1.5bn+, re-opening a computable band.
- E-Mobility division (rev +7% FY2025) reaching break-even would remove the largest OI drag (DCF −€1.0bn in 2025).
- 619% L/E with €7.8bn gross debt leaves no balance-sheet margin for a downturn or EV price war.
- Family control (IHO ~70%) subordinates minority interests to holding-level debt-service needs (dividend policy evidence).
- German auto-supplier cost base vs Chinese e-drive competition is a structural margin risk, not cyclical.
