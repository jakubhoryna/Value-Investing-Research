# SYM — Symbotic Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past WEAK; Future WEAK; SF FAIL (P17 normalized-read flag paired with an RPT opacity flag); EXPENSIVE — verdict **AVOID** on three gates.

Symbotic builds warehouse automation that works: revenue rose from $100m to $2,247m in six years and the first operating profits arrived in FY2026. But the business is one customer's capital programme. Walmart supplied about 85% of FY2025 revenue on cost-plus terms, the next-largest commitment ($7.5bn) comes from a joint venture Symbotic part-owns and funds, and operating income was negative in every fiscal year on record. Cash generation comes from customer prepayments, not from returns on capital. Nothing about price can rescue it: at $43.28 the stock trades at 33.5× FY2025 free cash flow and EV/OI has no meaning on negative operating income. A flip needs a decade-long change in customer mix, not a market drawdown.

## 2. Understanding

Symbotic Inc. is a Wilmington, Massachusetts–headquartered, founder-controlled Up-C holding company (founded 2006, de-SPAC 2022) whose Symbotic LLC designs, builds, installs and then services and licenses software for robotic case-handling and micro-fulfilment systems inside the North American distribution centres of a handful of large grocery and general-merchandise retailers and wholesalers — Walmart above all — paid by the customer at cost plus a contracted margin, with 15- to 20-year software and service tails. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Operating income was negative in all seven published years, between −$106m and −$223m. Worst-of-four normalized ROCE is **−99.7%**. The two cash-excluded reads are n.m. because customer prepayments exceed non-cash operating assets, so cash-excluded capital employed is negative in five of six years. FCFROCE reads positive (49.8% worst computable) only because FY2025 free cash flow of $788m was carried by a $606m rise in deferred revenue. Per-share OI growth is n.m. on sign. L/E is 867% on parent equity and 397% on total equity, both above P5, and P6 cannot apply. **WEAK.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth **FAIL**. Walmart was ~85% of FY2025 revenue and the top five ≈ 98%. A waiver is unavailable: Walmart ran its own robotics unit until selling it to Symbotic in January 2025, so it can integrate backward. Forces: **2 STRONG**. Customer power is STRONG, because contracts price at cost plus a specified net profit, and the 2025 agreement restricts sales to a named third party. Substitutes are STRONG, because conventional distribution centres and rival automation remain the default outside Walmart. Supplier power is WEAK (largest supplier ≈ 8.4% of costs). New entrants are WEAK (scale, a decade of engineering). Moat: installed-base switching costs, stable, but captured largely by the customer. Market YES. **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

P17: the founder-CEO takes no pay. The top earner (CTO, $36.96m, mostly stock) clears the fiscal-year read on the FCF-only leg (0.94×) but breaches the trailing-3y read at 2.48× — **flag**; the ≈$40m absolute trigger is not met. RPT: the CEO-controlled C&S supplies services under unwritten arrangements, aircraft and space, and two Symbotic officers also hold C&S posts — opacity **flag** despite a ≈1% cost share. A material weakness persists at FY2025. Capital allocation **CAUTION**: no repurchases, SBC 8% of revenue, $344m of strategic investments since FY2024, led by Exol, also a customer. **FAIL** (flag pair).
