Dino is the compounding machine of Polish retail: a founder-controlled rural proximity-supermarket chain that has retained every złoty for a decade and reinvested it at 20–26% returns on capital, compounding OI per share at 28%/yr on a flat share count. ## 2. Understanding
Dino builds and operates proximity supermarkets in small-town and rural Poland — ~2,900 owned-freehold stores on a standard ~400m² format, supplied from its own distribution centres and its in-house meat plant (Agro-Rydzyna), selling groceries to local shoppers within a short drive; Krotoszyn-HQ'd, founder Tomasz Biernacki holds ~51%, listed 2017, never paid a dividend, never bought back a share. **PASS.**
