# BMAG.SW — Bajaj Mobility AG: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past WEAK (ROCE decline); Future WEAK (moat eroding); Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (compensation); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID (Past + Future + SF)**.

Bajaj Mobility is KTM: an Austrian maker of premium off-road and street motorcycles that grew revenue from €1.3bn to €2.7bn between FY2016 and FY2023 on modest returns, then collapsed. Inventory built up at dealers, the operating company went through court restructuring in 2024–25, creditors took a 70% haircut, and Bajaj Auto took control. FY2024 lost €1.19bn at the operating line and FY2025 €0.48bn once the creditor-quota gain is taken out. Executive pay rose in FY2025 while the company was burning cash. A WATCH would need several years of positive returns rebuilt from that base, a moat that stops eroding, and pay inside the loss-year line; at today's price no trigger exists until operating income is positive.

## 2. Understanding

Bajaj Mobility AG (formerly PIERER Mobility AG and KTM Industries AG), an Austrian holding company listed on SIX and in Vienna and 74.9% owned by Bajaj Auto since November 2025, designs and builds premium off-road, enduro and street motorcycles under the KTM, Husqvarna and GASGAS brands, mainly in Mattighofen, Austria, with small models built for it by Bajaj in India and CFMOTO in China, and sells them, with parts, apparel and WP suspension components, to independent dealers and importers in Europe (47% of FY2025 revenue), North America (33%) and the rest of the world; it is emerging from the 2024–25 court restructuring of its operating subsidiary KTM AG and is winding down its e-bicycle unit. **PASS.**

One material unit (Motorcycles, 93% of FY2025 revenue); Bicycle (6.8% of revenue, 1.8% of segment assets, loss-making) and the holding column are immaterial. No listed subsidiary or holding exists, so this is not a group.
