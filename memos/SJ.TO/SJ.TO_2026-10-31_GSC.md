## 2. Understanding

Stella-Jones, a Montréal-headquartered company with about 40 treating plants in six Canadian provinces and 17 US states, buys timber and lumber, pressure-treats it with creosote, pentachlorophenol, copper or other preservatives and sells wood utility poles, crossarms and (since 2025) steel lattice towers and transmission poles to electric utilities (52% of FY2025 sales), railway ties and timbers to Class 1, short-line and commercial railroads (24%), treated residential lumber to home-improvement retailers and wholesalers (18%, the largest customer alone 14% of sales), industrial products such as marine pilings and bridge timbers to construction and government buyers (5%), and untreated logs and lumber left over from pole procurement (2%); 76% of FY2025 sales were to US customers. It is an acquisition-built leader in poles and ties, sales up from C$1.6bn to C$3.5bn since FY2015. **PASS.** Logs and lumber (2%) is the only other segment: one material unit.
