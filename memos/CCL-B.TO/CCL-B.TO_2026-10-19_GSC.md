## 2. Understanding

CCL Industries is a Toronto-headquartered, Lang-family-controlled manufacturer, founded in 1951 and now with about 26,000 employees. It sells packaging and identification products. Its main line is pressure-sensitive and film labels, plus tubes, aerosol cans, leaflets and polymer banknote substrate, which it converts for multinational consumer-goods, healthcare, chemical, automotive, electronics and government customers. It also sells Avery-brand printable labels, badges and office products through retailers, distributors and online. Its Checkpoint unit sells anti-theft and RFID labelling systems to apparel and general retailers. Innovia sells specialty polypropylene films to label-material makers and flexible-packaging converters. About 98% of sales are made outside Canada. **PASS.**
