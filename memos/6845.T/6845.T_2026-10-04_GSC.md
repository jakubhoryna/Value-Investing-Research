# 6845.T — Azbil: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS; Past ADEQUATE; Future ADEQUATE; Shareholder-friendliness PASS (1 flag: capital-allocation CAUTION); Cheapness EXPENSIVE. Final verdict: **WATCH**.

Azbil is a Japanese building-automation maker-installer-servicer, with a second leg in process and factory instruments. Over ten years operating margin doubled from 7.9% to 15.8%, operating income per share compounded 11.7% a year, and the balance sheet carries about ¥95bn of cash and short-term investments against ¥21bn of borrowings and leases. Two things stand between here and a BUY. Returns on capital are adequate rather than strong, because the cash pile dilutes them. And at ¥1,458 the shares trade at 24× FY2025 free cash flow and 14–16× EV/OI, so the band prints at ≈ ¥480. The verdict turns to AVOID if a second repurchase misstep or a governance negative completes an SF pair, or if machine-level price competition in automation reads as a second strong force.

## 2. Understanding
Azbil designs, makes, installs and maintains automation. Its main unit sells building-automation systems (controllers, sensors, motorised valves, building-management software, security), instrumentation engineering and long-term maintenance and energy-saving services to building owners, developers and contractors in Japan (52% of FY2025 sales). The second unit sells control valves, transmitters, flow meters, controllers and sensors, with engineering and maintenance, to petrochemical, steel, semiconductor and machinery plants (37%). A small third unit sells gas and water meters to utilities and residential air-handling systems (11%). It is vertically integrated, Tokyo-headquartered and 82% Japan-sourced, founded in 1906 as Yamatake, and moved to IFRS from April 2026. **PASS**.

## 3. Quality — past record
The worst-of-four normalized ROCE is **13.0%** and FCFROCE **7.1%**, both on end-of-period capital with cash. Ex-cash readings are 20.6% and 11.4%. ROCE_wc rose almost every year, from 9.8% in FY2016 to 16.3% in FY2025, so there is no decline. FCF collapsed to nil in FY2021–22 as payables ran down and inventories and receivables built. OI/sh grew 11.7% a year, FCF/sh 8.8% and BV/sh 6.6%, all above the 3% floor. FY2025 liabilities-to-equity was 30%, with net cash. **ADEQUATE**: the level misses STRONG, the trend does not.

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth PASS: the yūhō states that no customer reaches 10% of sales; no supplier concentration is disclosed. Forces, by segment:
- Customer power WEAK in building and plant automation (fragmented owners, proprietary installed base); **STRONG** in metering, where a few utilities buy by tender. Metering is 1% of profit, so the force is contained.
- Supplier power WEAK: standard components, many vendors.
- Substitutes WEAK: energy codes and plant safety make control unavoidable.
- New entrants WEAK: installed base, service network, contractor relationships.

Moat: **switching costs**. Azbil-fitted buildings are retrofitted and serviced by Azbil; 75% of building-automation revenue is recognised over time. Strengthening. Market growth MIXED: steady renewal demand, cyclical semiconductor capex. **ADEQUATE**.
