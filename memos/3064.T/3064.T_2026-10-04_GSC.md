# 3064.T — MonotaRO: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS; Past STRONG; Future ADEQUATE; Shareholder-friendliness PASS (1 flag: capital-allocation CAUTION); Cheapness EXPENSIVE. Final verdict: **WATCH**.

MonotaRO is a Japanese online seller of factory consumables and repair parts. Over ten years it compounded operating income per share at 19% a year and earned at least 28% on capital employed, with liabilities-to-equity of 62–138%. Price alone stands between here and a BUY. At ¥2,060 the shares trade at 48× FY2025 free cash flow and 22–23× EV/OI, against 8× and 7×. FY2025 FCF is depressed by the ¥51bn Mito distribution centre, which opens in May 2028, so the band prints at ≈ ¥343 (P12 edge ≈ ¥429). The verdict turns to AVOID if Amazon Business or Askul force a second strong force or margin erosion, or if repurchases at today's multiples become a pattern.

## 2. Understanding
MonotaRO resells maintenance, repair and operating supplies: about 28.9m listed SKUs of tools, consumables and parts (incl. private brand), with about 688k stocked for same-day shipping. It sells by web catalogue and e-procurement links to small and mid-sized Japanese factories, workshops and contractors (11.3m registered accounts), and to large enterprises through systems integration. It runs as a vertically integrated distributor with three main distribution centres in Ibaraki and Hyogo; Inagawa is leased. It is Osaka-headquartered, was founded in 2000, is 50.7% owned by W.W. Grainger and has small overseas units in Korea, Indonesia and India. **PASS**.

## 3. Quality — past record
The worst-of-four normalized ROCE is **28.4%**. The worst-of-four FCFROCE is **12.9%** on the lease-adjusted basis (11.0% on strict all-capex). ROCE stepped down from 30–35% (FY2016–21) to 24–28% (FY2022–25), mostly because capitalising the leased Inagawa centre added ¥23bn to FY2022 capital. It has risen since that step, so the decline is not sustained. FCF dips in distribution-centre build years. OI/sh grew 19.2% a year and BV/sh 24.5% a year. FY2025 liabilities-to-equity 74%, net cash. **STRONG**.

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth PASS. The yūhō states that no customer reaches 10% of sales. No supplier concentration is disclosed; suppliers are switched on price, and all freight is 7.6% of costs. Forces:
- Customer power WEAK: millions of small accounts with small baskets.
- Supplier power WEAK: thousands of makers and wholesalers.
- New entrants WEAK: an entrant must match the scale of the inventory and distribution centres.
- Substitutes **STRONG**: the same items are a click away at Amazon Business, Askul or a local dealer, at near-zero switching cost.

Tally: 1 strong force, contained: margins widened while Amazon was present. Moat: **cost**. Purchasing and fulfilment scale spread fixed distribution-centre cost; gross margin rose from 29.3% to 29.8% and the SG&A ratio fell from 16.4% to 16.0% in FY2025. Stable. Market growth YES: online penetration of fragmented MRO is still rising. **ADEQUATE**.
