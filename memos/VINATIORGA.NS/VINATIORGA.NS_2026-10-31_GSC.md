## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS, Past WEAK, Future STRONG, shareholder-friendliness FAIL, price EXPENSIVE: **AVOID**, on two gates (Past and SF).

Vinati is the world's largest maker of two niche chemicals, ATBS and IBB, with a claimed 65% global share held since at least FY2019, no debt and no customer above 10% of revenue. The franchise is real. What fails is how its cash and its owners are treated. A decade of plant building left maintenance-basis free cash flow, read on all capex because the constructed tier was not built, at 2.4% of capital over five years. In FY2024 Vinati absorbed a promoter-family company, Veeral Additives, whose plant it had financed with loans, for shares, with no positive profit shown. In FY2026 the Managing Director's pay jumped to INR 210.6m, above 5% of trailing free cash flow and of trailing net profit. Two flags fail SF. The trigger is INR 222.2 against INR 992.2.

## 2. Understanding

Vinati Organics, a Mumbai company founded in 1989 and controlled by the Saraf family, is a mid-sized, vertically integrated speciality-chemicals manufacturer that makes, in its own plants at Mahad and Lote in Maharashtra, ATBS (a speciality monomer for water-treatment, oilfield and fibre polymers), IBB (the key raw material for the painkiller ibuprofen), butyl phenols, isobutylene derivatives and, through its wholly owned subsidiary Veeral Organics, polymer antioxidants; it sells them to chemical, polymer and pharmaceutical manufacturers in India and more than 35 countries, with exports over 55% of sales. One reportable segment. **PASS.**
