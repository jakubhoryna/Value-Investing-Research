# HFG.DE — HelloFresh SE: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | WEAK | WEAK | FAIL | FAIR | **AVOID** |

HelloFresh fails three gates: Past (returns collapsed from a COVID windfall, leverage above the 200% flag), Future (an eroding scale moat inside a structurally shrinking niche facing unfavourable forces), and Shareholder-friendliness (remuneration reports rejected by owners three years running; €125m repurchased at ~25× EV/OI on peak earnings). The 96% drawdown from the 2021 high has made the stock optically cheap — three of four multiples now pass — but EV/OI fails decisively on FY2025 earnings, and the headline FCF that makes MCap/FCF look like 2.7× is an IFRS-16 artefact: after lease repayments, the company's own FCF was €18.9m, i.e. ~32× MCap. To flip this verdict, HelloFresh would need to show durable order growth, repaired governance, and through-cycle ROCE_xc above 10% — none of which is in evidence today.

## 2. Understanding

HelloFresh SE is a Berlin-headquartered, Frankfurt-listed Societas Europaea that sells subscription meal kits (HelloFresh, EveryPlate, Green Chef, Chefs Plate) and ready-to-eat meals (Factor, Youfoodz) plus small D2C food brands directly to household consumers who pay per delivered box, running its own fulfilment network across North America (62% of FY2025 revenue) and Europe/Australia (38%), where it is the global meal-kit category leader but in multi-year volume decline (orders −12.3% in 2025) from its COVID-era peak. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Only 5 of 10 fiscal years tabulated (FY2021–25; pre-2021 statements not retrieved — flagged in Appendix A; FY2020 revenue was ≈€3,750m, roughly half of 2022's peak, indicating COVID inflated the base). ROCE_xc fell 35.8% → 10.8% → 0.2% → 7.4% (2022–25, adjusted OI); on reported EBIT, 2024–25 were negative after €287m of impairments. Adjusted OI/share dropped from €2.17 (2021) to €0.66 (2025). FCFROCE_xc of 11–16% is flattered by IFRS 16; post-lease FCF was negative 2022–24, +€18.9m in 2025. L/E reached 210.5% at FY2025 (256% at Q1'26) — above the 200% flag without exceptional returns. **WEAK** — sustained ROCE decline plus leverage breach (P4).

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASSES (millions of consumers; commodity food suppliers). Forces are **UNFAVOURABLE**: zero switching costs, abundant substitutes (supermarkets, discounters, delivery apps, rival RTE), high rivalry and promo intensity. The moat is **Cost (scale)** — the largest meal-kit fulfilment network globally, delivering a 13.5% meal-kit AEBITDA margin where rivals failed — but it defends share of a shrinking niche, not the niche itself, and it is **eroding**: orders −12.3% in 2025, RTE retention damaged, Spain and Italy exits, 2026 guidance for further revenue decline. Market growth: NO for meal kits, MIXED for RTE. **WEAK** — eroding moat amid unfavourable forces.

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

**FAIL — strike: compensation.** Owners rejected the remuneration reports for FY2021, FY2022 and FY2023 (support fell 42%→37%, bottom 0.8% of ~52,000 global say-on-pay votes). Corroborating: the Jan–Feb 2022 €125m tranche was executed at ≥€50/share (~24–27× EV/OI on peak-COVID earnings), and €212m of 2021–23 repurchases cut net share count by only 2.6m — largely sterilising SBC dilution. Ownership itself is fine (Richter ~4–5.5%, single class); RPTs show nothing material; later buybacks (20m shares at €7.60 avg) were better priced. One strike decides.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Meal-kit category decline persists post-COVID; 2026 guidance is for a further 3–6% cc revenue drop.
- RTE (Factor), the supposed growth leg, posted a −1.2% AEBITDA margin in 2025 after US operational bottlenecks hit retention.
- Governance overhang: three consecutive rejected pay reports, activist pressure (Active Ownership Capital), and US securities-fraud investigations opened in early 2026.
- TBV is melting (−38% in 2025) as buybacks plus losses consume equity; L/E now >250%.
- Reported FCF metrics overstate cash generation vs. lease-adjusted reality; a working-capital model in volume decline releases payables in reverse.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

To upgrade Past/Future: ≥2 consecutive quarters of group order growth with meal-kit stabilisation and RTE back to positive AEBITDA. To clear SF: an approved remuneration report under a reformed pay system. Cheapness flips mechanically below ≈€2.7. Downside flip to DNAF-irrelevance: take-private at these levels would moot the file. **Next review: FY2026 results (~mid-March 2027), or earlier on the 2026 AGM say-on-pay outcome (held 2 June 2026; result not yet sourced) or order-trend inflection.**
