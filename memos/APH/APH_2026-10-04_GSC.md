# APH — Amphenol Corporation: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Amphenol passes understanding, rates STRONG on its past record and STRONG on its future, fails shareholder-friendliness on an ADVERSE capital-allocation judgment, and is EXPENSIVE — **AVOID**, decided at the shareholder-friendliness gate.

Two things block a BUY. ## 2. Understanding
Amphenol designs and manufactures electrical, electronic and fibre-optic connectors, interconnect systems, antennas, sensors and specialty cable — differentiated, designed-in components, not commodities — sold to thousands of equipment makers, contract manufacturers and network and web-service operators (IT datacom 36% of 2025 sales, industrial 19%, automotive 15%, communications networks 10%, defence 9%, mobile devices 6%, commercial air 5%); a Delaware corporation of 170,000 employees in 40 countries, headquartered in Wallingford, Connecticut, run as three decentralised segments of entrepreneurial general managers, 94 years old, and mid-way through its largest-ever deal, the $10.5bn CommScope CCS purchase closed 9 January 2026. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
Worst-of-four ROCE is 18.1% (end-of-period capital, cash in), worst FCFROCE 12.7%; every year clears 17% ROCE and the trend rises — 19–23% through FY2024, 32.5% in FY2025 as datacom demand and acquired income arrived. Operating income per share compounded 19.1% a year over FY2016–FY2025 and free cash flow per share 19.2%, two down years each. Book value per share grew 15.2% a year; tangible book value is negative in nine of ten years from acquisitions, so the acquisition audit runs in Appendix E. L/E is 169% at FY2025 and 188% at 30 June 2026 after the CCS debt — inside the flag, up from 97% two years earlier. **STRONG.**

## 4. Quality — future
No customer reached 10% of sales in 2023–2025; raw materials are metals, fibre and plastics bought locally from many vendors — breadth PASS both sides. Forces: customer power WEAK (thousands of buyers, parts qualified in for a platform's life); supplier power WEAK (standard materials, many sources); substitutes WEAK (no other function replaces a connection; the optical and wireless shifts land inside the portfolio); new entrants WEAK (scale, qualification cycles, a fragmented field Amphenol consolidates). Tally 0 strong. Moat: switching costs from design-in, stable, visible in the ROCE record. Market growth YES, led by AI data centres. **STRONG.** Thesis-breaker: 36% of sales to a datacom customer set that is few, large and able to dual-source.
