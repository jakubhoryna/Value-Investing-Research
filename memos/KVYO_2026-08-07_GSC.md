This is the strongest business of the US run so far — 30%+ compounding revenue, real switching costs, $1.06bn net cash, and derived FCF of $190m in FY2025 — but the framework has nothing to hold onto: GAAP operating income has been negative in all five reported years (SBC runs ~13% of revenue), so every ROCE read is negative and the record is five years old besides. ## 2. Understanding

Klaviyo sells subscription B2C-CRM software — email/SMS/push marketing automation running on a unified first-party customer-data platform — to ~175,000 mostly small-to-midmarket e-commerce brands (the payer is the merchant, priced by stored profile count), predominantly in the Shopify ecosystem, US-centric with growing international mix; Boston, founded 2012, IPO September 2023, founder-controlled dual-class. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Operating income negative in all five reported years — −$79.2m, −$55.0m, −$330.6m (IPO-year SBC catch-up), −$84.1m, −$67.8m — so worst-of-four normalized average ROCE is **−17.8%** (beginning-CE, with-cash; ex-cash variants deeper). L/E 32.1% — trivial; net cash $1.06bn. **WEAK** (level test fails; 5y history-limited caps besides).

## 4. Quality — future

Forces: customer power WEAK (atomised SMBs, meaningful data/workflow switching costs); **platform/supplier power STRONG** (Shopify controls the rails, has built native email tooling, and extracts a revenue share — contained but real); substitutes WEAK (crowded martech field, but adoption switching costs keep the threat low); new entrants WEAK (data-integration depth; the credible entrant is Shopify itself, already counted). ## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Shopify dependence: the platform can privilege native tools or reprice the partnership; it is competitor, landlord, and shareholder at once.
- SBC at ~13% of revenue: the gap between derived FCF and GAAP OI is the employees' claim; dilution is real even when sterilised expensively.
- Marketing-automation spend is quasi-cyclical SMB opex; a downturn hits both seat growth and email/SMS volumes.
- AI-native entrants could collapse the workflow layer Klaviyo monetises (its own "Agency" acquisition concedes the vector).
