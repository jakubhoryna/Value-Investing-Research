# TSLA — Tesla, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | WEAK | WEAK | FAIL | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** |

Tesla fails three gates simultaneously — the register's second triple failure after HFG, but at ~370× the valuation severity. The record is a boom-bust arc, not compounding: four loss-making leveraged years, a genuine 2020–22 quality spike (ROCE_xc peaked at 51.2%), then three consecutive down years to 7.3% in FY2025 — below even the ADEQUATE floor and a forced downgrade on sustained decline alone. The moat is eroding while competitive forces are the most hostile of any name analysed. Shareholder-friendliness is the worst on the register: a pay package worth up to $1T granted against $4.4B of operating income, engineered to hand the CEO 25% voting control under an explicit departure threat, plus live related-party entanglement with the CEO's private companies. At 257× FCF and 360× EV/OI, the market is paying for robotaxis and humanoid robots that do not yet exist as businesses. Nothing here flips short of a governance revolution and a multi-year quality recovery — neither is a watchable trigger.

## 2. Understanding

Tesla designs, manufactures, and sells battery-electric vehicles, energy-storage systems, and solar products directly to consumers, businesses, and utilities — bypassing dealers — as a Texas-incorporated, NASDAQ-listed corporation with revenues split across the US (~half), China, and Europe; it is the US EV volume leader but has ceded global EV leadership to BYD, and its automotive revenue is now declining while it repositions as an AI/robotics company. **PASS** — the current business is describable; the promised business (robotaxi fleets, Optimus robots) is not yet a business and is excluded from the analysis.

## 3. Quality — past record

Ten years, one shape: losses through FY2019 with L/E of 302–440% (P5 breach, no offsetting returns), a spectacular but brief peak (ROCE_xc 31.2% → 51.2% in 2021–22), then relentless decay — 25.1%, 13.7%, 7.3%. ROCE_xc cleared 15% in only 3 of 10 years and 10% in 5 of 10, failing even P3. OI/share is down 69% from the 2022 peak across three consecutive down years; FCF is propped up by a FY2025 capex cut, not operating improvement. The sustained-decline rule forces a downgrade regardless of level. **WEAK.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASSES (millions of retail customers; battery suppliers concentrated but multi-sourced). Forces are UNFAVOURABLE: rivalry HIGH (BYD and Chinese OEMs in a global price war Tesla is losing on cost), customer power HIGH (repeated price cuts reveal no pricing power), entrants HIGH, substitutes MEDIUM, suppliers MEDIUM. The brand moat — Marshall's test is a price premium for like-for-like function — is directly contradicted by years of price-cutting and is polarisation-damaged; the cost moat has passed to BYD. FSD/robotaxi network effects are speculative optionality, not a defensibility fact. Market growth YES, but contested growth without a moat is Marshall's textbook trap. Eroding moat amid unfavourable forces: **WEAK.** The moat-decides floor does not apply — there is no dominant, stable moat to floor.

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

**FAIL — strike: compensation & ownership.** In November 2025 shareholders approved a package worth up to $1 trillion over ten years — 12 tranches, ~423.7M shares, lifting the CEO from ~13% toward 25% voting control — after the board warned he might quit without it, and with his own shares voting. This followed an interim ~96M-share (~$29B) award granted after Delaware courts voided the 2018 package. Against FY2025 operating income of $4.4B, proportionality is not arguable. Related-party exposure compounds it: the SolarCity precedent and a live advisory push to invest Tesla cash in the CEO's private xAI. Buybacks: none. Dividends: none, while returns collapse.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Robotaxi/Optimus could succeed at scale, transforming the economics — but that is venture optionality the framework cannot underwrite, not an analysable business today.
- The comp package's EBITDA milestones ($50B → $400B adjusted) could coincide with genuine quality recovery if achieved.
- Chinese competition and lost US EV credits could accelerate the automotive decline faster than modelled.
- ~423.7M award shares plus milestone tranches guarantee heavy dilution of any per-share recovery.
- Key-person risk is total and now contractually entrenched.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

AVOID → WATCH requires all three failed gates to repair: comp structure rescinded or radically restructured with an independent board (no visible path), ROCE_xc rebuilt above 15% for multiple years, and moat erosion arrested. No scheduled review; a robotaxi/Optimus segment reaching disclosed, positive segment economics would justify a fresh look at the future gate only.
