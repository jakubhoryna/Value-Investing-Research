# 7011.T — Mitsubishi Heavy Industries, Ltd.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding **PASS** (per-unit route, not the group sentence), Past **WEAK**, Future **WEAK**, SF **PASS** no flag, Cheapness **EXPENSIVE** — verdict **AVOID**, carried independently by Past and Future.

MHI is worth owning for a sole-source national defence franchise and a three-firm gas-turbine oligopoly, both handed unusual demand at once. Three things stand between here and a BUY: nine years of returns averaging five per cent on capital, a customer-concentration failure no waiver here can clear, and a price four times the level at which the arithmetic turns cheap. The verdict flips only if returns hold near FY3/2026's level for four or five more years *and* the price falls by about seventy per cent.

## 2. Understanding

**Group-level: FAIL.** No single sentence covers gas turbines, nuclear islands, fighter aircraft, warships, steel-mill machinery, turbochargers and air conditioners sold to utilities, a defence ministry and distributors.

**Per unit — MODEL's "more equally weighted" route: PASS, ~70%.** **Energy (41%)** — differentiated, long-qualification thermal and rotating machinery sold to utilities and states worldwide on multi-decade aftermarket service; mature, supply-constrained. **Aerospace, Defense & Space (28%)** — sole-source prime to the Japanese state for combat aircraft, missiles, warships and launchers, plus Boeing fuselage work; growing fast. **Industrial Solutions (13%)** — mass-produced thermal and drive machinery sold through OEMs and distributors; mature. **Plant & Infrastructure (16%)** — the unit that resists the single sentence: steel-mill machinery, ships, chemical EPC, paper machinery and transport systems, sharing only a contracting model. I rely on the 6501.T precedent and say so; it is not automatic. Parameters in Appendix D.

## 3. Quality — past record

**WEAK, and not marginally.** Worst-of-four normalized ROCE over FY3/2018–FY3/2026 is **5.11%** on the MODEL-conforming NIBCL (reads 5.11/5.90/5.79/6.50%), 3.93% on a trade-payables-only floor and 5.79% on the widest defensible NIBCL; worst-of-four FCFROCE is **4.79%**. The 10% floor is missed by 400–600bp on every combination of the two live judgment calls, and a shortened FY3/2022–FY3/2026 window still gives 7.12% and 8.11%. Only FY3/2025 and FY3/2026 clear 10%, ex-cash. Growth clears easily off a depressed base (ΔOI/sh +28.5%, ΔFCF/sh +23.7%). L/E 163.2%, net cash ¥896bn. P4 does not fire. WEAK on level alone.

## 4. Quality — future

**WEAK, carried by breadth.** Defence and space revenue was ≈¥1.1tn, ≈22% of group revenue, and Japan's export rules leave the Ministry of Defense as effectively the only buyer of those platforms — one customer far above 10% on an arithmetic floor. The **captive-customer waiver was tested and fails on limb 2**: limbs 1 and 3 are the strongest in the register, but 5.11% is not evidence of a dominant moat, and FR.PA governs. Supplier leg passes. Forces: customer power **STRONG** (a monopsony on a fifth of revenue); supplier, substitutes and entrants **WEAK** — tally 1, which would give ADEQUATE had breadth passed. Moat: **Government**, strengthening but not yet converted into a return. Market growth YES.

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

**PASS, no flag.** P17 is bounded, not measured: total board remuneration of ¥2,210m sits against thresholds of ¥16,606m fiscal-year and ¥13,327m normalized, so no individual can breach on either read; outside seats run ¥15–18m against a ¥98m floor stop. RPT unmeasured — the yūhō truncated — and logged INDETERMINATE, which cannot pair. Capital allocation **SOUND**: the company states affirmatively that it runs no repurchases beyond statutory odd-lot buyouts, the issued share count has not moved in nine years, dividends are 25% of earnings and 11% of free cash flow, capex has fallen while ROCE rose, and cross-shareholdings are down from 27.7% of equity to 8.6%.

## C. Calculation notes

### C1. The operating-income line — ninth instance of the standing MODEL item, and the first Japanese IFRS name where the label rule has no object

MHI presents **事業利益 ("business profit")** and **no line labelled 営業利益** anywhere in the IFRS statements. The brief anticipated a clean 営業利益 and the answer is that this filer does not provide one — so the Japanese-IFRS cohort splits, and the "convention does not bite on Japanese filers" reading cannot be sustained as stated.

The construction is: 売上総利益 − 販売費及び一般管理費 + 持分法による投資損益 + その他の収益 − その他の費用 = 事業利益 (FY3/2026: 1,082,674 − 632,810 + 16,690 + 35,619 − 69,954 = 432,218 ✓). Two candidate objects therefore exist:

- **The label rule (SGO.PA / DG.PA / ML.PA):** no object. There is no line called operating income. This is the **MMB.PA failure mode**, now observed on a Japanese filer.
- **The definitional rule (SGO.PA's stated reasoning — revenue − COGS − SG&A):** gross profit less SG&A = **449,864** for FY3/2026, an object that exists but that *excludes* other income, other expenses and equity-method results.

The wedge is **¥17,646m, 4.1% of the reported figure**, and it runs the *other* way from the French cases: 事業利益 is the **lower**, more conservative number in FY3/2026 because その他の費用 exceeded その他の収益. Running the whole window on the definitional object instead moves worst-of-four ROCE from 5.11% to roughly 5.6% — non-determinative, the seventh free calibration point of nine instances.

This memo runs on **事業利益** for two independent reasons, and they agree: it is the reported subtotal closest in position to operating income, and it is the one that *includes* impairments, restructuring and disposal effects, so using it complies with MODEL's prohibition on add-backs. That is the exact inverse of 6501.T, where the only clean definitional object — "Adjusted operating income" — was simultaneously a prohibited add-back. Taken together the two Japanese names point the same way as TRI.PA: **the rule should be scoped by a materiality test rather than by filer nationality**, and it should be stated as "the reported subtotal that includes the most operating charges" rather than by label, which would give a determinate answer on Lagardère, Hitachi and MHI alike.

### C2. J-GAAP 特別損益 — the mirror-image problem, and why the window starts at FY3/2018

MHI's own transition is the cleanest available evidence for the item. FY3/2018 was reported twice: under J-GAAP as 営業利益 ¥126,530m with 特別利益 ¥31,303m and 特別損失 ¥17,723m **below** the operating line, and under IFRS as 事業利益 ¥58,176m with ¥142,782m of その他の費用 **above** it. Same year, same company, a **2.2× difference in the operating figure**, driven overwhelmingly by where charges sit relative to the line. FY3/2017 shows the same shape (営業利益 ¥150,543m against 特別損失 ¥69,483m). A ten-year window built by splicing J-GAAP FY3/2017 onto the IFRS record would therefore add a year whose OI is overstated relative to every other year by construction — the reason FY3/2017 is reported as a supplement in B1 and excluded from the window. For completeness: on a spliced ten-year record the worst-of-four rises to roughly 5.5%, which changes nothing.

### C3. NIBCL — the burden-of-proof call, and it matters more here than on any name in the register

MODEL's NIBCL is accounts payable + accrued expenses + income taxes payable + deferred income/revenue, with the burden on the item and ambiguous lines left **in** capital employed when material.

- **営業債務及びその他の債務 (¥1,000,863m) — deducted.** Trade and other payables; squarely inside the definition.
- **未払法人所得税 (¥86,557m) — deducted.** Income taxes payable, named in the definition.
- **契約負債 (¥2,161,881m) — deducted.** Customer advances and billings in excess of revenue on percentage-of-completion contracts. This is deferred revenue in substance and in name, and MODEL names deferred income/revenue explicitly, so it is not an ambiguous item and the burden-of-proof rule does not engage. It is nonetheless **39.4% of FY3/2026 capital employed on the primary basis** and the single largest judgment in the memo: deducting it lifts worst-of-four ROCE from 3.93% (trade payables only) to 5.11%. Both readings fail. The reader should note the direction — the treatment MODEL requires is the *generous* one here.
- **引当金 (current, ¥244,851m) — left in CE.** Provisions, including contract-loss provisions and product warranties. Ambiguous, material at 3.0% of assets, so it stays in capital employed. This is where MHI's long-cycle project accounting bites: a company that books a loss provision reduces both its operating income and, under this treatment, nothing in its capital base.
- **その他の流動負債 (¥230,058m) — left in CE.** The paradigm ambiguous "other" line, material at 2.8% of assets.
- **売却目的で保有する資産に直接関連する負債 (¥281,931m) — left in CE.** Held-for-sale liabilities; no basis for treating them as non-interest-bearing operating items.

The widest defensible NIBCL adds all three and is reported in B2 (worst-of-four 5.79%). The FY3/2018 and FY3/2019 その他の流動負債 lines are the pre-restatement prints (188,121 and 157,273); the restated FY3/2019 figure is 157,273 per document 10.

**Held-for-sale asymmetry at FY3/2026, quantified.** The 31-Mar-2026 balance sheet carries ¥548,409m of Logisnext assets (including ¥21,350m of cash) while FY3/2026 事業利益 excludes Logisnext's result. Stripping both sides gives CE_wc ¥4,472,001m and ROCE_wc **9.66%**, CE_xc ¥3,101,641m and ROCE_xc **13.94%** — the cleanest available read of the current business, and still short of 10% on the with-cash leg. It does not move the nine-year averages, which are what P2/P3 test.

**Cross-shareholdings (政策保有株式), quantified.** The governance report discloses the holding as a percentage of consolidated equity: **27.7% at 31 Mar 2021 and 8.6% at 31 Mar 2025**, i.e. ¥378,477m and ¥201,816m respectively against the equity figures in B1. These sit inside 非流動 その他の金融資産 and therefore inside capital employed, where they earn dividends and revaluation rather than operating income. Removing the *whole* non-current other-financial-assets line — an upper bound roughly 2.4× the disclosed cross-shareholding stock — lifts worst-of-four ROCE from 5.11% to **5.87%**. Removing only the disclosed cross-shareholdings would give something between 5.3% and 5.5%. The item is real, it is being reduced, and it is not what keeps MHI below the floor.
