GENDA is a seven-year-old roll-up of game centres, claw-machine routes, karaoke and adjacent leisure businesses, bought mostly with borrowed money and new shares. Revenue rose 4.5× in four years, but the capital employed rose 7× and the return on it fell every year, to 3.2% in FY1/2026; maintenance-capex free cash flow, on the all-capex reading the deal years force, turned negative. Three gates fail; nothing short of a different capital record would change that.

## 2. Understanding

GENDA is a Tokyo-based leisure-facilities group, founded in 2018 and listed in 2023, that charges consumers per play for claw-crane prize games, video and medal games and photo booths in its own GiGO game centres in Japan and in claw-machine "mini-locations" placed in retailers, cinemas and restaurants across North America and the UK, and also runs karaoke rooms and rents and sells karaoke equipment, photo studios, currency-exchange machines and food-and-drink outlets, with a small line of character merchandise and anime production; Japan earned 79.3% of FY1/2026 revenue, the United States 14.8%. **PASS.**

The filer reports two segments. Entertainment Platform (91.6% of FY1/2026 revenue) mixes amusement (75.6% of group revenue), karaoke (16.2%), lifestyle, tourism and food & beverage; it is the one material unit and is graded on its largest part, amusement. Entertainment Contents (8.4% of revenue, 3.8% of segment profit, 6.2% of segment assets) is immaterial. No listed subsidiary or holding: not a group.

## 3. Quality — past record

The audited consolidated record covers FY1/2022–FY1/2026 only (history-limited; STRONG unavailable). L/E is 297% with no P6 case. **WEAK** — 5.2% ROCE, plus leverage, FCF and decline.

## 4. Quality — future

Two STRONG forces. The moat is at most a cost source (prize and machine buying scale), stable but unproven by margins. Market growth MIXED: Japanese arcades recovered after a long contraction; North America is acquired growth. **WEAK** (2+ forces).
