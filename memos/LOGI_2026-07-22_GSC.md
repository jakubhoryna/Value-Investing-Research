# LOGI — Logitech International S.A.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | SF | Cheapness | FINAL |

| PASS | STRONG | WEAK | FAIL (P19) | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** |

Logitech is a genuinely superb operating business — worst-of-four 10y ROCE 27.2%, FCFROCE 30.8%, debt-free, double-digit per-share compounding — attached to a distribution structure and a capital-return machine the framework cannot pass. Three customers each exceed 10% of gross sales (Amazon 18%, Ingram Micro 14%, TD Synnex 12%), and the largest of them backward-integrates directly into Logitech's categories via Amazon Basics, so breadth fails with no waiver available: Future is WEAK. Independently, the buyback record fails shareholder-friendliness: the FY2022 tranche breaches 20× MCap/FCF on both dual reads (72.8× year, 23.1× normalized) — a hard strike — the FY2024 10-K discloses repurchases made expressly "to support equity incentive plans" (dilution-sterilisation, a strike at any price), and five of six window tranches are flagged, a dominant pattern. Two failed gates are structural; no price flips an AVOID. The stock also trades at 2.2× the trigger band.

## 2. Understanding

Logitech designs differentiated computer peripherals and video-collaboration hardware — mice, keyboards, webcams, headsets, gaming gear (Logitech G), conference cameras — sold overwhelmingly to distributors, e-tailers and retailers (Amazon, Ingram Micro, TD Synnex) who resell to consumers and enterprises worldwide; a ~45-year-old Swiss société anonyme dual-listed on SIX and Nasdaq, HQ Lausanne/San Jose, design-led with Asian own-and-contract manufacturing, ~$4.8bn revenue. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized averages: ROCE 27.2% (end-wc), FCFROCE 30.8% — both clear P2 with a wide margin; cash-excluded variants run above 60%. Per-share geo-means FY2017→FY2026: ΔOI/sh +16.9%, ΔFCF/sh +17.4%, ΔBV/sh +12.5%, ΔTBV/sh +14.8% — all far above the 3% floor, achieved while paying rising dividends and shrinking the count 171.8M→148.2M. The pandemic bulge (FY2021 OI $1.15bn) reversed through FY2023 and returns then recovered to above pre-pandemic levels for three consecutive years — reversion, not sustained decline. L/E 74%, no debt. **STRONG.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: customer test **FAIL** — Amazon 18%, Ingram Micro 14%, TD Synnex 12% of FY2026 gross sales (sourced, 10-K). Captive-customer waiver unavailable: Amazon demonstrably backward-integrates (Amazon Basics is named as a competitor in Logitech's own 10-K), switching costs are nil, and the concentration is channel choice, not structural scarcity of buyers. Forces: customer power STRONG, new entrants STRONG, suppliers WEAK, substitutes WEAK — two strong forces, the book's stop signal. Moat: brand (Logitech G, MX) — real in premium niches, stable there, eroding in commodity categories. Market growth MIXED. **WEAK.**

- Tariff/China manufacturing exposure despite ongoing diversification.
