## 1. Verdict

Marubeni is a Japanese sōgō shōsha: a trading house and investment holding that runs ten operating segments, from grain and fertiliser to copper mines, LNG, power plants, aircraft leasing and real estate. Under MODEL v4.5.x the per-unit route to understanding passes only if every material unit can itself be described in one six-parameter sentence. Finance, Leasing & Real Estate Business carried 29.3% of positive segment profit in FY3/26 (12.1% in FY3/25), and the filer names its sub-businesses — North American auto finance, aircraft and engine leasing, commercial-vehicle fleet management, general leasing, insurance, private-equity fund management, and domestic and overseas real estate — without their revenue shares. They differ in industry and in who pays, and J13 makes a named split without shares irreducible. The verdict would flip back to a full analysis only if the segment reporting changed so that every material unit is describable in one sentence. ## 2. Understanding

It sells to businesses and consumers in Japan, the Americas, Australia and Asia. It is a Tokyo-headquartered general trading company founded in 1858, with total assets of ¥10.5tn at 31 March 2026.

**Gate 0 test.** Finance, Leasing & Real Estate is material on segment profit (29.3% in FY3/26; 12.1% in FY3/25, before the one-off real-estate integration gain). Its business fields, as the integrated report lists them, are separately managed operations: auto finance in North America, aircraft and engine leasing (Aircastle), commercial-vehicle fleet management (Wheels), general leasing and non-bank (Mizuho Leasing), insurance, PE fund investment and management, and domestic and overseas real estate (Daiichi Life Marubeni Real Estate; residential development in India, Australia and the US). The filer's own segment description already names two industries, finance and leasing on one side and real estate on the other. Car buyers, airlines, fleet operators, fund investors and property buyers are different paying customers. The filer gives no revenue shares for these sub-businesses, so under J13 the unit is irreducible. No single six-parameter sentence covers it. **FAIL → DNAF.**

The same reading very likely applies to Food & Agri, Metals & Mineral Resources and Energy & Chemicals: each is material, and each names sub-businesses in different industries (for example copper mining versus steel-products trading) without shares. Finance, Leasing & Real Estate alone decides the verdict, so those three were logged but not worked through. The lens test and the group dependency check were not needed for the verdict. On the evidence the lens would be STD: there is no licensed bank or underwriting insurer among the material parts, the insurance business is an agency, and equity-method investments of ¥3.5tn are about a third of total assets, short of R4(c).
