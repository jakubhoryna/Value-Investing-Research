# DIVISLAB.NS — Divi's Laboratories Limited: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS, Past WEAK, Future WEAK, shareholder-friendliness FAIL, price EXPENSIVE: **AVOID**, on three gates (Past, Future and SF).

Divi's is one of the world's largest makers of generic drug ingredients and a contract manufacturer for large drug companies, with no debt and a decade of returns on capital above 15%. Three things fail. Free cash flow per share has not grown over ten years, and its return on capital fell by more than a third in the latest three years as capex ran far ahead of profit. One customer took more than 10% of revenue in every year it reported on, with no evidence that Divi's can hold its returns against it. And the Managing Director took INR 1,002.6m in FY2026, 12.4% of the year's maintenance-basis free cash flow: a pay strike. The trigger is INR 243.3 against INR 9,780.

## 2. Understanding

Divi's Laboratories, a Hyderabad company founded in 1990 and controlled by the Divi family, is a large, vertically integrated chemical manufacturer that makes, in its own plants in Telangana and Andhra Pradesh (Lingojigudem, Chippada near Visakhapatnam, and Kakinada since January 2025), generic active pharmaceutical ingredients and intermediates (about 30 molecules, the world's largest maker in 10 of them), custom-synthesised ingredients and intermediates made to order for innovator drug companies, and nutraceutical ingredients (carotenoids such as astaxanthin, beta-carotene and lycopene, and vitamins); it sells them to generic and innovator drug makers and nutrition companies in more than 100 countries, with exports 89% of revenue. One reportable segment. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

## 4. Quality — future

The filer reports one external customer above 10% of revenue in each of FY2019–FY2025 and is silent in FY2026, when its top five customers took 51%. That is a customer concentration. A strong moat cannot carry it here: the filings name no share for Divi's in the concentrated customer's product, and the FY2023 fall in revenue and returns came when pandemic-era custom-synthesis orders ended. So the concentration is a STRONG customer-power force, and no containment is shown: returns did not hold against it. Supplier power, substitutes and entrants are WEAK. The moat is cost, from scale and chemistry in its generic molecules: stable. Market growth is MIXED (generic pricing pressure; contract-manufacturing demand growing). **WEAK** (customer conc.).
