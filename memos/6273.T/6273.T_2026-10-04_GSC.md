# 6273.T — SMC Corporation: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS, Past **WEAK** (P4: with-cash ROCE down three years running, worst-of-four 11.6%), Future ADEQUATE, SF PASS (one flag: capital-allocation CAUTION), Cheapness EXPENSIVE → **AVOID (Past gate)**.

SMC is a pneumatic-components specialist: a debt-free, single-segment components maker with a 28–33% operating margin through most of the decade. The record clears the 10% floor on level, but operating income has been flat at ¥190–196bn for three years while capital employed grew by ¥430bn of new plant, inventory and cash, so return on capital has fallen every year since FY3/23. The verdict flips to WATCH if FY3/27 breaks that streak; Q1 operating income was up 66%, and the guided ¥219bn would already lift the with-cash return. Price is far from any conversation: ¥73,450 against a ¥18,311 band.

## 2. Understanding
SMC designs, makes and sells pneumatic automation components (air cylinders, valves, air-preparation units, fittings) plus electric actuators, temperature-control chillers and sensors, sold through direct sales staff and distributors to machine builders and factories in semiconductors, electronics, autos, machine tools, food and medical worldwide. Greater China is its largest market (28% of FY3/26 sales); production is centred on Japanese plants, with factories in China, Vietnam, Singapore, India, the US and Czechia. It is a 67-year-old Tokyo company. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
Worst-of-four normalized ROCE is **11.6%** (end-of-period, cash in CE); the cash-excluded reads are 18.4–20.2%. Worst FCFROCE is **4.1%**, depressed by the capex build. End/with-cash ROCE ran 14.3% → 9.8% → 9.4% → 8.5% across FY3/23–26, three consecutive declines, mirrored on the beginning-of-period series (15.6% → 9.4%). The cause is real: margin fell from 31.3% to 22.6% as depreciation from the new plants arrived. Per-share growth: OI +4.2% a year, BV +9.1%, FCF −7.2%. L/E 9.3%. **WEAK** — P4 forces the downgrade from an ADEQUATE level.

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth PASS: no customer reaches 10% of sales (yūhō), and inputs are commodity materials with in-house machining. Forces: customer power WEAK (fragmented, designed-in parts); supplier power WEAK; substitutes WEAK (SMC sells the electric alternative itself); **new entrants STRONG** — management describes Chinese customers shifting to local and Taiwanese makers on price, a preference for domestic products, and continuing price competition. Tally 1 strong, contained. Moat: switching costs from the designed-in catalogue and short lead times; stable. Market growth YES. **ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Capital allocation is **CAUTION**: seven straight years of buybacks at 13–28× EV/OI and 22–96× maintenance FCF, with no price criterion. The just-completed ¥50bn programme executed at ≈19–23× EV/OI. All of it is cash-funded and sits on ¥727bn of idle cash.
