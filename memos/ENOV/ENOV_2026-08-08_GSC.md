# ENOV — Enovis Corporation: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS · Past **WEAK** · Future **ADEQUATE** · Shareholder-friendliness **FAIL** (P17 hard strike) · Cheapness **EXPENSIVE** → **AVOID**.

A competitive orthopedic franchise inside a balance sheet that has destroyed most of its own equity. The operating businesses are respectable — Reconstructive grew 8% organically in FY2025 and 6.3% in Q2 2026, ahead of its market — but the corporate result is four consecutive years of negative GAAP operating income totalling roughly **$2.0bn**, of which $1.05bn is a single FY2025 goodwill write-off. Book equity fell $3.45bn → $1.49bn in three years while debt rose $40m → $1.3bn. Not a price question — at $27 the equity is already at book, and the book is entirely intangible.

## 2. Understanding

Enovis designs and manufactures orthopedic medical devices in two roughly equal halves: implants for hip, knee, shoulder, foot and ankle reconstruction sold to hospitals and surgery centres through surgeon relationships; and braces, supports and recovery devices sold through clinics, DME distributors and insurers. Mid-cap US-listed device company created in April 2022 when Colfax separated ESAB and renamed the medical remainder, since expanded by debt-funded acquisition. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four ROCE **−16.2%**; FCFROCE **−1.3%**. Operating income was negative in every one of FY2022–25 — $(71)m, $(66)m, $(776)m, $(1,124)m — summing to **$(2,037)m**; adding back the FY2025 impairment of $1,049.8m, which is not permitted, still leaves $(74)m. Per-share growth is unreadable on a negative base: ΔOI/sh and ΔFCF/sh n.m., ΔBV/sh **−25.8%/yr**, tangible book roughly $(439)m. Reported leverage of 157% sits under the flag, but the meaningful reading is net debt of $1.27bn against FY2025 free cash flow of $19.9m — **63×**. **WEAK** on every limb, bound-proof.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth passes both sides — thousands of hospitals, surgery centres, clinics and distributors; titanium, cobalt-chrome, polyethylene and contract machining widely sourced — though the customer-disclosure sentence was not verified and is flagged. Customer power **STRONG** (hospital and group-purchasing consolidation, migration to surgery centres, payer-set bracing reimbursement, and Enovis lacks the scale of the larger implant makers); supplier power, substitutes and new entrants **WEAK**. Tally 1 strong — contained. Moats by segment: **Ingrainedness** in Prevention & Recovery, stable — DonJoy, Aircast, Chattanooga are what the channel stocks by habit; **switching costs** in Reconstructive, stable on 8% organic growth against a slower market. Market growth YES. **ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

The FY2025 fattest number is **$9.42m** to former CEO Matthew Trerotola, incoming CEO Damien McDonald at $9.24m. Against a fiscal-year ceiling of **$0.996m** — five percent of free cash flow, the only positive leg in a year with a $1.18bn net loss — that is **9.5×**; on the trailing read both legs are negative (FCF $(11.5)m, net income $(681)m). Breach on both: **P17 hard strike.** Capital allocation is separately **CAUTION** — roughly $1.25bn of debt-funded acquisitions FY2021–24, culminating in Lima Corporate, followed by $1.42bn of goodwill written off within twenty-four months and book equity down 57%. ADVERSE-class on the yardsticks, but the limb requires the same allocators without course change, and the deal-making chief executive is gone and acquisitions have stopped. The strike decides regardless. **FAIL.**
