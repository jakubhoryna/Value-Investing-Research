Bridgestone is the world's premium tire duopolist (with Michelin) and the register's second tire test after ML.PA — and it lands the same way for the same reason: a decade of flat per-share operating profit (ΔOI/sh −0.0%/yr across FY16–25) on a growing capital base, returns that averaged 9.2% with-cash and are now three years in decline (10.2 → 8.8 → 7.5%), and mid-single-digit cash returns. Flip needs the "Dan-Totsu"/premium strategy to hold ROCE above 10% across a rolled window — FY2026 guidance (adjusted OP ¥515bn) starts the test.
