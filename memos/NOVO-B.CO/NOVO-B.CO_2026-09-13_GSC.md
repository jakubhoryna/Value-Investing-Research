## 2. Understanding

Novo Nordisk makes and sells prescription peptide medicines — mainly one molecule, semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy injectable and pill, 49% of 2025 sales), plus insulins (17%) and a small Rare Disease unit (6%) — bought by three US wholesalers on terms set by PBMs, insurers and government (US 56% of sales) and by health systems elsewhere; a 103-year-old, Foundation-controlled (77% of votes) Danish group, integrated from API to fill-finish, selling in ~170 countries. PASS.

## 3. Quality — past record

Levels exceptional, growth leg not. Worst-of-four normalized ROCE 56.0% and FCFROCE 34.0% (end-of-period, with-cash) clear the STRONG bars four times over; operating income per share compounded 11.6% a year, book value 17.0%, tangible book 5.2%. Free cash flow per share compounded 0.5% over FY2015–25, below the 3% floor: FY2025 subtracts DKK 60bn of expansion capex and DKK 30bn of Akero booked as intangibles. Leverage 180%. FCFROCE fell three straight years on end-of-period bases — a documented build-out, not secular decline; P4 not applied (Appendix C). **ADEQUATE**.

## 4. Quality — future

Suppliers PASS. Forces: customer power STRONG (PBMs, insurers and the federal government set net price — rebates 69–75% of US gross sales, then MFN and Medicaid cuts); substitutes STRONG (tirzepatide beat CagriSema head-to-head); supplier WEAK; entrants WEAK. Market growing in volume, deflating in value. **WEAK** on three grounds.
