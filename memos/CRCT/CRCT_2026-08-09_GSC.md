# CRCT — Cricut, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Past **ADEQUATE** (worst-of-four ROCE 12.6%, FCFROCE 30.3%, operating income per share +6.4%/yr, returns rising every year) · Future ADEQUATE (1 demonstrated strong force, contained) · SF **PASS**, with a live INDETERMINATE on compensation that could change the gate · **FAIR** — MCap/FCF passes at 7.0× while EV/OI fails at 12.9×/10.2×, price **+33%** above the band → **WATCH — the run's only one**.

Cricut is a bubble that deflated into a decent business. Revenue fell from $1.31bn in 2021 to $709m and the market priced it accordingly — but underneath, operating income has *risen* since the trough ($80m → $70m → $76m → $96m), margin 9.0% → 13.5%, returns on capital 10.3% → 25.0%, with roughly $600m paid out, no debt and $256m of cash still on hand. What stops it being cheaper than it looks is the source of that cash: four years of free cash flow at 2.4× operating income, most of the excess from unwinding pandemic-era inventory — a finite release. Price the enterprise on operating income and the band sits at $3.15–4.38.

## 2. Understanding
Cricut sells consumer cutting machines and a razor-and-blade ecosystem around them — Design Space software, the Cricut Access subscription, branded materials — to home crafters and small makers through retail partners and its own site; Utah-HQ'd, dual-class, controlled by Petrus Trust Company. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **12.64%** (beginning-basis, with-cash; best variant 23.33%) clears the 10% floor and misses the 15% one — the only reason this is not a STRONG. Everything else supports the upgrade: worst FCFROCE **30.25%**, operating income per share **+6.4%/yr**, no debt, L/E 69.1%, and a return path that **rose every year** (10.3 → 12.5 → 14.8 → 25.0%) — the opposite of the trend problem that killed three other names in this run. Numerator and denominator both: capital employed fell $778m → $385m as inventory drained and cash went out, while revenue stopped falling ($712.5m → $708.8m) and margin expanded 270 basis points. **ADEQUATE.**

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth PASS both sides (fragmented consumer and retail-partner base; multi-vendor contract manufacturing). Forces: customer power WEAK · supplier power WEAK · **substitutes STRONG — demonstrated**: a 46% revenue fall from peak is the doing-without form measured directly, and Silhouette, xTool and Glowforge compete on the machine · new entrants WATCH (low-cost Asian entrants price aggressively). Tally 1 strong, contained. Moat: **Switching costs** — Design Space libraries, the Access subscription and materials compatibility keep the installed base sticky; subscription is the most durable line here. Stable at a lower level rather than eroding: two flat revenue years, expanding margin. Market growth MIXED. **ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness
Comp: **INDETERMINATE — the live item here.** The FY2025 proxy discloses salary ($535,400) and bonus ($806,131) but not the equity line or total; ceilings are **$3.835m** (fiscal-year) and **$3.220m** (normalized). The prior year is disclosed and does not reassure: **FY2024 total $21.70m against a $3.14m ceiling — 6.9×**, on a $17m multi-year grant. If that was the one-time award the plan describes, FY2025 passes on disclosed components; if more than roughly $2.5m of equity was granted again, both reads breach and the gate turns to FAIL. Heightened scrutiny, named re-test; an estimate cannot convict. RPT: policy disclosed, none surfaced. Control: Petrus Trust majority voting power, Nasdaq "controlled company" — documented caveat. Capital allocation: **SOUND** — repurchases of $20–38m a year at around 7× free cash flow, inside the bargain lines; dividends of $294m (2023) and $202m (2025) funded from real cash as working capital released; no debt, no empire-building. **PASS.**

## 6. Price
At $5.84: **MCap/FCF 7.0× — inside the ≤8 threshold**, the first primary this run has passed; **EV/OI 12.9× / 10.2×** fails. The split is the whole story: free cash flow has run at 2.4× operating income for four years on liquidating inventory, so the equity multiple flatters a business whose earnings power is the operating line. The band binds on EV/OI at **$3.15 / $4.38**, the free-cash-flow leg above at $6.72; price is **+33%** above the all-cash bound. **FAIR** — among the nearest-to-band names in the register.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers (context for the WATCH)
What would need to be true: revenue genuinely stabilised rather than pausing on the way down, the subscription base holding, margin gains sticking as the inventory tailwind ends. The honest bear case is that a hobby installed base decays quietly — machines last, materials get substituted, subscriptions lapse — and the two flat years are a plateau before the next leg down, taking operating income and the band with it. The compensation retrieval is the other live risk, and it is binary.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review
To BUY-relevance: price ≤ ≈ $4.38, or operating income above ~$128m. Quality upgrades to STRONG if the worst-of-four return clears 15% on a rolled window — plausible within two years on this trajectory. Next review: **FY2026 10-K (~Mar 2027)**, plus the proxy retrieval. **Alert < ≈ $4.40.**
