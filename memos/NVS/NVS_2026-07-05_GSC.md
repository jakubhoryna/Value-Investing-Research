## 2. Understanding

Novartis develops, manufactures and sells patent-protected prescription medicines — oncology (Kisqali, Pluvicto, Scemblix), immunology (Cosentyx, remibrutinib), neuroscience (Kesimpta, Zolgensma), and cardio-renal-metabolic (Entresto, Leqvio, Fabhalta) — to payers, health systems, wholesalers, hospitals and pharmacies across 100+ countries (~50% US); a Basel-domiciled Swiss AG with a single class of registered shares, no controlling shareholder, and top-5 global scale. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

A perimeter caveat dominates: a same-business decade is unavailable (Alcon 2019, Sandoz 2023 spins), so the clean pure-play record spans only ~3 years (balance sheet IM-only from YE2023). On that window returns are strong and rising: worst-of-four normalized-average ROCE **15.6%** (ROCE_xc 13.5→21.0%), FCFROCE worst **17.5%** — both clear the STRONG numeric bars, but thin history (<P1) blocks STRONG. Per-share OI and FCF rose fast (flattered by a 2022–23 impairment trough and buyback-driven share reduction); BV/sh is flat as Novartis returns >100% of earnings. Leverage is comfortable (L/E 140%, net debt/FCF ~1.1×, single-A), though net debt has climbed from ~$1bn (2021) to ~$22bn on debt-funded returns. **ADEQUATE** — rising toward STRONG, capped by clean-history length. *(Judgment call, App. C.)*

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth **PASS**: economic customers (payers/patients globally) are diversified — a US wholesale-channel concentration (3 distributors) is intermediary/pass-through with ready alternatives; suppliers broad. Forces are the concern — two read STRONG: **customer power** (payers strengthening under US IRA price negotiation — Entresto was an early pick — and MFN pressure) and **substitutes** (structural generic/biosimilar erosion at every LoE, plus in-class branded rivals). Supplier power and new-entrant threat are WEAK. A strict 2-strong tally signals WEAK; I float to **ADEQUATE** via a qualified moat-decides call: an IP + R&D-scale moat, a deep diversified late-stage pipeline (pelacarsen, remibrutinib, atrasentan, radioligands) and a 30-year record of renewing the portfolio give genuine — if treadmill — durability. Market growth positive long-term, MIXED near-term (2026 Entresto trough). **ADEQUATE.** *(Judgment call.)*
