## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past WEAK (ROCE decline); Future ADEQUATE; Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (buybacks); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID** (Past + SF).

SiteOne is the only national full-line wholesale distributor of landscape supplies in the United States, a roll-up of regional distributors that sells to more than 440,000 landscape professionals with no customer above 2% of sales. Its gross margin has risen from 31% to 35% over the decade, but returns on capital have not kept pace with the capital the acquisitions brought in: ROCE fell three years running from the 2021 peak, to 7–9% in 2024–25. Two things stand in the way. The record: the decline sits on a ten-year ROCE of 11.1%. Capital allocation: repurchases in 2025 and 2026 were made clearly above conservative worth, and buying continues under a price-blind authorisation. At $103.16 the stock trades at 20.2× FCF and 26.0× EV/OI against a $9.03 trigger. A flip needs returns back above 15% for two clean years, repurchases held to worth, and a far lower price.

## 2. Understanding

SiteOne Landscape Supply, Inc. is a Roswell, Georgia-based, NYSE-listed (since May 2016; formerly John Deere Landscapes, carved out of Deere in 2013) wholesale distributor that buys hardscapes, irrigation supplies, nursery goods, landscape accessories and outdoor lighting (77% of 2025 sales of $4.70bn) and fertilizer, control products, ice melt and equipment (23%) from about 6,000 suppliers, plus its own LESCO and other brands (15% of sales), and resells about 180,000 products from 673 branches in 45 US states and five Canadian provinces to more than 440,000 landscape contractors, lawn-care and pest-control operators, golf courses, sports-turf managers and municipalities, with new construction about 34% of sales and maintenance and repair the rest.

One reportable segment; no listed subsidiary or holding. **PASS.** Lens STD.

## 4. Quality — future

No customer exceeds 2% of sales and the top ten are under 4%; the top ten suppliers are about 30% of purchases, with no single supplier disclosed above 10%. No concentration flag. All four forces are weak: fragmented small customers, 6,000 suppliers, no doing-without substitute for professional landscape work, and scale that dulls entry. Heritage Landscape Supply, owned by The Home Depot since 2024, is the entrant to watch. The moat is cost: purchasing scale and local branch density, stable to strengthening (gross margin 31.3% in 2016, 34.8% in 2025). Market growth is MIXED: organic sales flat over 2023–25 and in the first half of 2026, with volume down under price. **ADEQUATE.**
