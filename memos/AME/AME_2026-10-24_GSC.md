## 2. Understanding

**Group sentence.** AMETEK designs and makes differentiated electronic instruments and electromechanical devices — process, analytical, test and measurement instruments, aerospace and power instruments, precision motion and automation parts, medical components, specialty metals and aircraft thermal systems — and sells them to process plants, laboratories, pharmaceutical, semiconductor and medical-device makers, aircraft and engine makers and operators, utilities and data-centre operators. It is a decentralised, acquisition-built group of operating businesses headquartered in Berwyn, Pennsylvania, with about half its sales outside the US, listed on the NYSE since 1930, with $7.4bn of FY2025 sales, enlarged by the $5.0bn Indicor Instrumentation purchase of August 2026. **PASS.**

**Electronic Instruments (EIG, 66.5% of FY2025 sales).** EIG makes analytical, test and measurement instruments — process and analytical instruments (70%) for process plants, laboratories, pharmaceutical and semiconductor makers, and aerospace and power instruments (30%) for aircraft and engine makers, airlines, the military, utilities and data centres — sold by direct engineers and distributors worldwide. FARO and Indicor sit here.

**Electromechanical (EMG, 33.5%).** EMG makes automation and engineered solutions (70%: motion control, motors, surgical and implantable components, specialty metals and interconnects) for equipment and medical-device makers, and aerospace products (30%: motor-blowers and heat exchangers, plus MRO services) for aircraft makers and operators.
