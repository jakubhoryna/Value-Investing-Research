# AZTA — Azenta, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Past **WEAK** — operating income was negative in every window year (Σ three-year −$151m); returns on capital run −1.9% to −3.6% and FCFROCE 0.4% · Future ADEQUATE · SF **FAIL** (P17 hard strike, both reads: $10.37m against ceilings of $1.92m and $0.48m; P19 CAUTION on $1.5bn of repurchases) · EXPENSIVE (36.6× free cash flow; no positive operating income to price) → **AVOID (three gates)**.

Azenta sold its semiconductor automation business for $3bn in 2022 and has spent the years since failing to turn the proceeds into an operating company. Revenue is roughly flat at $594m, operating losses have narrowed but not ended, and $1.5bn went into buying back a fifth of the shares at roughly double today's price. What is left is a genuinely sticky business — sample storage and genomics services customers do not move casually — inside a shell that still earns nothing, at 0.8× book with $543m of cash and securities. The framework does not reward cheap assets that do not earn, and will not overlook a $10.4m package in a year with a $59.5m net loss.

## 2. Understanding
Azenta provides sample-management infrastructure and services to biopharma and academic research — automated cold storage and consumables, outsourced biorepository and cryogenic storage, and genomics services including sequencing and synthesis — sold to pharma, biotech, hospitals and research institutions; Massachusetts-HQ'd, the life-sciences remainder of the former Brooks Automation. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
There is nothing to compound. Operating income was **−$73.1m, −$51.3m and −$26.8m** across FY2023–25, a three-year sum of −$151m, so every normalized return variant is negative: **−1.86% to −3.55%**. Free cash flow ran −$21.9m, +$12.4m, +$38.3m — worst FCFROCE **0.35%**, positive and trivial. The trend is the record's one encouraging feature: losses halved twice and free cash flow turned. But three years of improvement from a deep loss is not a return record. The balance sheet is pristine and idle — $543m of cash and securities, no funded debt, L/E 19.4% — and total assets fell $3.72bn → $2.06bn as the proceeds were paid out. **WEAK.**

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth PASS both sides (a fragmented base of pharma, biotech and academic customers; diversified reagent and instrument supply). Forces: customer power WATCH — biopharma R&D budgets set the pace and 2023–25 showed how quickly they contract · supplier power WEAK · substitutes WEAK — sample storage has no substitute once samples exist · new entrants WEAK (Thermo Fisher and Revvity are rivals, not entrants; validated cold-chain custody is slow to build). Tally 0 strong. Moat: **Switching costs** — a customer's biorepository is physically and contractually hard to move, and the genomics workflows sit inside validated protocols; genuine but confined to the storage side. Market growth YES. **ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness
Comp: **P17 hard strike.** FY2025 net income is negative, so the read runs on the free-cash-flow leg alone: ceiling **$1.916m** against CEO Marotta's **$10.371m — 5.4×**; the trailing-3-year ceiling is **$0.479m — 21.6×**. The figure is secondary-sourced with a parsing caveat, which the cushion makes irrelevant. Capital allocation: **CAUTION** — **$838.5m of repurchases in FY2023 and $661.7m in FY2024**, ~$1.5bn retiring a fifth of the shares at an average well above today's price, in years with no operating income to value the stock against and no published price discipline, funded by selling the profitable half of the business. Course since changed (nil in FY2025), so not ADVERSE. Two independent negatives; the strike alone fails the gate. **FAIL.**

## 6. Price
At $32.04 the equity is $1.40bn, of which $543m is cash and securities — the stock trades at **0.81× book**. **MCap/FCF 36.6×**; EV/OI does not exist, the denominator being negative. The band therefore has one computable leg, free cash flow at **$7.00**. The restoring condition is unusually close: with leases the only debt, **any operating income above ≈ $7.3m gives a positive no-cash bound** — one profitable year makes this name properly measurable. **EXPENSIVE** on what can be measured today.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers (context for the AVOID)
The bull case is arithmetic: cash and securities are 39% of market capitalisation, the stock is below book, guidance points to 3–5% organic growth with ~300 basis points of adjusted EBITDA margin expansion, and even modest operating profitability would change every measure here. The bear case is that this has been the bull case for four years while the operating line stayed negative — and $1.5bn of shareholder money bought stock at roughly twice today's price during the wait.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review
Quality needs sustained positive operating income — the FY2026 result is the first fair test — and then several years of it before a normalized return means anything. The comp strike clears only on a much smaller package or a transformed earnings base. Next review: **FY2026 results (~Nov 2026)**. **Alert < ≈ $7.**
