# CNR.TO — Canadian National Railway Company: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS (STD lens, one segment), Past WEAK (12.4% ROCE), Future WEAK (customer conc.), shareholder-friendliness PASS with a buyback caution, cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID (Past + Future)**.

CN owns an irreplaceable transcontinental rail network that links three coasts, and few businesses face less threat from new entrants. The record is weaker than the franchise. Return on capital has held at 12–14% of a C$56bn asset base, below the 15% bar. It has fallen three years running as properties and the pension surplus grew while operating income stood near C$6.6bn. Per-share operating income has compounded 5.6% a year, almost entirely through buybacks. Shippers are organisations and the filer never rules out a customer above 10% of revenue. That leaves a concentration that the falling returns do not contain. At C$172.39 the stock trades at 27.3× maintenance FCF, about 4.8× the C$36.15 trigger. A BUY would need the returns to rise above 15% and a price far below today's.

## 2. Understanding

Canadian National Railway Company is a Montreal-headquartered Class I freight railroad. It moves bulk, merchandise, intermodal and automotive freight for industrial shippers, ports and logistics companies over its own network spanning Canada and the US Midwest to the Gulf of Mexico. It is a long-established, investor-owned former Crown corporation and one of two transcontinental Canadian railroads. PASS.

CN reports one segment; its commodity groups are product groups reported for the whole company and are not units. Not a group; no listed holdings.
