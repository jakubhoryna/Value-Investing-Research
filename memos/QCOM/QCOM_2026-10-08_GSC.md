# QCOM — QUALCOMM Incorporated: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past STRONG; Future WEAK (QCT customer concentration); Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (acquisitions); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID**, was WATCH.

Qualcomm is two businesses: a fabless chip maker (QCT, about 71% of segment earnings) and a patent licensor (QTL). The ten-year record is excellent and stays STRONG on the maintenance-capex basis. The v4.5.0 re-test moves two gates. Future falls because the chip business sells a fifth or more of revenue each to Apple and Samsung, both of which are replacing Qualcomm silicon with their own, so no strong-moat pass carries the concentration and nothing contains it. Shareholder-friendliness falls because Alphawave, bought in December 2025 for $2.3bn, was a listed company with operating losses in both of its last two years. Repurchases above worth and a pay flag sit behind that strike. At $177.12 against a $51.55 trigger, price is not close either.

## 2. Understanding

Qualcomm designs Snapdragon chip platforms that contract foundries make and that handset, car and device makers (mostly Chinese, Korean and US OEMs) buy, and it licenses its cellular standard-essential patents to virtually every handset maker; a US-headquartered, mature leader now pushing into cars, PCs and data centres. **PASS.** Lens STD. A group of two material units.

## 4. Quality — future

Three customers took 21%, 20% and 13% of FY2025 revenue (Apple, Samsung, Xiaomi), and two took 24% and 20% in the first nine months of FY2026. QTL's licensing moat is stable and carries its concentration at ADEQUATE. Market growth is MIXED. S = 1.29. **WEAK.**
