# HUBS — HubSpot, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | **WEAK** | **WEAK** | **FAIL** (P17 + P19 hard strikes) | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** |

An impressive growth franchise — revenue ~31%/yr for a decade, FCF/share ~64%/yr, net cash — failing three gates independently. The earnings engine has never run: GAAP operating income was negative in nine of ten window years, reaching just $7.4M (0.2% margin) in FY2025; every normalized ROCE reading is negative. The future gate trips the book's stop signal — two strong forces in a category generative AI is actively commoditising, exactly what the 70% drawdown prices. Governance fails twice: CEO pay 8.8× its hard-strike line, and $500M of buybacks at $501.67 — 46× trailing MCap/FCF, 59% above today's price. A flip needs years of real GAAP margins plus a changed capital-allocation record.

## 2. Understanding

HubSpot sells differentiated subscription software — a multi-hub CRM platform spanning marketing, sales, service, content, operations, and commerce — paid for by 288,706 mostly small and mid-market business customers (none >10% of revenue; ~49% arrives via Solutions Partners) in 135+ countries, in the application-software industry, as a vertically integrated freemium-to-tiered cloud vendor (97.8% subscription revenue; hosted substantially on AWS), HQ Cambridge, Massachusetts — a prominent, 21-year-old, 2014-IPO company, ~8,900 employees, first GAAP operating profit in FY2025. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four 10-year normalized average ROCE is **−17.5%** (beg/xc); all four variants are negative (best −4.0%) against the ≥10% floor — nine straight years of GAAP operating losses, P18 adjustments included. FCFROCE is strong (worst-of-four **12.1%**), but the cash rides on ~$400–500M/yr of SBC added back and a deferred-revenue float; diluted shares rose every year, 35.2M → 53.2M. Growth is stellar where measurable (FCF/sh +64%/yr; BV/sh +31%/yr); ΔOI/sh n.m. on negative bases. L/E 87% (FY2025), inside P5 after 2016–18 breaches. Returns floor fails → **WEAK**.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: customer PASS (sourced); supplier PASS with logged concentration — AWS hosts "the substantial majority" of the platform; GCP/Azure stand ready. Forces: customer power **WEAK** (289k atomized customers); supplier power **WEAK** (hyperscaler alternatives); substitutes **STRONG** (Salesforce down-market, Zoho/Pipedrive/Monday, Microsoft bundling, AI-native GTM tools); new entrants **STRONG** (genAI has collapsed point-tool build cost; freemium-tier switching costs low). Tally: **2 strong → stop signal**. The moat — switching costs from CRM data gravity — is real in the mid-market core, not dominant; at best stable under AI pressure. Market growth: YES. **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

**Hard strike (P17):** CEO Yamini Rangan's FY2025 total of **$20,126,620** is 8.8× the $2.30M line (5% of NI $45.9M, the lower leg). **Hard strike (P19):** FY2025 buyback — 997k shares at $501.67 avg ($500.0M) — implies ~$26.7B MCap: **46.3× MCap/FCF**, **~3,422× EV/OI**, both far past 20×; the diluted count still rose (dilution-sterilisation character). Director fees pass ($349,883 < $500K); RPTs clean; insiders 3.7%. A fresh $1.0B program (Feb 2026) extends the pattern. **FAIL — two independent hard strikes.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- GenAI commoditisation of the marketing/CRM stack compresses growth and pricing faster than margins scale — the bear case being priced.
- SBC (~13–17% of revenue; $528M FY25) means FCF overstates owner earnings; dilution outran the first buyback.
- Salesforce and Microsoft bundling down-market threaten the mid-market core, not just the entry tier.
- Capital allocation: $1.5B authorized after buying 59% above today's price.
- Band degeneracy: no computable trigger while OI ≈ $7M; the ≈$28 reference leg rests on one barely-positive year.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

AVOID is triple-locked; no single event flips it. Past needs sustained GAAP profitability lifting worst-of-four normalized ROCE above 10% — years out even on optimistic scaling. Future needs the forces tally to improve. SF needs a changed comp-to-earnings ratio and repurchases demonstrably below conservative worth. **Next review:** FY2026 10-K (~Feb 2027) — re-anchor the band and re-run P19 on 2026-program repurchases; earlier only on a corporate event.
