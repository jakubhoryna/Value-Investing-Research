## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS (five material units); Past WEAK (7.3% ROCE); Future ADEQUATE (unit customer conc.); Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (acquisitions); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID** (Past + SF).

Sdiptech owns about thirty small engineering firms, nearly half its sales in the United Kingdom, making niche equipment for transport, energy, water and safety infrastructure. The firms earn 20–30% adjusted EBITA margins, but the goodwill and earn-outs paid for them now make up most of the group's assets, so operating income has averaged under 8% on capital. In 2025 it wrote down SEK 500m on eleven units it had bought and then decided to sell. Price is far from range: debt-equivalents exceed seven times FY2025 operating income, so no positive price passes.

## 2. Understanding

Group sentence: Sdiptech AB is a Nasdaq Stockholm-listed group (net sales SEK 5.2bn in 2025; 47% in the United Kingdom, 15% in Sweden) that owns 31 small companies in four business areas, plus 11 non-core units being sold, mostly bought from founders with earn-outs, which make and service specialised infrastructure equipment — refrigerated-transport partitions, road and rail maintenance systems, power connectors, temporary power, water-treatment and waste-handling equipment, fire-protection and security systems — for transport operators, vehicle builders, utilities, industry, contractors and public authorities.
