# OXIG.L — Oxford Instruments plc: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS (STD lens, two-unit group), Past WEAK (ROCE decline), Future ADEQUATE (weighted), shareholder-friendliness FAIL (buybacks), cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID (Past + SF)**.

Oxford Instruments makes niche scientific instruments: electron-microscope detectors, atomic-force and Raman microscopes, scientific cameras and benchtop NMR, plus plasma etch and deposition tools for compound-semiconductor fabs. The instrument franchise earns low-twenties segment margins and is worth studying. The ten-year record is not: an operating loss in FY2017, a goodwill write-off in FY2025 and the new Severn Beach plant hold the worst-of-four ROCE at 11.2%. Two capital-allocation findings fail the company: the FY2026 buyback of £62.2m at about 29× maintenance FCF, still running with no price limit, and the First Light Imaging purchase at about 55× its operating profit. At 2988p the stock trades at 40.7× MCap/FCF, about 5.1× the 587p trigger. A BUY needs a clean record and a disciplined allocator first; price comes last.
