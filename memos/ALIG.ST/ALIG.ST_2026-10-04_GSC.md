# ALIG.ST — Alimak Group AB (publ): GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Alimak passes understanding, rates WEAK on its past record and ADEQUATE on its future, passes shareholder-friendliness, and is EXPENSIVE — **AVOID**, decided at Past.

Alimak is worth understanding for its installed base: rack-and-pinion hoists, building-maintenance units and height-safety equipment that need inspection, parts and service for decades, sold to a broad customer list with no customer above a tenth of revenue. What stands between here and a BUY is the return record. The worst normalized ROCE over FY2016–2025 is 7.5% and the best is 9.2%, so no reading reaches the 10% floor. Two acquisition waves (Facade Access Group and Avanti in 2017, Tractel in 2022) roughly quadrupled capital employed while operating income only tripled. The price is also far from the band: 19.7× free cash flow and 15.6–16.4× operating income. The verdict leaves AVOID only if returns on the enlarged base rise above 10% and stay there; at the current base that needs operating income of roughly SEK 1.2bn.

## 2. Understanding

Alimak Group AB is a Stockholm-headquartered Swedish company, listed since 2015 and now anchored by Investment AB Latour (29.8%), that designs, manufactures (26 production and assembly sites in 15 countries) and services differentiated vertical-access equipment — rack-and-pinion construction hoists and industrial elevators, facade-access building-maintenance units, wind-turbine service lifts, and Tractel height-safety and load-handling products — sold as equipment plus a 39% service and parts stream to rental companies, contractors, building owners, industrial plants and wind-turbine operators worldwide, organised in five divisions since the 2022 Tractel acquisition. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized ROCE is 7.5% (closing capital including cash); the best read is 9.2%. Worst FCFROCE is 6.4%. Yearly ROCE on closing capital never exceeded 11.5%; it fell to 4.2% in the Tractel year and has run at 7.7–8.1% since. Operating income per share compounded 6.9% a year and free cash flow per share 6.8%, both above the 3% floor, helped by two rights issues priced into the share count. Tangible book turned negative in 2022 and stays negative (−SEK 697m at FY2025). Liabilities-to-equity is 87%. **WEAK** — every return reading sits below the 10% floor.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth passes: the annual report states no customer exceeds 10% of revenue, and the group avoids single-supplier dependence for its components. Customer power is WEAK — fragmented buyers, specified installations, service tied to the installed base. Supplier power is WEAK. Substitutes are WEAK — mast-climbing hoists, building-maintenance units and fall protection are often required by building codes or safety regulation. New entrants are STRONG in construction equipment and new-build facade projects, where projects are tendered against regional and local rivals; Construction's margin halved to 8.3% in Q2 2026. One strong force, contained by the service base. The moat is switching costs on installed equipment that needs certified parts and inspection; it is stable. Markets are MIXED. **ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

CEO pay of SEK 12.1m sits far below both P17 lines (SEK 30.2m fiscal-year, SEK 29.0m three-year). The chair's fee is SEK 1.0m. No related-party transactions are disclosed. The CEO holds 119,000 shares and 395,750 purchased call options. Capital allocation is **SOUND**: the dividend is paid from free cash flow, buybacks are small incentive hedges, and Pro-Bel (July 2026) cost about 8.3× trailing adjusted EBITA. The logged judgment line is Tractel: at least 13.5× its EBITA, funded by a SEK 2.5bn rights issue in 2023, and since then group ROCE has stayed below the pre-deal level. **PASS.**
