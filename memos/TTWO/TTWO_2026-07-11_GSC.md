# TTWO — Take-Two Interactive Software: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | WEAK | WEAK | FAIL | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** |

A rare all-gates fail. The ten-year record aggregates to a **negative** operating result (Σ OI FY2017–26 = −$7.29B, sourced) after the Zynga acquisition's amortisation and ~$5.7B of goodwill impairments; the paying customer base is four platform gatekeepers (Apple, Sony, Google, Microsoft, each >10% of revenue — sourced, 10-K); and management is compensated through a related-party management agreement whose FY2026 cost ($79.5M, sourced) is more than triple the P17 hard-strike ceiling. The stock trades at ~98× trailing FCF on GTA VI anticipation (release 19 Nov 2026, sourced). Nothing here is one bad year: the concentration is structural, the ZMC agreement runs to 2029, and the buyback record shows repurchases executed at 34–75× EV/OI. No plausible re-rating of the gates is visible; the verdict would need the Future and SF findings to change, not just GTA VI to ship.

## 2. Understanding

Take-Two is a developer and publisher of differentiated interactive entertainment — premium console/PC franchises (Grand Theft Auto, NBA 2K, Red Dead Redemption, Borderlands, via the Rockstar Games and 2K labels) and free-to-play mobile games (Zynga: Toon Blast, Match Factory!, Color Block Jam) — whose paying customers are chiefly four digital platform gatekeepers (Apple, Sony, Google, Microsoft) remitting consumer spending net of platform fees; a Delaware corporation operating as a multi-label IP owner with internal global studios, HQ New York; a prominent ~30-year-old top-three Western publisher, ~78% of revenue recurrent consumer spending, currently mid-way through the GTA VI release cycle. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

All four normalized-average ROCE variants are negative — worst-of-four **−23.1%** (beginning-CE, ex-cash); Σ OI FY2017–26 is −$7.29B as reported (Zynga amortisation and FY24/FY25 goodwill impairments of ~$5.7B; no add-backs per doctrine — the impairments are real capital destroyed). Worst-of-four FCFROCE is 5.7%, below even the STRONG floor, and FCF was negative FY2023–25. ΔFCF/share −5.3%/yr geometric; ΔOI/share n.m. (negative terminal); ΔTBV/share −2.7%/yr with TBV negative FY2023–25. L/E 167% at FY26 (within P5) but 330% at FY25, repaired by a $1.25B equity issuance. **WEAK** — the record fails P3 at every point, with sustained deterioration.

## 4. Quality — future

Customer breadth **FAIL**: five largest customers = 80.6% of net revenue; Apple, Sony, Google, and Microsoft each >10% (sourced, FY2026 10-K). The captive-customer waiver is unavailable — Sony and Microsoft demonstrably backward-integrate (large first-party studios). Forces: customer power STRONG (gatekeepers set take-rates unilaterally); supplier power WEAK (mostly internal development; the NBA licence is the named exception, <10% of the cost base); substitutes WEAK for the console franchises, STRONG in Zynga's casual-mobile segment; new entrants WEAK for AAA, STRONG in mobile. Moat: genuine Rockstar/NBA 2K brand, stable — but a moat cannot cure a breadth FAIL. Market growth YES. **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

**FAIL — P17 hard strike, multiply corroborated.** CEO/Chairman Zelnick is paid via a related-party management agreement with ZMC Advisors (to March 2029): FY2026 cost $16.6M fee + $62.9M RSU expense = **$79.5M** (sourced) vs a P17 ceiling of $23.1M (5% of FY26 FCF $461.5M; the NI leg is negative) — also above the ~$40M indexed scrutiny line, and itself an RPT. P19: FY18/FY19/FY22 buybacks executed at implied **74.9× / 46.7× / 34.3× EV/OI** (hard strikes >20×), the FY22 tranche at 186× MCap/FCF, months before a $12.7B all-time-high acquisition of GAAP-unprofitable Zynga — of which ~$5.7B of goodwill has since been impaired (acquisition audit adverse). Sold $1.25B of equity at lows-adjacent FY26. No dividend.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- The verdict does not depend on GTA VI failing — a record-breaking launch is consistent with AVOID because concentration, comp, and allocation are unchanged by a hit.
- Platform take-rate compression (regulatory pressure on Apple/Google) would soften customer power at the margin but not cure four >10% customers.
- FY2027 will optically transform OI and FCF; anchoring the gates on a single GTA VI year would violate the ten-year normalisation.
- ZMC agreement expiry (March 2029) is the earliest structural SF re-test.
- Bias check: hotness and availability (GTA VI glow) named and handled — the verdict rests on the record, not the release calendar.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

AVOID flips only if all of: a renegotiated (or internalised) executive comp structure within P17; a post-GTA-VI decade whose normalized ROCE clears 10% on the worst variant; and evidence of allocation discipline. Customer concentration is structural to the industry and unlikely ever to pass breadth without a waiver the facts don't support. **Next review: FY2027 10-K (~May 2027)** — first GTA VI fiscal year restores a computable band and resets the FCF leg; earlier only on a change-of-control or comp-structure event.
