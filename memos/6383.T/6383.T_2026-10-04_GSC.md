Daifuku is a leading global material-handling integrator, and its record is good: cash-excluded returns above 20%, per-share operating income compounding at 17% a year, net cash and almost no goodwill. What blocks it is the electronics book. Cleanroom systems for chip fabs are now 38% of sales, and one chipmaker bought 13.8% of everything Daifuku sold in 2025. The FY2024 filing did not name it, so this is a fab build-out cycle, not a permanent feature. The verdict re-tests to WATCH if a future securities report drops TSMC below the 10% line. Price is a separate wall: 41.8× FCF, 5.2× the strict band.

## 2. Understanding
Daifuku designs, manufactures and installs automated material-handling systems for distribution centres, retailers and factories (storage, sortation, conveying), overhead transport for semiconductor and flat-panel fabs, automotive assembly conveyance, and airport baggage handling. Its customers are businesses and airport operators, with electronics the largest industry at 38% of FY2025 sales. It is a vertically integrated engineer-manufacturer-integrator that also earns lifecycle service revenue, headquartered in Osaka with large US, Korean and Chinese subsidiaries. Founded in 1937, it is now a global market leader. **PASS.**
