## 2. Understanding

Daifuku, founded in 1937 and headquartered in Osaka, designs, makes, installs and services automated material-handling systems. Its products are storage, sortation and conveying systems for distribution centres and factories, overhead transport for semiconductor and flat-panel fabs, automotive assembly-line systems and airport baggage handling. Companies and airport operators buy them as capital projects. Electronics makers are the largest buying industry, 38% of FY12/25 sales, and TSMC alone bought 13.8%. Daifuku integrates engineering, manufacturing and installation, and sells worldwide through Japanese, US, Korean, Chinese and other subsidiaries. It bought Eisenmann (German painting and conveying systems) on 1 July 2026. **PASS.**
