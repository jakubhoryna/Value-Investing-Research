# TEAM — Atlassian Corporation: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | SF | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | WEAK | ADEQUATE | FAIL (P17 + P19 hard strikes) | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** |

Atlassian is a genuinely sticky, capital-light collaboration-software franchise whose GAAP economics have never worked: ten years produced a cumulative **operating loss of −$578m** (sourced, IFRS FY16–21 / US GAAP FY22–25) against $6.75bn of derived FCF — a gap that is almost entirely stock-based compensation ($1.36bn in FY2025 alone, ≈96% of that year's FCF). The cash machine is real but shareholders fund it through dilution; buybacks then sterilise that dilution at 36× implied MCap/FCF. Past WEAK and SF FAIL each independently force AVOID. A flip needs sustained positive GAAP operating income (worst-of-four ROCE ≥10%) plus a reformed capital-return record — FY2028 at the earliest even on an optimistic OI trajectory.

## 2. Understanding

Atlassian sells subscription work-management and developer-collaboration software (Jira, Confluence, Loom, Bitbucket, Trello, Rovo) — differentiated products bought mostly online at low price points, increasingly via solution partners, by organizations from dev teams to large enterprises worldwide; a Delaware-domiciled (since 2022, ex-UK plc), founder-controlled dual-class company, HQ Sydney/San Francisco, ~$5.2bn revenue, ~300k+ paying customers, transformed FY21–24 from perpetual server licences to cloud subscriptions. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized ROCE is **−17.4%** (Σ OI −$578m ÷ Σ CE_xc end-of-period; all four variants negative, best −2.8%) — six of ten years show operating losses, including the three most recent. FCFROCE is superb on the same denominators (worst 33.3%), but P2/P3 require both, and the FCF-vs-OI wedge is SBC, not accounting noise. FCF/sh compounded 30.5% geometric; ΔOI/sh n.m. (sign-crossing). L/E 349% breaches P5 without the P6 conditions (liabilities are mostly deferred revenue, noted, not excusing). **WEAK** — P4 on level, not trend.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASS: no customer >10% of revenue (10-K disclosure sentence present, FY23–25); supplier side concentrated in AWS hosting but several hyperscalers stand ready (ready-alternatives exception, logged). Forces: customer power WEAK; supplier power WEAK; **substitutes STRONG** (AI-native dev tooling and seat compression; GitHub/GitLab bundling); new entrants WEAK (switching costs + marketplace ecosystem are barriers to success). Tally 1 strong, contained. Moat: switching costs/ingrainedness — Jira/Confluence deeply embedded — stable, with an explicit AI-erosion watch. Market growth YES. **ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

**P17 hard strike:** highest-paid employee (CRO Brian Duffy, $23.6m FY2025, sourced from DEF 14A) against 5% of lower(FCF $1,415m, NI −$257m) — the NI leg is negative, so no comp level passes; founders' own comp is nominal ($54k), which does not cure the test. **P19 hard strike, twice:** FY2025 repurchases (4.0m sh @ $196.02 avg, $780.7m) imply MCap/FCF **36.3×** (>20×) and EV/OI n.m. (OI<0 — unpassable at any price); and diluted shares still *rose* 256.3m→261.8m across $1.32bn of FY23–25 buybacks — textbook dilution-sterilisation, a strike at any price. Dual-class (85.2% founder voting power) logged as caveat. **FAIL.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- SBC (~26% of revenue) is the business model's real cost; any Past flip on GAAP OI must survive it, not add it back.
- AI-native development tools compress seat counts — the single strong force and the moat's named erosion vector.
- Founder control (85% votes) forecloses external governance pressure on comp or buyback discipline.
- Cloud-migration tailwind (server EOL) is now lapped; growth deceleration would expose the cost base.
- Solution-partner channel concentration (>10% of AR at times) is a receivables, not a revenue, risk — watch it.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

To WATCH: sustained positive GAAP OI lifting worst-of-four ROCE ≥10% (FY2028 earliest), **and** SF cure — comp passing a positive-NI test plus a full program year of buybacks below the P19 flag lines (or abstention). Price alone can never flip it while OI<0. Next review: FY2026 10-K (~Aug 2026) — OI inflection check and P19 re-test of the $1.5bn September-2024 program; otherwise structural, low priority.
