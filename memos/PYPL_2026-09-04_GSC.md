# PYPL — PayPal Holdings, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS, Past ADEQUATE, Future WEAK, shareholder-friendliness PASS on one flag, price FAIR — **AVOID**, unchanged and on the same single gate.

The takeover is over. Stripe and Advent walked on 27 August after the board rejected $60.50 as inadequate; the shares fell 12.7% and now sit at $56.82, still 21% above the top of a band that has not moved a cent. The lapse removes a price distortion, not a business problem, and the business is where this name fails. Two primary items are new. A transformation programme v3 failed to carry targets at least $1.5bn of gross run-rate savings over two to three years, ~$400m by year-end, against a $120–140m charge in the second half — a cost answer to a revenue problem, reaching none of the three strong forces. And Lores's disclosed inducement package puts his first reported fiscal-year total on course to clear the absolute compensation scrutiny line, which would pair with the standing capital-allocation caution and fail shareholder-friendliness at the April 2027 proxy. Nothing moves a gate today.

## 2. Understanding

Unchanged and still **PASS**: PayPal Holdings is a US-listed Delaware financial-technology company earning volume-based transaction fees from hundreds of millions of consumer accounts and tens of millions of merchants for digital payment processing — branded checkout (PayPal, Venmo), unbranded processing (Braintree), P2P, BNPL, credit and value-added services — across roughly 200 markets with the US its largest revenue geography, as the incumbent leader in online branded checkout, growing revenue mid-single-digits while losing checkout share to platform wallets.

## 3. Quality — past record

**ADEQUATE, unchanged.** No new fiscal year and no new interim statements since v3: the four normalized ROCE reads stand at 14.2 / 13.2 / 22.2 / 20.6%, worst 13.2% against the 15% line, with worst-of-four FCFROCE 16.0%. Per-share growth is still the strong leg — OI/sh compounding 19.1%, FCF/sh 12.2%, flattered by a share count down a fifth since 2021. H1'26 operating income sits 3.9% below H1'25, and the newly surfaced $120–140m transformation charge falls in the second half, so reported FY2026 tracks down on both counts. Leverage 317% headline, roughly 100% excluding custodial funds.

## 4. Quality — future

**WEAK, unchanged, and untouched by anything in this delta.** Supplier breadth still fails: transaction expense of $8,550m in H1'26 is 50.2% of revenue and routes to the card duopoly plus two concentrated processors. Customer breadth passes. Force calls stand at customer STRONG, supplier STRONG, substitutes STRONG, new entrants WEAK — three against a stop signal of two. The moat, two-sided network plus brand plus vaulted credentials, remains **eroding**. The savings programme addresses organisational layers, marketing efficiency and automation; it widens no acceptance economics, slows no wallet substitution, restores no checkout share. Market growth YES, and that remains the problem.
