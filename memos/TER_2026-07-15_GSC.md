## 1. Verdict

Teradyne is a genuinely good business — the #1/#2 name in a semiconductor automated-test-equipment (ATE) duopoly with Advantest, now riding an AI-test demand wave — and its ten-year record clears the STRONG bar (worst-of-four ROCE 21.5%, FCFROCE 17.7%, ~50% leverage). It is not ownable here, for two independent reasons. First, shareholder-friendliness FAILs on a P19 hard strike: in 2025 the company spent $702.1m repurchasing 6.3m shares at an average $112.21 — a price implying ~40× MCap/FCF and ~27× EV/OI, i.e. buying its own stock far above any conservative worth. Second, price makes it academic: at $353 the shares trade at ~87× EV/OI and ~125× MCap/FCF, roughly 15× the trigger band. The rally (~$74 in Apr-2025 → ~$484 in Jun-2026 → $353 now) has turned a good cyclical into an extreme valuation. The verdict flips only if the company stops repurchasing above worth **and** price collapses ~94% to the band — not a realistic near-term path.

## 2. Understanding

Teradyne designs and sells automated test equipment — chiefly semiconductor SoC and memory testers (Semiconductor Test, the dominant unit), plus Product/System Test, Wireless Test (LitePoint) and Robotics (Universal Robots cobots, MiR AMRs) — to semiconductor makers, foundries and OSATs (and, for robotics, industrial manufacturers); a ~65-year-old Massachusetts-HQ'd global leader in an Asia-weighted, deeply cyclical capital-equipment industry. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized averages (FY2016–25) are **21.5% ROCE** and **17.7% FCFROCE** (both the end-of-period, cash-inclusive cut), comfortably above the 15%/8% STRONG floors; every one of the four cuts clears. Per-share compounding is strong — OI/sh +13.6% 10y CAGR, FCF/sh +6.1%, TBV/sh +9.1% — all above the 3% floor. Returns are cyclical, not declining: ROCE peaked 37.6% (2021), troughed 17.0% (2023), and has recovered 18.5%→18.9%. FY2016's GAAP operating loss is a ~$338m Wireless-Test goodwill+intangible impairment, diluted by averaging. L/E ~50% — no leverage flag. **STRONG.**

## 4. Quality — future

Customer breadth **FAILs**: FY2025 had one direct customer at **19%** of revenue, two >10% specifiers (12%, 10%), five-largest 44% — and rising from ~10% in 2023. The captive-customer waiver is applied (ASML/TSMC/QCOM precedent: foundry/OSAT buyers cannot realistically backward-integrate into ATE, switching costs are high, the buyer base is structurally concentrated), which caps Future at ADEQUATE. Forces: customer bargaining power **STRONG** (concentration, though Advantest is the alternative and switching costs bind), the other three WEAK (duopoly barriers, test is essential, thin substitutes) — 1 strong, contained. Moat: switching costs + scale + duopoly ingrainedness, stable-to-strengthening on AI test. Market growth YES. **ADEQUATE** (waiver-capped; concentration is a live watch item).

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

**Buybacks (P19) — HARD STRIKE:** 2025's $702.1m / 6.3m shares at avg **$112.21** implies ~40× MCap/FCF and ~27× EV/OI (lower-EV variant), far above the 20× hard-strike line — overpriced repurchases of own stock, not below-worth. **Comp (P17) PASSES:** CEO total $14.1m is 3.1% of the lower of FCF/NI (FCF $450m) — under the 5% strike and well under the ~$40m absolute trigger; 120:1 pay ratio. **RPT clean** (none disclosed since 1 Jan 2025). Dividend modest ($76m). One hard strike ⇒ **FAIL.**
