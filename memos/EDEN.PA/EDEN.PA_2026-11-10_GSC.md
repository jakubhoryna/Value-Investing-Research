## 2. Understanding

Edenred SE — demerged from Accor in 2010, Paris-listed and headquartered, €3.0bn 2025 total revenue — runs prepaid payment networks across 44 countries, led by Brazil and Italy in which employers and fleet operators pay issuance and service fees, affiliated merchants pay redemption commissions, and the float held between loading and redemption earns interest. **PASS.**

- **Benefits & Engagement** (69% of FY2025 total revenue): meal and food vouchers and cards, other employee-benefit and engagement programmes (Reward Gateway), paid for by over a million employer clients and the merchants that accept them; Brazil, Italy and France lead.
- **Mobility** (23%): fuel, toll, maintenance and EV-charging cards and fleet-expense software sold to fleet operators and freight carriers, with Brazil and Europe leading.
- Payment Solutions & New Markets (7.6% of revenue, 3.7% of EBIT): immaterial; Edenred Pay North America moved to the equity method from 1 August 2026.

FY2015–FY2016 statements print EBIT above net financial expense with associates and other income and expenses below it: OI = profit before tax + net financial expense (FY2016 294 + 58 = 352; FY2015 280 + 47 = 327). **Financial-income removals (J3).** Other revenue is "the interest generated by investing cash" between issuance and reimbursement (FS25 note 4.2) on funds that "remain Edenred's property" (note 4.7) — interest on the company's own cash: out of OI and, as `derived`, out of CFO (the CFS starts from net profit). FY2016 statements split it: €58m unrestricted float, €8m restricted cash. Interest income on cash and marketable securities in net financial expense (FY2025 36) is removed from CFO. Dividends from equity-accounted companies (CFO line) and the equity-accounted share (OI) are removed while positive; the H1-2026 share (−1) is a loss and stays in TTM OI. "Other financial income and expenses" (bank fees, miscellaneous interest; net negative every year) is ambiguous and negative → stays. Hedging instruments adjust the cost of debt and stay.

**Segregated customer funds — nothing netted (J13).** Restricted cash (€1,661m FY2025; France 755, Belgium 332, UK 195, US 109 …) "must be clearly segregated from the Group's other cash … the funds remain Edenred's property and are invested in locally regulated interest-bearing financial instruments" (FS25 note 4.7); €179m is measured at fair value through OCI on Level 2 inputs and the maturities are not printed.
