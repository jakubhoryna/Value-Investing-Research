# 6301.T — Komatsu Ltd.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS · Past **WEAK** (worst-of-four ROCE 9.8% — a knife-edge miss of the 10% floor that the missing early years would widen; FCFROCE 3.5% — unambiguous) · Future ADEQUATE · SF PASS (1 flag — capital-allocation CAUTION) · Cheapness EXPENSIVE → **AVOID (Past gate)**.

Komatsu is the world's #2 construction- and mining-equipment franchise at a cyclical earnings peak: FY24–25 printed record OI on mining demand and a weak yen, and the stock has re-rated to 1.9× book. The framework's complaint is structural, not cyclical: across eight years the business converted just 3.5% of its capital into free cash — retail-finance receivables, dealer inventory and working capital absorb the P&L — and even peak-era averages leave with-cash ROCE below the floor. The verdict flips only if a full window holds ROCE above 10% with cash conversion transformed; the FY27 guide (OI −10%) points the other way first.

## 2. Understanding
Komatsu manufactures and sells construction and mining equipment — excavators, dump trucks, dozers plus parts, service and the Komtrax/autonomous-haulage ecosystem, with a captive retail-finance arm — through exclusive dealers to contractors and miners worldwide (~85% of sales overseas; #2 globally behind Caterpillar), plus a small industrial-machinery unit; Tokyo-HQ'd, 105 years old, US-GAAP filer. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **9.81%** (end-of-period, with-cash; best variant 11.2%) — under the 10% floor by a knife-edge, and one-directionally: the unretrieved FY17–18 years (OI ¥174–272bn on a ¥3tn+ base) would pull every average lower. Worst FCFROCE **3.50%** — the number that decides; CFO is chronically consumed by finance receivables and inventory (FY19 FCF ¥10.5bn, FY23 ¥22.9bn in *good* years). Path (end-wc): 12.0 → 7.4 → 4.8 → 8.0 → 11.0 → 11.6 → 12.3 → 9.4%. Growth fine (ΔOI/sh +5.8%, ΔEPS +6.2%, ΔBV/sh +10.5% geo). L/E 77.3%; debt ¥1.34tn against the finance book — captive-consistent treatment (App C). **WEAK.**

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth PASS both sides (no ≥10% customer; diversified supply). Forces: customer power WEAK (fragmented contractors; even majors need the dealer-service network) · supplier power WEAK · substitutes WEAK (no substitute for iron; electrification is within-category transition) · **new entrants STRONG** — Chinese OEMs (SANY, XCMG, LiuGong) are clearing the barriers in emerging markets and mining value tiers. Tally **1 strong, contained** (parts/service annuity and AHS lock-in hold the profitable core). Moat: **Ingrainedness** — the exclusive dealer/service network and Komtrax installed base that make switching a fleet costly; stable. Market growth MIXED-to-YES (mining capex secular, construction cyclical). **ADEQUATE.**
