# WMT — Walmart Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS, Past ADEQUATE, Future STRONG, shareholder-friendliness **FAIL** on a §2·1C·3 capital-allocation verdict of **ADVERSE**, cheapness EXPENSIVE — final verdict **AVOID (SF gate)**, unchanged from v1.

The business remains worth owning for the reason v1 gave: the deepest cost moat in physical retail, breadth on both sides, and a profitable e-commerce and advertising arm bending the margin curve upward. What stands between here and a BUY is not price arithmetic but the allocation record. MODEL v4.0.0 abolished the mechanical P19 strike that carried v1's FAIL and replaced it with a judgment; re-read under that judgment, the record still fails — and it fails on the forward evidence, not on history. ## 2. Understanding

Walmart sells groceries (~60% of US sales) and general merchandise at everyday low prices to hundreds of millions of mass-market households — plus advertising and marketplace services to sellers — through ~10,600 stores and clubs and a top-two US e-commerce operation, as a vertically integrated Bentonville-HQ'd retailer earning ~82% of revenue domestically. PASS.

Restated, not re-derived — FY2026 is still the latest completed fiscal year. Worst-of-four normalized 10y averages (FY2017–26): ROCE 15.0% (end_wc), FCFROCE 10.1% (end_wc). ### E4. The control-structure ruling — MC.PA governs, not KNEBV, and the caveat is held UNPAIRED

§2·1C·1: "Dual-class/control structures: documented caveat, a strike only when paired with value-destructive allocation."

- **Walmart has no dual-class structure, no double-voting rights, no loyalty shares and no voting cap.** One class of common stock; the Walton family's ≈44–46% of votes equals its ≈44–46% of capital. Under §2·1C·1's own words, significant outright holdings by insiders are a **positive**.
- **KNEBV does not govern.** KNEBV v2's second flag was an *entrenchment structure* — a dual-class arrangement conferring votes disproportionate to capital — paired with a CAUTION-grade acquisition finding. Walmart has no such structure.
