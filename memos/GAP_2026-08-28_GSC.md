# GAP — The Gap, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past **WEAK**; Future **WEAK**; shareholder-friendliness PASS (capital allocation CAUTION, one flag unpaired); cheapness FAIR. **AVOID on two gates**, unchanged.

The Q2 print looks transformational and is not. Reported operating income of **$676m against $292m** is a 132% increase until the **$417m one-time credit to cost of goods** for recovered IEEPA tariffs comes out; underlying, it is **≈$259m, down 11%**, on sales down 2% and comps down 1%. Adjusted gross margin rose 20bp. None of that moves a gate: worst-of-four ROCE is still 8.6% against a 10% floor, operating income per share is still *falling* 2.4% a year, and apparel remains the most substitutable corner of retail. What the quarter changes is the arithmetic — on the 1 August balance sheet the band re-anchors to **$5.76–12.62**, and the shares sit **+65%** above it, the closest to CHEAP in this batch and still beside the point.

## 2. Understanding

Unchanged. A specialty-apparel company designing and marketing casual clothing, accessories and personal-care goods under four brands — Old Navy (>50% of sales, value), Gap, Banana Republic, Athleta — sold to individual retail consumers through ~3,570 company-operated and franchised stores plus e-commerce, sourcing finished product from ~200 independent third-party factories in ~30 countries, San Francisco-headquartered and concentrated in North America. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Restated, not re-derived — fiscal 2026 does not close until ~January 2027, so no year can be appended. Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **8.6%** (range 8.6–11.5%) against the 10% floor; worst-of-four FCFROCE **7.2%**. Operating income per share fell from $3.69 (FY2015) to $2.90 (FY2025), a geometric **−2.4%/yr**; FCF/share flat at +0.2%. The FY2016–18 returns that look high are a pre-ASC 842 artefact — capitalising leases cuts them to the low teens. On the 1 August sheet liabilities of $8,905m against equity of $3,951m give **225%**, of which $4,227m is lease debt; ex-leases the company is net-cash. **WEAK** on sub-10% returns and shrinking per-share operating income.

## 4. Quality — future

Restated, and the quarter corroborates it. Breadth passes both legs: millions of diffuse consumers; ~200 vendors, the two largest at 9% and 8% of purchases. Customer power WEAK, supplier power WEAK, **substitutes STRONG** — Shein, Temu, Amazon, Zara, Target, off-price and resale all compete for the same wallet — and **new entrants STRONG**, barriers being low and repeatedly crossed at scale. Two strong forces, no durable moat. The Q2 brand split is the thesis in miniature: Gap +10% and Banana Republic +3% against Old Navy **−4%** and Athleta **−12%** — the half carrying the volume is losing ground, the recovering half is the smaller half. **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

CEO Dickson's FY2025 total of **$17.19m** is 42% of the 5% line on the lower of net income and FCF. **v1's caveat on the chair's $526k is withdrawn** — the chair stop calibrates to ≈$1.02m and the top seat pays $366k against $672k. No breach. RPT clean. Capital allocation **CAUTION**: 413m diluted shares retired to 362m at single-digit-to-low-teens multiples, but fiscal 2026 spent **$601m in 26 weeks against $261m of free cash flow** — 3.9× all of FY2025, at ~$23.48 against a band topping at $12.62, with no published criterion. One flag, unpaired. **PASS.**

- Tariffs cut both ways: a $417m credit flattered Q2, ~$512m of refunds are still landing, and the rate assumption changed twice in one quarter.
- Upside risk: at +65% price sits closer to the band than anything else here, so a de-rating plus durable ROCE recovery would force a re-read — but quality, not price, binds.
