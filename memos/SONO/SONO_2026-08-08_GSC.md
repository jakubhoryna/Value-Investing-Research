# SONO — Sonos, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past **WEAK** (worst-of-four ROCE −1.64%; reported OI fell four consecutive years, 155 → −50); Future **WEAK** (breadth FAIL both sides — Best Buy 15% of revenue, Inventec builds "a majority" of product — plus a 2-strong tally); SF **FAIL** (P17 hard strike: Conrad $8.28m vs $5.41m/$4.88m FCF-leg ceilings, both reads — pay set against a three-loss-year record); EXPENSIVE (TTM EV/OI 28.9×/24.9× after a +42% recovery rally) — **AVOID (4 gates)**.

A genuine product franchise (multi-room audio ecosystem, ~16m households) that self-immolated with the 2024 app rewrite, lost its CEO to it, and burned $679m of buyback cash across the decline — and whose recovery (TTM OI +$66m, gross margin 46.5%) the market has already re-rated to 25–29× operating income. The ecosystem lock-in is real; what the record shows is that it protects revenue, not operating income, against ecosystem giants whose speakers are features of larger platforms. The FY2026 10-K restores a computable band; nothing before the 2027 proxy moves the SF gate.

## 2. Understanding

Sonos designs wireless multi-room speakers, soundbars and components (hardware ~all of revenue, tied to a proprietary app/software platform), manufactured by contract partners (Inventec majority; China/Malaysia/Vietnam), sold through retailers — Best Buy 15% of revenue — plus direct e-commerce, predominantly US/EMEA; a Santa Barbara-based, 2018-IPO company that replaced its CEO in 2025 after a failed app relaunch. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **−1.64%** (beg-xc; best variant 5.80%) — negative on both beginning-basis variants; worst-of-four FCFROCE 6.93% also misses its 8% floor. Reported OI: 155.0 → 89.5 → −20.6 → −48.1 → −50.5 — four consecutive declines (P4 redundant on a sub-floor level, noted). FCF held positive (working-capital harvest and capex cuts, not earnings) — the OI/FCF wedge is inventory drawdown plus $82m/yr SBC added back. Per-share: OI/sh 1.10 → −0.42; BV/sh 4.05 → 2.96 despite $679m of buybacks. L/E 93–132%, no debt beyond leases. **WEAK.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: customers — **FAIL**: "Best Buy... accounted for 15% of our revenue in fiscal 2022" (10-K, quoted; the concentration is structural to US specialty retail); waiver denied — retailers reallocate shelf freely (WHR/retail precedent). Suppliers — **FAIL-leaning**: Inventec manufactures "a majority of our products"; the ready-alternatives exception (Foxconn-class CMs stand ready) is arguable but transition lead times are real — logged as a second breadth negative rather than a clean fail. Forces: customer power **STRONG** (retail concentration + the platform giants control discovery); supplier power WEAK-to-moderate (CM alternatives exist); substitutes WEAK-to-moderate (TV speakers/phone audio = doing-without at the low end); new entrants **STRONG** — Apple, Amazon, Google and Bose demonstrably enter and price speakers as ecosystem features. Tally **2 strong → WEAK**. Moat: switching costs (installed multi-room ecosystem — the household's speakers all speak Sonos) — real, **stable but demonstrated fragile** by the app crisis. Market growth MIXED (premium audio flat; home-theatre attach growing). **WEAK.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Ecosystem giants price speakers at hardware cost as platform features — the structural ceiling on Sonos margins.
- Brand/trust damage from the 2024 app fiasco is repaired in sentiment but the demonstrated single-point-of-failure (software) remains.
- Tariffs on China/Malaysia/Vietnam manufacturing hit COGS directly; Inventec concentration compounds it.
- Best Buy shelf-space decisions swing double-digit revenue shares.
- Bull case: TTM inflection is real — OI +$66m, GM 46.5%, net cash $204m; two clean years would rebuild the record the window lacks.
