# RBREW.CO — Royal Unibrew A/S: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS, Past ADEQUATE, Future ADEQUATE, shareholder-friendliness PASS (one capital-allocation CAUTION flag, unpaired), cheapness FAIR — **WATCH**.

Royal Unibrew is a local-scale brewer and soft-drink bottler that earned 20%-plus on capital through 2021 and then bought its way into Norway and the Netherlands, stepping returns down to the mid-teens. The record still clears the quality bar on every construction, and cash conversion is steady. What stands between here and a BUY is price: the stock trades at 12.6× derived free cash flow and 11.9× operating income, against a band of ≈ DKK 182–183 — more than halfway down from DKK 405. The PepsiCo licence loss (≈13% of revenue from end-2028) and the 2027 hedge roll-off are the known holes. A clean recovery of returns toward 18–20% would not move the band much; only price does. Evidence that the PepsiCo exit cuts operating income faster than management claims, or a return to buybacks at ≈25×, would turn the verdict toward AVOID.

## 2. Understanding
Royal Unibrew brews, bottles and distributes its own beer, soft-drink, malt, cider and ready-to-drink brands (Faxe, Albani, Lapin Kulta, Hartwall, Crodo, Lorina) plus licensed partner brands (chiefly PepsiCo), selling mainly to grocery retailers and the on-trade in Denmark, Finland, the Baltics and Norway (Northern Europe, two-thirds of revenue), Italy, France and the Netherlands (Western Europe) and export markets (International). It is a Faxe-headquartered, widely held, mid-cap acquirer that is #1 or #2 locally rather than a global brand owner. **PASS.**

But returns stepped down from 21–25% (FY2018–21) to 13.7–15.4% (FY2023–25) as the Hansa Borg, Vrumona and other deals added capital faster than profit.
