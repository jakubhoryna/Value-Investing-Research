# SHOP — Shopify Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | SF | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | WEAK | STRONG | FAIL | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** (Past WEAK + SF FAIL) |

Shopify is a genuinely strong franchise on the future-looking gate — a switching-cost moat that is strengthening, broad customer and supplier bases, a growing market — attached to a ten-year financial record the model cannot excuse and a compensation regime that fails shareholder-friendliness outright. Six of the ten fiscal years 2016–2025 produced operating losses; the worst-of-four normalized 10-year ROCE is **0.5%** against the 10% ADEQUATE floor. The CEO's FY2024 pay of **$150.0m** was 1.9× the P17 hard-strike cap, and FY2023's top pay ($76.0m to Nejatian) breached its cap 11.5×. Neither gate is price-fixable: a flip requires roughly two more years of FY2024–25-grade returns rolling into the window (Past re-test plausibly ~FY2028 at the earliest) *and* sustained comp discipline. Price is academic at 81× FCF and ~107× EV/OI, but the report is complete per the framework.

## 2. Understanding

Shopify sells subscription commerce software plus attached merchant services — payments processing above all, plus capital, shipping, and point-of-sale — to millions of mostly small and mid-sized merchant businesses (the merchants pay; their shoppers do not) in the e-commerce platform industry; a vertically-integrated software/fintech operator (logistics ambitions divested 2023) headquartered in Ottawa with customers concentrated in North America and operations global; a prominent, 20-year-old, large-cap ($163B) company that has transformed from pure SaaS into a payments-led platform (~75% of revenue is now merchant solutions). **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized 10y ROCE **0.47%** (end-of-period, with-cash); best variant 1.45%. Worst-of-four FCFROCE **7.11%**. Operating losses in 6 of 10 years, including −$1,418m in FY2023 (as reported, embedding the logistics-divestiture impairment — no add-backs). Returns only crossed the P3 line in FY2024–25 (ROCE_wc 10.5%/11.6%) — two years cannot carry a ten-year test. ΔOI/sh and ΔFCF/sh geo-means n.m. (sign change); ΔBV/sh +40%/yr is capital raised, not earned. Leverage trivial (L/E 12.7%). **WEAK** — the record fails P3 outright; the recent inflection is real but young.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth: customers PASS (millions of merchants, none near 10%); suppliers PASS via the ready-alternatives exception — cloud hosting and payments processing are effectively concentrated (Google Cloud; Stripe behind Shopify Payments) but multiple at-scale vendors stand ready (AWS/Azure; Adyen/Braintree). Forces: customer power WEAK (fragmented SMBs, high switching costs); supplier power WEAK (contained by alternatives); substitutes WEAK (WooCommerce/Wix offer lower value, replatforming costs deter; marketplaces are a channel Shopify integrates); new entrants WEAK (scale + app ecosystem + switching costs). Tally 0 strong. Moat: **switching costs**, strengthening — ecosystem depth and payments attach deepen lock-in. Market growth YES. **STRONG.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

**FAIL — P17 hard strike.** FY2024 top pay (Lütke) **$150.0m** vs cap 5% × lower(FCF $1,597m, NI $2,019m) = **$79.9m** — 1.9×. FY2023 top pay (Nejatian) $76.0m vs $6.6m cap — 11.5×. FY2025 ($35.0m vs $61.6m cap) passes, but the gate is defined by the worst, never averaged. Corroborating flag pair: founder-share control (~40% voting power, approved with only 54% in 2022) paired with value-destructive allocation — Deliverr bought for $2.1B (Jul 2022, largest-ever deal), divested to Flexport within ~12 months for a non-cash 13% stake, impairment through FY2023 OI. Outside-director top total $767k > $500k stop (one-time new-hire grant; run-rate $344–388k under). No buybacks ever — P19 moot; SBC dilution (~$449m FY2025) runs unsterilized. RPT clean. No dividend.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Agentic/AI commerce could reroute discovery and checkout away from merchant storefronts; Shopify is currently positioning as a beneficiary, but the channel shift is unproven.
- Effective supplier concentration (Stripe, Google Cloud) is a margin lever outside Shopify's control if alternatives prove costlier to activate than disclosed.
- ~75% of revenue is payments-led merchant solutions — interchange/processing economics and GMV cyclicality now dominate the model.
- Founder control (~40% votes) means governance reform arrives only if Lütke chooses it.
- FY2024–25 profitability is two years old; a spending re-acceleration (the 2021–22 pattern) would reset the Past clock.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

To WATCH: (a) Past reaches ADEQUATE — needs FY2024–25-grade returns sustained so the 10y worst-of-four clears 10%, plausibly ~FY2028 at the earliest, and (b) SF PASS — top-employee comp inside the P17 line across the disclosed proxy window with no new strikes. Price alone can never flip it. To BUY additionally requires both primaries ≤ threshold (≈ $7.7 today's basis). **Next review:** 2027 proxy (~Apr 2027) P17 re-test; FY2026 10-K (~Feb 2027) re-anchors the band; no price alert — the gates, not the price, are the constraint.
