## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past WEAK (ROCE decline, with liabilities at 200.9% of equity and FCF growth unreadable); Future ADEQUATE; Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (flag pair); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID** (Past + SF).

Core & Main is one of two national distributors of underground water, sewer, storm-drain and fire-protection products in the United States, with about 17% of a fragmented market, no customer above 1% of sales and no supplier above 7% of purchases. What stands in the way: returns on capital have fallen three years running since the 2022 price-inflation peak, and liabilities sit just past twice equity; capital allocation carries three flags — repurchases above conservative worth, a $769m acquisition year with acquired earnings withheld, and one-time grants above regular pay. At $41.97 the stock trades at 13.5× FCF and 16.1× EV/OI against an $8.17 trigger, pulled down by the $685m tax-receivable liability. A flip needs two clean return years, leverage under the line, two flags gone and a far lower price.

## 2. Understanding

Core & Main is a St. Louis-based, NYSE-listed (IPO July 2021, CD&R-owned 2017–2024, formerly HD Supply Waterworks) specialty distributor that buys pipe, valves, hydrants and fittings (67% of FY2025 sales of $7.65bn), storm-drainage products (16%), water meters and metering systems (9%) and fire-protection pipe and sprinkler products (8%) from more than 5,000 manufacturers and resells them, with fabrication, project take-off and delivery services, from over 370 branches in the United States and Canada to more than 60,000 paying customers — underground utility and fire-protection contractors, municipalities and private water companies — for municipal water and sewer work (44% of sales), non-residential construction (38%) and residential lot development (18%).

One operating and reportable segment; no listed subsidiary or holding. **PASS.** Lens STD.

## 3. Quality — past record

STD lens, seven fiscal years, history-limited (the record starts with the IPO prospectus; no full audited year exists before FY2018, and FY2018's balance sheet was never published), so STRONG is unavailable. Worst-of-four ROCE is 12.5% over the window and 14.6% over five years; FCFROCE on maintenance capex, which equals all capex in every year, is 9.8% and 11.0%. Three findings bind. ROCE fell in FY2023, FY2024 and FY2025 on every series (20.9% → 17.2% → 16.1% → 13.9% on beginning capital): a J6 decline, with the level bars below P2. ## 4. Quality — future

No customer reaches 10% of sales: the largest is about 1%, the top 50 about 12%. The largest supplier is about 7% of product purchases, the top ten 45%, with alternate sources for most products. No concentration flag. All four forces are weak: tens of thousands of small contractor customers; thousands of manufacturers; no substitute for buried pipe, valves and hydrants built to local specifications; one national rival and hundreds of regional ones, behind purchasing scale, local specification knowledge and exclusive lines. The moat is cost — purchasing scale and branch density — and stable to strengthening (gross margin 24.1% in FY2020, 26.9% in FY2025). Market growth MIXED: municipal repair demand grows, residential and non-residential construction is cyclical, and sales grew 0.8% and 2.8% in two of the last three years. **ADEQUATE.**
