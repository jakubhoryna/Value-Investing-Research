# DUOL — Duolingo, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | SF | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | WEAK | WEAK | FAIL (P19 hard strike) | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** (three gates) |

A genuinely impressive consumer product — 130M+ MAU, the top-grossing education app on both stores, revenue up 6.4× in six years, and a first year of real GAAP profit — that fails the model on three independent gates. The financial record is six years old and returns-poor (worst-of-four average ROCE ≈ 1%); the business sits between an unavoidable Apple/Google toll-booth on the supply side and free LLM tutors on the substitute side (the market repriced exactly this, −72% from the May 2025 high); and management began buying back stock in Q1 2026 at ~27× conservative EV/OI — a P19 hard strike. A flip would need years: a sustained double-digit-ROCE record, evidence the AI-substitution threat is contained, and buyback discipline. The band sits at ≈ $41 against $126 today.

## 2. Understanding

Duolingo is a founder-controlled (dual-class), Pittsburgh-based consumer-app company that sells gamified language, math, music and chess learning on a freemium model — paying customers are individual consumers buying Super/Max subscriptions billed almost entirely through the Apple App Store and Google Play (≈ 82% of revenue), plus advertisers, in-app purchasers, and Duolingo English Test takers — a mobile consumer-internet/edtech business, global in users, US-listed, founded 2011, IPO 2021, ~$1.0B revenue and newly GAAP-profitable. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Six fiscal years only (2020–2025; IPO 2021) — under P1, STRONG is unavailable regardless. The record itself is WEAK: cumulative OI 2020–25 is just $43.7M on ~$2.8–4.2B of cumulative capital employed — worst-of-four normalized ROCE ≈ **1.0%** (wc/end; wc/beg 2.2%; both cash-excluded variants n.m. on near-zero/negative denominators). FCFROCE looks strong (19.6–29.2%) but is flattered by $137M of SBC add-back and deferred-revenue float; cumulative FCF ($824M) is 19× cumulative OI. Trajectory improves (2025 ROCE_wc 9.4% end / 15.4% beg), and leverage is modest (L/E 47.9%, no financial debt), but the record is the record. **WEAK** — worst-of-four ROCE far below the P3 10% floor.

## 4. Quality — future

Customer breadth PASS (millions of consumers). Supplier breadth **FAIL**: the effective suppliers are two — Apple and Google, the unavoidable distribution-and-payment rails (10-K: reliance on "a limited number of third-party platforms"; Apple 62.5% and Google 22.7% of receivables) — no ready alternatives exist. Forces: customer power WEAK; supplier power **STRONG** (take rates set unilaterally); substitutes **STRONG** (free LLM tutors and doing-without — a live, repricing threat); new entrants WEAK (brand + 130M-user scale). Tally 2 strong → stop signal. Moat: Brand (with network/gamification reinforcement), stable-to-eroding under AI. Market growth YES, but growth is attracting the substitution. **WEAK** (breadth FAIL and ≥2 strong forces).

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Free LLM tutors (ChatGPT et al.) erode willingness to pay for gamified drilling — the central substitution risk, already visible in decelerating DAU growth.
- Apple/Google take rates and policy control ~82% of the revenue pipe; any adverse term change hits gross margin directly.
- FCF quality: ~38% of 2025 FCF is SBC add-back; deferred-revenue float reverses if bookings growth slows.
- 2025 net income ($414M) is inflated by a $232M deferred-tax valuation-allowance release — one-time.
- Buyback program ($374M remaining) deployed at 25–36× EV/OI destroys per-share value at these prices.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

To BUY additionally requires price < ≈ $41. Next review: FY2026 results (~Feb 2027); earlier if price approaches ≈ $45 or on material AI-substitution or buyback news.

## D. Future-performance worksheet

- **Customer breadth: PASS.** Paying customers are millions of individual subscribers, DET test-takers, and a broad advertiser base; no customer approaches 10% of revenue (no concentration disclosure exists — here because there is nothing to disclose; the AR concentration is with payment platforms, not customers).
- **Supplier breadth: FAIL.** Look-through: the effective supply base is the mobile distribution-and-payment duopoly. The 10-K names reliance on "a limited number of third-party platforms such as the Apple App Store and the Google Play Store to distribute our products and collect payments" as a principal risk; cost of revenues "predominantly consists of third-party payment processing fees" plus hosting and AI costs; Apple alone is 62.5% of receivables. Cost of revenues is $288.1M — the platform-fee component [not separately disclosed; inference: the predominant share, i.e. > $150M] exceeds 10% of COGS+opex ($902M ⇒ line = $90.2M) for Apple alone with high confidence. The ready-alternatives exception cannot apply: this is not deliberate concentration for bulk terms — no alternative vendor can serve iOS distribution at all. Both answers are not yes → breadth FAIL.
- **Forces (binary):** customer power **WEAK** — atomized consumers. Supplier power **STRONG** — Apple/Google set take rates and policies unilaterally; switching impossible. Substitutes **STRONG** — free LLM chatbots deliver conversational language practice at zero price; doing-without is easy for a motivation-driven category; adoption switching costs (streaks, leagues) are real but psychological, not economic. New entrants **WEAK** — brand, 130M-user scale, and a decade of learning data are barriers to success even as AI lowers content-creation barriers. **Tally: 2 strong → Future WEAK** (book stop signal; memo completed per framework).
- **Moat:** Brand (single source; network/gamification features reinforce it — top-grossing education app on both stores). Direction: stable-to-eroding — the brand is intact, but the *category's* pricing power is under AI attack.
- **Market growth: YES** — online language learning and English certification grow; the caveat is that the growth vector (AI) is the same force enabling substitution.
- Captive-customer waiver: not applicable (the concentration is supplier-side; the waiver is customer-side only).
