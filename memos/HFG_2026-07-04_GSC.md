# HFG — HelloFresh SE: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | WEAK | WEAK | FAIL | FAIR | **AVOID** |

HelloFresh fails three gates: Past (returns collapsed from a COVID windfall, leverage above the 200% flag), Future (an eroding scale moat inside a structurally shrinking niche facing unfavourable forces), and Shareholder-friendliness (compensation reports rejected by owners four sourced years running — most recently at the 2 June 2026 AGM — and €125m repurchased at ~25× EV/OI on peak earnings). The 96% drawdown from the 2021 high has made the stock optically cheap — three of four multiples pass — but EV/OI fails decisively on FY2025 earnings, and the headline FCF that makes MCap/FCF look like ~2.7× is an IFRS-16 artefact: after lease repayments, the company's own FCF was €18.9m, i.e. ~32× MCap. This REVIEW closes the one open flip condition from v1: the 2026 say-on-pay vote failed (45.3% for, 54.7% against), so the SF strike stands on fresh primary evidence. To flip the verdict, HelloFresh would need durable order growth, repaired governance, and through-cycle ROCE_xc above 10% — none in evidence.

## 2. Understanding

HelloFresh SE is a Berlin-headquartered, Frankfurt-listed Societas Europaea that sells subscription meal kits (HelloFresh, EveryPlate, Green Chef, Chefs Plate) and ready-to-eat meals (Factor, Youfoodz) plus small D2C food brands directly to household consumers who pay per delivered box, running its own fulfilment network across North America (62% of FY2025 revenue) and Europe/Australia (38%), where it is the global meal-kit category leader but in multi-year volume decline (orders −12.3% in 2025) from its COVID-era peak. **PASS** (restated, unchanged).

## 3. Quality — past record

Restated from v1, unchanged — no new fiscal year. Only 5 of 10 fiscal years tabulated (FY2021–25; gap flagged in Appendix A). ROCE_xc fell 35.8% → 10.8% → 0.2% → 7.4% (2022–25, adjusted OI); on reported EBIT, 2024–25 were negative/nil after €287m of impairments. Adjusted OI/share dropped from €2.17 (2021) to €0.66 (2025). FCFROCE_xc of 11–16% is IFRS-16-flattered; post-lease FCF was negative 2022–24, +€18.9m in 2025. L/E 210.5% at FY2025 (256% at Q1'26) — above the 200% flag without exceptional returns. **WEAK** — sustained ROCE decline plus leverage breach (P4).

## 4. Quality — future

Restated from v1, unchanged. Breadth PASSES (millions of consumers; commodity food suppliers). Forces UNFAVOURABLE: zero switching costs, abundant substitutes (supermarkets, discounters, delivery apps, rival RTE), high rivalry and promo intensity. Moat: Cost (scale) — the largest meal-kit fulfilment network globally — but it defends share of a shrinking niche and is **eroding** (orders −12.3% in 2025, Q1'26 revenue −13%, Spain/Italy exits, 2026 guidance for further decline). Market growth: NO for meal kits, MIXED for RTE. **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

**FAIL — strike: compensation.** New evidence this version: at the 2 June 2026 AGM, owners rejected the FY2025 Compensation Report — 45.3% for, 54.7% against (valid votes ≈ 51.6% of capital) — the fourth sourced rejection (FY2021–23 reports previously; FY2024 vote not sourced). Supervisory-board discharge drew 21.4% dissent; the treasury-share authorization was nonetheless renewed (95.9% for). Corroborating strikes from v1 stand: the Jan–Feb 2022 €125m buyback tranche at ≥€50/share (~24–27× EV/OI on peak earnings) and 2021–23 repurchases that largely sterilised SBC dilution. Ownership itself is fine (Richter ~4–5.5%, single class); RPTs show nothing material. One strike decides.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Meal-kit category decline persists; Q1'26 revenue −13%, guidance for a further 2026 decline.
- RTE (Factor), the supposed growth leg, posted a −1.2% AEBITDA margin in 2025 after US retention damage.
- Governance overhang deepened: fourth rejected pay report, activist pressure (AOC has rolled its collar to Dec-2026 puts at €4.40 / calls at €6.00), US securities-fraud investigations from early 2026.
- TBV is melting (−38% in 2025) as buybacks plus losses consume equity; L/E >250% at Q1'26.
- Reported FCF overstates cash generation vs. lease-adjusted reality; working capital unwinds in volume decline.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

To upgrade Past/Future: ≥2 consecutive quarters of group order growth with meal-kit stabilisation and RTE back to positive AEBITDA. To clear SF: an AGM-approved compensation report under a reformed pay system — the 2026 vote has now failed, so the earliest realistic clearance is the 2027 AGM. Cheapness flips mechanically below ≈€2.7. **Next review: FY2026 results (~mid-March 2027), or earlier on order-trend inflection or an announced pay-system reform.**
