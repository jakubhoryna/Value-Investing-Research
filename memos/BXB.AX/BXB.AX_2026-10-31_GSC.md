## 2. Understanding

Brambles Limited is a Sydney-headquartered pooling company operating in about 60 countries. Under the CHEP brand it owns about 352 million reusable wooden and plastic pallets, crates and containers. It issues them from about 750 service centres, then collects, inspects, repairs and reissues them. Consumer-staples manufacturers, fresh-produce growers, retailers and automotive and general manufacturers pay issue, daily-hire and transfer fees, plus compensation for equipment they lose. **PASS.**
