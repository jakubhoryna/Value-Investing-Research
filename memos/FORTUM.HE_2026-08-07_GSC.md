# FORTUM.HE — Fortum Oyj: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS · Past **WEAK** (worst-of-four ROCE 7.0% on the post-Uniper perimeter; the decade behind it contains the −€2.4bn 2022 loss and a third of book equity destroyed) · Future ADEQUATE · SF PASS (state-control caveat unpaired; capital-allocation CAUTION on the Uniper legacy) · Cheapness EXPENSIVE → **AVOID (Past gate)**.

Fortum today is a clean, low-carbon Nordic generator — hydro and nuclear at genuinely low cost — wrapped around the crater of the Uniper adventure: ~€7bn invested into a German gas utility that the 2022 energy crisis vaporised, taking attributable equity from €13.7bn to €7.7bn. The slimmed company earns 6–9% on capital, declining two years running as Nordic captured prices normalise, and pays out 90% of comparable earnings — a bond-like utility priced at 19–23× operating profit. The framework files it where the record says: quality WEAK, price EXPENSIVE, and the state-majority owner a permanent caveat. Flip requires the merchant fleet to prove ≥10% returns across a cycle — hedge-book arithmetic makes that a price-regime question, not a management one.

## 2. Understanding
Fortum generates and sells Nordic power — hydro (~45 TWh-class), nuclear (Loviisa plus Swedish stakes), some CHP — plus consumer energy sales and district heat remnants, earning merchant power prices optimised through hedging; Espoo-HQ'd, **Finnish state 51%**, the post-2022 rump of the group that briefly owned Uniper and exited Russia at a loss. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
On the comparable post-Uniper window (FY2022–25): worst-of-four normalized ROCE **7.0%** (beginning-basis; end-basis 7.3%, ex-cash ~10%) — path 5.8 → 9.5 → 8.1 → 6.0%, **two consecutive declines standing; a third at FY2026 fires P4 formally** (the level already fails). FCFROCE 9.8% is collateral-flattered by 2022's margining unwind (CFO €4,970m); ex-2022 it reads **5.1%**. The unbuilt years bound the average down, not up: 2016–19 old-Fortum earned utility returns (4–6% class) and 2020–22 carried Uniper — the **2022 total-operations attributable loss was −€2,416m** (primary-sourced) and the Russia exit wrote off the rest. Dividend-heavy per-share record: EPS 0.85 (FY25) vs €0.56-class a decade ago — modest growth, all of it distributed. L/E 89.8%; financial net debt €1.5bn (rising as the payout exceeds FCF). **WEAK.**

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth PASS both sides (Nord Pool clearing + retail base; no ≥10% counterparty). Forces: customer power WEAK (system-price taker, not negotiator) · supplier power WEAK (fuel-light fleet) · substitutes WEAK — electricity is the substitution *winner* · **new entrants STRONG — demonstrated**: subsidised wind build-out keeps compressing Nordic captured prices, and entry continues policy-driven regardless of returns. Tally **1 strong, contained** by the fleet's cost position. Moat: **Cost** — large existing hydro/nuclear with near-zero marginal cost and unreplicable permits; stable. Market growth MIXED (electrification demand vs relentless supply build). **ADEQUATE.**
