# LULU — lululemon athletica inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Full re-derivation, all five gates: Understanding PASS, Past ADEQUATE, Future **WEAK**, SF PASS, Cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID**, on Future alone.

A high-quality business that fails on competitive position. Returns are exceptional — worst-of-four normalized ROCE 33.1%, ~56% gross margin, no borrowings — and shareholder-friendliness clears once the buyback record is judged rather than scored: repurchase pricing has fallen through five consecutive tranches from $373 to $123, the last two inside the bargain line. Gate 1B does not clear. Two forces are strong, the US brand moat is eroding across a third consecutive period, and the founder now both advises and capitalises the challengers taking that share. One reclassification — new entrants — separates AVOID from WATCH, and nothing supports making it. Price is secondary: at $121.07 the stock is 86% above its own trigger, and cheap-looking EV/OI multiples mask poor conversion of operating income into owner cash against ~$680m of annual capex.

## 2. Understanding

lululemon designs and sells premium technical athletic apparel, footwear and accessories — differentiated branded goods — direct to individual retail guests through 816 company-operated stores, e-commerce, outlets and a small franchise and wholesale tail, as a vertically integrated single-share-class Delaware corporation headquartered in Vancouver, sourcing from contract manufacturers in South and South-East Asia, reporting three segments (Americas 66%, China Mainland 19%, Rest of World 15% of Q1 FY2026 revenue); a ~$13.7bn, 19-year public company past its US growth peak, mid-turnaround under an incoming CEO. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized ROCE 33.1% and FCFROCE 18.1% clear the 15%/8% bars on all four averaging conventions; operating income per share compounded 14.8%, tangible book 21.0%; leverage 0.77, no interest-bearing debt. Two things cap the rating. Free cash flow per share shrank 0.8% a year, under the inflation floor — a working-capital and tariff-timing artifact, but the metric is the metric. And only five of ten window years were gathered; the shortfall is **record-limited**, not history-limited, so the cap is liftable and the rebuild is specified in Appendix A. Verdict **ADEQUATE**.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth passes on both limbs, the supplier limb narrowly: sourcing is concentrated, but the largest effective supplier stays under a tenth of COGS plus operating expenses. Forces: customer WEAK, supplier WEAK, **substitutes STRONG** — dupes and every major brand, zero switching cost — and **new entrants STRONG**, since Alo and Vuori each scaled past ~$1bn this decade against lululemon's full scale, which settles empirically whether barriers to *success* exist. Two strong. The moat is Brand and it is eroding where it matters: Americas comps −5% in Q1 with product margin −500bp, a third consecutive decline, against China Mainland +20%. June guidance cut to revenue down-to-flat. Market MIXED. Verdict **WEAK**.

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Compensation clean on both reads with 3–5× headroom and far under the indexed absolute trigger; director fees inside the seat norm; no material related-party transactions; single share class. Capital allocation reads **CAUTION** — one flag. FY2023–24 repurchases were clearly above worth, said plainly. But a failing verdict needs the behaviour to be live, and it is not: execution fell $373 → $317 → $236 → $167 → $123, the last two tranches at 5.5–8.0× EV/OI, cash-funded and count-reducing. It stays at CAUTION rather than sound because the improvement is price-inherited, not published, and still above the ~$65–97 worth line. No pair. **PASS.**
