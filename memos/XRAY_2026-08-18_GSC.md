# XRAY — DENTSPLY SIRONA Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS; Past WEAK; Future WEAK; shareholder-friendliness FAIL; cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID** on three gates.

The assets are real — a 140-year brand estate, the CEREC installed base, a consumables franchise earning a ~40% segment margin. None of it reaches the owner. Over FY2016–FY2025 worst-of-four normalized ROCE was **−4.9%** on capital employed near $7bn, about $6.1bn of goodwill and intangibles was written off across five impairment events, book value per share fell 17.2% a year, and liabilities reached 305% of equity. Two distributors have repeatedly exceeded a tenth of revenue while openly promoting competing equipment. Nothing between here and a BUY is a price problem: the band cannot be computed because reported operating income is negative, and the loose bound needs roughly $336m of OI against last year's −$422m. The verdict flips on sustained positive operating income and a resolved distribution structure — not on a lower price.

## 2. Understanding

Dentsply Sirona is the world's largest diversified manufacturer of professional dental products — endodontic, restorative and preventive consumables, imaging and treatment-centre equipment, CEREC chairside CAD/CAM, implants and clear aligners, plus a Swedish-based urology and enterology continence-care line (Wellspect) — a Delaware-incorporated, Charlotte-headquartered vertically integrated producer that manufactures mainly in Germany, Sweden and the United States and is paid principally by a small number of dental distributors (Henry Schein, Patterson, Benco, Burkhart) and secondarily by dental practices and laboratories in over 150 countries, with about two-thirds of revenue outside the US and EMEA its largest region, currently in its fourth year of restructuring under its third CEO since 2022. **PASS.**

## C. Calculation notes

**NIBCL composition.** NIBCL = accounts payable + accrued liabilities + income taxes payable. Deferred revenue sits inside accrued liabilities and is therefore already deducted. Noncurrent deferred income taxes and other noncurrent liabilities are **left in CE** on the burden-of-proof rule. Short-term debt and the current portion of long-term debt stay in CE throughout.

**Burden-of-proof call, logged.** From FY2019 the current portion of operating lease liabilities is embedded in accrued liabilities ($43.7m FY2019, $48m FY2020, $50m FY2021, $54m FY2022, $56m FY2023; not separately disclosed FY2024–FY2025). Deducting the whole accrued-liabilities line therefore over-deducts a debt-equivalent item and **understates CE by ~$45–56m**, which **flatters** ROCE by roughly 0.1pp. The flattering direction is retained deliberately: it works against the WEAK conclusion, so the conclusion survives its own most generous treatment.

**Leases.** ASC 842 adopted 1 January 2019; FY2019–FY2025 balance sheets already capitalise, and lease liabilities are treated as debt-equivalent in CE, EV and L/E. **P18 not triggered** for the pre-adoption years: undiscounted operating-lease commitments at 31 December 2018 were $133.2m against total assets of $8,687m — 1.5%, far below the 10% capitalisation threshold. The FY2019 adoption-year artifact (ROU asset $159.3m appearing, current lease liability entering accrued liabilities) is flagged; it does not shift any verdict.

**Operating income as reported — no add-backs.** FY2017 (−$1,562m), FY2018 (−$958m), FY2020 (−$12m), FY2022 (−$937m), FY2023 (−$85m), FY2024 (−$879m) and FY2025 (−$422m) are struck by goodwill and intangible impairments of $1,651m, $1,086m, $157m, $1,287m, $307m, $1,014m and $650m respectively — roughly **$6.15bn of cumulative impairment on a business whose peak market capitalisation was about $15bn**. Multiyear averaging has been left to dilute them per §2·1A. The adjusted-basis alternative is stated for contrast only: FY2025 adjusted OI was $508m (13.8% margin) and adjusted EBITDA $667m, which on the same end-of-period CE_wc of $4,399m would read 11.6% — i.e. **ADEQUATE-class on management's basis and WEAK on the framework's**.
