Electrolux fails three gates independently. The decade record shows worst-of-four normalized ROCE of 5.0% and FCFROCE of 1.5%, three consecutive negative-FCF years, tangible book value now negative, and liabilities at 12.2× equity — a leverage breach so far past P5/P6 that the company needed an emergency SEK 9.1bn rights issue in June 2026 at SEK 16.75, tripling the share count. No price flips this without a rebuilt operating record; nothing here is close. First honest re-read: FY2026 annual report, the first post-recapitalization year with Midea partnership economics visible.

## 2. Understanding

Electrolux manufactures major household appliances — refrigerators, washers, dishwashers, ovens, vacuum cleaners — under the Electrolux, AEG, Frigidaire and Zanussi brands and sells them to big-box, electrical and kitchen retailers across Europe (~44% of FY25 sales), North America (~34%) and Latin America (~22%), as a 107-year-old vertically integrated listed Swedish manufacturer controlled by Investor AB (~30.8% capital / 41.6% votes post-issue), currently a former industry leader in decline: three loss years, an emergency 2026 recapitalization, and a new North American production partnership with Midea. **PASS.**
