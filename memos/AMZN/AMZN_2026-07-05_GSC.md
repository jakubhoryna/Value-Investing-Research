REVIEW of the v1 (2026-06-24) memo, re-run on v3 mechanics — no new fiscal year (Q2 2026 reports 30 Jul), so the ten-year record and both quality verdicts are restated, not re-derived. Amazon remains a genuinely good business at a price the framework cannot call cheap. Three high-return engines — a scale-advantaged retail/logistics network, the #1 cloud platform (AWS, re-accelerating to ~28% YoY in Q1), and a fast-growing ad arm on the retail flywheel — compound on ex-cash capital at 22–29% in normal years behind one of the widest, most multi-sourced moats in the market. Nothing about quality blocks a BUY; only valuation does.

## 2. Understanding

Amazon is the world's leading online retailer and third-party marketplace, paired with AWS (the leading public-cloud provider) and a top-three digital-advertising business; it sells to hundreds of millions of consumers, millions of sellers, and millions of cloud customers, as a single-class US-listed corporation earning ~74% retail / ~17% AWS / ~9% ads, ~22% international, and is the growing leader in each core market. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Restated from v1 (no new FY). Worst-of-four normalized average ROCE_xc is strong (typically 22–29%; ROCE_xc ≥ 15% in 9/10 years, the lone miss the 2022 overbuild trough at 7%). OI/share compounded 0.43→7.39 and TBV/share 1.51→34.97 over the decade. Leverage is conservative — net cash, with early-year L/E dominated by interest-free operating liabilities (a negative-working-capital model), not financial risk. The blemish that caps this below STRONG: FCF/share went nowhere (1.08→0.71; negative 2021–22) as capex absorbed the cash — a deliberate-reinvestment artifact, not return erosion. Strong returns and equity compounding; failed per-share cash-flow growth. **ADEQUATE.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASSES decisively on both sides. Forces MIXED: customer power and new-entrant threat LOW (immense scale barriers), but retail rivalry (Walmart, Temu, Shopify-DTC) and cloud competition (Azure, Google Cloud) are intense with credible substitutes — barring STRONG. The moat is exceptional and multi-sourced (cost/scale in logistics and data centres, marketplace + Prime network effects, AWS switching costs and data gravity, brand, ingrainedness) and stable-to-strengthening on the AWS/ads side; the Q1 AWS re-acceleration is confirming, not verdict-moving. Moat-decides floors this at ADEQUATE. **ADEQUATE** (top of band).

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- AI/AWS capex may not earn its cost of capital — the FCF-recovery thesis rests on the current data-centre buildout matching the historic AWS franchise return.
- Cloud is a genuine three-way contest; sustained Azure/Google Cloud share gains would erode AWS pricing power and the profit centre.
- Retail is structurally low-margin, now contested by a resurgent Walmart and price-led Temu/Shein, capping the consolidated return profile.
- Regulatory/antitrust pressure (US, EU) on marketplace practices, bundling, and labour could force costly structural changes.
- Founder influence + the Blue Origin RPT is a low-probability governance tail if related-party flows scale.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

To **BUY**: price into the ~$56 EV/OI band (a catastrophic ~77% drawdown) — the dominant path — or, secondarily, a durable collapse in capex intensity lifting maintenance-basis FCF against a much larger OI base. To **AVOID**: evidence the AI capex is structurally value-destructive (AWS growth/margin rolling over while spend stays elevated) would pull Future toward WEAK. **Next review:** Q2 2026 results (30 Jul 2026) for the AWS-growth/capex read; the FY2026 10-K (~Feb 2027) for the cleanest capex-moderation/FCF-recovery signal; sooner if AMZN trades below ~$90 or AWS/AI capex guidance is cut materially.
