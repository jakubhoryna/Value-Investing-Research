The business did not change in four days; the rules did. Under the v4.5.0 acquisition stop, every deal in the trailing three fiscal years is judged against conservative worth, and Kiona (September 2023) cost an implied €214.9m for a company with €4.4m of adjusted pre-deal EBITDA, at least 49× even before depreciation. ## 2. Understanding

CAREL Industries S.p.A. — founded 1973 near Padua, listed in Milan since 2018 and controlled through a voting syndicate of the Rossi Luciani and Nalini family companies, with €629m of revenue in 2025 — designs, makes and sells electronic controllers, sensors, valves, humidifiers and related software (including Kiona's building-energy SaaS) that makers of air-conditioning, heat-pump, data-centre-cooling and commercial-refrigeration units, plus dealers, contractors and food retailers, pay for and build into their systems; HVAC is 72% of revenue and Europe, the Middle East and Africa 64%. **PASS.** Lens STD; one operating segment under IFRS 8, so no group rules apply.

## 3. Quality — past record

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth passes: the filer states no customer reaches 5% of revenue, and no supplier concentration is disclosed. All four forces are called WEAK: fragmented OEM buyers locked in by design-in, multi-sourced standard components, regulation that raises control content, and entrants who have not dented 13–17% margins in a decade. The moat is switching costs, stable but re-contested at each OEM platform redesign, so durability is not shown. Market growth YES (heat pumps, data centres, natural refrigerants). **ADEQUATE.**
