Understanding PASS (four material units); Past WEAK (8.6% ROCE); Future ADEQUATE (unit customer concentration); Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (compensation); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID** (Past + SF).

Thermo Fisher sells the instruments, reagents, laboratory supplies and outsourced drug-development and manufacturing services that drug makers, laboratories and hospitals rely on, much of it locked in by validated processes. The capital record is the problem: acquisitions have left $65bn of goodwill and intangibles, so the company earns under 9% on the capital it employs, and FCF returns have fallen four years running since the pandemic peak. The CEO's 2025 compensation actually paid reached $116m, repurchases since 2023 ran at 30–38× EV/OI with the programme still open, and Olink was bought with no operating profit. At $676.54 the stock trades at 38.0× EV/OI against a $27.82 trigger. A flip needs returns above 10%, pay inside the lines and capital spent at or below worth.

## 2. Understanding

Group sentence: Thermo Fisher is a Waltham, Massachusetts-based US group, built since the 2006 Thermo Electron–Fisher Scientific merger largely by acquisition (Life Technologies 2014, FEI 2016, Patheon 2017, PPD 2021, Olink 2024, Solventum's filtration business 2025, Clario 2026), that makes and distributes laboratory and bioproduction products and runs contract drug manufacturing and clinical research for pharmaceutical and biotech companies, hospitals and clinical laboratories, universities, governments and industrial customers, about half of revenue from outside the US.

- **Life Sciences Solutions** (22% of segment revenue, 2025): reagents, instruments and consumables for molecular and protein biology, genetic analysis (Applied Biosystems, Invitrogen, Olink proteomics), and bioproduction media, purification resins, filters and single-use systems bought by pharma and biotech manufacturers and research labs.
- **Analytical Instruments** (16%): chromatography and mass spectrometers, chemical-analysis and field instruments, and electron microscopes, with their consumables, software and service, bought by pharma, academic, government, industrial, semiconductor and environmental labs.
- **Specialty Diagnostics** (10%): immunodiagnostic test kits and analysers (allergy, autoimmune, drugs-of-abuse, tumour markers), microbiology culture media, transplant typing, and a healthcare distribution channel, bought by hospitals, clinical and reference laboratories.
- **Laboratory Products and Biopharma Services** (51%): laboratory consumables, equipment and chemicals; the Fisher research-and-safety distribution channel for own and third-party products; contract development and manufacturing of drugs (Patheon); and clinical-trial services (PPD, Clario) bought by pharma and biotech companies.
