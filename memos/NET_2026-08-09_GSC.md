# NET — Cloudflare, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS, Past WEAK, Future ADEQUATE, shareholder-friendliness FAIL (P17 hard strike on both reads, twice over), cheapness EXPENSIVE (410× FY2025 FCF; EV/OI n.m.) — **AVOID on three gates.**

Cloudflare runs one of the internet's great physical assets — an edge network in 330+ cities with a free-tier data flywheel — and has never printed a positive GAAP operating year in nine tries (Σ −$1.19bn). In 2025 the board granted both co-founders ~$60m packages against a $13m ceiling; the stock, up again on AI-edge enthusiasm, trades at 410× last year's free cash flow with convertible debt exceeding seven times any operating income the company has ever earned — meaning no positive trigger band exists even on heroic assumptions. Three gates fail independently; none is near-dated.

## 2. Understanding
Cloudflare sells cloud-delivered network services — CDN/edge delivery, zero-trust security, DDoS protection, and serverless compute (Workers, Workers AI) — by subscription and usage to businesses from self-serve to large enterprise globally (organisations pay; no customer near 10%), a dual-class founder-controlled San Francisco company, public 2019, mid-way through a capex ramp positioning its edge for AI inference. **PASS.**
