Understanding PASS; Past WEAK — on a knife-edge, and robustly so; Future ADEQUATE; shareholder-friendliness PASS (one flag — capital-allocation CAUTION); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID (Past gate)**.

Dolby is the first name of this US run with a real business under the framework's terms: a royalty machine on an ingrained audio/imaging standard, net cash, a shrinking share count, and FY2025 FCF of $436m against a $5.7bn market cap (13×). The flip is arithmetic, not narrative: the FY2026 window-roll drops a strong FY2016, so only a materially stronger FY2026 (Atmos/Vision mix approaching half of revenue) lifts the average past 10%.
