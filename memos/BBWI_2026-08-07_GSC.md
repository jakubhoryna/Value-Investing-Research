# BBWI — Bath & Body Works, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS · Past WEAK · Future ADEQUATE · SF PASS · Cheapness FAIR → **AVOID** (one gate: Past, on the growth limb alone).
This is the register's new nearest-miss and its most watchable AVOID: elite returns (worst-of-four ROCE 28.5%, FCFROCE 16.9%), a PASS on shareholder-friendliness anchored by buybacks executed *below* the bargain lines, and a price sitting **+1.8% above the all-cash band bound** — but operating income per share has shrunk on every base year in the window (−7.5%/yr from FY2021, −2.9%/yr even off the post-COVID FY2022 base), and P3 demands flat-to-positive. The engine is superb; the direction fails. The flip is dated and concrete: TTM OI ($1,148m) is already within 3% of the ≈$1,180m that turns the FY22-base OI/share reading flat at the FY2026 10-K.

## 2. Understanding
Bath & Body Works designs and sells differentiated branded body care and home fragrance — shower gels, lotions, fine-fragrance mists, candles, soaps — to North American consumers (the retail customer pays) through ~1,900 company-operated US/Canada stores, e-commerce, and international franchisees, in the specialty beauty and home-fragrance retail industry; a Delaware C-corp that is the continuing entity of L Brands (heritage to 1963) after the August 2021 Victoria's Secret spin-off, HQ Columbus, Ohio, ~$7.3bn revenue, mature, prominent, negative book equity by construction of two decades of buybacks. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
Levels are elite: worst-of-four normalized ROCE **28.5%** (range 28.5–44.6%), worst-of-four FCFROCE **16.9%** — multiples of the floors. But the record shrinks: revenue down all five years (7,882→7,291), OI down all four transitions (2,009→1,126), **ΔOI/sh −7.5%/yr geometric, and still −2.9%/yr off the FY2022 ex-COVID base** — the P3 growth limb (flat-to-positive nominal) fails on every base year; ΔFCF/sh is −1.9%/yr (FY21 base) / +5.8% (FY22 base). No clean P4 returns-decline (ROCE and FCFROCE both V-shaped, recovering since FY2023/24). Leverage: equity negative from buybacks → L/E n.m.; net debt/FCF 3.1×, derived net financial expense ≈$250m → coverage ≈4.5× — elevated, serviceable. **WEAK — growth limb; the level is the flip engine.**

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth: customers PASS (fragmented consumers); suppliers PASS with a logged look — production concentrated in the Ohio "Beauty Park" contract-manufacturer cluster, deliberate for speed-to-market, with ready alternative vendors (the P15 exception). Forces: customer power WEAK; supplier power WEAK; substitutes WEAK (habitual low-ticket category, high brand adoption); new entrants **STRONG** — social-media-native fragrance/body brands scale fast and cheap, and the category's post-2021 revenue erosion is partly their bite. Tally 1 strong, contained. Moat: Brand (category-leading share, ~37m-member loyalty program), **stable-to-eroding** — held ADEQUATE, not STRONG, precisely because the erosion question is open. Market growth MIXED (fragrance up, mall traffic down). **ADEQUATE.**
