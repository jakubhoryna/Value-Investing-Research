# 7751.T — Canon Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS · Past **WEAK** (worst-of-four ROCE 6.1%; FCFROCE 4.9%) · Future ADEQUATE · SF PASS (1 flag — capital-allocation CAUTION) · Cheapness EXPENSIVE (though the cheapest name of this run's nine on EV/OI) → **AVOID (Past gate)**.

Canon is a patents-and-installed-base conglomerate managing the world's slowest melt: office printing (still the profit centre) declines structurally while medical (Toshiba Medical, 2016), litho equipment and network cameras grow into the gap. Per-share numbers look respectable (EPS +11.5%/yr, helped by escalating buybacks), but the framework prices the capital, and ¥5–6tn of capital has earned 2.6–7.9% for a decade — never once touching 10%. FY25's ¥448bn of distributions against ¥214bn of FCF, bridged with debt, adds the SF flag. Flip requires the portfolio transition to lift a full window's returns above the floor — no current trajectory does that arithmetic.

## 2. Understanding
Canon manufactures and sells office multifunction printers and consumables (~half of profit, annuity-like and declining), medical imaging systems (CT/MRI, ex-Toshiba Medical), lithography and OLED equipment, network cameras, and interchangeable-lens cameras — differentiated hardware with patent estates and installed-base annuities — to businesses, hospitals, chipmakers and consumers worldwide; Tokyo-HQ'd, 89 years old, US-GAAP filer, founder-era chairman-CEO (Mitarai, 90) still presiding. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **6.12%** (end-of-period, with-cash; best variant 7.12% — the spread is small because the cash pile is modest against goodwill-heavy assets). Worst FCFROCE **4.87%**. Path: 4.8 → 7.0 → 7.6 → 3.9 → 2.6 → 6.5 → 7.5 → 7.4 → 5.2 → 7.9% — **no window year reaches 8%**, let alone 10%. ΔOI/sh geo +10.2% and ΔEPS +11.5% (FY16 trough base plus a fifth of the share count retired — stated); ΔBV/sh +4.7%. L/E **67.6%**. FY24 carries a ~¥180bn-class medical-goodwill-era impairment inside as-reported OI (commented, not added back). **WEAK** (below P3 across every variant).

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth PASS both sides (no ≥10% customer; diversified supply). Forces: customer power WEAK (fleet contracts, consumable lock-in) · supplier power WEAK · **substitutes STRONG** — paperless workflows erode the printing annuity (doing-without form) and smartphones already absorbed consumer cameras · entrants WEAK (patent estates, installed bases, service networks). Tally **1 strong, contained** (medical/industrial legs diversify the exposure). Moat: **Switching costs** — office MPS contracts and consumables, hospital equipment relationships; stable in aggregate, eroding in print. Market growth MIXED (print down, medical/litho up). **ADEQUATE.**
