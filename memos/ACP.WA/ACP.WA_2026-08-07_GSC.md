Asseco is CEE's largest software house — and structurally a holding pyramid: Asseco Poland atop Formula Systems atop listed Israeli subsidiaries, with PLN 5.5bn of minority interests owning roughly half the economics that consolidate into its accounts. The group machinery says what it says: 10 years, PLN 5.6–7.5bn of acquisition goodwill, and with-cash returns of 4–10%, never touching the floor. Cash conversion is better than book returns (FCFROCE 8.7% — amortisation of acquired intangibles darkens OI), the 2023 tender buyback was executed at a third of today's price, and the 2025 Sapiens disposal just landed PLN 7.2bn of group cash — the next allocation round decides whether this pyramid ever compounds for the parent's shareholders. The gate math doesn't wait for that answer.

## 2. Understanding
