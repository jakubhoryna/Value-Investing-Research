# 7936.T — ASICS Corporation: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | ADEQUATE | ADEQUATE | **FAIL** (P19 hard strike) | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID (SF gate)** |

ASICS is a genuine turnaround-into-quality story — operating margin rebuilt from a 2018–2020 trough (including a FY2020 operating loss) to 17.6% and a record ¥142.5bn OI in FY2025, on a strengthening performance-running brand. But the board spent ¥85bn repurchasing its own stock in FY2024–25 at average prices of ¥1,556–¥3,716, and three of those four programmes imply 24–33× MCap/FCF at execution — far beyond the 20× hard-strike line, in record-profit years, at multiples of any conservative worth estimate, partly rationalised as offsetting dilution from the ASICS Foundation share disposal. That is a single-strike SF FAIL under §2·1C; the price (41× MCap/FCF, 23× EV/OI) makes the same point from the other side. A flip requires a full programme year of buyback discipline below the 14×/16× flag lines — or abstention — evidenced at the FY2026 results.

## 2. Understanding

ASICS designs and markets differentiated performance running, other sports, and lifestyle footwear (plus a smaller apparel/equipment line) under the ASICS and Onitsuka Tiger brands — production outsourced to third-party contract factories chiefly in Southeast Asia — selling to consumers worldwide (Japan roughly a seventh of sales, per segment tables) through fragmented wholesale sporting-goods and run-specialty accounts and a growing owned DTC/e-commerce channel; a 77-year-old Kobe-headquartered operating company, a global top-tier performance-running franchise mid-way through a profitability transformation. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

FY2016–25, JGAAP throughout. Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **12.8%** (end-of-period, cash-in; best variant 18.1%) — above the 10% floor, below the 15% STRONG bar. Worst-of-four FCFROCE **9.6%**. The window is a valley: ROCE fell from ~9% (2016) to −1.4% (2020, operating loss), then climbed to 33% (2025); the decline is historical, not current, so no forced downgrade (logged). Per-share growth clears the floor emphatically — OI/sh +22.7%/yr and FCF/sh +13.4%/yr endpoint CAGR — though FY2022's FCF was −¥32.7bn (inventory build). L/E 115%, within P5. **ADEQUATE** — the level, not the trend, keeps it from STRONG.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASS both sides: customers are millions of consumers through fragmented wholesale plus DTC (no >10% customer disclosed); suppliers are interchangeable contract footwear manufacturers across Vietnam/Indonesia/China with ready alternatives. Forces: customer power WEAK; supplier power WEAK; substitutes WEAK (running participation entrenched; rival brands are rivalry, not substitution); **threat of new entrants STRONG** — On and Hoka went from zero to multi-billion franchises inside a decade in ASICS's core channel, proof that barriers to entry and success are surmountable. Tally 1 strong, contained: ASICS gained run-specialty share alongside the entrants. Moat: **Brand** (performance-running heritage and credibility with serious runners; Onitsuka Tiger in lifestyle), strengthening — gross margin 49.5%→56.8% in five years is pricing-power evidence. Market growth YES. **ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

P17 clean: top pay (Chairman/CEO Hirota) ¥297m FY2024, above ¥400m FY2025 — ~0.5% of lower(FCF, NI) vs the 5% strike, and far below the ≈US$40m (≈¥6bn) indexed trigger; outside-director pools imply fees within Japanese norms. RPT: nothing material identified; professional (non-founder) management; cross-shareholdings fully sold — a governance positive. Dividends rising (¥28/sh FY2025). **P19 hard strike:** FY2024–25 repurchases of ¥85.0bn across four programmes at implied **14.0× / 23.7× / 27.6× / 33.4× MCap/FCF** (11.2–19.0× EV/OI) — three above 20×, in record years, no trough or below-worth defence; the Aug-2024 and Feb-2025 tranches explicitly offset Foundation-disposal dilution (sterilisation-adjacent, logged). **FAIL.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Running-boom normalisation: FY2023–25 growth (+18–20%/yr) rests partly on a participation cycle; a fashion rotation away from performance running would compress the record margins the market is capitalising.
- Entrant pressure: On/Hoka demonstrated the brand moat is contestable; losing run-specialty share would undercut the Future call.
- FX: a strong yen reverses part of the translation tailwind behind FY2024–25 profits.
- Inventory cyclicality: FY2022's −¥32.7bn FCF shows how fast working capital can consume cash in a downturn.
- Capital allocation: continued buybacks at 25×+ multiples destroy per-share worth even as reported profits rise.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

AVOID rests solely on the P19 strike — quality gates pass. Flips to WATCH on a full programme year of repurchases below the 14×/16× flag lines (or abstention with dividends/reinvestment instead), evidenced at the FY2026 tanshin (~mid-Feb 2027) and FY2026 securities report; TTM data show no material Q1-2026 repurchase, so the next programme announcement is the watch item. Cheapness would additionally need ~−80% of price or several years of profit compounding. A Past upgrade to STRONG requires the worst-of-four ROCE average to clear 15% as trough years roll out of the window (mechanically possible by ~FY2028).
