Three independent failures, any one disqualifying. The business is admirable — a decade of 13% revenue compounding, 14.9% geometric growth in OI per share, no equity issuance, a real brand — and none of it survives the gates. Past fails on leverage: L/E 267.1% breaches P5, P6 does not rescue it. Future fails on the force tally: supplier power and substitutes both STRONG. SF fails on a P19 hard strike — every treasury purchase in the window served Stock Grant Plan delivery to executives, which is dilution sterilisation and strikes at any price. Underneath sits the quiet problem: worst-of-four ROCE 10.78%, ADEQUATE at best, for a company whose reputation implies far more.

## 2. Understanding

**PASS.** Brunello Cucinelli S.p.A. is a founder-controlled (Foro delle Arti, 50.05% of capital and 64.91% of votes), Solomeo-headquartered, listed 48-year-old personal-luxury-goods house selling differentiated very-high-price cashmere-led ready-to-wear, knitwear and accessories — and, less obviously, Umbrian operating real estate and a 43% stake in its yarn spinner — to affluent end consumers through directly-operated boutiques and e-commerce (67.3% of sales) and multibrand and franchise wholesale (32.7%) across the Americas (37.0%), Europe (35.1%) and Asia (27.9%), manufacturing entirely in Italy through 400-plus contracted artisan workshops.

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized ROCE 10.78% (wc, end-of-period), FCFROCE 9.07% — ADEQUATE, not STRONG. No sustained decline: 11.9% in FY2016 through the FY2020 loss year, then 8.1 → 13.3 → 17.0 → 18.5 → 15.2%. Per-share growth is clean — OI/sh +14.9%, FCF/sh +11.6% geometric, far above the 3% floor, on a flat 68.0m share count. Leverage breaks it: L/E 267.1% against P5's 200%, and P6 does not apply — Cucinelli is not borrowing cheaply to retire underpriced stock and equity has not shrunk. **WEAK** (P4).

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASSES both sides: no customer near 10% (67.3% direct-to-consumer, fragmented wholesale); largest supplier Cariaggi Lanificio is 10.27% of raw materials but 1.05% of COGS plus opex. Forces — customer power WEAK (price-insensitive buyers, no volume leverage); **supplier power STRONG** (the effective input is finest-grade cashmere from a narrow fibre market and few Italian spinners, one of which Cucinelli bought 43% of; Made-in-Italy locks production to Umbrian workshops it cannot relocate); **substitutes STRONG** (Loro Piana, Zegna, Hermès at comparable value; doing-without costless; switching costs nil); new entrants WEAK (entry easy, success takes decades). Tally 2 strong = the stop signal. Moat: brand, strengthening. Market growth YES. **WEAK.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

**FAIL — P19 hard strike (dilution sterilisation).** Treasury purchases of €11.7m (FY2024) and €8.0m (FY2025) served only the 2022–24 and 2024–26 Stock Grant Plans; the 123,500 shares bought went free of charge to executive directors and top employees. Sterilising SBC dilution strikes at any price; no non-plan repurchase exists to weigh against it and all three redemption limbs fail. P17 lines are €5.01m (year) and €5.36m (3-year) against a strategic-executive aggregate of €11.18m, but no individual figure is disclosed — **INDETERMINATE**, never a pair-completer. Related parties are numerous and recurring: property from the controlling holder (€10.4m) and the founder's brother (€7.4m), family payroll, associate yarn — collectively a documented negative. Dividends €68.8m, 50.9% payout, rational. The 64.91%-of-votes control structure is a logged caveat, not a strike.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Leases are 54% of total liabilities; further retail expansion pushes L/E away from P5 even as the business improves.
- Cashmere fibre is volatile and geographically concentrated; the Cariaggi stake hedges price but concentrates exposure.
- Retail at 67.3% of sales turns a demand slowdown into operating deleverage against a fixed lease base.
- Brunello Cucinelli is the brand's meaning as well as its controlling holder; succession is unhedgeable.
- The compensation-linked repurchase pattern is structural to the Stock Grant Plan architecture, not a one-off error.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

To WATCH, all three must clear: L/E back inside 200% (roughly €0.4bn of equity accretion against a static lease book, or genuine lease contraction); a repurchase programme with a published worth-referenced criterion and no compensation-delivery purpose; and a force turning WEAK, plausible only on suppliers. BUY additionally needs the €8.71 band — arithmetic, not a forecast. Downward: a third consecutive ROCE decline fires P4 independently of leverage.

**Next review:** FY2026 AR (~March 2027) — resolve the P17 INDETERMINATE against the Remuneration Report, re-test P19 for any non-plan repurchase, roll the L/E and returns window.
