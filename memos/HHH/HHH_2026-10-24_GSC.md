# HHH — Howard Hughes Holdings Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS, Past WEAK, Future ADEQUATE, shareholder-friendliness FAIL and cheapness EXPENSIVE give **AVOID** on two gates.

HHH owns an irreplaceable land estate and the rental buildings on it, and since June 2026 a specialty insurer. The record is what fails: over ten years the business has earned about 3% on the capital it employs, free cash flow summed negative, and per-share operating income has no growth read that clears a floor. Shareholder-friendliness fails on three independent strikes: chief-executive pay rose in a loss year, the 46.6% holder is paid an advisory fee for capital allocation, and Vantage was bought at about 34 times its pre-deal operating income. At $70.19 the stock sits at 24.5 times FCF and about 36 times operating income, and no positive price meets the operating-income leg while debt-equivalents exceed seven times that income. A different verdict needs a different record, years of it.

## 2. Understanding

Howard Hughes Holdings (The Woodlands, Texas; listed parent since August 2023 of The Howard Hughes Corporation, a 2010 spin-off from General Growth Properties) develops master-planned communities in Nevada, Texas, Maryland and Arizona and sells their residential land to national homebuilders and lots to individuals, leases offices, shops, apartments and other buildings on that land to businesses and households, sells condominium units it builds in Ward Village, Honolulu, to individual buyers, and since 4 June 2026 owns Vantage, a Bermuda and US specialty insurer and reinsurer selling policies through brokers to businesses and to other insurers; Pershing Square, its 46.6% holder, advises it under a services agreement as it turns into a diversified holding company. **PASS.**

It is a group with three material units on the FY2025 segment note; Vantage, consolidated after the fiscal year-end, is not a reportable segment and carries no weight.
