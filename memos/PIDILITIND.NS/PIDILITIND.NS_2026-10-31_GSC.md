## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS, Past STRONG, Future ADEQUATE, shareholder-friendliness FAIL, price EXPENSIVE: **AVOID**, on one gate (shareholder-friendliness).

Pidilite owns India's household names in adhesives and sealants, led by Fevicol, and sells them through a dense dealer network to carpenters, painters and plumbers. Its record is strong: worst-case returns of 22.4% on capital and 14.7% in free cash flow, operating profit per share up about 12% a year, almost no debt. What fails is how the company has treated outside owners' money in two separate ways. In FY2018 it bought back 1% of its shares at about 79 times free cash flow, under the Executive Chairman who still chairs it. In FY2025 the former managing director's year total, valued at grant, came to 6.6% of free cash flow. Two flags on distinct behaviours fail the test. The trigger is INR 146.8 against INR 1,461.6. AVOID would lift if either flag clears: the chair who oversaw the buyback leaves office, or the pay record reads clean, and the price is not part of that.

## 2. Understanding

Pidilite Industries, a Mumbai company incorporated in 1969, is a large, mature, vertically integrated maker of branded adhesives, sealants, waterproofing and construction chemicals and art materials (Fevicol, Fevikwik, M-Seal, Dr. Fixit, Fevicryl) that it sells mainly through dealers and distributors to carpenters, painters, plumbers, contractors and households, and of industrial adhesives, synthetic resins and organic pigments that it sells to packaging, textile, paint, ink, paper and leather manufacturers, about 90% in India, with plants and subsidiaries in India and abroad. **PASS.**

- **Business to Business (B2B):** industrial adhesives, synthetic resins, organic pigments and pigment preparations and project construction chemicals, sold to manufacturers in packaging, textiles, paints, printing inks, paper and leather, in India and abroad (21%).
