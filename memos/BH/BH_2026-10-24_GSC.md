# BH — Biglari Holdings Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS, Past WEAK, Future WEAK, shareholder-friendliness FAIL and cheapness EXPENSIVE give **AVOID** on three gates.

Biglari Holdings is a small burger chain, two niche insurers and a few oil fields, wrapped around limited-partner interests in funds run by its chairman. The operating businesses earned about 3% on the capital they employ over ten years; counting the fund results as well, the latest five years earned less than nothing. The chairman's own company took $11.4m of service fees in 2025, more than twelve times his salary, while the company's money, passed through his funds, keeps buying its stock at prices its earnings do not support. At $396.19 the stock is 44 times free cash flow and 64 times operating income. A different verdict needs a different structure and years of a different record.

## 2. Understanding

Biglari Holdings (San Antonio, Texas; led since 2008 by its controlling chairman, dual-class since 2018) sells Steak n Shake burgers and shakes to diners, through company-run restaurants and through franchise partners and franchisees who pay it fees and royalties; writes truck physical-damage and non-trucking liability insurance for truckers (First Guard), and garage liability, commercial property, homeowners and dwelling-fire insurance through agents (Southern Pioneer); produces oil and gas in the Permian Basin and offshore Louisiana for purchasers of crude and gas; licenses the Maxim brand; and holds limited-partner interests in The Lion Fund partnerships, run by its chairman's firm, which invest mainly in a few listed stocks, its own included. **PASS.**
