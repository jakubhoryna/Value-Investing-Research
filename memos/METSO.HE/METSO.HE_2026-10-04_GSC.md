# METSO.HE — Metso Oyj: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS; Past ADEQUATE (history-limited); Future STRONG; Shareholder-friendliness PASS (one flag — capital-allocation CAUTION); Cheapness EXPENSIVE — **WATCH**.

Metso sells crushers, grinding mills, flotation and filtration equipment to mines and quarries, and then sells the wear parts, spares and services that keep that installed base running; aftermarket is 57% of sales. Since the 2020 combination with Outotec and the exit from smelting and refining, operating income has settled at €735–805m and ROCE in the mid-teens. Cash conversion is the weak leg: FCF covered only 43% of operating income over the record, because inventories and the Metals exit absorbed cash in FY2022–24. At €16.93 the stock trades at 22.4× FCF and 21.3× / 20.7× EV/OI. A BUY needs the price near €4.27–4.73, or operating income roughly three times FY2025. A longer record at FY2025's cash conversion would strengthen Past; another dividend paid ahead of FCF would sharpen the capital-allocation flag.

## 2. Understanding
Metso designs and sells rock-crushing, screening, grinding and mineral-separation equipment and the consumable wear parts, spare parts and field services for it, paid for by mining companies (Minerals, 76% of FY2025 sales) and by quarry, aggregates and construction contractors (Aggregates, 24%). Sales are spread almost evenly across Europe, the Americas, Asia-Pacific and Africa/Middle East/India. It is a Helsinki-listed, widely held company whose current form dates from the June 2020 demerger of Metso's minerals business and its combination with Outotec; the Finnish state holding company Solidium owns 14.9%. **PASS**.

## 3. Quality — past record
The record runs FY2018–FY2025: the minerals business existed only inside the old Metso group before that, so the label is history-limited and STRONG is unavailable. Worst-of-four normalized ROCE is **12.4%** (end-of-period, cash in CE); worst FCFROCE is **5.4%**. ROCE has risen from about 10% before the combination to 14.5–15.9% in FY2023–25; FCF lagged and only caught up in FY2025 (€627m). Per share, operating income grew 10.4% a year over FY2019–25 and FCF 6.7% over FY2020–25. L/E is 170%. **ADEQUATE**.

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth passes: no customer reached 10% of sales in FY2024 or FY2025, and the supply base runs to about 17,000 vendors. All four forces are WEAK. Mines cannot build mills or flotation cells, and a mill or crusher in service ties its owner to the OEM's liners, spares and service for decades. Inputs are steel, castings and components available from many sources, and no process substitutes for comminution. Chinese OEMs are the entrant threat, contained so far by reference plants and the service network. The moat is switching costs on the installed base. It is stable. Market growth: YES (copper and electrification metals; infrastructure aggregates). **STRONG**.
