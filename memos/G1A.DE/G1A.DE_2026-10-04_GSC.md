GEA is worth studying for its installed base of separators, homogenisers, valves, pumps, dryers and milking systems, which generates a large service and spare-parts stream. The decade record does not clear the quality floor. Worst-of-four normalized ROCE is 8.8% and FCFROCE 7.7%. The recovery is real, but the window decides. The price is far away as well: 24.1× FCF and 17.1–17.6× EV/OI against a band at €22.10. The verdict turns to WATCH only if the rolling window lifts the worst read to 10%. That needs roughly two more years at current returns as FY2016–FY2017 roll out.

## 2. Understanding
GEA designs, manufactures and services process equipment and plants: separators, homogenisers, valves and pumps; spray dryers, freeze dryers, evaporators and complete dairy, beverage and food lines; pharma processing systems; milking robots and barn equipment; and industrial refrigeration and heat pumps. The customers who pay are dairy, food, beverage, pharmaceutical and chemical producers, plus dairy farms. It is a German Aktiengesellschaft headquartered in Düsseldorf and sells worldwide, with about half of revenue from Europe. It is an established global leader, restructured since 2019 from a holding of business areas into divisions, with service at about 40% of revenue. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
**WEAK.** Worst-of-four normalized ROCE is **8.8%** (beginning of period, with cash). The cash-stripped reads are 10.5–10.6%, and the worst FCFROCE is 7.7%. The trend rises: about 5% over FY2016–20 against 12.7% over FY2021–25, so there is no sustained decline. The early half carries a €248m goodwill impairment in FY2019 and repeated restructuring costs, which stay in (no add-backs). OI/share compounded 9.2% and FCF/share 9.9% (FY2015→FY2025). BV/share is flat (+0.2%) after €1.2bn of buybacks. L/E is 148%.

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth **PASSES**. The segment note says no individual customer is significant to group revenue. Procurement is spread across about 1,400 key suppliers of steel, castings and standard components. Forces by unit: components, service and farm technology have all four forces WEAK. Plant engineering (Nutrition Plant Engineering, about a third of revenue at a 9.8% H1 2026 EBITDA margin) has customer power **STRONG**, because large dairy and food groups tender multi-year projects. That is one strong force, contained. The moat is **switching costs**: validated process lines, OEM spare parts and service on a large installed base. It is stable. Market growth is YES: food, dairy and pharma processing demand grows steadily. **ADEQUATE.**
