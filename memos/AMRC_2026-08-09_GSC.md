# AMRC — Ameresco, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Past **WEAK** (worst-of-four ROCE 3.56%; FCFROCE negative on every variant, Σ 5y FCF −$2.38bn; L/E 300%) · Future ADEQUATE · SF **FAIL** (P17 hard strike: $3.34m vs $2.21m/$2.73m ceilings) · EXPENSIVE (26.7×/26.1× EV/OI, both band bounds negative) → **AVOID (three gates)**.

Ameresco is a balance sheet pretending to be an operating company. Over five years it converted $2.4bn of negative free cash flow and $1.4bn of new debt into energy assets that lift operating income 6.6% a year while returns on capital *fell* from 5.0% to 3.2% — and interest now eats two-thirds of operating profit ($123m of OI, $44m of net income). The business is defensible: federal ESPC contract vehicles are a real Government moat and the backlog is genuine. But five straight years of building assets that earn 3% on the capital they absorb, against equity now leveraged 3:1, is the record. The compensation strike is almost incidental to a name whose band needs operating income to more than double.

## 2. Understanding
Ameresco designs, builds, finances and operates energy projects — building retrofits under long-dated energy savings performance contracts for federal agencies, states, universities and hospitals, plus owned renewable assets it keeps (landfill-gas and RNG plants, solar, storage) selling energy under long-term offtakes; Framingham-HQ'd, founder-controlled via Class B shares. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **3.56%** (end-basis, with-cash; best variant 4.35%) — path 5.0 → 5.3 → 2.5 → 3.0 → 3.2%. **FCFROCE is negative on all four variants**: free cash flow ran −$356m, −$648m, −$614m, −$321m, −$436m — structural, not cyclical, since the energy-asset model consumes cash by design and monetises it over twenty years. Operating income grew $95.4m → $123.2m (**+6.6%/yr**) on a flat diluted count while total assets went $1.75bn → $4.54bn: 160% more capital bought 72% more profit. **L/E 300%**, P6 let-out denied on its face. **WEAK** by three independent routes — level, cash returns, leverage.

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth: supplier side PASS; customer side check-limited — federal agencies in aggregate are a large block and no single-customer disclosure was retrievable (App A); a FAIL there deepens rather than changes the verdict. Forces: **customer power STRONG — demonstrated**, ESPC work is won in competitive procurements against Johnson Controls, Honeywell, Schneider and NORESCO, and the margin path shows it (7.8% → 6.4%) · supplier power WEAK · substitutes WATCH — agencies can appropriate capital directly instead of contracting an ESCO · new entrants WEAK — federal IDIQ vehicles, qualified-ESCO status and bonding are hard to acquire. **Tally 1 strong, contained.** Moat: **Government** — contract vehicles plus incumbency on twenty-year installed contracts; stable. Market growth YES. **ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness
Comp: the FCF leg is negative, so both reads run on net income alone. FY2025 ceiling **$2.214m** against a CEO total of **$3.340m** (breach 1.51×); trailing-3-year ceiling **$2.725m** (breach 1.23×). Both breached — **hard strike**. The figure is secondary-sourced with a parsing caveat, so a primary tie-out is a live open item — but the gate does not rest on it. RPT: none surfaced; check limited. Founder control via Class B shares — documented caveat. Capital allocation: **CAUTION** — no repurchases (itself a finding) and no dividends; the evidence sits on the reinvestment lever, ~$1.83bn of capex over five years against $542m of cumulative operating income, debt-funded, executed while ROCE fell from 5.0% to 3.2% and leverage reached 300%, with $300–350m more committed for 2026. Real assets, long offtakes, nothing extractive — not ADVERSE. **Even if the strike softened to a single-read flag, it pairs with this CAUTION and the gate still fails.**

## 6. Price
At $25.65 the equity is $1.36bn against $1.88bn of debt — the enterprise is three-quarters borrowed. MCap/FCF n.m.; **EV/OI 26.7× / 26.1×** (unrestricted cash only; crediting $92.5m of restricted project cash gives 25.3×). Both band bounds are negative — seven times operating income does not cover the debt. The condition restoring the no-cash bound is **operating income ≈ $275m** against $123m today: the band is not far away, it is 2.2× away. **EXPENSIVE.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers (context for the AVOID)
The bull case is that consolidated returns understate mature-asset economics — assets under construction earn nothing while sitting in capital employed, and 100–120 MWe are due in service during 2026. A fair accounting point, and five consecutive years of it have produced a declining return rather than a lagging one. The bear case needs only the interest line. No data repair moves any of the three gates.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review
Past requires ~$385m of operating income on today's capital plus positive free cash flow, which contradicts the model. Price requires OI ≈ $275m merely to make the band computable. The strike clears at the 2027 proxy on a package under ~$2.2m or net income above ~$67m. Next review: **FY2026 10-K (~Feb/Mar 2027)**. Alert: n/a.
