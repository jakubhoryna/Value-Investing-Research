# MU — Micron Technology, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | ADEQUATE | **WEAK** | **FAIL** | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** (Future WEAK + SF FAIL) |

The textbook business the model exists to flag, at the textbook moment: a commodity — DRAM and NAND bits — sold into a three-player oligopoly that produced an operating loss in FY2023 and a $33.3bn single-quarter operating profit in Q3 FY2026 on the AI/HBM shortage; the stock has repriced ~10× in a year to ~$1.14tn, capitalising boom earnings as permanent. The verdict does not rest on price: Future is WEAK (one customer = 17% of FY2025 revenue, plus two strong forces), and SF FAILs on a P19 hard strike — the FY2024 repurchase (3.2M shares at $93.75, sourced) ran 85× period EV/OI, 866× MCap/FCF, 21× even on ten-year normalized OI. Neither gate is price-fixable.

## 2. Understanding

Micron designs and manufactures commodity semiconductor memory — DRAM (~76% of FY2025 revenue, incl. HBM), NAND, and NOR — sold as components and SSDs to organisations (cloud/AI data-centre operators, smartphone/PC OEMs, auto and industrial) worldwide; a vertically integrated, Idaho-headquartered fab owner (US, Taiwan, Japan, Singapore; CHIPS-Act-funded expansion), ~48 years old, one of three firms controlling essentially all global DRAM supply, in its strongest-ever up-cycle. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Violently cyclical, adequate only on the rubric's letter. Worst-of-four 10y normalized ROCE **10.7%** (end/with-cash; best variant 14.2%) clears the 10% floor but not 15%; worst-of-four FCFROCE is **2.8%** — $52.7bn of decade OI converted to just $14.0bn FCF, negative in FY2016 and FY2023 (−$6.1bn), because capex runs a structural 23–49% of revenue. An oscillation, not a sustained decline. ΔBV/sh +17.1%/yr genuine; ΔOI/sh +55.6%/yr a trough-base artifact; ΔFCF/sh n.m. L/E 53%. **ADEQUATE** — returns clear the floor; cash conversion and cyclicality bar more.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth **FAIL**: one customer was 17% of FY2025 revenue (10% FY2024; none ≥10% FY2023) — HBM concentration, new and rising. Waiver unavailable: the customer dual-sources HBM across SK Hynix and Samsung (qualification cycles, not prohibitive locks); no dominant moat. Supplier side passes (multi-sourced materials; EUV single-sourcing is capex, not COGS — noted). Forces: customer power **STRONG** (interchangeable commodity, dual-sourcing giants); supplier power WEAK; substitutes WEAK; new entrants **STRONG** — state-funded CXMT/YMTC scaling through the capital barrier. Tally 2 strong. Moat: contested oligopoly cost/scale position; leadership rotates, the cycle sets prices — no durable single source. Market growth YES — attracting exactly the capital now flooding in. **WEAK** (breadth FAIL; independently ≥2 strong forces).

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Comp clean: Mehrotra FY2025 total $30.94M vs the $83.4M hard-strike line (5% × lower(FCF, NI)), below the ~$40M indexed trigger; top outside director $412K < $500K. RPT: none in FY2025 per proxy. Buybacks **strike**: FY2024's $300M at $93.75 avg (sourced, 10-K) = **85× EV/OI, 866× MCap/FCF, 21× on 10y-normalized OI** — above 20× on every denominator, no below-worth escape (implied EV $111bn vs conservative normalized worth ≈ $40bn). Corroborating: FY2021/22 executions at ~35×/~27× MCap/FCF (est. prices) and a sterilisation pattern ($7.3bn spent FY2019–25, diluted count −1.6%). **FAIL** (P19 hard strike).

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- The cycle turns as 2025–27 announced capacity (Micron's own $19.6bn 9M capex; Samsung, SK Hynix, CXMT) lands into decelerating AI capex — the pattern after every shortage.
- HBM concentration (17% and rising) turns one design-loss or in-housing decision into a revenue cliff.
- Chinese entrants permanently compress commodity DRAM/NAND pricing, shrinking the profit pool to HBM.
- CHIPS-Act agreements add build-out obligations regardless of cycle position.
- Upside risk to the verdict: HBM proves durably differentiated — re-opens the Future gate, not the SF gate.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

AVOID is structural, on two gates. Future flips only if customer concentration falls back below 10% and the entrant threat stalls — not price-achievable. SF flips only on a sustained buyback record below the 14×/16× flag lines or at demonstrated discounts to worth (FY2026 YTD's $650M at ~4× period EV/OI is one clean data point, not a record). Cheapness is moot until both flip. **Next review: FY2026 10-K (~Oct 2026)** — band and B1 re-anchor; earlier only on a concentration-disclosure change or buyback-policy event.

## D. Future-performance worksheet

- **Customer breadth: FAIL.** 10-K Note 28: one customer 17% of FY2025 revenue (primarily CMBU/cloud); 10% FY2024; none ≥10% FY2023. Concentration is new, HBM-driven, and rising. Waiver test: (i) backward integration improbable — yes — but switching costs are NOT prohibitive: HBM buyers dual-source across SK Hynix/Samsung/Micron via qualification cycles; (ii) no dominant stable moat; (iii) structural concentration — arguably. Two of three conditions fail → **waiver denied** (contrast ASML/TSMC/QCOM, where lock-in was prohibitive and the moat dominant).
- **Supplier breadth: PASS.** 10-K: "generally multiple sources of supply for raw materials." Wafer/chemical/gas suppliers are oligopolies with ready alternatives among each other (P15 exception). EUV lithography is single-source (ASML) but is equipment capex, outside the COGS+opex test — logged as a real dependency, not a breadth fail.
- **Forces (binary):** Customer power **STRONG** — commodity bits, interchangeable across three suppliers, buyers are hyperscalers/OEMs with dual-sourcing leverage. Supplier power **WEAK** — materials multi-sourced; equipment oligopoly serves all producers symmetrically. Substitutes **WEAK** — no functional substitute for DRAM/NAND; doing-without not viable. New entrants **STRONG** — CXMT (DRAM) and YMTC (NAND) are scaling on state capital despite the barrier, already compressing commodity-tier pricing; barriers to entry are enormous but demonstrably being bought. **Tally: 2 strong → book stop-signal; Future WEAK by force count independently of breadth.**
- **Moat:** cost/scale/process position within a three-firm oligopoly. Process leadership rotates (Samsung, SK Hynix, Micron have each led nodes); pricing is cycle-set; HBM qualification adds stickiness but is shared with two rivals. No single durable source among the six — at best a contested cost position. Not a floor.
- **Market growth: YES** (AI/HBM demand) — and attracting record industry capex, the classic precursor of the next glut.
