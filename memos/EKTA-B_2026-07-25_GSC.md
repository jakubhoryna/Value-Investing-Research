## 2. Understanding

Elekta is a Stockholm-headquartered, founder-controlled (dual-class) medical-technology manufacturer that builds, sells and services differentiated capital equipment — linear accelerators, Gamma Knife and MR-guided radiotherapy systems, brachytherapy — plus the oncology workflow software (MOSAIQ, Elekta ONE) that runs it, selling to the hospitals, clinics and public health authorities that pay for it across 130+ countries (Americas 27%, EMEA 40%, APAC 33%), as a vertically integrated manufacturer whose economics rest as much on the service annuity across ~7,500 installed devices as on new-system orders. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalised averages FY2021–FY2026: **ROCE 7.6%** (best 9.7%), **FCFROCE 5.4%** (best 6.9%) — both under P3. Decline, not a dip: ROCE on beginning capital employed ran 10.0%, 10.3%, 8.7%, 11.3%, 4.5%, **1.3%**. Per-share growth fails the 3% floor everywhere — ΔOI/sh −34.3% CAGR, ΔFCF/sh −4.0%, ΔBV/sh −2.8%; ΔTBV/sh n.m. on negative tangible book. L/E 280% breaches P5 and the P6 conditions do not hold. Only seven fiscal years were retrievable, capping the verdict at ADEQUATE regardless; the record reaches **WEAK** unaided.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth passes both sides: no customer reaches 10% of net sales (disclosed); procurement is dual-sourced with no supplier above the line (no disclosure sentence — inference logged). All four forces call **WEAK**: many customers who cannot backward-integrate and face high switching costs, gross margin holding 37–38% for seven years; dual-sourced suppliers; substitutes mostly complements, hypofractionation a decade-scale drift; entrants blocked by approval, installed base and service network. Tally 0 strong. Moat source **switching costs** — stable in the installed base, eroding at the innovation edge given SEK 2,190M of development impairment and the Kaiku wind-down. Plausible, not demonstrably durable → **ADEQUATE**.

Short-term debt (2,937) and the current portion of lease liabilities (256) stay in CE per rule. This is deliberately more conservative than Elekta's own capital-employed definition, which deducts all interest-free liabilities and reports SEK 14,477M against my 15,898.

**Goodwill** stays in capital employed. Elekta has made repeated small acquisitions across the window (FY2022 SEK 175M through FY2026 SEK 62M cash), so the non-acquisitive deduction is not available.

| Force | Call | Reason |

Guarded against the late-pipeline glow: market growth has been present throughout the window while returns fell, so it is not load-bearing here.

**Verdict: ADEQUATE.** Breadth passes, zero strong forces, and the moat is plausible and identified — but it is not demonstrably durable or strengthening, which is what STRONG requires.
