# SOON.SW — Sonova Holding AG: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |

Sonova is the global #1 hearing-aid maker — a genuinely decent oligopoly business with worst-of-four ROCE of 13.9%, mid-single-digit per-share compounding, and modest leverage. What disqualifies it is a decade of repurchase practice: every buyback programme tranche from 2018 through 2023 was executed between 21× and 35× on the implied EV/OI and MCap/FCF dual-reads — CHF ~1.68bn spent at an average of roughly CHF 169–337 per share against a framework bargain line near CHF 50 today. Four independent hard strikes; the flagged pattern is the entire record. A flip requires the v3.5.0 redemption clause: the 2025 turnover of CEO, CFO, and Chair satisfies limb (c), and the 2022–25 authorization lapsing ~70% unused arguably approaches limb (b), but no worth-referenced repurchase policy exists (limb a), so the strikes stand. Even were SF cured, the price sits ~300% above the trigger band.

## 2. Understanding

Sonova develops, manufactures, and sells medical-grade hearing aids (Phonak, Unitron), cochlear implants (Advanced Bionics), and audiological-care services through ~3,900 own retail clinics; payers are independent audiology practices and wholesale chains, government purchasers (US VA, European health systems), insurers, and out-of-pocket consumers; it is a vertically integrated Swiss stock corporation (HQ Stäfa, founded 1947, production in Switzerland/Asia/Mexico) spanning wholesale and retail in the global hearing-care industry, the market leader by share, currently divesting its consumer-electronics (Sennheiser Consumer Hearing) sideline. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four 10y normalized averages: ROCE 13.9%, FCFROCE 13.3% (both end-of-period, with-cash) — above the 10% ADEQUATE floor, below the 15% STRONG bar. ΔOI/sh 6.4% and ΔFCF/sh 4.6% geometric, clearing the 3% floor; ΔBV/sh 3.5% alongside CHF ~2.3bn of buybacks and rising dividends. L/E 114.5%, well under P5. Returns oscillate 12–16% with no disqualifying sustained decline, though FY2024–26 sit below the FY2022–23 peak. **ADEQUATE** — solid but never breaches 15% on the conservative denominator.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASS: "no single customer accounts for more than 10% of total sales" (AR disclosure); supplier base broad at 74% gross margin, custom-ASIC foundry dependence noted with ready alternatives. Forces: customer power WEAK (fragmented payers, channel switching costs); supplier power WEAK; **threat of substitutes STRONG** (OTC hearing aids since 2022, Apple AirPods hearing-aid feature intercepting entry-level users; "doing without" is the industry's oldest substitute); new entrants WEAK (regulatory, channel, and R&D barriers to success in the Rx segment). Tally: 1 strong, contained to the low end. Moat: ingrainedness — Phonak as the audiologist channel's default — stable in the Rx core, eroding at the consumer edge. Market growth YES (demographics, penetration). **ADEQUATE.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Goodwill-heavy balance sheet (intangibles ≈ CHF 2.6bn vs equity 2.6bn) leaves no tangible cushion; Sennheiser impairment shows the write-down path.
