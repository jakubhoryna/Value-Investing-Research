# BERG-B.ST — Bergman & Beving AB (publ): GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

Understanding PASS (three material units); Past WEAK (5.5% ROCE); Future ADEQUATE (unit customer conc.); Shareholder-friendliness FAIL (flag pair); cheapness EXPENSIVE — **AVOID** (Past + SF).

Bergman & Beving owns niche Nordic and UK companies that make or source fastening, roofing and building components, safety and fire-safety equipment, workwear and protective gear, and workshop machinery for builders, installers and industry. Ten years of reported accounts show returns on capital of about 6%, a 2017 spin-off of the Momentum Group business, a SEK 270m goodwill write-off in FY2025 and leverage of 235% of equity. Quality, not price, stands between here and a BUY: debt-equivalents exceed seven times operating income, so no buy price exists. The verdict would need ROCE above 10% on both windows, L/E back under 200% and an acquisition and pay record that clears the flags.

## 2. Understanding

Group sentence: Bergman & Beving AB is a Nasdaq Stockholm-listed group (listed as B&B Tools since 2001, renamed after the 2017 distribution of Momentum Group; revenue SEK 4.97bn in FY2026, about 1,350 employees, 65% of revenue outside Sweden) that owns, buys and develops niche companies which manufacture under their own brands or source from Asian and European suppliers and sell fastening elements, metal roofing, heat exchangers and inspection hatches, personal-safety, perimeter, fire-safety and measurement equipment, safety shoes, gloves, eyewear and hearing protection, and workshop machinery and tools, mainly to Nordic industrial resellers and building-materials retailers, contractors, installers and industrial companies in Sweden, Norway, Finland, the UK and the rest of Europe (FY2025: industry 43%, construction 39% of revenue).

- **Core Solutions** (32.4% of FY2026 revenue): ESSVE fasteners, Raintite and other metal-roof products, HC Coils heat exchangers and inspection hatches, sold to building-materials retailers, resellers and contractors for renovation, public buildings and infrastructure.

- **PPE & Utilities** (32.7%): Zekler eyewear and hearing protection, Arbesko safety shoes, Guide gloves and Luna consumables, sold mainly to Nordic industrial resellers and building-materials retailers.

Machinery & Equipment (9.7% of revenue, 9.8% of segment assets, 8.9% of positive segment results) is immaterial: sheet-metal and welding machinery and tools for workshops.

## 4. Quality — future

The group discloses that its ten largest customers take about 25% of revenue; no unit rules out a customer above 10%, so each carries `customer conc.`, called a STRONG customer-power force. In Core Solutions and Safety Technology it is contained: margins rose in FY2026 and products are specified or certified. In PPE & Utilities, which sells mainly through resellers, it is not: margins fell to 5.7% and Skydda's Nordic arm was sold to Ahlsell after a SEK 270m goodwill write-off. Supplier, substitute and entrant forces are weak elsewhere. Moats are switching costs and brands, stable in two units and eroding in PPE. S = 1.58: **ADEQUATE**.
