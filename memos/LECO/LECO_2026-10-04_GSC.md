## 2. Understanding

Lincoln Electric Holdings, Inc. is a ~$4.2bn-revenue, Cleveland-based manufacturer, founded in 1895, that makes arc-welding filler metals and brazing and soldering alloys (54% of sales), welding and cutting equipment (26%) and robotic welding and assembly systems (21%). It sells them through industrial distributors, retailers and direct to fabricators, OEMs and integrators in general fabrication, energy, heavy industry, automotive and construction. It manufactures in 20 countries, and the Americas segment is 64% of sales. **PASS.**
