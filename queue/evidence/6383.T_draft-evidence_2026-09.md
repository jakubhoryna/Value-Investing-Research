### Gate 1 — Do I understand it?
**Pass.**

Daifuku builds the automated systems that move material inside buildings: automated storage and retrieval, sortation and conveying for distribution centres and factories; **automated material handling for semiconductor fabs and flat-panel plants — overhead hoist transport moving wafer carriers between tools in a cleanroom**; airport baggage handling; and car-wash and automotive assembly conveyance. **It is the same brownfield-density mechanism as Kardex and Interroll at an order of magnitude more scale, plus a semiconductor capital-equipment business the smaller names do not have.**

### Open items and data gaps
- **No semiconductor-versus-intralogistics revenue split** was disclosed in either document read `[sourced absence]`. **The single most decisive gap in this memo.**
- **H1 operating income figures are rounded to ¥0.1bn** in the results presentation (¥56.6bn and ¥51.1bn), so the LTM operating income of ¥106,316m carries up to ±¥100m of rounding — **0.1%, immaterial to every multiple above but stated for completeness.** The fiscal 2025 and balance-sheet figures are to the million.
- **Segment income does not reconcile to group operating income**: segments sum to ¥68.6bn against a reported ¥56.6bn, a ¥12.0bn corporate-and-elimination gap that the presentation does not itemise `[computed]`.
- **The convertible bond is treated as debt in the enterprise value** — conservative in direction, since it raises EV. **On conversion it becomes equity and adds roughly 13.4m shares** `[inferred from the fiscal 2025 basic-to-diluted gap; the conversion price and terms were not obtained]`. **The fully diluted ownership arithmetic in this memo therefore rests on the company's own diluted EPS rather than on stated conversion terms.**
- **Interest-bearing debt is taken as short-term borrowings plus the long-term convertible bond.** The tanshin notes the current portion of convertible bonds is "included in current liabilities" without a separate figure at 31-12-2025, **so the fiscal 2025 net cash figure of ¥199,872m is an upper bound** and the fiscal 2025 ROIC of 29.6% is very slightly overstated. The 30-06-2026 figures are complete.
- **The fiscal 2024 comparative is a nine-month transitional period.** No growth rate spanning 1 January 2025 should be taken from a provider without checking it.
- **The previous close of ¥5,661 is derived** from price and change, not read from a field. Immaterial — the price itself carries a closing stamp.
- **No free-cash-flow or capital-expenditure figure** was obtained for either period.
