### Verdict — *sourced lines and tables only; verdict prose dropped*
RTL is **not** obviously a value trap on price. It trades at roughly **7.8x enterprise value to the adjusted EBITA management guides for 2026** `[computed]`, its streaming business crossed into profit in H1 2026 — **+€31m against a €34m loss a year earlier** `[sourced]` — and it acquired Sky Deutschland on 1 June 2026 for **€68m of upfront cash on a cash-free, debt-free basis**, against **€250m of annual synergies targeted within three years** `[sourced]`. Those are not the facts of a melting ice cube being marked down.
**First, the transformation arithmetic does not work at the pace it is running.** Streaming adjusted EBITA is guided at **~€100m for FY2026** `[sourced]`. Linear TV advertising ran at **€977m in H1 2026 and is declining ~4% a year on the company's own guidance** `[sourced]` — call it €78m of revenue lost annually `[computed]` at broadcast incremental margins, which are high. **Streaming's entire annual profit is roughly two years of the linear decline it is meant to replace** `[computed, using an assumed 50–70% incremental margin — labelled as an assumption, not data]`. That is a treadmill. It can still be a good outcome; it is not yet evidence of durable returns.
**Second, and this is the finding that decides the file: RTL has written a call option on its own shares at €36.26, and today's price is €32.55.** The Sky consideration includes **variable consideration capped at €377m, payable to Comcast if RTL's share price exceeds €36.26 within five years of closing** `[sourced]`. That strike sits **11.4% above the current price** `[computed]` and the cap is **7.5% of the current market capitalisation** `[computed]`. **The re-rating that the entire value case depends on is the event that triggers a payment of up to €377m.** An investor buying for the discount to intrinsic value is buying an instrument whose payoff has been deliberately truncated just above the money.
Add a controlling shareholder at roughly 76% — free float is **22.77%** `[sourced]` — and the minority's position is: a structurally declining core, a transformation that runs to stand still, a written call just above the price, and no ability to force anything.

### Gate 1 — Do I understand it?
**Marginal pass. Three businesses, two of which are easy and one of which is not.**

FY2025 shape `[all sourced]`:

| | FY2025 revenue | Change | Adjusted EBITA / EBITDA |
|---|---|---|---|
| Group | **€6,010m** | **−3.8%** | **€661m adjusted EBITA (−8.3%)**, ~11% margin |
| Fremantle (content production) | €2,040m | −9% | €175m adjusted EBITDA, 8.6% margin |
| Streaming (RTL+, M6+) | €509m | +26% | Near breakeven in Q4 |

H1 2026 `[all sourced]`:

| | H1 2026 | Change |
|---|---|---|
| Group revenue | €2,890m | +3.9% (**+0.1% organic**) |
| Group adjusted EBITA | €239m | +49% |
| Adjusted EBITA margin | 8.3% | from 5.8% |
| Profit from continuing operations | €61m | from €6m |
| Linear TV advertising | €977m | **−4.0%** |
| Digital advertising | €255m | +10.9% |
| Streaming revenue | €299m | +27.2% |
| Streaming adjusted EBITA | **+€31m** | from −€34m |
| Fremantle revenue | €835m | −7.7% |
| Fremantle adjusted EBITA | €60m | from €39m; margin 7.2% from 4.3% |
| Paid subscriptions | 8.73m | +20.7% |

**The advertising and streaming businesses are straightforward.** Sell audiences to advertisers; sell subscriptions to viewers. The economics are transparent and the decline in one and growth in the other are both plainly disclosed.

**Fremantle is not.** A €2bn content production business is hit-driven, and its revenue fell 9% in FY2025 and a further 7.7% in H1 2026 while its margin rose from 4.3% to 7.2% `[sourced]` — revenue down, margin up, which usually means mix or the absence of low-margin volume rather than improvement. Nothing available explains which.

**Sky Deutschland makes gate 1 harder, not easier, and the memo should say so.** It was acquired on a **cash-free and debt-free basis** `[sourced]`, which is the standard formulation — but for a pay-TV business the economically important liabilities are **multi-year sports and content rights commitments**, which are contractual obligations rather than debt and are typically outside such a definition. **No disclosure of Sky Deutschland's revenue, profitability, or content commitments was obtained in this run** `[sourced absence — the closing announcement contains none]`. A one-month contribution of **€61m of adjusted EBITA in June 2026** `[sourced]` is reported, against a full-year expected contribution of **approximately zero** `[sourced]`. **A single month contributing €61m while twelve months contribute nothing is a seasonality pattern that cannot be understood without the underlying rights calendar**, and that calendar has not been seen.

Gate 1 passes only because the two-thirds of the group that matters most is legible. It passes with the acquisition marked as an unexamined box.

#### The core is in structural decline and the disclosure says so plainly — *sourced lines and tables only; verdict prose dropped*
German television advertising fell **9–10% in FY2025**, with RTL outperforming the market `[sourced, management]`. RTL guides linear TV advertising down a further **~4% in 2026** `[sourced]`. Group revenue fell **3.8%** in FY2025 and adjusted EBITA fell **8.3%** `[sourced]` — **profit falling faster than revenue, which is the signature of a business losing operating leverage on a fixed cost base.**
H1 2026's headline +3.9% revenue growth was **+0.1% organic** `[sourced]`. Essentially all of the growth was Sky.

#### The replacement arithmetic — *sourced lines and tables only; verdict prose dropped*
- Guided decline: **~4% per year** `[sourced]` → about **€78m of revenue lost annually** `[computed]`
- Streaming adjusted EBITA guided at **~€100m for FY2026** `[sourced]`, up from a full-year loss
That is not a reason to dismiss the streaming business — 8.73m paid subscriptions growing 20.7% `[sourced]` is a real asset, and the profitability inflection is genuine and ahead of the company's own guidance, which was raised from €25–50m to ~€100m `[sourced]`. **It is a reason to reject the claim that the transformation has changed the group's trajectory.** Group adjusted EBITA guidance for FY2026 is **~€725m** `[sourced]` against €661m in FY2025 — an improvement, but one that arrives with **€1bn of acquired Sky revenue** `[sourced]` attached. **Guidance for adjusted EBITA was maintained at ~€725m when Sky was added to the revenue line, which is the company stating that Sky contributes nothing to 2026 profit.** `[sourced, computed]`

#### Returns on capital — not computed, and that is a gap not a verdict — *sourced lines and tables only; verdict prose dropped*
**No return on invested capital, ROCE or ROE figure was obtained for RTL in this run** `[sourced absence]`, and the balance sheet detail needed to compute one — total equity, goodwill, invested capital — was not retrieved. **Gate 2 here therefore fails on the direction and durability of profit, not on a measured return.** That is a real limitation and it is recorded in the gaps below.

#### Capital allocation — one very good decision, and a distribution that flatters
**The Sky acquisition looks, on the terms disclosed, like an exceptionally good trade.** €68m of upfront cash — reduced from an originally communicated €150m by working-capital and debt-like adjustments `[sourced]` — for Sky's Germany, Austria and Switzerland operations, taking combined DACH subscriptions to **12.4m** `[sourced]`, with €250m of annual synergies targeted within three years and European Commission approval granted unconditionally on 22 April 2026 `[sourced]`.

**But a price that low is information, not just a bargain.** Comcast sold a business with a large subscriber base for less than one month of its reported adjusted EBITA. The plausible explanations `[inference]` are the content and sports rights commitments that a cash-free, debt-free formulation excludes, and a standalone cost base that requires the €250m synergy programme to make the business viable at all. **Both readings are consistent with the disclosed fact that Sky adds ~€1bn of revenue and ~zero EBITA in 2026.**

The **FY2025 dividend of €5.50 per share, an 86% payout, of which €4.74 came from the RTL Nederland sale proceeds** `[sourced]`, means the underlying distribution was **€0.76** `[computed]`. Consensus expects a 2026 yield of **7.57%** `[sourced]`, implying roughly **€2.43 per share** `[computed]`. **The gap between €0.76, €2.43 and €5.50 across two years means the sustainable dividend is genuinely unclear from the disclosure**, and any thesis resting on the headline yield should not be built until it is.

#### The contingent consideration — the structural point — *sourced lines and tables only; verdict prose dropped*
Restated here because it is a capital-allocation fact as much as a valuation one. **Variable consideration of up to €377m becomes payable to Comcast if RTL's share price exceeds €36.26 at any point within five years of the 1 June 2026 closing; RTL may settle in shares, cash, or a combination** `[sourced]`.
- Settlement may be in shares, so the alternative to the cash cost is dilution `[sourced]`

### ⟪ `claude_2026-09-07_review-session_due-reviews_v1.md` → (c) RTL Group — two bounds, and the upper one is the unusual half ⟫
**The trigger is two bounds, not a price, and the register has no column for the second.**

- **Lower bound (floor), the one to carry as the trigger:** **EUR 20.70 — 10x consensus FY2026 earnings of ~EUR 2.07 per share, −36.4%** from EUR 32.55 `[computed]`. **The memo explicitly rejects an adjusted-EBITA trigger for this name** — 7.8x EV/FY2026 guided adjusted EBITA becomes **15.5x** consensus earnings, because more than half of adjusted EBITA is consumed by depreciation, amortisation, interest, tax and minorities. The EBITA-based thresholds (7x EUR 28.93, 6x EUR 24.22, 5x EUR 19.51) are in the memo **"for completeness and should not be adopted as the register trigger"** `[sourced — memo, verbatim]`.
- **Upper bound (ceiling), and this is the finding that decided the file:** **EUR 36.26, +11.4%** from EUR 32.55 `[computed]`. Variable consideration of **up to EUR 377m** becomes payable to Comcast if RTL's share price exceeds **EUR 36.26** at any point within five years of the 1 June 2026 Sky Deutschland closing; RTL may settle in shares, cash or a combination `[sourced]`. **The cap is 7.5% of market capitalisation** `[computed]`. **RTL has written a call on its own shares 11.4% above the price — the company has contracted to pay a third party up to 7.5% of its market value precisely in the event that the re-rating a value thesis depends on begins.**

**Register cell text proposed:** `EUR 20.70 (10x cons. FY26 EPS ~2.07) — floor; ceiling EUR 36.26 (Sky contingent consideration, cap EUR 377m = 7.5% of mkt cap) ⌈`

**A new legend mark is required.** Proposed **`⌈` = row carries an upper bound as well as a lower one.** RTL is currently the only such row, and **the ceiling is above the price while every other bound in the register is below it** — a sweep reading the trigger column alone cannot see it.
