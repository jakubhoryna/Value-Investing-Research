### ⟪ `claude_2026-09-08_ERRATA-and-supplement_candidate-dossiers_v1.md` → 1.2 What is now known about Cotes, and what the decline still needs ⟫
**Cotes A/S** `[primary — the acquired company's own announcement, 13 July 2026]`: CAREL acquired **100% of the share capital**; **the consideration was NOT disclosed**; founded 1986, based in **Aarhus, Denmark**; **more than 120 employees**; headquarters in Denmark, production in **Poland**, sales entities in Denmark and Germany plus an international distributor network; designs and makes **desiccant dehumidifiers for industrial applications** — wind energy, pharmaceuticals, electronics, food, refrigerated warehousing and battery manufacturing. **⚠ An undisclosed consideration on a 120-plus-employee acquisition is a governance datapoint, not only a modelling gap** — the same treatment the corpus already applies to Interroll's Royal Apollo. **Add it to the IR question list.**

**So the H1 2026 net-cash decline of EUR 11.3m remains unexplained, and it is now a cleaner question because the obvious answer is eliminated.** **The leading candidate is the ordinary dividend** — Italian issuers typically pay in the first half — with working capital and capex behind it `[assumption — none of the three was obtained]`. **Do not record any of them as the cause without the cash-flow statement.**

**The substantive point stands and is strengthened: a company growing 23% organically that still consumed net cash in the half is either funding heavy working capital or distributing more than it earned in cash, and which one it is decides whether the organic growth figure means what it appears to mean.** **First question of any CAREL memo, unchanged — with Cotes removed from the list of answers.**
