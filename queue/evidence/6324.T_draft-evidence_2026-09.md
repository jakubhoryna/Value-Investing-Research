### Gate 1 — Do I understand it?
**Pass.**

Harmonic Drive Systems makes strain-wave gears — precision speed reducers that convert a fast, low-torque electric motor into slow, high-torque, near-zero-backlash rotation inside a robot joint. **It is the single most specific listed exposure to robot content there is: an articulated industrial or humanoid robot needs one reducer per axis, and the reducer is the part that determines repeatability.** Secondary markets are semiconductor and flat-panel production equipment, machine tools, medical devices and aerospace actuation.
