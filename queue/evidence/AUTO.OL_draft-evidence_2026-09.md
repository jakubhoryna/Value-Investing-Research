### Verdict — *sourced lines and tables only; verdict prose dropped*
**The bear case, and it is the loudest single fact on this row.** **On 13 August 2026 AutoStore announced a strategic supply agreement with Amazon establishing "a global framework for the supply of AutoStore's products and solutions to Amazon" — and the company's own report states, verbatim, that "it does not contain any purchasing commitments at this time."** `[sourced]` **The Frankfurt line's 52-week range is EUR 0.43 to EUR 1.27 — the shares have roughly tripled inside a year** `[sourced, from the stale quote page, whose RANGE is usable evidence of a move even though its PRICE is not]`. **A framework agreement with no commitments, on a share that has tripled, is the exact shape of a narrative the framework has no gate to score.** §5.

### 1. The business
**AutoStore sells cube storage: aluminium grids of stacked bins with robots running on top, retrieving bins to picking ports.** **The economic claim is density — the same throughput in a fraction of the floor area, retrofitted into buildings that already exist.** **That is brownfield productivity, and it is the closest thing in the listed universe to the mechanism the register already owns in Kardex.**

**It sells through a partner network rather than direct** `[general structure; the partner count was not disclosed in the documents read]`, **and it reports in USD while listing on Oslo Børs in NOK — the Diageo and Hexagon convention problem in a third name.**

**FY2026 guidance: revenue "around USD 700 million"** `[sourced]` — **+29.9% on FY2025 `[computed]` — with the caveat that "investments are expected to moderate margins slightly in the second half of the year compared to those achieved in the first half"** `[sourced, verbatim]`.

**Three readings the table earns.**

**The margin swing is enormous and it is operating leverage, not price.** **EBIT margin went from 16.2% to 33.1% on revenue up 62.7%** `[computed]`. **A business whose EBIT margin doubles in a year is a business with a high fixed cost base recovering volume — the same signature the corpus rejected in Harmonic Drive's +231% guided swing and flagged in Sandvik's +48% EBITA on +24% revenue this run.** **The framework's consistent answer is that a trough-to-peak margin move is not evidence of durable returns; it is evidence of operating gearing.** `[inference]`

**Backlog cover is thin for this band. USD 596.1m against an annualised H1 revenue run-rate of USD 715.6m is 0.83 years** `[computed]` — **against Krones' nine months, KION's 5.2 months, and Daifuku's record ¥741.1bn.** **Cube storage is a shorter-cycle, higher-turn business than plant engineering, which is a genuine structural difference and not a weakness.** **But it means the revenue visibility that makes Daifuku and Andritz readable is not present here.**

**Order intake grew slower than revenue in the half — 36.2% against 62.7%** `[computed]` — **so the backlog is being converted faster than it is being replaced.** **Backlog rose only 12.6% year on year while revenue rose 62.7%.** `[computed]` **That is the opposite of GEA's shape this run and it is the single most useful leading indicator on the row.**

**Gate 1 verdict: PASS.** **A grid, bins, robots and ports, sold through partners into warehouses. It is describable in a sentence and the sentence is the whole business.** **The one caveat is that its listed history begins in 2021, so the record spans the post-pandemic e-commerce capex boom and its unwind and nothing before it** — **the condition MODEL question 8d was written for, and the same caveat the screen attached to the name before it was analysed.**

**Put plainly: the entire operating business — the grids, the robots, the working capital, the R&D that produced them — runs on ninety-one million dollars of tangible committed capital.** **Everything else on the shareholders' side of the balance sheet is what somebody paid for the company before it listed.**

**That is not a criticism of the business and the memo should not be read as one.** **It is a statement about what gate 2 is being asked.** **A framework asking "does this business earn well?" gets 260% pre-tax. A framework asking "does the capital shareholders committed earn well?" gets 14.1% pre-tax and 10.9% on net profit.** **Both are correct answers to different questions, and the framework has never chosen which one it is asking.**

**Deleveraging is real and fast.** **Net debt fell from USD 120.5m to USD 36.8m in six months while interest-bearing debt fell USD 69.8m** `[sourced]`. **On 12 August 2026 the board authorised a buyback of up to USD 75m running to 31 December 2026, with repurchased shares to be cancelled** `[sourced, verbatim]`. **Both are the actions of a business generating more cash than it needs, which the income statement makes entirely plausible.**
