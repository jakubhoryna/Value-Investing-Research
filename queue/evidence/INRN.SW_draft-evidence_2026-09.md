### Verification — the correction, and it generalises
**The first quote page fetched for Interroll displayed a last price of CHF 2,930.00 with no timestamp.** Its day range (CHF 2,865–2,915), its market capitalisation (implying CHF 2,869) and its dividend yield (implying CHF 2,857) **all agreed with one another to within 0.5%**, and all were wrong. **A second fetch of the same ticker with an explicit locale parameter returned CHF 1,606.00 with a session stamp.**

**Three lessons, and the second is the important one:**

1. **Internal consistency is not evidence of currency.** A stale page is stale in every field at once, so its own cross-checks reconcile perfectly. **The share-count, dividend-yield and P/E reconciliations catch wrong *instruments*; they cannot catch wrong *dates*.**
2. **The timestamp is the only field that carries date information, so a page without one must be discarded whole — not repaired from its other figures.** **The `d` "derived and bounded" mark proposed earlier in this run is withdrawn.** It was built on this page and it would have written a 44% error into the register with three ticks beside it.
3. **A missing timestamp is a retry signal, not a result.** The same URL returned a usable quote on a second attempt with a different query string.
