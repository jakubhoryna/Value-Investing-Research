### Verdict — *sourced lines and tables only; verdict prose dropped*
**ROIC including goodwill and intangibles: 9.4% in FY2025, 9.2% in FY2024** `[sourced, company-disclosed]`. Excluding them: **22.7% and 22.1%** `[sourced]`. Heineken publishes both numbers on the same line of the same release, which is more honest than most, and the goodwill-inclusive figure **does not clear a 10% bar in either year**.
Everything else is a good half-decade of operational work sitting on top of that. H1 2026 organic operating profit **+6.7%**, margin **14.6% (+55bp)**, net profit beia **+10.2% organic**, EPS beia **+11.6% organic** `[sourced]`. Africa & Middle East operating profit **+30.8% organic**, Asia Pacific **+17.7%** `[sourced]`. A €400–500m annual gross-savings programme running at the upper end of guidance `[claim]`. Buybacks retiring stock. None of it changes the fact that **on the capital shareholders have committed, this business has earned single digits for two consecutive disclosed years**.
- **No permanent CEO.** Dolf van den Brink stepped down **31 May 2026** and, as of the H1 results, no successor had been named `[sourced]`. A €40bn group has now run for over three months without one, in the middle of a 5,000–6,000 role reduction `[sourced]`.
- **Leverage is going the wrong way.** Net debt/EBITDA(beia) **2.2x at FY2025 → 2.6x at 30-06-2026** `[sourced]`, following completion of the **FIFCO** acquisition on **30 January 2026** `[sourced]`.

### Gate 1 — Do I understand it?
**Pass on the business, marginal on the entity.**

Heineken brews and sells beer, cider and increasingly non-alcoholic and licensed products across more than 180 breweries in over 70 countries. **FY2025: 281.6 mhl total volume, of which 234.0 mhl beer** `[sourced]`. Four reporting regions: Europe, Americas, Africa & Middle East, Asia Pacific. The product is simple; the geography is not.

Three complications a reader must hold:

1. **"beia" everywhere.** Every headline number — revenue, operating profit, net profit, EPS, net debt/EBITDA — is *before exceptional items and amortisation of acquisition-related intangibles*. **This is not a minor adjustment for a company carrying €20bn of intangibles**, and the framework should never compare a beia margin to a peer's reported margin without saying so. This memo uses beia throughout because that is the only basis on which the company guides, and flags it here rather than silently.
2. **Two listed entities.** Heineken N.V. (HEIA) and Heineken Holding N.V. (HEIO) both trade in Amsterdam and report separately on the same day. **This memo is on Heineken N.V.** Any register row must say which.
3. **Licensed volume is growing very fast off a small base** — H1 2026 **10.4 mhl, +23.2% organic** against beer volume of 115.6 mhl at **−0.1%** `[sourced]`. Licensing is high-margin and capital-light, and it is doing disproportionate work in the profit line while the core beer volume is flat to down.

**H1 2026 regional operating profit (beia)** `[sourced]`: Americas €850m (+2.2% organic), Europe €580m (+0.6%), Asia Pacific €480m (+17.7%), Africa & Middle East €316m (+30.8%). **Europe and the Americas are 64% of the profit and are growing at ~1-2%; the growth is entirely in the emerging-market regions.** `[computed]`

- **Margin is expanding while volume falls.** −1.6% total volume in FY2025 with +41bp of margin and +4.4% organic operating profit `[sourced]`. Price, mix and cost are all working.
- **The cost programme is real and quantified**: €400–500m of annual gross savings, tracking at *"the upper end"* `[claim]`, alongside a **5,000–6,000 role reduction over two years** `[sourced]`.
- **Emerging-market profit growth is exceptional** — Africa & Middle East +30.8% organic in H1 2026 `[sourced]`.
- **Capital returned:** first €750m buyback tranche completed 20 January 2026 with **10,572,215 shares cancelled** on 14 April 2026; second tranche **5,415,780 shares for €380.1m** through 31 July 2026 `[sourced]`. Dividend policy widened to **30–50%** of net profit beia `[sourced]`.
- **Free operating cash flow fell 15%** in FY2025 while operating profit beia rose 4.4% organically `[sourced]`. That divergence deserves an explanation the release does not give, and it is the same pattern the framework flagged on Alimak.
- **Leverage rising on acquisition.** 2.2x → 2.6x `[sourced]` after FIFCO closed 30 January 2026. Management calls FIFCO *"circa 2% to 3% accretive to EPS"* `[claim]` — **EPS accretion funded with debt is not evidence of value creation**, and on a 9.4% ROIC base, buying assets is precisely what has kept that number in single digits.
- **The CEO vacancy.** Announced departure effective **31 May 2026**, with press coverage at the time noting no successor lined up `[sourced]`. The H1 release names no permanent appointment. **A framework that weights capital allocation cannot be indifferent to who is making it.**
