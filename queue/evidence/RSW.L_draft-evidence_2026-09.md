**Source: Renishaw plc RNS "Shareholdings update", 26 November 2025** `[primary — company announcement]`.

| Holder | Stake |
|---|---|
| **Deltam Holdings Limited** (joint McMurtry/Deer family company) | ***"50.25% of the issued share capital of Renishaw"*** |
| Family members' separate personal holdings, outside Deltam's governance arrangements | *"2.64% of the issued shares"* |
| **Family bloc, combined** | **≈52.89%** `[computed]` |

**Inside Deltam:** McMurtry family interest **72.12%** (discretionary trusts for Richard McMurtry, Ben McMurtry and Yvette Pridmore in equal parts, originating from the David McMurtry Will Trust via Lady Teresa McMurtry's estate); Deer family interest **27.88%** (John Deer personally and jointly with Eileen Deer; Philip Deer via bare trust) `[primary]`.

**The internal voting compact, which is the part that actually matters:** Deer representatives **can require Deltam to vote against special resolutions**; McMurtry representatives **can require it to vote for ordinary resolutions**; where the Deltam board disagrees, Deltam votes at its discretion `[primary]`. **The UK Takeover Panel confirmed Deltam is not obliged to make a mandatory offer under Rule 9.1** `[primary]`.

**What this is, stated without a gate label:**

- **A single vehicle holds an absolute majority of the ordinary shares.** Ordinary resolutions cannot be carried against Deltam, and the McMurtry side can compel Deltam to carry them.
- **It is below the 75% special-resolution threshold** (52.89% including the personal holdings), and the Deer side holds an internal veto over special resolutions. **So minority holders retain a blocking position on special resolutions that the family bloc cannot override on its own** `[computed, from the 75% statutory threshold]`.
- **There is no dual-class structure in evidence: economic and voting interests appear aligned one-for-one.** This is materially different from the control blocks the register has flagged elsewhere (`NKE`'s dual-class caveat, the `KALMAR` class A/B question). **Worth stating plainly because "family control" and "voting control disproportionate to economics" are different facts, and only the second is the usual objection.**
- **Succession risk is now structurally reduced rather than increased.** The vehicle was created to hold the founders' estates together; the alternative — trusts selling into the market — was the live overhang before it.
