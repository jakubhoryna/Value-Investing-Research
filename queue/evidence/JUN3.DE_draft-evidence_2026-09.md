### Verdict: **TRACK — provisional. Gate 3 is unassessed, and the reason is a data gap, not a judgement.** — *sourced lines and tables only; verdict prose dropped*
**Gate 1 passes.** **Gate 2 is the interesting one and it lands in the same place as Diageo and Kardex did this run: the answer depends entirely on which numerator you use.** Jungheinrich's **reported ROCE for 2025 is 8.3%** `[sourced]`. **On adjusted EBIT the same capital base yields 16.3%** `[computed]`. **The gap is EUR 220m of one-off charges — 49% of adjusted EBIT** `[computed]`.
**What makes it a TRACK rather than a shelved file:** unlike Diageo's brand impairments, **Jungheinrich's one-offs are identifiable, dated and mostly genuinely non-recurring** — a Russian exit, a plant strike, a transformation programme. And unlike KION, **order intake is growing: +7.7% in H1 2026, with the automation segment up 22.5%** `[sourced]`.

### Gate 1 — Do I understand it?
**Pass, with one complication that is genuine and is not a presentational one.**

Jungheinrich makes and services electric forklifts and warehouse trucks and, increasingly, the automated systems around them. Two segments `[sourced, H1 2026]`:

| | Incoming orders | Revenue | EBIT | EBIT margin |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **ITS** — Industrial Trucks & Services | EUR 2,417m (**+4.9%**) | EUR 2,241m (−1.9%) | EUR 133.5m (PY 204.2m) | **6.0%** (PY 8.9%) |
| **AWE** — Automation & Warehouse Equipment | EUR 588m (**+22.5%**) | EUR 471m (+12.9%) | **−EUR 6.7m** (PY −7.6m) | **−1.4%** |

**The complication is the fourth revenue stream.** Fiscal 2025 revenue of EUR 5,502m splits into new business EUR 3,214m, short-term rental and used equipment EUR 775m, customer services EUR 1,576m and **financial services EUR 1,473m — 26.8% of group revenue** `[sourced/computed]`. **Jungheinrich runs a captive leasing book, and that changes what any return-on-capital number means.** See gate 2.

**Customer services of EUR 1,576m — 28.6% of revenue** `[computed]` — is the installed-base annuity that the Krones file spent three IR questions trying to size and could not. **Jungheinrich discloses it. That is a real disclosure advantage and it should be noted as such.**

### Gate 2 — Is it good? — *sourced lines and tables only; verdict prose dropped*
**The disclosed figure: ROCE 8.3% for fiscal 2025** `[sourced]`, against a 2026 forecast originally of **14.0–18.0%**, revised on 23 July 2026 to **12–16%** `[both sourced]`. **The ROCE definition is not disclosed in any release obtained** `[sourced absence — FY2025 release, Q1 2026 release and the July ad-hoc all checked]`.
**So the honest statement of gate 2 is a range, not a number: 8.3% reported, 16.3% adjusted, and EUR 220m of one-off charges in between.** The one-offs `[sourced]`:
| | FY2025 | H1 2026 |
|---|---|---|
| Sale of the Russian subsidiary | EUR 109m | EUR 20.5m |
| Transformation programme | EUR 93m | EUR 5.9m |
| Disposal of discontinued technology development | EUR 18m | — |
| Residual effects of the Lüneburg plant strike | — | EUR 7.4m |
| **Total** | **EUR 220m** | **EUR 33.8m** |
**These are more defensible as genuinely exceptional than Diageo's are.** A forced exit from Russia and a plant strike are external events; a brand impairment is a company marking its own past acquisition arithmetic. **But they are still running at EUR 33.8m a half — 18.9% of adjusted EBIT** `[computed]` — **and the transformation programme has no disclosed total cost or target saving** `[sourced absence]`, which is precisely the disclosure Diageo does provide and Jungheinrich does not.

#### The captive finance arm is a measurement problem, and it is a new one for this register — *sourced lines and tables only; verdict prose dropped*
- **Free cash flow guidance collapsed from "over EUR 250 million" to "more than EUR 50 million"** `[both sourced]` — an 80% cut in one statement. **H1 2026 free cash flow was −EUR 99m against +EUR 57m** `[sourced]`.
- **The dividend was cut about 65%** — ordinary EUR 0.78 → EUR 0.27, preference EUR 0.80 → EUR 0.29 `[sourced]` — while the payout ratio was held at **"28 per cent, as in the previous year"** `[sourced, verbatim]`. **A constant payout ratio on collapsed earnings is not a policy decision, it is arithmetic**, and it means the dividend carries no information about management's confidence.
- **Guidance was cut twice in five months** — the transformation programme forced a 2025 adjustment, and the July 2026 ad-hoc cut EBIT, margin, ROCE and free cash flow together. **Causes given: the Lüneburg strike, material costs, and *"a continuing highly competitive market environment"*** `[sourced, verbatim]`. **The third of those is the one that does not go away.**
- **Order intake is growing while the register's directly comparable name shrinks.** Jungheinrich H1 2026 orders **+7.7%**; KION's H1 2026 orders **−6.6%, Q2 −19.7%** `[sourced, from the KION file]`. **The same end market, opposite directions.**

### Gate 3 — Is it cheap? — *sourced lines and tables only; verdict prose dropped*
**The two-class structure is now settled, and it was the stated blocker on this name.** `[sourced — Jungheinrich investor relations]`
**The convention follows from the company's own disclosure, not from a choice:** Jungheinrich states that *"at the end of 2025, the market capitalization of Jungheinrich's shares totalled EUR 3,613 million"* `[sourced, verbatim]`. **EUR 3,613m ÷ 102,000,000 = EUR 35.42, which is a preference-share price** `[computed]`. **The company therefore values all 102m shares at the listed preference price, and the register should do the same: market capitalisation = 102,000,000 × the JUN3 price.**
**A session-dated price was obtained on a second attempt: EUR 26.62, stamped "Sep 4, 5:35:03 PM GMT+2", Xetra** `[sourced]`. Day range EUR 26.50–27.04; **52-week range EUR 21.72–38.16.** A first fetch of the same ticker returned EUR 25.52 stamped 14 May — **nearly four months stale, and the staleness was a URL artefact, not a data gap.**
**What still blocks gate 3 is the balance sheet, not the price.** Equity, total assets and the net credit or net debt position — including and excluding financial services — **were not disclosed in any of the four primary releases reached** `[sourced absence]`. **Without net debt there is no enterprise value, and with a captive leasing book at 26.8% of revenue the industrial/financial split is not optional.** **A market-capitalisation-to-EBIT ratio is not a substitute and none is offered here.**

### Open items and data gaps

- **ROCE definition undisclosed**; the EUR 2,747m capital-employed figure is inferred and labelled as such.
