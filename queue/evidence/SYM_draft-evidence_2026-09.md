**85% is not near the line; it is 15 points past it.** The rule fires in the DROP direction with a wide margin, and it would still fire if the disclosure were out by ten points.

**Two things worth adding, because they make the case stronger rather than weaker.**

1. **The backlog does not diversify the concentration the way its headline suggests.** GreenBox is $11.6bn of the $22.5bn — **52%** `[computed]` — and GreenBox is Symbotic's own joint venture rather than an arm's-length third-party customer. **Under the standing instruction to flag related-party revenue, the largest single component of the backlog is a related party, and the second is Walmart.** A PC session should confirm the JV's ownership and consolidation treatment from the same 10-K before the disposition is written, but the direction is not in doubt.
2. **A demand failure is not a business failure, and the disposition should say so.** `TODO-queue_v4` already frames it that way. The name reopens on customer diversification, which is reported quarterly.
