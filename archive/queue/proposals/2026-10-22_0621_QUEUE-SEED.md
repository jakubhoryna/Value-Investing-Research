## 7. Method notes

- The SEC `www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/{zero-padded CIK}/…` URLs answer HTTP 301 to the unpadded CIK path; curl needs `-L` to follow it. Without it the file saves empty.
- The price endpoint's `events=split` reports a spin-off price adjustment as a split (FTV, 2025-06-30). Treat any "split" dated at a known separation as an adjustment, and confirm the share count from the filing.
- SEC 10-Q cover pages give the latest share count in one grep ("shares of common stock outstanding at …"), the cheapest primary count for US decay checks.
