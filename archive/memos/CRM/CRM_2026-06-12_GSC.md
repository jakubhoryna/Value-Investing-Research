**Date:** 2026-06-12 (data through Q1 FY27, Apr 30, 2026; price as of Jun 11, 2026 close)
**Mode:** ANALYSE (v1) | **Framework:** Good Stocks Cheap (Marshall), project §1–§9

## Takeaway

**AVOID.** Salesforce fails two gates: **Past = WEAK** (ROCE_xc cleared 10% in only 2 of the last 10 fiscal years, 15% in none) and **Shareholder-friendliness = FAIL** (one strike: buybacks). At $166.45 the stock is also **EXPENSIVE** on GSC multiples despite sitting at a 52-week low, ~31% below a year ago.

**What the business is.** The world's largest CRM software vendor. Customer 360 — a subscription suite spanning sales, service, marketing, commerce, analytics (Tableau), integration (MuleSoft), Slack, and now AI agents (Agentforce) and data infrastructure (Data 360, Informatica) — sold to businesses of all sizes, ~65% Americas. FY26 revenue $41.5B (+10%), FY27 guide ~$46B, FY30 target $63B. Understanding: PASS.

**Why Past is WEAK.** GAAP operating profitability is a three-year phenomenon. For FY17–FY23, ROCE_xc ran 1.4–6.4% — a serial acquirer (Tableau $15.7B, Slack $27.7B) piling goodwill onto capital employed while GAAP operating margin hovered near zero. Only after the 2023 activist intervention did margins inflect: ROCE_xc 8.0% → 11.8% → 13.5% (FY24–FY26). The trend is genuinely strong, but the rubric is a level test over the trailing decade, and the decade fails. FCFROCE_xc passed ≥8% in all ten years (23.3% in FY26), but the OI→FCF wedge is structural and flattering: stock comp ~9% of revenue, deferred-revenue float, and acquired-intangible amortization. Earliest mechanical Past upgrade: ~FY2030 results (Feb 2030), if current profitability holds long enough for 6 of the trailing 10 years to clear 10%.

**Why SF fails: the March 2026 leveraged recap.** On March 11, 2026 Salesforce executed a **$25B accelerated share repurchase funded entirely by $25B of new senior notes** (eight tranches, ~5.36% weighted coupon, ~$1.34B/yr new interest) at a ~$195 reference price — roughly 11.5× FY26 FCF. Q1 FY27 equity collapsed from $59.1B to $34.2B; **L/E hit 211.6%**, above the framework's 200% flag; tangible book is now **−$31.7B**. This caps a pattern: FY23–FY26 the company spent $32.05B repurchasing stock at ~$160–290 (15–25× contemporaneous FCF, 2–3× a defensible ceiling) while the diluted share count fell only 18M — most of the spend offset stock-comp dilution. Cumulative buybacks now $59.3B. The May 2026 proxy added 34M shares to the equity plan. One strike on buyback discipline disqualifies; Benioff's $49.4M comp (80.7% say-on-pay support) and clean RPTs are context, not separate strikes.

**Future is ADEQUATE, not the problem.** Breadth passes (no ≥10% customer; suppliers are three substitutable hyperscalers plus own data centers and a multi-model AI stack — a marginal pass, unlike PYPL's unavoidable card networks). The moat — switching costs and system-of-record ingrainedness — is **stable in the installed base**: RPO $72.4B (+14%), and over half of Agentforce/Data 360 bookings come from existing customers (Agentforce ARR $1.2B, +205%). But the frontier is contested: AI-native substitutes and in-house builds (HIGH), Microsoft rivalry (HIGH), entrants (MED-HIGH). Forces tilt unfavorable; moat evidence holds the rating at ADEQUATE. (Pending "moat-decides" amendment not applied — not yet adopted.)

**Cheapness: EXPENSIVE.** At $166.45 (MCap $136.3B on 819M shares; EV $164.3B): MCap/FCF **9.5** (miss vs <8, within the 1.25× allowance), EV/OI **19.7** (clear miss vs <7), MCap/BV **3.98** (pass vs <5), MCap/TBV **n.m.** (negative TBV; §2 escape clause). The FCF lens alone would read near-FAIR — the divergence is the SBC/float wedge again. Net debt $28.0B ≈ 1.9× FY26 FCF; interest coverage ~4.2× OI.

**Trigger band: CHEAP below ≈$55** (binding constraint: EV/OI at the 1.25× allowance; verified at $55 all three live metrics pass). Threshold prices: 8×FCF → $140.68; 5×BV → $209; 7×OI via EV → $37; EV/OI @1.25× → $54.85. If one weighted FCF more than GAAP OI, the flip is ≈$141 — but the framework prices the OI lens, and the OI lens says the equity is nowhere near cheap.

**What would change the verdict.** (1) Past: FY30 earliest. (2) SF cure: visible deleveraging below the 200% flag, buyback discipline at defensible multiples (or suspension), no further debt-funded repurchases. (3) Price into the band. All three are required for BUY; (1)+(2) without (3) → WATCH. **Next review: FY27 10-K, ~March 2027** status check.

## Appendix A — Gate worksheets

### A1. Understanding (Gate 0): PASS
- **Product:** Customer 360 subscription suite — Sales Cloud, Service Cloud, Marketing & Commerce, Data 360 (fka Data Cloud) + Informatica, Tableau/analytics, MuleSoft integration, Slack, Agentforce AI agents, Platform.
- **Customer:** businesses of all sizes, all industries, worldwide; no customer ≥10% of revenue or receivables (FY26 10-K).
- **Geography:** ~65% Americas (~61% US), ~24% Europe, ~10% Asia-Pacific.
- **Status:** #1 worldwide CRM share (per IDC, cited in 10-K); FY26 revenue $41.525B (+10%); FY27 guide $45.9–46.2B (+~11%, ~3pts from Informatica); FY30 target $63B.
- **Recent structural events:** Informatica acquisition closed Nov 2025 (~$8B; $399M FY26 revenue; goodwill +$5.5B); Contentful acquisition announced Jun 1, 2026 (terms undisclosed); $25B debt-funded ASR Mar 11, 2026; ~$5B Anthropic stake within $7.6B strategic investments (Jun 2026 reports).

### A2. Past (Gate 1A): WEAK
- ROCE_xc ≥10% in **2 of 10** years (FY25, FY26); ≥15% in **0**. Rubric requires the level test over the trailing decade → WEAK.
- FCFROCE_xc ≥8% in **10 of 10** years. Wedge drivers: SBC ~9% of revenue ($3.51B FY26), deferred-revenue working-capital float, amortization of acquired intangibles. Both series shown in B2.
- Trend note (recorded, not rating-bearing): ROCE_xc FY23→FY26: 1.6% → 8.0% → 11.8% → 13.5%, post-activist margin program.

### A3. Future (Gate 1B): ADEQUATE

**Breadth:** PASS. No customer concentration (10-K). Supplier side: AWS/Azure/GCP hyperscalers — three substitutable vendors plus Salesforce's own first-party data centers; AI model supply is multi-vendor (incl. in-house). Judged a marginal pass; contrast PYPL (AVOID) where Visa/Mastercard are unavoidable, price-setting suppliers.

**Five forces:**
| Force | Rating | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Customer power | LOW-MED | Fragmented base, no ≥10% customer; multi-year subscriptions; some seat-count pressure in macro softness |
| Supplier power | MED | Hyperscaler compute, but 3 substitutable vendors + own DCs + multi-model AI |
| Substitutes | HIGH | AI-native CRM startups; in-house agent builds on foundation models; per-seat → consumption-pricing risk |
| New entrants | MED-HIGH | Foundation-model layer lowers app-build barriers; distribution/data gravity still costly |
| Rivalry | HIGH | Microsoft (Dynamics + Copilot bundle), ServiceNow, HubSpot at low end |

**Moat:** switching costs + system-of-record ingrainedness (data gravity, AppExchange ecosystem, Trailhead skills base). **Stable in installed base:** RPO $72.4B (+14% Y/Y at Q1 FY27); >50–60% of Agentforce/Data 360 bookings from existing customers; Agentforce ARR $1.2B (+205%), Agentforce+Data 360 combined ARR $3.4B. Contested at the frontier (new-logo, AI-native competition). Market growing. Forces tilt unfavorable; segment-level moat evidence (TROW precedent: ground rating in disaggregated durability, not headline mix) holds Future at **ADEQUATE**. Pending §2 "moat-decides" rule not applied.

### A4. Shareholder-friendliness (Gate 1C): FAIL — one strike (buybacks)

Worst-parameter-wins. **Strike: buyback discipline.**
- FY23–FY26 repurchases $32.05B at ~$160–290/sh. Q4 FY26 monthly averages: $237.71 / $256.60 / $234.94 — i.e., 15–25× contemporaneous FCF vs ~8× FCF conservative ceiling.
- Net effect: diluted shares FY22→FY26 fell only ~18M on ~$32B spent — spend largely offset SBC dilution rather than shrinking the equity base.
- **Mar 11, 2026: $25B ASR funded by $25B new senior notes** (8 tranches, 4.50–6.70%, wtd ~5.36%, ~$1.34B/yr interest), under a $50B authorization (Feb 2026). Initial delivery ~103M shares at ~$195 reference (~11.5× FY26 FCF); final settlement Q3 FY27. Shares out: 923M (Feb 25) → 819M (May 21). Equity $59.1B → $34.2B; **L/E 211.6%** (>200% flag); TBV −$31.7B. Dividend simultaneously raised to $0.44/q.
- Equity plan expanded +34M shares (May 2026 proxy).
- Cumulative buybacks since inception: $59.29B.

Context (pass, not strikes): Benioff comp FY26 $49.4M (FY25 $55.1M; FY27 target ~$48M, 100% performance equity, options struck $195.38 on 3/20/26); say-on-pay 80.7% For (493.9M/117.7M, May 28, 2026 AGM); insider ownership Benioff 2.8% (22.79M sh), D&O 3.5%; single share class; RPTs minimal and disclosed (TIME-related $450K to CRM; $1.11M aircraft reimbursement to Benioff). Serial-M&A pattern (Tableau $15.7B, Slack $27.7B, Informatica ~$8B, Contentful pending) recorded as capital-allocation context.

## Appendix C — Judgment calls

1. **FY17 ASC 606 restatement** used for the revenue/OI series (comparability across the window).
2. **FY17 equity = $7.500B derived** from the balance-sheet identity; a stray XBRL StockholdersEquity value (8.23) failed A = L + E and was rejected.
3. **NIBCL convention:** current liabilities − short-term debt − current finance **and operating** lease liabilities; i.e., leases treated as interest-bearing financing, excluded from NIBCL. Consistent with prior memos.
4. **Operating leases excluded from EV debt** (~$2.6B); finance leases included. Q1 FY27 finance-lease balance estimated at $0.535B (latest disclosed split).
5. **Capitalized contract costs left in TBV** (not treated as an intangible to strip).
6. **Share count 819M** (May 21, 2026 cover count) used for MCap and trigger band. ASR final settlement (Q3 FY27) will deliver further shares; fully-settled count likely ~795–800M — thresholds will shift slightly upward on settlement.
7. **MCap/TBV excluded** under the §2 escape clause (buyback-driven negative TBV), as with prior negative-TBV cases.
8. **Pending "moat-decides" §2 amendment not applied** (not yet adopted by Jakub); Future rated under current rules.
