CATL is the world's largest battery maker and has grown operating income per share by about a third a year. The v1 memo rested on an aggregator series and an unresolved customer table; this version rebuilds ten years from the audited annual reports and the prospectus. The customer question is now answered: the largest customer took 13.7% of FY2025 revenue (15.0% in FY2024). That is flagged, but contained, so the Future grade is ADEQUATE. The Past now decides. The company funds part of its supply chain with bank acceptance bills (¥103bn at FY2025) backed by pledged deposits. ## 2. Understanding

CATL manufactures lithium-ion batteries: EV power-battery systems (75% of FY2025 revenue, 541 GWh) and grid-storage systems (121 GWh), plus battery materials, recycling and mining. Its paying customers are global vehicle makers and storage integrators. It is a vertically integrating manufacturer, headquartered in Ningde, Fujian, producing mainly in China with plants in Germany, Hungary and Indonesia under construction or ramping. Founded in 2011, listed on ChiNext in 2018 and in Hong Kong in 2025, it has been the industry share leader for nine years. **PASS.** Lens STD. One operating segment (AR2025 segment note), so no group rules apply.
