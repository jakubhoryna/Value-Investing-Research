## 2. Understanding

HelloFresh sells subscription meal kits (69% of H1 2026 revenue, sourced) and ready-to-eat meals under Factor and Youfoodz (28%) direct-to-consumer, chiefly in North America (62% of FY2025 revenue) and Europe/ANZ, earning a gross-margin spread over grocery through forecast-driven procurement and owned fulfilment, and spending it on marketing to replace churning subscribers. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth passes: millions of consumers, broad ingredient and packaging supply. Forces: customer power WEAK, supplier power WEAK, substitutes STRONG, new entrants STRONG — two strong, the stop signal. Tenured-customer order rates and AOV (+6.5% cc in Q2) are improving, but on a shrinking base. Moat eroding; meal-kit market shrinking, ready-to-eat contested. **WEAK.**
