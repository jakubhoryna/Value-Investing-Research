Group: two material reportable units (FY2025 segments), each one sentence. North America: meal kits and ready-to-eat meals sold direct to US and Canadian consumers. International: the same products sold direct to consumers in Europe, Australia and New Zealand. Neither unit is irreducible: meal kits and ready-to-eat share the paying customer and the filer's own description, "direct-to-consumer food solutions".
