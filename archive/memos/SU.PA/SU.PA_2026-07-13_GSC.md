# SU.PA — Schneider Electric SE: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | SF | Cheapness | FINAL |

| PASS | ADEQUATE | STRONG | **FAIL** (P19 hard strikes) | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** |

The global leader in low/medium-voltage distribution and data-center power — ingrained channel moat, zero strong forces, a decade of improving returns — attached to a capital-allocation record the framework cannot excuse. The AVEVA minority buyout (Jan 2023, ≈ €11.6bn implied equity for a statutory-breakeven business), the SEIPL buy-in (Dec 2025, €5.5bn for 35%), and FY2025 buybacks at 28× MCap/FCF that merely sterilise plan dilution each independently breach P19's hard lines. ## 2. Understanding

Schneider Electric sells differentiated electrical-distribution hardware (breakers, switchgear, UPS, cooling), industrial-automation equipment, and connected software/services (EcoStruxure, AVEVA), mostly through electrical distributors (~45% of sales) to building, data-center, industrial, and infrastructure customers worldwide (N. America 36%, Asia-Pac 27%, W. Europe 24%); a French SE, ~190 years old, €40bn revenue, #1–2 in its core markets, its AVEVA and Indian minorities now bought in. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Worst-of-four normalized 10y ROCE **10.4%** — clears P3's 10% line, short of P2's 15%. Worst-of-four FCFROCE **7.7%**, below the 8% STRONG bar. The trend redeems: single-year ROCE_wc rose 8.3% (2016) → 13.5% (2025), no sustained decline. Per-share growth clears the 3% floor easily: ΔOI/sh +11.7%, ΔFCF/sh +9.1% (10y geo-mean). BV/sh +1.6%/yr, read against a rising dividend (€2.00 → €4.20). TBV negative throughout (acquired goodwill+intangibles €31.1bn vs parent equity €24.2bn) — triggers the Gate-1C acquisition audit. L/E 157%, within P5. **ADEQUATE** — returns one tier below the moat's implied economics, the residue of paying up for acquisitions.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASS both sides: filings state no customer exceeds 10% of revenue; suppliers broad, electronics multi-sourced. Forces: customer power WEAK (fragmented, distributor-mediated, spec-in switching costs) · supplier power WEAK · substitutes WEAK (no functional substitute for electrical distribution; electrification is the tailwind) · new entrants WEAK (installed base, certification regimes, channel ingrainedness). Tally 0 strong. Moat: **ingrainedness** — Schneider is the default spec for electricians, panel builders, and now data-center architects — stable-to-strengthening. Market growth YES (electrification, AI data centers; backlog €25.4bn, +18%). **STRONG.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness
