# ANDR.VI — ANDRITZ AG: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding **FAIL** (one-level decomposition: two material units each combine sub-businesses with different paying customers, shares not given) → **DO NOT ANALYSE FURTHER**. Past, Future, shareholder-friendliness and cheapness were not run.

ANDRITZ is a plain business to describe at the top: it builds and services process equipment for pulp and paper mills, hydropower stations, metals plants and environmental and energy processes. The problem sits one level down. Its Metals segment, 21.5% of FY2025 revenue, is run as two named sectors: Metals Processing, which sells strip-processing lines and furnaces to steel and aluminium producers, and Metals Forming (Schuler), which sells presses mainly to car makers. The filer reports their trends separately and tests their goodwill as separate units, but gives no revenue share for either. Environment & Energy, 19.1% of revenue, is likewise four named divisions (Separation, Pumps, Clean Air Technologies, Feed & Biofuel) serving what the filer calls "various industries", again without shares. MODEL v4.5.x fails Gate 0 for such a unit. The DNAF is a parameter-driven result, not a finding of incomprehension or obfuscation. It flips back to a full analysis if the filer discloses shares that put one side under the line, or re-segments.

## 2. Understanding
**Group sentence.** ANDRITZ designs, builds and services process plants and equipment: pulp mills, paper, tissue and board machines and their service (Pulp & Paper); turbines, generators and pumped-storage equipment (Hydropower); metal-forming presses and strip-processing lines (Metals); and separation, pumping, air-emission, feed and biofuel equipment (Environment & Energy). Its paying customers are pulp and paper producers, utilities and state power companies, steel and aluminium producers, car makers and suppliers, and municipal and industrial plant owners. It manufactures in Europe, the Americas and Asia and sells worldwide (Europe 31% and North America 27% of FY2025 revenue). It is a long-established Austrian group headquartered in Graz, anchored by its chairman's holding company.

**Gate 0 test.** Pulp & Paper and Hydropower can each be described in one six-parameter sentence: the first sells to pulp, paper and board producers (its three sectors, Service, Paper & Textile and Pulp & Power, all serve them), the second to utilities and power-station owners. Metals and Environment & Energy cannot:
- **Metals.** The filer names two sectors, Metals Processing and Metals Forming (Schuler), and explains their results apart: both "were confronted with lower investment activity by steel and automotive producers" (FY2025), Processing's margin was flat while Forming's improved, and the August 2026 investor presentation reads "improving demand in the steel industry" against "challenges in automotive sector". Each is a separately managed operation with its own goodwill unit (Metals: Forming €229.9m, Metals: Processing €45.5m, Metals: Sanzheng €91.2m at 31 Dec 2025). The paying customers differ (steel and aluminium producers against car makers and suppliers). The filer gives no revenue share for either, and under J13 that alone makes the unit irreducible; Schuler's size makes both sides far larger than 10% on any reading, but this memo rests on the rule, not on that inference.
- **Environment & Energy.** The filer names four divisions (Separation, Pumps, Clean Air Technologies, Feed & Biofuel) and reports their order trends apart, with no revenue shares. Its own segment description says the unit serves "various industries", and the paying customers differ: feed mills and biomass pelletisers, mining, chemical and municipal wastewater operators, and power and industrial plants buying emission control. Irreducible on the same rule.

Either unit alone fails Gate 0. **FAIL → DNAF.**

The lens test (STD on the evidence: no bank, insurer or finance arm) and the group dependency check (no listed subsidiary or listed holding; the Smart Press Shop joint venture is unlisted and equity-accounted) were not needed for the verdict.
