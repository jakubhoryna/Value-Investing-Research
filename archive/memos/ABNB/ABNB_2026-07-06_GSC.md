# ABNB — Airbnb, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |

This REVIEW is the Tier-1 P17/P19 re-examination of the v1 (2026-06-11) SF verdict, plus a band recompute on v3 mechanics. The business case is unchanged — a float-funded two-sided network converting ~38% of revenue to FCF on negative ex-cash capital — but the capital-allocation record now fails the framework outright: every buyback year 2022–2025 was executed at implied EV/OI of 27–49×, above the 20× hard-strike line in each of the four years. Under §2·1C one hard strike is an SF FAIL; the verdict moves WATCH → AVOID on the SF gate. The flip back requires a demonstrated change in repurchase discipline, not a price move.

## 2. Understanding

Unchanged from v1. Airbnb, Inc. is a Delaware-incorporated, San Francisco-headquartered online marketplace — the category-defining leader, still growing ~10%/yr — connecting 5M+ hosts offering short-term and longer-stay accommodations, experiences, and services with guests across 220+ countries, earning service fees paid by both guests and hosts on each booking, with revenue diversified across North America, EMEA, Latin America, and Asia-Pacific. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Unchanged; no new fiscal year (FY2025 remains latest; next annual data ~Feb 2027). Six computable years (IPO Dec 2020): ROCE_wc 19–24% since 2022, FCFROCE_wc 42–47%, ex-cash CE negative-to-negligible (float-funded), L/E 149–188% and mostly customer float. **ADEQUATE** — STRONG-grade returns, but the short window and pre-2021 losses preclude STRONG under P2.

## 4. Quality — future

Unchanged. Breadth PASS both sides (atomised guests and 5M+ hosts). Forces on the v1 read: customer power WEAK, supplier power WEAK, substitutes STRONG (hotels, Vrbo, direct booking), new entrants WEAK-to-contained behind two-sided liquidity — one strong force, contained. Moat: Network reinforced by Brand (~90% direct/unpaid traffic), stable overall, eroding at the edges in regulated cities. Market growth YES — which is exactly what keeps Booking's capital pointed here. **ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Re-tested under v3 (P17 two-tier, P19). **P17 passes cleanly:** CEO comp $242K vs the 5%-of-lower(FCF, NI) ceiling and the ≈$40m indexed absolute trigger — nowhere close; founders hold ~30% economically. **P19 hard strike:** program repurchases of $1.5B/$2.3B/$3.4B/$3.8B (2022–25) were executed at average prices of $108.70/$125.11/$137.92/$126.27, implying EV/OI at execution of **36.8× / 49.2× / 31.5× / 27.4×** — every year above the 20× hard-strike line (2022–23 also breach 20× on MCap/FCF). Repurchases ran 1.7–2.4× SBC and shrank the diluted count 8.4%, so the sterilisation limb alone doesn't strike — but the framework's own conservative worth is ≈$43–59/sh, and $126–138 is no discount to it. The v1 dual-class flag (20-vote Class B, founder voting agreements, Chesky 31.9% of votes) stands but is no longer load-bearing. **FAIL — P19 hard strike (buybacks > 20× EV/OI in all four program years).**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Capital-allocation risk is now realised, not prospective: ~$11B deployed at 27–49× EV/OI is value transferred from continuing holders.
- Booking Holdings' alternative-accommodations push erodes the network moat from the demand side.
- Regulatory contagion: more NYC-style bans in top urban markets shrink supply where it matters most.
- SBC of $1.6B/yr (13% of revenue) makes reported FCF flatter than owner reality.
- Margin compression as Experiences/Services scale at uncertain returns.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

To WATCH: a changed repurchase pattern — buybacks suspended at rich prices, or executed only at demonstrably discounted multiples — evidenced across at least a full program year (FY2026 10-K, ~Feb 2027), with the dual-class flag still logged. To BUY from there: price below ≈ $43. Past/Future re-tests ride the same review. Next review: FY2026 10-K + 2027 proxy; a major-market regulatory ruling accelerates it.
