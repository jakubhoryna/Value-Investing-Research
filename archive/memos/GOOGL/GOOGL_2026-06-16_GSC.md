# GOOGL — Alphabet Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | STRONG | ADEQUATE | PASS (caveat) | EXPENSIVE | **WATCH** |

Alphabet is one of the highest-quality businesses in existence: a dominant, multi-moat franchise earning 37–62% returns on ex-cash capital every year for over a decade, with operating income up ~8× and a fortress balance sheet. It is worth owning for the durability and reinvestment runway of Search, YouTube, Android and a now-profitable, fast-growing Cloud. What stands between here and a BUY is price alone — the market caps the business at ~60× free cash flow and ~33× EV/operating income, multiples 7–12× the framework's ceilings. This is a WATCH, not a BUY: a wonderful company at an unforgiving price. The verdict flips to BUY only on a severe, broad market dislocation (an ~80%+ price decline toward the trigger band) — or, less likely, if a structural break (adverse antitrust remedy, or generative AI genuinely cannibalising search monetisation) impairs the franchise, which would instead push the verdict toward AVOID.

## 2. Understanding

Alphabet is a US-domiciled holding company whose subsidiary Google sells digital advertising (Search, YouTube, Network — the large majority of revenue) to millions of advertisers worldwide, alongside cloud-computing services to enterprises, plus hardware, app-store and subscription revenue from consumers, operating as the global leader in search and online video and the #3 cloud provider, growing, under a founder-controlled dual-class structure. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Exceptional. ROCE (ex-cash) ran 37–62% in all 11 measured years; ROCE (incl. cash) 16–36%; FCFROCE (ex-cash) 27–53% — every level far above the STRONG bar. Revenue rose 6.1×, operating income 7.8× and FCF 6.1× over the decade, with diluted shares ~10% *lower* than 2021, so per-share growth exceeds these. Leverage is negligible (total-liabilities/equity 20–44%, well inside the 200% flag; the business sits in a large net-cash position). A single soft year (2022 ad slowdown) interrupts nothing. **Verdict: STRONG** — returns and growth comfortably clear P2.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASSES: advertisers are massively fragmented (no 10% customer); input/creator bases are broad, though distribution leans on Apple and advanced-chip foundries. Moat is wide and multi-source — network effects (Search, YouTube), scale/cost (compute, data, custom TPUs), brand ("to google"), and switching/ingrainedness (Android, Chrome, Workspace) — and on the FY2023–25 evidence it is holding or strengthening even as AI arrives. Forces are MIXED, not favourable: generative-AI "answer engines" are the first credible *substitute* to the search-ad model in 20 years, with well-funded new entrants and intense cloud/AI rivalry. Market growth: YES. **Verdict: ADEQUATE** (high, borderline STRONG) — capped only because the AI substitution threat keeps forces MIXED; upgrades to STRONG if search monetisation keeps proving resilient.

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

CEO pay (~$11M FY2024; a higher equity-grant year otherwise) is trivial against $129B operating income and equity-weighted. Founders hold ~$270B of stock — enormous alignment. Buybacks (~$60–70B/yr) exceed stock comp, so they genuinely shrink the count rather than sterilise dilution, at non-egregious prices; a modest dividend (initiated 2024) sits alongside high-return reinvestment — rational allocation. Related-party dealings are immaterial. **Verdict: PASS — caveat:** Page + Brin control ~52.7% of votes via 10-vote Class B shares; outside holders (GOOGL = 1 vote, GOOG = 0) have negligible governance recourse. Flagged, not disqualifying, given alignment and clean stewardship.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- **AI substitution:** generative chat/answer engines erode Search query volume or per-query monetisation — the central structural risk.
- **Antitrust:** the US v. Google search-monopoly remedies (and EU/ad-tech actions) could force behavioural or structural changes to default-search economics.
- **Capex/AI returns:** the multi-year datacenter build (capex ~3.6× in two years) must earn its cost of capital; sustained FCF compression if it doesn't.
- **Governance:** super-voting founders mean no external check if stewardship ever deteriorates or succession falters.
- **Valuation/sentiment:** the AI-leader re-rating leaves little margin of safety; multiple compression alone could halve the price.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

- **→ BUY:** a broad market crash dragging GOOGL toward ~$60–80 (EV/OI band); a full REVIEW required before any action, as the band ages with fundamentals.
- **→ AVOID:** a Future downgrade to WEAK (search monetisation demonstrably cannibalised, or an adverse structural antitrust remedy) or an SF strike.
- **→ Future STRONG:** continued search-revenue resilience plus Gemini share gains through FY2026–27.
- **Next review:** FY2026 10-K (~Feb 2027); a US v. Google remedy ruling; or price < ~$120.

## D. Future-performance worksheet

**Breadth.** Customers: advertisers number in the millions, fully fragmented; no customer ≈ 10% of revenue; consolidation implausible → **YES.** Suppliers/inputs: YouTube creators and ad partners broad; notable dependencies on Apple (search-default distribution) and advanced-chip foundry (TSMC/Nvidia), though custom TPUs cut this — Alphabet holds the leverage → **YES (with flags).** Breadth **PASS.**

**Five forces.**

| Force | Rating | One-line reason |
|---|---|---|
| Customer power | LOW | Fragmented advertisers; unmatched intent reach |
| Supplier power | MEDIUM | Apple distribution + concentrated advanced-chip supply |
| Substitutes | MEDIUM-HIGH | Generative-AI answer engines; retail→Amazon; social→TikTok/Meta |
| New entrants | MEDIUM | Core infra near-impossible to replicate; AI "answer" layer contestable |
| Rivalry | MEDIUM | Intense in Cloud & AI; historically low in Search |

Overall: **MIXED** (historically favourable; AI shifts it).

**Moat.** Network effects + scale/cost (TPUs, data) + brand + switching/ingrainedness — four reinforcing sources. Mechanism: scale and data compound result quality and ad monetisation while ecosystem embedment (Android/Chrome/Workspace) raises switching costs. Direction: **stable-to-strengthening** on the FY2023–25 record (search revenue grew through the ChatGPT era; AI integrated via Overviews/Gemini), with genuine but as-yet-unrealised substitution risk.

**Market.** **YES** — digital ads still growing; Cloud growing fast off a large backlog and now profitable; AI a new vector. Marshall's caveat applied: growth is supporting evidence, the moat is the basis.
