# ROCK-B.CO — ROCKWOOL A/S: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding **FAIL** (one-level decomposition: the material Systems unit is irreducible) → **DO NOT ANALYSE FURTHER**. Past, Future, shareholder-friendliness and cheapness were not graded.

Rockwool is easy to describe in its main business: the Insulation segment melts stone into non-combustible insulation sold to builders' merchants, distributors and contractors. The difficulty is the second segment. The filer says Systems "comprises the three business units Rockfon, Grodan and Rockpanel". Rockfon sells acoustic ceilings and walls, and Rockpanel façade boards, to the construction trade. Grodan, "the second largest business unit in the Systems segment", sells stone-wool growing media, sensors and software to vegetable and cannabis growers. These are different industries with different payers, and the filer prints no revenue split between them. Systems is 17% of revenue and 14–15% of segment profit, above the materiality line, so under MODEL v4.5.0 the unit is irreducible and Gate 0 fails. This is a parameter-driven result on a long-standing segment structure, not incomprehension of the business. It flips back to a full analysis if Rockwool shows Grodan below 10% of Systems, re-segments, or disposes of Grodan, or if a MODEL ruling changes how such a segment is tested.

## 2. Understanding
**Group sentence.** ROCKWOOL A/S, a Danish manufacturer founded in 1937 and controlled through 10-vote A shares by the ROCKWOOL Foundation (23% of capital, 31% of votes) and the Kähler family, melts basalt and recycled stone in regional factories into stone wool, which it sells as non-combustible building insulation, acoustic ceilings, façade boards and horticultural growing media to builders' merchants, distributors, contractors and commercial growers, mostly in Western Europe (60% of H1 2026 revenue), Eastern Europe and North America; it lost control of its four Russian factories on 13 January 2026.

Segment EBIT sums to EBIT before the Russian value adjustment (488 + 82 = 570; FY2024 restated 575 + 102 = 677; FY2023 431 + 87 = 518, the last from the quarterly segment tables of the Annual Report 2023 on the pre-2025 split). The EUR 392m loss from the value adjustment of the Russian business is not allocated to a segment.

**Gate 0 test.** Insulation can be described in one six-parameter sentence. Systems cannot:
- **Its sub-businesses are separately managed operations.** The filer names three business units in the segment and reports on each (Rockfon, Grodan, Rockpanel, AR 2025 pp. 20–21), without printing their revenue shares.
- **They differ in industry and paying customer.** Rockfon "makes acoustic systems for ceilings and walls" and Rockpanel "boards for façade cladding", both paid for by the construction trade (distributors, contractors). Grodan "makes soilless growing media solutions (stone wool substrates, sensors, software and services) designed for growing food and cannabis more efficiently in controlled environments", paid for by greenhouse and indoor growers; its 2025 revenue moved with "declines in the cannabis market", not with construction. The filer's own segment description lists "acoustic ceilings and walls, growing media solutions, cladding boards, rainwater management, and prefab building systems" — building products and horticulture.
- **The shares are not printed, and the harsher reading governs.** Where the filer names such sub-businesses without their shares, the unit is irreducible (J13). Grodan is "the second largest" of three named units, so it is shown above 10% of Systems unless Rockfon alone exceeded 80% of the unit, which nothing in the filings supports.
- **No exception applies.** These are not two sides of one marketplace, nor one product sold through different channels; stone wool is the shared input, not the product the payer buys.

Systems is above the P22 line on revenue and on segment results, so it is a material irreducible unit. **FAIL → DNAF.** The lens (STD on the evidence: no bank, insurer or finance part) and the group dependency check (no listed subsidiary or listed holding) were not needed for the verdict.
