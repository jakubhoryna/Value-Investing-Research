# SPOT — Spotify Technology S.A.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | WEAK | WEAK | FAIL (P19 hard strikes) | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** |

A great product, profitable since 2024 — and a triple-gate fail; any one gate alone carries the verdict. Seven loss years dominate the decade: worst-of-four normalized ROCE is 4.3% against the 10% floor, and no price fixes a record. The future gate fails structurally — three major labels plus Merlin supply ~72% of label streams and set royalty economics on a cost line that is 68% of revenue: supplier-breadth FAIL plus two strong forces. And every buyback tranche in company history breaches the P19 20× hard line — FY2025's €439M at a €571.36 average lands at 51.3× EV/OI and 41.9× MCap/FCF in the record year, ~5× the worth line, and the stock now trades 26% below that execution price. A flip needs a multi-year profitable record (~2029 earliest), a structural break in label power, and repriced buyback discipline — three independent improbabilities, none price-fixable.

## 2. Understanding

Spotify is the world's largest audio-streaming platform (music, podcasts, audiobooks — a commoditised licensed catalog differentiated by personalization and ubiquity), paid by 290M consumer Premium subscribers (89% of FY2025 revenue) and by advertisers on the free tier (11%), in the audio-streaming industry, as a Luxembourg-incorporated, founder-voting-controlled holding company (NYSE direct listing 2018; Ek + Lorentzon 69.3% of votes via non-economic beneficiary certificates) whose input costs are set by three major labels plus Merlin — 751M MAUs, 184 markets, a prominent, 20-year-old, newly profitable €89bn large-cap. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Nine balance-sheet years (FY2017–25; FY2016 balance sheet unsourced — flag). Worst-of-four normalized ROCE **4.3%** (end, with-cash) versus the 10% floor; the ex-cash variants (27.8–39.9%) merely show CE is mostly cash and float. Worst FCFROCE 17.3% passes 8% — deferred revenue and payables fund the model — but P3 turns on ROCE, and seven of ten years posted operating losses. ΔFCF/sh +44.4%/yr geometric, but off a €0.50/sh base and at scale only since 2023; ΔBV/sh (+49.7%) issuance-inflated, unhelpful. L/E 80% passes. P18 lease capitalisation (2017–18) leaves the average unchanged. **WEAK** — the returns level decides.

## 4. Quality — future

Customer breadth PASS (751M consumers; diversified advertisers). Supplier breadth **FAIL**: UMG, Sony, Warner and Merlin supply ~72% of label streams against a €11.7bn royalty-dominated cost line — each major individually clears 10% of COGS+opex, catalogs are must-have, no ready alternatives. Forces: customer power WEAK; **supplier power STRONG** (few must-have licensors, non-renewable multi-year deals); **substitutes STRONG** (identical catalogs at Apple/Amazon/YouTube, zero-price YouTube, playlist-migration tools cap switching costs); new entrants WEAK. Tally 2 strong → stop signal. Moat: switching costs (taste profile, playlists, ubiquity) — real, stable, not dominant; margin capture structurally shared with labels — no floor case. Market growth YES, attracting exactly the bundlers above. **WEAK** (breadth FAIL and force tally, independently).
