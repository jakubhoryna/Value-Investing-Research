# GEBN.SW — Geberit AG: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | ADEQUATE | STRONG | **FAIL** | EXPENSIVE | **AVOID** |

Geberit is a fortress-quality business — a ~26% worst-of-four ROCE, ~22% FCFROCE, a dominant plumber-ingrainedness moat, and gushing free cashflow — that the model nonetheless rejects on capital allocation alone. Management systematically repurchases its own shares for cancellation at 22–26× operating income and 24–26× free cashflow, versus the framework's 20× P19 hard-strike line and 7× conservative-worth ceiling. That is a single hard strike, so Shareholder-friendliness FAILs and the verdict is AVOID regardless of the excellent gates above it. Absent the buyback pricing, Geberit would be a STRONG-future WATCH name — but it also trades at ~24× FCF today, ~275% above the trigger band, so even a disciplined buyback policy would leave it far out of range. What would flip it to WATCH: a full program year of below-worth (or halted) repurchases. Nothing flips it to BUY near today's price.

## 2. Understanding

Geberit develops, manufactures and distributes behind-the-wall sanitary systems (installation/flushing systems, cisterns, supply and drainage piping) and bathroom-ceramics/systems, sold mainly to sanitary wholesalers and installed by plumbers across Europe (with a smaller ceramics-led international tail), by a vertically integrated Swiss-HQ'd manufacturer that is Europe's clear category leader. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Returns are STRONG-caliber: worst-of-four normalized-average ROCE ~25.9% and FCFROCE ~22.4% (both cash variants 24–34% and 20–26% respectively), stable across the window, on a modestly levered balance sheet (L/E 154%, below the 200% flag). What caps it is growth: per-share operating income and FCF are flat-to-negative — geo-mean ≈ −2.1%/yr and −2.3%/yr off an anomalous 2021 COVID-renovation peak, with revenue essentially flat at CHF ~3.1bn since 2018 and OI parked at CHF ~755–770m for four straight years. That fails P2's 3% nominal growth floor, so STRONG is unavailable. **ADEQUATE** — outstanding returns, mature/flat top line. (5-year aggregator window; the growth-clause cap is robust to a longer history.)

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth PASS: customers are fragmented wholesalers/plumbers (no single >10% of revenue); inputs (polymers, metals, ceramics feedstock) are commodity with many vendors. Four forces all WEAK — customer power (fragmented channel, brand pull-through), supplier power (commodity inputs), substitutes (competing systems blunted by plumber training and switching costs), new entrants (potable-water approvals, installed base, ingrainedness) — 0 strong = favourable. Moat: **ingrainedness** (plumber preference for the behind-the-wall standard), reinforced by brand and switching costs; dominant and stable. Market growth MIXED (mature, cyclical European construction/renovation). **STRONG.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Compensation PASS: highest-paid (CEO Buhl) ≈ CHF 4.4m (US$4.95m) — ~0.7% of the lower of FCF/NI (CHF 597.8m → 5% line CHF 29.9m) and far under the ~CHF 40m absolute; board fees within Swiss norms; broad employee ownership. RPT clean (no GEB loans). Dividends appropriate (71% payout, mature industry). **Buybacks — P19 HARD STRIKE:** every program repurchased shares for cancellation at 22–26× EV/OI and 24–26× MCap/FCF — 2024–26 program's 2025 tranche (145k sh, CHF 81m, avg CHF 559) ≈ 25.1× / 26.4×; 2022–24 (1.27m sh, CHF 600m, avg CHF 474) ≈ 22.0× / 24.1×; 2020–22 (avg CHF 605) ≈ 24.3× / 26.0×. All breach the 20× hard line on both bases, at no discount to worth. **FAIL (P19).**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- The AVOID is a capital-allocation verdict, not a quality one — the business itself is among the best European industrials in the register.
- End market is mature and cyclical; a European construction downturn compresses OI further (2022–24 already did), extending the distance to any band.
- Buyback discipline is unlikely to change while the balance sheet is strong and the stock stays richly valued — the strike is structural to Geberit's distribution policy.
- FX: a strong CHF chronically drags reported growth (offsetting the local-currency operating story).
- Substitute risk is low but non-zero (Viega/Uponor/REHAU systems); the moat is stable, not strengthening.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

To WATCH: a full program year of repurchases at or below conservative worth (or a halted buyback), clearing the P19 strike — then the name is a STRONG-future WATCH, still gated on price. To BUY: that plus price into the ≈ CHF 139 band (≈ −73%), effectively a severe cyclical dislocation. Next review: **FY2026 results (~mid-Mar 2027)** to re-test the buyback multiple and reset OI/FCF; earlier only on a price alert < ≈ CHF 200 or a change in distribution policy.
