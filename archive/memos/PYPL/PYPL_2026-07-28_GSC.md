# PYPL — PayPal Holdings, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Understanding | Past | Future | Shareholder-friendliness | Cheapness | FINAL |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PASS | **ADEQUATE** (was STRONG) | **WEAK** | **FAIL** (was PASS) | FAIR | **AVOID** |

Three things moved since v1, and two of them are corrections rather than news. Re-run on v3.6.0 mechanics, the past record is ADEQUATE, not STRONG: the rubric tests the **worst of four** normalized averages, and PayPal's worst ROCE read is 13.2% (with-cash, end-of-period) against the 15% STRONG line — v1 graded on the ex-cash leg alone. Shareholder-friendliness fails outright: the P19 dual-read buyback worksheet, never run in v1, produces **hard strikes in seven consecutive years (FY2016–FY2022)**, topped by FY2021 repurchases at 62× EV/OI and 55× MCap/FCF on the tranche year. The v3.5.0 redemption clause does not rescue them — allocator turnover is satisfied, but there is no published worth-referenced repurchase criterion (the 10-K states the programmes exist to offset equity-compensation dilution and make "opportunistic" purchases) and no programme year executed at or below the bargain lines. Only the third change is news: Q2'26, reported this morning, has management claiming branded checkout "further stabilized," while transaction-margin dollars grew 1%, GAAP operating income fell 5%, and active accounts declined sequentially. That is not the four-quarter share-stabilisation evidence v1 set as the flip condition. AVOID stands, now on two independent gates instead of one, and the price has risen 35% to $56.07 — roughly 66% above a trigger band that is itself materially lower than v1 printed, because v1 built the band on MCap/TBV, a qualifier that never binds.

## 2. Understanding

Unchanged from v1 and still PASS: PayPal Holdings is a US-listed (Delaware) financial-technology company earning volume-based transaction fees from hundreds of millions of consumer accounts and tens of millions of merchants for digital payment processing — branded checkout (PayPal, Venmo), unbranded processing (Braintree), P2P, BNPL, credit and value-added services — across ~200 markets with the US its largest revenue geography, as the incumbent leader in online branded checkout, growing revenue mid-single-digits while losing checkout share to platform wallets. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

**Downgraded to ADEQUATE.** Worst-of-four normalized ROCE (FY2016–25) is **13.2%** (with-cash, end-of-period basis); the four reads are 14.2 / 13.2 / 22.2 / 20.6%. P2 STRONG requires the worst ≥ 15%, so STRONG is unavailable and P12 with it. Worst-of-four FCFROCE is 16.0%, comfortably above the 8% line. Growth is genuinely strong — OI/share compounded 19.1% and FCF/share 12.2% a year over the window, far above the 3% floor, helped by a 21% reduction in share count since 2021; BV/share +6.3%, TBV/share only +1.1% as buybacks consumed tangible equity. Headline L/E is 317% at 30 June 2026, above the P5 flag, but ~$42bn of liabilities are non-interest-bearing customer funds matched by segregated assets; ex-customer-funds leverage is ~100% and the group runs close to net cash. Leverage passes on that judgment (Appendix C). **ADEQUATE.**

## 4. Quality — future

Unchanged: **WEAK**. Breadth — customers PASS (no single customer >10%); suppliers **FAIL**, since transaction expense, the largest cost line, flows to the Visa/Mastercard duopoly and, on the 10-K's own concentration disclosure, two payment processors. No captive-customer waiver is available (it is customer-side only). Forces, re-rendered as the binary P16 calls v1 omitted: customer power **STRONG** (merchants steer and de-prioritise the button; consumers multi-home at zero cost), supplier power **STRONG** (network duopoly prices the largest cost line), substitutes **STRONG** (Apple Pay, Google Pay, Shop Pay, A2A rails, stablecoins), new entrants **WEAK** (licensing and trust barriers are real — but the dangerous entrants are already inside). Three strong forces, versus the two that stop the analysis. Moat: a real two-sided network plus brand trust and ingrained vaulted credentials, direction still **eroding** — Q2'26 shows TPV +10% while transaction-margin dollars grew 1% and GAAP operating margin contracted 171bp, which is the signature of volume migrating to lower-margin unbranded processing. Market growth YES, and that is precisely what drew the platforms in. **WEAK** (breadth FAIL; ≥2 strong forces; eroding moat).

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

**FAIL — P19 hard strike, multiple.** The dual-read worksheet (Appendix E) tests every in-window repurchase year on tranche-year and trailing-three-year normalized OI and FCF. FY2016–FY2022 all breach 20× on both reads of at least one basis; FY2021 is the extreme at 62.2×/77.5× EV/OI and 54.5×/58.8× MCap/FCF against an average repurchase price of ~$225. FY2023 flags (14.0× EV/OI); only FY2024 and FY2025 are clean, and neither reaches the bargain lines. The v3.5.0 redemption clause requires all three limbs: allocator turnover is met (Schulman→Chriss→Lores; Rainey→Miller), but there is no board-level worth-referenced price criterion and no bargain-line or refusal year — partial satisfaction changes nothing. RPTs immaterial; single share class. The buyback programmes state a dilution-offset purpose, but repurchases ran ~6.6× share issuance in FY2025 — not a sterilisation programme, logged not struck.

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Branded-checkout share loss to platform wallets continues as agentic and one-tap checkout become default, hollowing the highest-margin volume even while headline TPV grows.
- Mix shift into unbranded Braintree volume keeps transaction margin compressing — Q2'26 already shows TPV +10% against TM$ +1%.
- A third strategy reset in four years under Lores consumes cash in transformation charges without restoring branded growth.
- Buyback discipline does not change: another full year above the flag lines keeps SF failing well past the window roll.
- Conversely, the bear case on price: if the market re-rates on stabilisation rhetoric, the name drifts further from any band the framework would act on.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

AVOID → WATCH now requires **both** gates, and the SF gate is the slow one. Future flips on four consecutive quarters of branded-checkout TPV at or above e-commerce market growth **with transaction-margin dollars compounding** — Q2'26 supplies management's assertion but not the arithmetic. SF flips only when the struck tranches roll out of the ten-year window (FY2022's strike survives until the FY2031 record) or PayPal satisfies all three v3.5.0 redemption limbs — realistically, a published worth-referenced repurchase policy plus a programme year executed at or below 8× MCap/FCF or 7× EV/OI. Cheapness is not the constraint and would not rescue either gate if it were. Next review: FY2026 results (~early Feb 2027), which drop the FY2016 tranche, reset the OI/FCF base, and re-test the FY2026 repurchase year; earlier only on a published buyback policy change or four clean quarters of branded-checkout evidence.
