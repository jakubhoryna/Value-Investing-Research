## 2. Understanding

Brambles Limited is a Sydney-headquartered pooling company, listed on the ASX since 2006 and operating in about 60 countries with about 12,000 employees. Under the CHEP brand it owns about 352 million reusable wooden and plastic pallets, crates and containers. It issues them from about 750 service centres, then collects, inspects, repairs and reissues them. Consumer-staples manufacturers, fresh-produce growers, retailers and automotive and general manufacturers pay issue, daily-hire and transfer fees, plus compensation for equipment they lose. **PASS.**
