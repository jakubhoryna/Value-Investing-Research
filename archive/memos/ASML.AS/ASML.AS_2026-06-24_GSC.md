# ASML.AS — ASML Holding N.V.: GSC Memo

ASML is worth owning for one of the strongest moats in all of hardware: it is the sole maker of EUV lithography systems, the only machines capable of patterning leading-edge logic and memory, sold to the handful of chipmakers that can afford them. The historical record is exceptional — ex-cash ROCE has run 21–77% every year for a decade while operating income per share compounded ~600% — and capital allocation is rational and owner-aligned. The only thing between here and a BUY is price: after a ~+140% one-year run to near all-time highs, the stock trades at ~52× operating income and ~54× free cash flow, roughly 7× the framework's ceilings. The verdict flips to BUY only on a severe de-rating into the trigger band, or as the band rises with growth; nothing in the quality or shareholder gates needs to change.

## 2. Understanding

ASML designs, manufactures and services photolithography systems — EUV and DUV scanners plus metrology, inspection and computational-lithography software — that print circuit patterns onto silicon wafers; it sells (and services, ~25% of sales) to a small set of leading-edge logic and memory manufacturers (TSMC, Samsung, Intel, SK Hynix, Micron) concentrated in Taiwan, Korea, China and the US, operating as a Dutch-incorporated, dual-listed (Amsterdam/Nasdaq) global technology leader and the undisputed monopoly in EUV. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Decade returns are extraordinary and rising: ex-cash ROCE 21–77% (≥15% in all eleven measured years), ex-cash FCFROCE 13–68%. Operating income/share +607% (2016→2025), FCF/share +732%, tangible book/share +323%; diluted share count fell ~12%. Capital employed is structurally light because customers prepay large deposits (contract liabilities), which inflate non-interest-bearing current liabilities. Leverage briefly flagged (L/E 3.1× in 2022, a buyback-driven equity-compression artifact) but ASML is net cash (≈€8.9bn) and returns are exceptional, clearing the flag under the exceptional-returns carve-out. **STRONG.**

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth FAILs the strict test on both sides: the customer base is a handful of leading-edge chipmakers (and has consolidated as fewer firms reach the leading edge), and the supply base has single points of failure (Zeiss SMT is the sole optics supplier; Cymer the light source). Forces are MIXED — concentrated, powerful customers, but they have no EUV alternative, so pricing power sits with ASML; substitutes (nanoimprint, design shifts) and escalating China export controls are real tail risks. The moat — EUV monopoly, High-NA extending the lead, thousands of patents, the exclusive Zeiss partnership, deep switching costs and fab-workflow ingrainedness — is dominant and stable-to-strengthening. Applying the moat-decides rule, the stable moat floors this at ADEQUATE despite the breadth FAIL; the genuine concentration and tail risks hold it short of STRONG. **ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Pay is a rounding error against profit (CEO €7.0m, total Board of Management €24.3m vs €11.3bn operating income), performance-linked (EBIT margin, relative TSR, ROAIC, technology and ESG metrics; three-year vesting + two-year hold), with say-on-pay carried 91–93%. Governance is clean: two-tier board, no dual-class or control structure, no related-party loans, no severance, clawback in place. Buybacks shrank the share count ~12% over the decade at prices far below today's, and dividends are modest and rational against a net-cash balance sheet. No empire-building M&A. No strike. **PASS.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- Cyclicality: wafer-fab-equipment spending swings hard; a downturn compresses orders and operating income.
- China export controls: China was ~29% of 2025 revenue (by geography) and restrictions on DUV/EUV access are tightening — a live, escalating policy risk.
- Customer concentration: a capex cut or loss at one of a few large chipmakers materially moves results.
- Supplier single points of failure: Zeiss optics and Cymer light sources are irreplaceable in the near term.
- Valuation: at ~52× EV/OI near all-time highs, multiple compression is the dominant threat to forward returns even if the business executes.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

Cheapness is the only failing gate: BUY requires a de-rating into the ~€140 band (improbable absent a severe shock) or growth lifting the band over time — so re-test after each annual result. Past would weaken only on a multi-year ROCE collapse; Future would move toward WEAK if nanoimprint takes real share, the EUV monopoly is genuinely challenged, or export controls structurally shrink the market — and toward STRONG only if breadth concerns eased (unlikely). SF would flip on debt-funded buybacks at any price or value-destructive M&A (none evident). **Next review:** FY2026 results (Jan 2027), a major China export-control ruling, or price entering the trigger band.
