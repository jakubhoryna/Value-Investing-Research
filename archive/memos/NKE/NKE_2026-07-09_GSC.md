## 2. Understanding

Nike designs and markets differentiated athletic footwear (~65% of revenue), apparel and equipment under the Nike, Jordan and Converse brands — manufactured entirely by independent contract factories, principally in Vietnam, Indonesia and China — sold to consumers through owned stores and digital (NIKE Direct, ~38% of revenue) and to wholesale retailers and distributors (~60%), across North America (~44%), EMEA, Greater China and APLA; a 62-year-old, Knight-family-controlled (dual-class), Oregon-based prominent incumbent, currently in a turnaround under a first-year CEO. **PASS.** (Unchanged from v1.)

Market growth YES. **ADEQUATE.**
