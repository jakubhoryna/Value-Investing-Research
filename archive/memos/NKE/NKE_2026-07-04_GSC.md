# NKE — NIKE, Inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

|Understanding|Past|Future|Shareholder-friendliness|Cheapness|FINAL|
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
|PASS|ADEQUATE|ADEQUATE|PASS (flags)|EXPENSIVE|**WATCH**|

Nike is the book's own exemplar of a brand moat, and the record shows why: worst-of-four normalized ROCE of 20.9% over FY2016–25 with a capital-light, fully outsourced model. What it is not is a growth business — OI/share went nowhere in nine years — and it is mid-way through a self-inflicted reset (DTC overreach, lifestyle-franchise fatigue, China) under a new CEO, down ~75% from the 2021 peak. Even so, at $44 the primaries sit at ~2.5× their thresholds: the fall is from an extreme price, not to a cheap one. Between here and a BUY stands roughly a 60–75% further decline, or a doubling of OI toward the FY2021 level with the price flat. Verdict flips to AVOID if the brand's slide vs On/Hoka/New Balance proves structural rather than cyclical, or if Hill's first full-year compensation crosses the $30m ceiling.

## 2. Understanding

Nike designs and markets differentiated athletic footwear (~65% of revenue), apparel and equipment under the Nike, Jordan and Converse brands — manufactured entirely by independent contract factories, principally in Vietnam, Indonesia and China — sold to consumers through owned stores and digital (NIKE Direct, ~38% of revenue) and to wholesale retailers and distributors (~60%), across North America (~44%), EMEA, Greater China and APLA; a 62-year-old, Knight-family-controlled (dual-class), Oregon-based prominent incumbent, currently in a turnaround under a first-year CEO. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Return levels clear STRONG thresholds: worst-of-four 10y normalized ROCE **20.9%** (≥15%) and FCFROCE **17.4%** (≥8%); ROCE_xc averaged ~33%. Growth fails: OI/share geo-mean **−0.6%/yr** FY2016→25 (under the 3% floor), FCF/share +3.4%, BV/TBV/share +2.6% against ~$45B of dividends and buybacks (diluted shares −15%). Returns ended the window at decade lows (FY2025 ROCE_wc 12.8%, off 29.2% in FY2021), a decline examined and confirmed as the second bar to STRONG. Leverage fine: L/E 177% latest (P5 200%); FY2020 COVID spike to 289% was transitory. **ADEQUATE** — elite return levels on a shrinking earnings base.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth **PASS** both sides: 10-K states no customer ≥10% of revenues; supply is 15 footwear + 67 apparel contract manufacturers, largest single footwear factory ~11% of footwear production (≈4–5% of COGS+opex) with ready alternative vendors. Forces: customer power WEAK (fragmented consumers; wholesalers need Nike more than the reverse); supplier power WEAK (OEMs lack brand/distribution to integrate forward); substitutes WEAK (no different-function replacement for athletic shoes); **new entrants STRONG** — On and Hoka scaled to multi-billion brands within a decade; outsourced manufacture and digital DTC have lowered both entry and success barriers. Tally 1 strong, contained by the moat: **Brand** (Jordan alone $7.0B FY2026), dominant and stable at its core though its heat has weakened at the lifestyle edge. Market growth YES (global sportswear). **ADEQUATE.**

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

Comp: highest-paid FY2025 was departing CEO Donahoe at **$28.4m** — under the $30m ceiling he breached in FY2023 ($32.8m); Hill's stub-year $26.0m annualizes uncomfortably close (5%-of-FCF test irrelevant at ~$161m). Top outside director Cook $375k < $500k stop. RPT trivial (Knight Chairman-Emeritus salary $500k; one family hire $127k). Buybacks far exceed SBC sterilization (shares −15%) but were executed at ~20–25× EV/OI in FY2022–24 and paused ($123m) at FY2026's lows — flagged, not struck per META/GOOGL precedent. Dividend raised 24 straight years. Dual-class Swoosh LLC control: documented caveat. **PASS.**

## C. Calculation notes

- **OI derivation.** Nike reports no operating-income subtotal; OI = gross profit − total selling & administrative expense (demand creation + operating overhead), every year. Interest and other income/expense excluded. No add-backs: FY2025 OI carries restructuring/severance in overhead; FY2026 prelim OI includes a **$986M IEEPA tariff-recovery accrual** that per the 8-K "largely offsets" tariffs expensed earlier in FY2026 — treated as-reported, roughly tariff-neutral on the year, but it is a receivable (AR +26% to $5.9B), not cash.
- **CE build, FY2025 (latest audited, $M):** CE_wc = TA 36,579 − NIBCL 10,059 = **26,520**; NIBCL = AP 3,479 + accrued liabilities 5,911 + income taxes payable 669. Left in CE (burden of proof / debt-equivalent): current operating-lease liabilities 502, current LTD, all "deferred income taxes and other liabilities". CE_xc = 26,520 − (cash 7,464 + STI 1,687) = **17,369**. Goodwill ($240M) left in: immaterial and Nike is non-acquisitive.
- **Pre-ASC842 lease capitalization (P18).** Undiscounted operating-lease commitments ran 13.8–17.6% of total assets FY2015–FY2019 (> 10% trigger). PV at **3.0% (assumption**, within Nike's 2016–19 senior-note coupon range 2.375–3.875%), beyond-five-years lump run off at the year-5 rate: PVs $2,600 / 2,828 / 3,383 / 3,454 / 3,256M for FY2015–19. CE raised by the PV (right-of-use asset add); OI raised by imputed interest (3% × beginning PV: +78/+85/+101/+104M FY2016–19); FCF = CFO − capex + rent (661/731/820/829M) − imputed interest. Symmetry note: this recast makes pre-842 FCF richer than post-842 FCF by roughly the lease-principal component (~$0.6B/yr), since post-842 operating-lease payments remain fully inside CFO; the worst-of-four FCFROCE (17.4%) still clears the 8% bar by 2× on unadjusted figures. Post-842 (FY2020→): balance sheets already capitalize; lease liabilities ($3,052M FY2025; $3,091M FY2026) treated as debt-equivalent in CE, EV and L/E.
- **TBV build FY2026:** equity 14,865 − goodwill 240 − identifiable intangibles 259 = **14,366** ($9.70/sh on 1,481M diluted).
- **EV build, 2026-05-31 BS ($M):** debt = current LTD 2,000 + LTD 5,942 + operating leases 3,091 = 11,033; redeemable preferred $— ; no NCI. All-cash-excess EV = 65,297 + 11,033 − 9,027 (cash 7,563 + STI 1,464) = **67,303**; no-cash-excess EV = **76,330**.
- **FY2026 status.** Ten-year window is the audited FY2016–FY2025 record; FY2026 (unaudited 8-K) supplies the valuation-side OI and balance sheet per §4 timeliness rule. FY2026 FCF is not derivable (no CF statement); the implied ~$2.4B is an inference from the cash walk, labeled as such, not used in the band.
