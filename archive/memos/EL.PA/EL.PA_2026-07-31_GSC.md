# EL.PA — EssilorLuxottica: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding PASS · Past WEAK · Future STRONG · Shareholder-friendliness FAIL · Cheapness EXPENSIVE → **AVOID** (two independent gate failures: Past and SF).

This is a genuinely dominant franchise — the only vertically integrated global eyecare/eyewear platform, with Ray-Ban, Oakley, Varilux, 18,000 stores and the lead position in AI glasses — earning returns on capital that do not clear the framework's floor: €40.8bn of goodwill and intangibles from serial dealmaking holds worst-of-four normalized ROCE at 4.5%. The capital-allocation record independently fails: every year since 2020 the company repurchased shares at 22–37× implied EV/OI to feed employee share plans, and it paid ~27× pre-deal operating income for GrandVision. A ~45% drawdown to the 52-week low does not bridge a trigger band that sits ~85–90% below the current price. A flip requires either returns on capital roughly doubling (structurally implausible on this asset base before the window rolls far into the 2030s) or is barred outright by the standing buyback pattern — the Feb-2026 programme repeats it at €211.51 average.

## 2. Understanding
EssilorLuxottica designs, manufactures and distributes differentiated branded ophthalmic lenses, frames, sunglasses and now AI wearables, selling to consumers through ~18,000 own stores and e-commerce (Direct-to-Consumer, ~half of revenue) and to independent eyecare professionals, opticians and retail chains (Professional Solutions); it is a French SA controlled de facto by Delfin S.à r.l. (32.4% of capital, formally its parent), vertically integrated from R&D and production (Italy, France, China) through wholesale, retail and vision-care insurance (EyeMed); revenue is global (North America largest, then EMEA, Asia, LatAm), HQ Paris/Charenton; it is the industry's #1 by a wide margin, formed October 2018 from Essilor (1849) and Luxottica (1961), currently repositioning as a "med-tech" and smart-eyewear platform with Meta. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record
Window FY2019–FY2025, 7 years, history-limited (combined entity exists only from Oct 2018); STRONG unavailable, moot. Per-share growth is healthy off the 2019 base (ΔOI/sh geo +11.4%, ΔFCF/sh +6.9%) and leverage modest (L/E 50–67%, TBV negative on acquisition goodwill). Returns level decides: **WEAK** (below P3).

## 4. Quality — future
Breadth PASS both sides: atomized consumers/ECP base, no 10% customer; vertically integrated supply with broad input vendors (acetate associate Mazzucchelli). Forces: customer power WEAK, supplier power WEAK, substitutes WEAK (corrective eyewear structurally required; contacts/surgery long-standing and contained; smart glasses are currently EL's own growth vector), new entrants WEAK (brand + scale + retail footprint + vertical integration). Tally 0 strong. Moat: **brand** (Ray-Ban, Oakley, Varilux, channel ingrainedness), durable and strengthening via the Ray-Ban-Meta lead. Market growth YES (presbyopia/myopia, wearables). **STRONG.**

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers
- Wearables concentration: the growth narrative runs substantially through one partner (Meta) whose devices could commoditise EL's hardware role.
- Med-tech transformation (Nuance Audio, Optegra clinics, AI diagnostics) is capital-consuming with unproven returns — the ROCE problem may deepen.
- Delfin control (32.4%, Milleri chairs both) aligns with long-term building but not with minority-friendly capital allocation, as the buyback record shows.
- Tariff and FX exposure on a US-heavy revenue base; Q2-26 already leaned on tariff refunds.
- GLP-1/none identified for eyewear demand, but myopia-management reimbursement changes could slow the highest-margin growth line.
