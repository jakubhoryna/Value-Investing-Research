## C. Calculation notes

**Lens.** STD: no banking or insurance licence at group level and no consolidated prudential supervision; the trust company and broker-dealer serve the company's own products.

**J1 OI line.** The GAAP net operating income (restructuring $177.3M, acquisition amortisation and impairments and share-based pay all inside) is the lowest operating subtotal; the adjusted line excludes recurring charges and is never OI.

**Financial income out (J3).** Non-operating income already sits below OI. Inside OI, three items leave, each where positive in the fiscal year (a net loss stays): (1) capital allocation-based income — the equity-method result of the GP interests in affiliated OHA funds, the accrued carried-interest change net of the basis-difference amortisation and impairment the filer books in the same line (the class nets per year); its pass-through compensation (2025 58.5) stays in OI as a recurring operating charge; (2) fair-value gains on the OHA earn-out liability booked in operating expenses; (3) supplemental savings plan market credits inside compensation (the costs of other years stay). In CFO: dividends earned on T.
