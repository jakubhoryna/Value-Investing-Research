# LULU — lululemon athletica inc.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict

| Gate | Understanding | Past | Future | SF | Cheapness | FINAL |
|------|---------------|------|--------|----|-----------|-------|
| Result | PASS | ADEQUATE | **WEAK** | PASS | FAIR | **AVOID** |

lululemon is a genuinely high-return business — worst-of-four normalized ROCE ~33%, ~56% gross margins, no net debt, a real brand — trading, after a ~77% fall from its 2024 peak, at a cheap EV/OI (5.3–6.1×). It nonetheless fails the model at **Gate 1B (Future)**: premium activewear now carries two strong forces — abundant zero-switching-cost **substitutes** (dupes, Alo, Vuori, Athleta, Nike) and demonstrably **low barriers to new-entrant success** (Alo and Vuori each scaled from nothing to ~$1B+ this decade) — and the brand moat, while intact internationally, is **eroding in the core US market** (FY2025 Americas comps −3%, product margin −340bps). Two strong forces is the book's stop signal. **This verdict pivots on the new-entrants call**: an analyst who judges lululemon's scale/community/vertical retail as dulling that threat lands at one strong force → Future ADEQUATE → **WATCH** (trigger ~$76–96 normalized). I resolve the doubt toward AVOID per the model's "when in doubt, quit." What would flip it to WATCH: US comps stabilising and dupe/new-entrant share loss abating — evidence the moat is holding, not receding.

## 2. Understanding

lululemon designs and sells premium technical athletic and lifestyle apparel, footwear, and accessories (differentiated branded goods) directly to individual retail "guests" via ~811 company-operated stores and e-commerce plus limited wholesale/licensing — a vertically integrated, single-share-class Delaware retailer HQ'd in Vancouver, sourcing from Asia (40% of product from Vietnam, 34% of fabric from Taiwan), revenue ~71% Americas / 16% China Mainland / 13% RoW, a large-cap (~$13.5B) ~19-year public company now past its US growth peak, with a CEO transition and a founder-led proxy contest live. **PASS.**

## 3. Quality — past record

Returns are exceptional: worst-of-four normalized ROCE **33.1%**, FCFROCE **18.1%** (thresholds 15%/8%); annual ROCE_xc ran 46–66%. OI/share compounded **+14.8%/yr** and TBV/share **+21%/yr**; leverage is conservative (L/E 0.70, essentially all operating leases, zero interest-bearing debt). Two things bar STRONG: (i) only **5 fiscal years** were analysed (FY2021–25) — below the 10-year window, which caps the rating; and (ii) **FCF/share growth −0.8%/yr**, under the 3% floor (FCF is depressed by working-capital swings and FY2025's first-year tariff hit, not structural). Verdict **ADEQUATE** — returns would support STRONG on a full, clean-FCF record. lululemon's pre-2021 history (public since 2007) was one of high returns and rapid growth; not re-verified here.

## 4. Quality — future

Breadth **PASS** (millions of guests, no >10% customer; ~51 manufacturers + 65 fabric suppliers, largest single supplier <10% of COGS+opex — though specialty-fabric and Taiwan concentration are flagged). Forces (binary): customer power **WEAK** (atomised consumers, pricing power), supplier power **WEAK** (many vendors, low switching, lululemon a desirable buyer), **substitutes STRONG** (dupes + every major brand, zero switching cost, value gap closing), **new entrants STRONG** (low entry barriers, weak fabric IP, Alo/Vuori proved rapid scaling). Tally **2 strong → WEAK**. Moat: **Brand** (verified premium pricing, ~56% GM, Morningstar "narrow moat"), direction **stable internationally, eroding in the US**. Market: **MIXED** (category grows mid-single-digit; US maturing/contested, China +29%). Verdict **WEAK** (both the ≥2-strong-forces and the eroding-moat conditions apply).

## 5. Shareholder-friendliness

CEO total comp **$14.6M** (FY2024; $16.5M/$15.7M prior) — well under the $30M and 5%-of-lower(FCF,NI)=$46M strikes, mostly performance-linked; highest outside director ~$434k (< $500k). **No material related-party transactions** disclosed; no dividend (rational given high ROCE). Verdict **PASS**, with caveats: buybacks of **$2.8B in FY2024–25 at ~$236–313 avg** (stock now $119) sit above the model's conservative value — a real capital-allocation concern, though FCF-funded and count-reducing (111M vs 122M shares), not dilution-sterilisation; management owns **<1%** (founder Wilson holds 8.4% but is an adversary); the Mirror/Studio deal (~$500M, ~$440M written off, exited) was a corrected misstep; and a classified board + anti-takeover provisions sit under an active founder proxy fight. A strict reading of the buyback test could score a strike (→ FAIL); it does not change the final verdict.

Cheapness **FAIR**. Trigger band: **CHEAP below ≈ $64** on reported FY2025 FCF, **≈ $76–96** on 5–3yr normalized FCF (binding: MCap/FCF); the EV/OI limit sits far higher (~$134). No P12 edge (Past ADEQUATE). Qualifiers not distorted (goodwill/intangibles only ~$191M).

## 7. Risks & thesis-breakers

- **Substitution/new entrants** structurally strong — dupes and well-funded challengers (Alo, Vuori) are taking US share and compressing the brand premium.
- **US demand deterioration** (Americas comps −3%, traffic and conversion down) may be structural maturation, not a fixable soft patch.
- **Tariffs**: FY2025 gross profit hit ~$275M; management expects further FY2026 gross- and operating-margin decline; policy is volatile post the Feb-2026 Supreme Court IEEPA ruling.
- **Leadership vacuum**: CEO departed Jan 2026, interim co-CEOs, permanent search open, founder proxy contest — strategy execution risk at the worst time.
- **Capital-allocation discipline**: continued buybacks above conservative value would compound the FY2024–25 overpayment.

## 8. Flip conditions & next review

To **WATCH**: reclassify new entrants as WEAK (scale/brand dulls the threat) → Future ADEQUATE — defensible today and the single hinge of this verdict; or evidence the US moat has stopped eroding (comps back to positive, share stabilising). To **BUY** thereafter: price into the trigger (~$76–96 normalized / ~$64 reported) with returns intact. To a firmer **AVOID**: a third year of US comp/margin decline confirming structural moat erosion. **Next review:** fiscal 2026 results, appointment of a permanent CEO, or a US comparable-sales inflection — whichever comes first; also price into the normalized trigger band.

## D. Future-performance worksheet

**Breadth.** Customer: **PASS** — B2C retail, millions of guests, no single customer >10% (women's/men's/accessories = 63/24/13% of revenue; channels store/e-comm/other = 45/44/10%). Supplier: **PASS (flagged)** — ~51 product vendors (top-5 47%, largest 15% of products) and ~65 fabric suppliers (top-5 48%, largest 20% of fabric); converting to COGS+opex, no single supplier exceeds ~10%; ready alternatives exist for most categories, but specialty technical fabrics are "available short-term from only one or a limited number of sources," there are no long-term contracts, and 34% of fabric originates in **Taiwan** (geopolitical single-point risk). No captive-customer waiver used.

| Force | Call | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Customer power | WEAK | Atomised individual consumers; no backward integration; historical full-price selling. |

Not dominant/stable enough to neutralise the substitute and entrant forces — hence no rescue of the force calls; the eroding-moat condition independently supports WEAK.

**Market growth.** **MIXED** — global activewear grows mid-single-digit; lululemon's core US market is maturing and contested while China/international remain a large runway.
