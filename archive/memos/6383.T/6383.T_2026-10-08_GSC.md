# 6383.T — Daifuku Co., Ltd.: GSC Memo

## 1. Verdict
Understanding **FAIL** (one-level decomposition: a material "Other" unit) → **DO NOT ANALYSE FURTHER**. Past, Future, SF and cheapness were not run.

Daifuku itself is not hard to understand. It is one business, material-handling automation, reported as six company-based segments. The problem is the sixth. Daifuku reports a residual segment called "Other", made up of the subsidiaries outside its five reportable segments. In FY12/25 it carried 22.7% of external sales, 16.8% of positive segment income and 17.4% of segment assets, and H1 2026 shows it at 15.4% of segment income. MODEL v4.5.x fails Gate 0 for a material "Other". Daifuku's own filing describes this "Other" in one sentence as the same material-handling business. The DNAF therefore rests on the plain text of the rule, read the harsher way under J13, and not on any incomprehension. The verdict flips back to a full analysis if the segment reporting removes the material "Other", or if a MODEL ruling says that a filer-described, single-business "Other" is tested like any other unit. On that second reading the v4.5.0 re-test reopens: the TODO line expected AVOID → WATCH (55%) under R7.

## 2. Understanding
**Group sentence.** Daifuku designs, manufactures, installs and services automated material-handling systems: storage, sortation and conveying for distribution centres, retailers and factories, overhead transport for semiconductor and flat-panel fabs, automotive assembly lines and airport baggage handling. Its customers are organisations that buy these systems as capital projects. It is a vertically integrated engineer-manufacturer-integrator headquartered in Osaka, founded in 1937, and among the global leaders in its field. Daifuku acquired Eisenmann GmbH (Germany; painting, surface-treatment and material-handling systems for automotive lines) on 1 July 2026.

| Other | subsidiaries outside the reportable segments that combine the parent's components with local parts to sell, install and service material-handling systems | 22.7% | 16.8% | 17.4% | **yes** |

Each material unit can be described in one six-parameter sentence. The filer describes the parent, North America and Suzhou as above. For "Other", its securities report says the "other consolidated companies combine the components of material handling systems delivered by the Company and other parts locally produced or procured to sell, install and provide after-sales services".

**Gate 0 test.** The per-unit route fails on one point. Under the rule, "an irreducible portfolio unit above that line, or a material 'Other', fails Gate 0", and Daifuku's "Other" is above the P22 line on all three measures: sales 149,994 of 661,225; income 17,379 of 103,468; assets 150,458 of 863,517 (¥m). The text sets no exception for an "Other" the filer describes. Where a rule allows two readings, J13 makes the harsher one govern, so the describable-residual reading is not taken here. It is logged for Jakub, not applied. **FAIL → DNAF.**

The lens test (STD on the evidence: not a licensed bank or insurer, no material financial part) and the group dependency check (Contec, the one unit run as a separate company group, is immaterial at 3.1% of sales and 3.3% of assets, whatever its listing status, which was not checked) were not needed for the verdict.
