A 31-year-old Paris proprietary trading house running systematic arbitrage on its own €170m of capital, with a €245m third-party fund business it has never managed to grow. Owners are treated well — 40% insider ownership, a chairman paid €0.4m against €25m of profit, no related-party dealings, four-fifths of earnings distributed — and the capital has returned 15–16% for a decade with no debt.
