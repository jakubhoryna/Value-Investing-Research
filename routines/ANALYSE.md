2. **Pick.** No web work. A key qualifies when it passes all of these checks:

Write `queue/rows/{D}_{T}_ANALYSE.md` (`row_type: QUEUE`) only if there are prior or retired markers to record, plus the run note. Finish the PR as `GSC ANALYSE {D} — queue exhausted` and stop.

   - **Gate 0 is the best sentence the filings allow (MODEL §2 Gate 0) [v4.6.0; G1, G2].** Read the business review, the segment note and the product and customer disclosures until the six-parameter sentence can be written, naming the products, paying customers and industries as specifically as the filings allow, in the plain, specific word rather than the filer's label.      - **The only FAIL (P30)** is a listed business that cannot be understood from its filings after the best attempt. The test reads the latest annual report's business description, segment note and revenue disclosures (with the interim and the filer's investor materials where they add disclosure) and asks one question: **do the filings show what the company sells, and who pays for it, for more than half of its revenue?** Incomprehension: the filings disclose, but what they disclose does not let the analyst state the products or the paying customers of more than half of revenue in plain words. Obfuscation: the filer withholds what would answer the question, so that no segment, product, customer or related-party disclosure lets more than half of revenue be described by product and paying customer. Both are observable facts about the filings, never a judgment of intent or of how profitable the business is. The §6.4 memo states `Gate 0: FAIL — incomprehension: {parameter}` or `Gate 0: FAIL — obfuscation: {what is withheld}`, with its evidence, the best sentence attempted and the documents searched.

     - **STD** for every other company, however financial the product: non-bank and BNPL lenders, payment and e-money institutions, unlicensed brokers, leasing companies, asset managers, exchanges, utilities, and every non-financial group with a captive finance arm. It also covers what the retired too-hard pile stopped: (a) a bank or insurer that does not fit FI; (b) a group with a material unit or controlled subsidiary that is a licensed insurer, or a licensed bank funded mainly by customer deposits, and is not a captive finance arm; (c) total assets more than half unlisted non-controlled stakes, or securities that are neither cash-like (S1/S2) nor material listed holdings. STD reads them on its own conservative rules, never a softer one:
       - deposits, policyholder and insurance-contract liabilities and every other funding of a deposit-taking or insurance activity count in L/E as liabilities;

An insurance activity's insurance-contract liabilities, unearned premiums included, are float funding and never NIBCL, on both readings.
       - **The P31 test.** A class is the category summed (all associates and JVs, all S3, the insurance portfolio), and the classes are tested together. They are read on exclude both alone (income out of OI and FCF, J3; carrying value out of CE in both cash variants) where both limbs hold: at every balance-sheet date B2 uses (the eleven of the P1 window, the opening one included; the record that exists, if shorter), their summed carrying value is under 5% of consolidated total assets; and in every fiscal year of the window, the absolute value of the include reading's OI less the exclude reading's OI (the classes' income, with any net fair-value, disposal or impairment loss on class assets that the include reading keeps) is under 5% of the exclude reading's OI, a year with exclude-reading OI ≤ 0 failing this limb unless that difference is nil. Below the line, a class's income and its fair-value, disposal and impairment results leave OI and FCF whatever their sign.
       - **Above the line, two readings, every class together (none mixed between readings).** Exclude both, as above: the income and the fair-value, disposal and impairment results of class assets out whatever their sign. Include both: the equity-accounted result as reported (after the investee's interest and tax, never grossed up) and the interest and dividend income are in OI, added where the filer books them below its operating line; the cash they brought in is in FCF (dividends, distributions or interest the filer classifies in investing moved into CFO where the cash-flow statement shows them; a return of capital and discount accretion collected at maturity not added; nothing added where the filer does not show where a receipt sits, logged); the carrying value stays in CE in both cash variants. Fair-value changes and disposal results of class assets stay out of OI and FCF, except that on the include reading a net loss on them (a fair-value, disposal or impairment loss on the carrying value) is in OI, added where the filer books it below its operating line or in net financial items, as the include reading's income is (J3's one-way rule; J13).
